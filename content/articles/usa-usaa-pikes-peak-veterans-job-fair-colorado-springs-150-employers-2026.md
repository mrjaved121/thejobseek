**US hiring 2026** continues in Colorado. **Pikes Peak Workforce Center** and **USAA** will host the annual **Community & Veterans Job Fair** on **Tuesday 10 November 2026** in Colorado Springs, with **up to 150 employers** ready to talk to job seekers about current openings. Anyone looking for work can attend.

## When and where is the job fair?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Date | Tuesday, 10 November 2026 |
| Time | 10am to 2pm |
| Venue | Colorado Springs Event Center, 3960 Palmer Park Blvd., Colorado Springs, CO 80909 |
| Employers | Up to 150 |
| Who can attend | Veterans, transitioning service members, military spouses and the wider community |

## Which industries are hiring?

The workforce centre says employers will come from **a variety of industries**. The full, up-to-date list of participating employers is published on the official event page. Attendees can speak directly with hiring managers about real vacancies.

## How should I prepare?

- **Create or update your ConnectingColorado.gov account** before the event, as the workforce centre recommends.
- Check the employer list and pick the companies you most want to meet.
- Bring several copies of your CV and a short introduction about your skills.
- Veterans: be ready to explain how your military experience fits civilian roles.

Pikes Peak Workforce Center, the American Job Center for El Paso and Teller counties, also offers free CV help, career coaching and mock interviews.

## How do I apply?

Go to the job fair and register through the official **[event page](https://ppwfc.org/community-veterans-job-fair-2026/)**, where the employer list is kept up to date.

## What it means for job seekers

- **One day, many employers.** Job fairs are a fast way to get in front of hiring managers.
- **Free career support is available** before and after the fair.
- **Military spouses are welcome** and often find flexible roles at these events.
