**US hiring in 2026** is getting a boost in advanced manufacturing. Pittsburgh 3D printing company **Tronix3D** will spend nearly **$4 million** to expand its plant and create **47 new full-time jobs** over three years, Pennsylvania Governor **Josh Shapiro's** office announced on **30 October 2026**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Tronix3D |
| Location | 6901 Lynn Way, Pittsburgh, PA |
| Investment | Nearly $4 million |
| New jobs | 47 full-time, over 3 years |
| Jobs kept | 18 |
| State support | $200,000 PA First grant |

## What is changing

Tronix3D is moving up a floor in its building, from the third to the fourth, and growing its production space from **3,700 to 12,000 square feet**. The bigger shop floor will let it take on more work for **defence and robotics** customers. The company also makes parts for the automotive, aerospace and medical sectors.

The state's **$200,000 Pennsylvania First grant** will help pay for new machinery and equipment.

## Likely roles

The announcement did not list job titles, but a growing additive manufacturing plant typically needs:

- 3D printing / additive manufacturing technicians
- CNC machinists and finishing operators
- Quality inspectors
- Manufacturing and design engineers
- Production supervisors and logistics staff

## Why it matters

3D printing lets companies make complex parts quickly and in small batches, which is valuable for defence and robotics projects. As more manufacturers adopt it, workers who understand both the machines and the design software are becoming harder to find, and that pushes up demand and pay.

## How to apply

No official careers page for these roles was published with the announcement. Interested candidates should watch Tronix3D's own website and official social channels for vacancies, and check Pennsylvania's free state job service, PA CareerLink, for listings.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Defence work is driving hiring.** Firms with defence contracts are adding staff across the US.
- **Hands-on skills count.** Experience with 3D printers, CNC machines or CAD software is valuable.
- **Pittsburgh is a growing hub** for robotics and advanced manufacturing.
- **Hiring is spread over three years,** so there may be several waves of openings.
