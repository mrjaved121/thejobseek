**Hyundai Translead**, the trailer-making arm of Hyundai Motor Company, has raised its hiring target in Illinois to **3,750 full-time jobs**. Governor **JB Pritzker** announced the bigger plan in Joliet on **22 October 2026**. The company had first promised about **2,500 jobs** when it unveiled the project in March.

The investment has also grown, from about $450 million to **$600 million**. The company says demand has been strong enough to add a **third shift**, which is where most of the extra jobs come from.

## What kind of work is coming

These are factory jobs in heavy manufacturing. The official announcement does not list job titles or pay, but trailer production normally needs:

- Production and assembly workers
- Welders and fabricators
- Maintenance and industrial technicians
- Forklift, logistics and warehouse staff
- Quality control, supervisors and engineers

The state's own training plan gives a clue about the most needed skills. Illinois is paying for a new **Manufacturing Training Academy** at **Joliet Junior College** that will train industrial maintenance and advanced manufacturing technicians and **double the college's welding capacity**.

## Why it matters for the region

The old Caterpillar site in Joliet once employed about 6,500 people, and local leaders see the project as a return of large-scale factory work. Both sites sit close to major freight routes, and the company says hundreds of its customers are within a short drive.

Across Illinois, companies in the state's EDGE incentive programme have now promised about **6,000 new jobs** in 2026.

## How to apply

No public application date has been given. Job seekers should:

1. Watch the official Hyundai Translead website, [hyundaitranslead.com](https://www.hyundaitranslead.com/), for careers updates.
2. Search for openings on the state's free job portal, [Illinois JobLink](https://illinoisjoblink.illinois.gov/).
3. Contact [Joliet Junior College](https://www.jjc.edu/) about welding and manufacturing courses linked to the new academy.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Welding is a strong ticket in.** A recognised welding certificate is likely to stand out with this employer.
- **Shift work is part of the deal.** A third shift means evening and night hours, which can suit people who want higher shift pay.
- **Get local training early.** The college academy is being built for these plants, so finishing a course now puts you ahead when hiring peaks.
- **Live nearby or plan to.** Will County, south-west of Chicago, is the hiring area.

This adds to a run of big US factory announcements this year, including [Lockheed Martin's plan for 6,000 new US jobs](/usa/usa-lockheed-martin-6000-jobs-munitions-expansion-2026/).
