Telford aviation equipment maker **Aviramp** is hiring **50 new staff** in its biggest recruitment drive to date, Shropshire Live reported on 27 October 2026. The roles are in **installation and assembly**, and some involve travelling to airports overseas, making this a strong lead for anyone searching for **UK jobs for foreigners** and locals with hands-on skills.

## About the company

Aviramp builds **step-free boarding ramps** that let passengers walk on and off aircraft without stairs. It was set up in **2011** by Graham Corfield and is based at Stafford Park in Telford, Shropshire. By the end of 2025, more than **950 of its ramps** were in service at airports across America, Asia, Europe and Australasia.

After a strong 2025, the business is moving from its current headquarters to **larger premises**. The new site and the moving date have not yet been announced.

## The 50 roles

| Detail | What we know |
|---|---|
| Number of jobs | 50 |
| Main job areas | International installation, assembly |
| Factory work | Finishing, assembling and preparing ramps for shipping |
| Field work | Travelling to install ramps at airports worldwide |
| Location | Telford, Shropshire |
| Salary | Not published |

Commercial director **Wayne Lawrence** said the company needs more people to reach its next stage of growth.

## Who might suit these jobs

- **Assembly technicians** and **fabricators** with factory or workshop experience
- **Installation engineers** who are happy to travel abroad for work
- **Logistics and dispatch staff** for packing and shipping finished ramps
- Mechanically minded people with experience in **aviation ground equipment** or vehicle building

## A note for overseas applicants

Aviramp has not said whether it will sponsor visas. If you are outside the UK, you will need the right to work here before you can take one of these jobs. You can check whether any employer holds a sponsor licence on the UK government's public register of licensed sponsors. People already in the UK on a visa that allows work can apply now.

## How to apply

All of the new vacancies are listed on Aviramp's official careers page: **[aviramp.com/career](https://aviramp.com/career/)**. Read each job description for location, shift pattern and travel requirements.
