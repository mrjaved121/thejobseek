**Manama jobs** in manufacturing are growing. **Olayan Kimberly-Clark** has added a **new production line** at its Bahrain plant, and says the expansion has helped grow its workforce by **more than 10% in 2026**, with **73%** of staff being Bahraini. The news was published on 3 November 2026.

## What has Olayan Kimberly-Clark announced?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Olayan Kimberly-Clark Bahrain |
| Change | New production line at the Bahrain facility |
| Workforce growth (2026) | More than 10% |
| Bahrainisation rate | 73% |
| Products | Kimberly-Clark brand products |
| Markets | Bahrain and regional customers |

The extra line raises production capacity, makes operations more flexible and strengthens the supply chain, supporting Bahrain's industrial growth plans.

## What did the company say?

Plant Manager **Khaldoun Tahboub** described the expansion as a proud moment for the team, reflecting years of work and a shared aim of building a world-class manufacturing operation that creates opportunities for people.

## Which jobs do plants like this hire for?

Consumer-products factories typically recruit:

- Machine and production line operators
- Maintenance and electrical technicians
- Quality control inspectors
- Warehouse, logistics and forklift staff
- Engineers and shift supervisors

These are typical roles for this type of plant, not a confirmed vacancy list.

## How do I apply?

Olayan Kimberly-Clark has not announced a specific recruitment drive. Bahraini job seekers can register with the Ministry of Labour's national employment services and check Tamkeen's programmes, which often support manufacturing hiring.
