**Falcon Voyage Construction** is hiring skilled tradesmen and site workers for construction projects in the **United Arab Emirates**. The company is looking for **plumbers, electricians, helpers and general labour staff** to work on sites in **several emirates**. Candidates who are already in the UAE and candidates living abroad are both welcome to apply.

Selected workers will receive an **employment visa**, **accommodation** and **transport** to and from the work sites.

## Jobs available

| Position | Typical work on site |
|---|---|
| **Plumber** | Installing and repairing water supply and drainage pipes, fitting bathrooms and kitchens, and testing for leaks |
| **Electrician** | Laying cables and conduits, installing wiring, sockets, lights and distribution boards, and fault-finding |
| **Helper** | Supporting plumbers, electricians and other tradesmen, carrying tools and materials, and preparing work areas |
| **Labour staff** | General construction site work: loading and unloading materials, cleaning and clearing the site, and basic manual tasks |

The exact tasks depend on each project. They will be explained at the interview.

## Who should apply

- Plumbers and electricians with hands-on experience on building or construction sites
- Helpers and labourers who are physically fit and ready for outdoor site work
- Workers who can join soon, whether they are inside the UAE now or abroad

Experience on construction sites in the UAE or elsewhere in the Gulf is useful, but helpers and labour staff can also apply if they are willing to learn on the job.

## How to apply

Send an email to **[info@thejobseek.com](mailto:info@thejobseek.com?subject=Job%20application%20-%20Falcon%20Voyage%20Construction)** with these documents attached:

1. **Your CV** (PDF or Word), with your phone and WhatsApp number
2. **A copy of your passport** (front page)
3. **A recent passport-size photo**
4. **Trade certificates or experience letters**, if you have them
5. **If you are in the UAE:** a copy of your current visa or Emirates ID, and say whether you can transfer or are on a visit/cancelled visa

**Email subject:** write the job and your name, for example *"Electrician – Mohammed Ali – in Sharjah"* or *"Plumber – Ramesh Kumar – Nepal"*. This helps your application reach the right person quickly.

Shortlisted candidates will be contacted by phone or email for an interview. TheJobSeek forwards applications sent to this email only to Falcon Voyage Construction.

## Stay safe: applying is free

- **Never pay for this job.** Applying, the interview, the job offer and the UAE work visa are **free** for the worker. Under UAE rules, the employer pays for the work permit.
- **Only send your documents to info@thejobseek.com.** Ignore anyone who contacts you on WhatsApp or social media asking for money, a "processing fee" or a "visa fee" in the name of this job.
- **Check your offer before you travel.** A genuine offer will show your job title, salary and benefits. Your contract is registered with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE).

Read: [warning signs of a fake UAE job offer](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/) and [the 13 types of UAE work permit](/uae/uae-work-permit-types-mohre-13-explained/).

## What it means for job seekers

- **Good option for tradesmen moving to the UAE:** with visa, accommodation and transport provided, your main living costs on arrival are covered.
- **Know your rights on site:** UAE law limits working hours to 8 a day, overtime is paid extra, and outdoor work stops in the midday heat in summer. See [UAE working hours and overtime rules](/uae/uae-working-hours-overtime-pay-rules/).
- **Keep copies** of your offer letter, contract and visa, and make sure the salary in your MoHRE contract matches your offer.
