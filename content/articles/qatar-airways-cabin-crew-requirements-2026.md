**Qatar Airways** recruits cabin crew from around the world to be based in **Doha**. Here is what the airline asks for, based on its official cabin crew recruitment page.

## Requirements

| Requirement | Qatar Airways' rule |
|---|---|
| Arm reach | At least **212 cm** |
| Education | At least **10 years** of formal schooling |
| English | Fluent, written and spoken |
| Health | Good health and physical fitness |
| Relocation | Willing to move to **Doha, Qatar** |
| Experience | **2+ years** in hospitality, customer service, aviation or another customer-facing job is **preferred**, not required |

### What is arm reach?

Arm reach is how high you can reach while standing flat-footed, with one arm stretched up. It shows whether you can reach the overhead safety equipment on board. It is measured at the assessment, so practise beforehand.

## Benefits listed by Qatar Airways

- **Accommodation and transport** in Doha provided by the company
- **Travel benefits** for you and eligible family members
- **International medical insurance**
- Cabin crew training and career development

## How to apply

Apply through the **"Apply now"** button on the official recruitment page. It takes you to the Qatar Airways careers site, filtered for cabin crew jobs. You can also join the airline's talent community to hear about new openings.

## Tips

- **Measure your arm reach** before applying. It is a firm requirement.
- Use a clear, recent photo and keep your CV to the point.
- Highlight customer-facing experience, even if it is from a hotel, shop or restaurant.
- Qatar Airways does not charge recruitment fees. Treat payment requests as a red flag.
