Sydney's second airport is finally open, and it is set to become one of the biggest new employers in Western Sydney. For anyone exploring **Australia jobs for foreigners** with the right to work, or local job seekers, the airport's growth is worth tracking. **Western Sydney International Airport (WSI)** welcomed its first passenger flight on **Sunday 25 October 2026**, and chief executive **Simon Hickey** says **up to 8,500 jobs** will be needed across the airport precinct as it grows.

## Opening day facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Opened to passengers | 25 October 2026 |
| First flight | Jetstar JQ362 to the Gold Coast |
| Location | Badgerys Creek, about 45km from Sydney CBD |
| Hours | 24/7 |
| Workers who built it | About 10,000 (around half from Western Sydney) |
| Future jobs | Up to 8,500 at 10 million passengers a year |
| Long-term capacity | Up to 82 million passengers a year by the 2060s |

The airport had already started cargo operations in July. Its first terminal is roughly the size of Adelaide Airport, and WSI expects to get close to **10 million passengers a year** in the early to mid-2030s.

## Where the jobs will be

The 8,500 figure covers the whole airport precinct, not just the airport company. As flights and passenger numbers grow, hiring is likely across:

- **Airlines** – cabin crew, pilots, ground staff and check-in agents
- **Ground handling** – baggage handlers, ramp workers and aircraft cleaners
- **Security and border** – screening officers and security staff
- **Retail and food** – shops, cafes and restaurants in the terminal
- **Engineering and maintenance** – technicians, electricians and facilities staff
- **Freight and logistics** – cargo handlers, forklift drivers and warehouse staff
- **Transport** – bus, taxi and rideshare services

## How to apply

Start with the official [WSI current vacancies page](https://www.wsiairport.com.au/careers/current-vacancies). Many airport jobs are with airlines and service contractors rather than the airport itself, so also check the careers pages of the airlines flying from WSI.

## What it means for job seekers

- **This is a long-term story.** The 8,500 jobs build up over years as flights grow, so keep checking for new openings.
- **Live nearby? You have an edge.** A 24/7 airport needs shift workers who can get there easily, including at night.
- **Get the right cards.** Aviation security identification and forklift or security licences help for many airport roles.
- **Work rights:** overseas applicants need a valid Australian visa with work rights.
