Charlotte's banking scene is growing again, with well-paid **USA jobs for foreigners** and local finance professionals on the way. On **27 October 2026**, North Carolina Governor **Josh Stein** announced that **The Clearing House Payments Company** will open a new office in **Charlotte** and create **200 jobs**. The state says the roles will pay an **average of $162,322 a year**.

## About the employer

Founded in **1853**, The Clearing House runs some of the most important payment systems in the United States. Every business day it clears and settles **more than $2 trillion** in payments. A new Charlotte office puts the company in the heart of one of America's biggest banking cities. Governor Stein described Charlotte as the nation's second-largest financial centre.

CEO **David Watson** said Charlotte is an ideal place for the company to build on its existing presence in North Carolina. The company already has staff in Winston-Salem, as well as in New York.

## How the incentive works

The state's Economic Investment Committee approved a **Job Development Investment Grant (JDIG)** worth up to **$2.43 million over 12 years**. The money is only paid after the company proves each year that it has hit its hiring and investment targets. Because Mecklenburg is a Tier 3 county, up to **$810,000** more will go to a fund that helps rural areas build infrastructure.
