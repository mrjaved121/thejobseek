Most new jobs in the UAE start with a probation period. Here is what the law says about how long it can last and how either side can end it.

## How long probation can be

- **Maximum 6 months.**
- It **cannot be extended or renewed**.
- If you stay on after probation, the probation months **count as service**, including for gratuity.

## If your employer ends your job during probation

The employer must give you **14 days' written notice**.

## If you want to leave during probation

The notice depends on where you are going next:

| Your plan | Notice you must give |
|---|---|
| Move to **another employer in the UAE** | At least **1 month** in writing |
| **Leave the UAE** | At least **14 days** in writing |

**Recruitment costs:** if you move to another UAE employer during probation, the **new employer** must pay your old employer's recruitment costs, unless they agree otherwise. The same applies if you leave the UAE and come back on a new work permit **within 3 months**.

## Contract types to know

- **Fixed-term contracts:** all private-sector contracts are now fixed-term. They can be renewed, and renewals count towards gratuity. If both sides keep working after the end date, the contract is treated as renewed on the same terms.
- **Work models:** full-time, part-time (one or more employers), temporary (ends when the task is done) and flexible (hours or days change with business needs).

## Practical tips

- **Get your probation length in writing** in your offer letter and MoHRE contract.
- **Give notice in writing** (email is fine), and keep a copy.
- **Plan your next job before resigning**, so you give the right length of notice.
