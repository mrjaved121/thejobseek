Young people in the UAE who want a career in journalism, content or broadcasting are getting a new training route. **IMI Media Academy**, part of Abu Dhabi's **International Media Investments (IMI)**, and the **Abdulla Al Ghurair Foundation (AGF)** have signed a **five-year partnership** to prepare **29,000 young people** for work in media, Sharjah24 reported on **22 October 2026**.

The programme will run under the name **"Al Ghurair Voice Lab with IMI"**. It starts in the UAE and is planned to expand to other Arab countries later.

## Who signed the deal

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Partners | IMI Media Academy and Abdulla Al Ghurair Foundation |
| Signed by | Ali Al Hammadi (COO, IMI) and Dr Sonia Ben Jaafar (CEO, AGF) |
| Attended by | Dr Sultan Ahmed Al Jaber (IMI Chairman) and Abdul Aziz Al Ghurair (AGF Chairman) |
| Length | Five years |
| Target | 29,000 young people |
| First market | UAE, then the Arab region |

## What the training covers

The partners say the plan has several programmes aimed at different stages of a young person's path into media, including:

- Basic media skills for beginners
- Advanced professional upskilling for people already working
- Media content built into university courses
- Storytelling for school students
- National youth initiatives in the UAE
- Regional programmes in other Arab countries

The approach goes beyond classroom lessons. Trainees are meant to get **mentoring**, **hands-on practice** and **time in real workplaces**, with routes into **internships, placements and jobs**.

## Building on past work

The new plan follows the **Nomu Al Ghurair Siraj** programme, which has already trained **450 graduates**. The Al Ghurair Foundation, set up in 2015, says its education work has reached more than **433,000 young people**.

Speaking at the launch, Dr Ben Jaafar stressed that learning only matters if it connects to real opportunity. Khalid Al Hosani of IMI Media Academy said the aim is to build a pipeline of Arab media talent rather than simply run courses.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Media is changing fast.** Employers now want people who can write, film, edit video and understand social platforms and data, not just one skill.
- **Get ready now.** Start a portfolio: articles, short videos, podcasts or social media work you have produced yourself.
- **Watch for intakes.** Eligibility and application details have not yet been announced, so follow IMI Media Academy and the Al Ghurair Foundation's official channels.
- **Arabic and English together** are a strong advantage in Gulf newsrooms and agencies.
