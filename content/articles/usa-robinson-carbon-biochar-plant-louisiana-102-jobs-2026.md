**US hiring 2026** is reaching central Louisiana's green industry. Family-owned **Robinson Carbon** will invest **$16.3 million** in a **biochar plant** at **England Airpark** in Alexandria, creating **42 direct jobs** and an estimated **60 indirect jobs**, about **102** in total, Louisiana Economic Development (LED) announced on 4 November 2026.

## What is Robinson Carbon building?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Robinson Carbon (part of the Robinson family wood business, founded 1893) |
| Location | England Airpark, Alexandria, Rapides Parish, Louisiana |
| Investment | $16.3 million |
| Direct new jobs | 42 |
| Indirect new jobs (LED estimate) | About 60 |
| Jobs kept | 22 existing positions |
| Production start | Second quarter of 2027 |

## What will the plant make?

The plant will use **two rotary drum pyrolysis reactors** to turn about **100,000 metric tons** of Southern Yellow Pine leftovers from nearby mills into roughly **16,500 metric tons of biochar** a year. Biochar is used in farming, gardening and cleaning up polluted land. Once certified, the plant is also expected to generate about **40,000 carbon removal certificates** a year.

Robinson Carbon has moved a small pilot reactor from Indiana to Alexandria to **train employees** before full production starts.

## What jobs are likely?

Plants like this typically employ:

- Process and reactor operators
- Maintenance mechanics and electricians
- Quality and lab technicians
- Forklift, yard and logistics staff
- Supervisors and environmental compliance roles

These are typical roles for this kind of facility; the company has not published a vacancy list yet.

## How do I apply?

Hiring details have not been announced. Watch Robinson Carbon's official channels and the Louisiana Workforce Commission job portal for openings in Alexandria as the 2027 start date approaches.
