Thinking about becoming an electrician? Victoria is about to open one of the biggest training pipelines in the country, a notable piece of **Australia hiring 2026** news for school leavers and career changers. The state government said on **22 October 2026** that the **SEC Apprenticeship Academy** will open its doors in the **first quarter of 2027**, starting with a pilot group of **40 electrical apprentices**.

## The Academy at a glance

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Run by | State Electricity Commission (SEC) |
| Partners | NECA Education & Careers, Electrical Trades Union |
| Pilot intake | 40 electrical apprentices |
| Full programme | 2,000 apprentices over four years |
| Opening | Q1 2027 |
| Expressions of interest | Opening "in the coming weeks" |

The government describes it as **Victoria's largest electrical apprenticeship programme**. Apprentices will earn a wage while they learn on the job and in the classroom.

## Why Victoria needs more electricians

The release includes projections for Victoria's energy workforce up to 2040:

- The energy workforce is expected to grow from about **45,000 to around 68,000** people.
- Electricians working in energy are forecast to grow by **more than 90%**, to about **8,500**.
- Jobs installing **home batteries** could almost triple to around **2,600**.
- **Electric vehicle charging** jobs could quadruple to about **14,300**.
- Around **37%** of energy jobs are expected to be in **regional Victoria**, with almost 35,000 in Melbourne.

## Who should apply

The programme is aimed at people who want a long-term trade career in the energy sector – school leavers, young people looking for paid training, and adults wanting to switch careers. The government has not yet published full entry requirements, so watch for them when expressions of interest open.

## How to apply

The release says expressions of interest will open soon through **secapprenticeshipacademy.necaeducation.com.au**, the Academy website set up with NECA Education & Careers. Applications are not open yet, so bookmark the page and check back over the coming weeks.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Get ready now.** Brush up on maths and basic science – electrical aptitude tests usually cover both.
- **Check your eligibility for apprenticeships** in Victoria, including the work rights you need.
- **Think beyond power stations.** Batteries, solar, EV charging and grid upgrades will all need qualified electricians.
- **Regional applicants** have a good chance, as more than a third of future energy jobs are outside Melbourne.
