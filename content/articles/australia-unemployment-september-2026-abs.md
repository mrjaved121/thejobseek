Australia's labour market eased slightly in September. The **Australian Bureau of Statistics (ABS)** reported on **15 October 2026** that the seasonally adjusted unemployment rate was **4.7%**.

## Key figures for September 2026 (seasonally adjusted)

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Unemployment rate | 4.7% |
| Unemployed people | 732,000 (up 8,900) |
| Total employment | 14.78 million (down 39,300) |
| Full-time employment | up 34,400 |
| Part-time employment | down 73,700 |
| Participation rate | 66.8% (down 0.2 points) |
| Monthly hours worked | 2,011 million (up 0.1%) |

## Reading the numbers

The overall fall in employment came entirely from **part-time** work. **Full-time jobs grew**, and total hours worked edged up. That points to a market that is cooling, but where employers are still adding full-time staff.

## What it means for skilled migrants

- **Employer-sponsored roles remain the main route.** If you have a job offer, check the salary meets the current minimum, which rose on 1 July 2026. See: [Australia skilled visa salary thresholds for 2026–27](/australia/australia-skilled-visa-income-thresholds-2026/).
- **Full-time hiring is holding up,** which is good news for sponsored, full-time roles.
- **Expect more competition** for part-time and casual work.
- **Use official channels.** Check visa options with the Department of Home Affairs, and never pay for a job offer.
