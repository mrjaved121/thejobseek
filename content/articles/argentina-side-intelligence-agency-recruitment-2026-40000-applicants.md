Argentina's spy agency is recruiting in public for the first time. The **Secretaría de Inteligencia de Estado (SIDE)** has opened a **public recruitment drive** for future intelligence staff and received **more than 40,000 applications in 24 hours**. **Argentine citizens** with **secondary education** who will relocate to **Buenos Aires** can apply online, and media reports say the window runs to **November 30, 2026**.

## What is SIDE looking for?

SIDE says it wants a professional career service based on merit, training and performance. It is not only hiring classic field agents. Areas listed include:

| Area | Typical background |
|---|---|
| Strategic analysis | Politics, international relations, economics |
| Cyber intelligence | IT, information security |
| Technology | Engineering, computer science |
| Languages | Translators, language graduates |
| Regional studies | Area specialists |
| Operations and counterintelligence | Security, criminology, law |

The agency has not said how many vacancies exist or what salaries it will pay.

## Who can apply?

The basic rules are short:

- **Argentine citizenship**
- **Completed secondary school**
- **Willingness to live and work in Buenos Aires**

No previous intelligence or security experience is needed, so graduates in law, criminology, technology and languages can try.

## How does the selection work?

Candidates go through several stages:

1. Profile screening
2. Online general knowledge exam
3. Written tests
4. Physical and psychological checks
5. Personal interview
6. Background and security investigation

Those who do well may be offered the basic entry course at the **National Intelligence School**. Passing the course does not automatically mean a job.

## Why is this unusual?

Intelligence services usually recruit quietly through referrals and head-hunting. SIDE has instead put up posters around Buenos Aires and near universities, styled like the famous US "Uncle Sam" army adverts, with the slogan "We are looking for the best". The government presents the open call as a step toward a more transparent and professional service.

## How do I apply?

Apply only through SIDE's official channels, which are linked from the Argentine government's websites. Recruitment posters are also up around Buenos Aires and near universities. Be careful of fake sites asking for payment; government recruitment in Argentina is free.

## How can I prepare?

Refresh your general knowledge of Argentine and world affairs for the online exam, keep your documents and school certificates ready, and be honest in every form, since background checks look closely at past records. Language skills and certificates in IT or cyber security can help a profile stand out at the screening stage.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Huge competition:** 40,000 applicants in a day means only strong candidates will advance.
- **Tech and language skills stand out**, as SIDE wants specialists, not just field agents.
- **Prepare for long checks:** security vetting can take months.
- **Citizens only:** foreign nationals are not eligible.
