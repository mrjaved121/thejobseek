There is good news for Qataris weighing up **Qatar jobs** outside government. Figures shared on **27 October 2026** show that **more than 8,500 Qataris** have been appointed to roles in private-sector companies since 2022. The numbers came out at the first **Qatar Private Sector Nationalisation Award** ceremony, where the employers doing the most to recruit and develop national talent were honoured.

## Bigger hiring targets ahead

Qatar wants many more nationals working for private firms. Under the **National Development Strategy 2024–2030**, the plan is to:

- raise the Qatari share of private-sector employment from **17% to 20%**
- add about **15,400 Qataris** to the private-sector workforce

A law on localising private-sector jobs (Law No. 12 of 2024) took effect in **April 2025**, and the award was set up to reward companies that go beyond the minimum.

## The award in numbers

| Detail | Figure |
|---|---|
| Applications | 426 (59% individuals, 41% companies) |
| Sectors covered | 9 |
| Field visits to shortlisted firms | 56 |
| Interviews with shortlisted individuals | 59 |
| Qataris appointed in private firms since 2022 | 8,500+ |
| Next edition | 2028 |

Two categories – **healthcare services** and **manufacturing** – had no winner because entries did not meet the criteria.

## Employers that stood out

These are useful names for any Qatari job seeker to watch:

- **QNB** – won the most awards, including best at attracting national talent and best new-joiner programmes for both university and high-school graduates
- **Ooredoo** – IT and digital services
- **Katara Hospitality** – tourism
- **Education Above All Foundation** – education
- **Hassad Food** – food and agriculture
- **United Development Company** – construction
- **Economic Zones Company** – logistics
- **Microsoft Doha** – flexible working
- **Commercial Bank** – leadership development
- **Visit Qatar** – support for Qatari women
- **Qatar Red Crescent Society** – preparing future talent

Labour Minister **Dr Ali bin Samikh Al Marri** said success is not just about how many Qataris are hired, but also about the skills, career paths and future leaders those jobs create.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Look beyond the public sector.** Banks, telecoms, hospitality and logistics firms are actively competing for national talent.
- **Target graduate programmes.** QNB was recognised for programmes for both university and high-school leavers – ask employers about structured entry schemes.
- **Ask about development.** Award-winning firms were judged on training and career growth, so raise these points in interviews.
- **Apply directly** on each company's official careers page and keep your profile with the Ministry of Labour up to date.
