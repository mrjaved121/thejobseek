Searching for **Singapore jobs** where employers are struggling to hire? Singapore's **Ministry of Manpower (MOM)** has published its **2027 Shortage Occupation List**, listing **147 job titles** grouped into **30 occupational categories** in **seven industries** where workers are in short supply.

## What is the Shortage Occupation List?

MOM updates the list every November for the following year. Jobs on it are so hard to fill that **foreign Employment Pass applicants** in these roles earn **bonus points** under Singapore's COMPASS points framework. For locals, the list is a useful guide to careers with strong demand.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Published by | Ministry of Manpower (MOM) |
| Applies to | 2027 |
| Industries | 7 |
| Occupational categories | 30 |
| Job titles | 147 |

## Which jobs are in shortage?

Examples from the 2027 list include:

- **Technology and AI:** AI/machine learning engineers, AI applied researchers, forward deployed engineers, quantum scientists and engineers, software and platform engineering roles
- **Cybersecurity:** digital forensics specialists, cyber incident and threat investigators, penetration testers
- **Healthcare:** diagnostic radiographers, MRI technologists, patient-care and specialist pharmacists, senior nursing leaders, palliative care social workers
- **Chemicals and energy:** chemical engineering technicians (petroleum and natural gas), distributed control system operators and supervisors, plant controllers, materials and process engineers
- **Food science and sustainability:** specialty ingredient and alternative protein scientists, novel food biotechnologists, carbon project managers and carbon standards analysts
- **Maritime:** marine superintendents and marine technical superintendents

## Why does this matter now?

Singapore's job market is mixed. Unemployed citizens rose to **64,700** last quarter, the highest since 2021, with **4,800** redundancies. Yet these 147 roles still lack enough candidates, showing where skills are most valued.

## How do I apply for these jobs?

Search vacancies on Singapore's official MyCareersFuture portal and on employers' careers pages. Foreign professionals should check that their role and salary meet Employment Pass rules before applying.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Tech, cyber and healthcare skills are in high demand.**
- **Foreigners get an edge** in Employment Pass applications for listed roles.
- **Locals can reskill** into listed jobs using government-supported training.
