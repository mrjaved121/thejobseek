Qatar was the first country in the Gulf to introduce a **non-discriminatory minimum wage** that applies to everyone. If you are offered a job in Qatar, this is the lowest pay your employer can legally offer.

## The minimum wage

| Component | Minimum amount |
|---|---|
| Basic wage | **QAR 1,000 a month** |
| Housing allowance (if housing is not provided) | **at least QAR 500** |
| Food allowance (if food is not provided) | **at least QAR 300** |

So a worker who gets **neither** housing nor food from the employer must receive at least **QAR 1,800 a month** in total.

## Who it covers

The minimum wage applies to **all workers in Qatar**, of every nationality, in every sector, **including domestic workers**.

It was set by **Law No. 17 of 2020**, as part of Qatar's labour reforms. The same reforms let workers **change jobs without an NOC**.

## How you must be paid

Salaries must be paid through the **Wage Protection System (WPS)** into a bank account in Qatar. Under updated rules, monthly wages are due on the 1st of each month and must be transferred **within 7 days**. Read: [Qatar: salaries must be paid within 7 days](/qatar/qatar-wage-protection-salary-within-7-days-decision-50-2026/).

## Checking a job offer

- Make sure your **basic wage** is at least QAR 1,000. Allowances are on top and do not replace the basic wage.
- Check whether **housing and food** are provided. If not, the allowances must be paid.
- Get the salary breakdown **in writing** before you travel.

## If you are paid less

Keep your payslips and bank statements, then report the underpayment to Qatar's **Ministry of Labour**, which handles wage cases. You can also change employers without an NOC. See: [Changing jobs in Qatar without an NOC](/qatar/qatar-change-jobs-without-noc-notice-period/).
