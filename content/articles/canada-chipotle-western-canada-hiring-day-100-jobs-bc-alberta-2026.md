If you want a job in Western Canada quickly, today is the day to act. In a fresh sign of **Canada hiring 2026**, **Chipotle Mexican Grill** is holding a **Western Canada Hiring Day** on **Wednesday 28 October 2026**. Managers at **39 restaurants** across **British Columbia and Alberta** will run in-person interviews from **10am to 8pm local time**, with the goal of filling **up to 100 jobs**.

## Hiring day at a glance

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Chipotle Mexican Grill |
| Date | Wednesday 28 October 2026 |
| Time | 10am – 8pm local time |
| Restaurants | 39 in B.C. and Alberta |
| Jobs | Up to 100 |
| Job types | Full-time and part-time |
| Apply | jobs.chipotle.ca |

## Where the interviews are

**British Columbia:** Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Langley, Kelowna, West Vancouver, Victoria, Saanich, Langford, Port Coquitlam, North Vancouver, Richmond and Chilliwack.

**Alberta:** Calgary, Edmonton and Sherwood Park.

Check the careers site to find the nearest participating restaurant before you go.

## Pay and benefits

Chipotle describes its pay as competitive but has not published hourly rates for these roles. The benefits it lists include:

- **Free meals** while working
- **RRSP matching** to help you save for retirement
- **Paid holiday closures** on certain days
- **Tuition assistance** of up to **$5,250 a year**

Chipotle also says more than **65%** of its general managers in Canada were promoted from within, so entry-level crew roles can lead to management.

## How to apply

You can apply online at the official Chipotle Canada careers site, [jobs.chipotle.ca](https://jobs.chipotle.ca), or walk into one of the participating restaurants during the hiring day for an interview. Applying online first can save time on the day.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Go early.** Interview slots fill up, especially at lunch and dinner peaks.
- **Bring your availability.** Know which days and hours you can work, including weekends.
- **No experience? Still apply.** Crew roles often train new starters on the job.
- **Think long term.** With tuition help and internal promotions, this can be a step towards supervisor or manager roles.
- **Check work rights.** You need the legal right to work in Canada to be hired.
