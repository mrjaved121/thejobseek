Fresh graduates looking for **Bahrain jobs** in professional services have a new opportunity. The Labour Fund (**Tamkeen**) has signed a memorandum of understanding with **EY MENA** to set up an **EY Talent Hub in Bahrain**, which aims to create **125 jobs for Bahrainis** over three years from **2027 to 2029**. The deal was announced at Gateway Gulf 2026 on 2 November.

## What is the EY Talent Hub?

The hub will deliver specialised professional services to clients in Bahrain and across the region, staffed by Bahraini talent trained by EY.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Partners | Tamkeen (Labour Fund) and EY MENA |
| Support | Bahrain Economic Development Board |
| Jobs | 125 for Bahrainis |
| Programme | Three years, 2027–2029 |
| Main target | Fresh graduates and new labour market entrants |

## Which roles will be available?

The hub will hire across EY's core service lines:

- **Audit**
- **Consulting**
- **Tax**
- **Strategy and transactions**

Staff will also build skills in **AI, digital transformation, cybersecurity, sustainability and financial services**, which are some of the fastest-growing areas in the region.

## What did the partners say?

Tamkeen Chief Executive **Maha Abdulhameed Mofeez** said the agency directs its support to projects that create lasting, high-value jobs for Bahrainis in growing sectors. EY MENA Assurance Partner **Nader Rahimi** said the aim is to give Bahraini professionals the practical experience and confidence to lead as technology changes the skills employers need.

## How do I apply?

Hiring for the hub runs from 2027. Watch EY's official **[Bahrain careers page](https://www.ey.com/en_bh/careers)** for graduate and entry-level roles, and keep your CV ready with your degree, internships and any professional certificates.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Graduates are the priority.** This is one of the clearest entry routes into Big Four firms in Bahrain.
- **Digital skills give an edge.** AI, data and cybersecurity knowledge will stand out.
- **Accounting and finance students** should consider starting ACCA or CPA papers early.
