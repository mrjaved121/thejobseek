**Employment Insurance (EI)** gives temporary income to workers in Canada who lose their job. If you are moving to Canada for work, it is part of the safety net you pay into from your first paycheque. Here are the official rules for **EI regular benefits** in 2026.

## How much EI pays

| Item | 2026 figure |
|---|---|
| Benefit rate | **55%** of your average insurable weekly earnings |
| Maximum weekly benefit | **$729** |
| Maximum insurable earnings | **$68,900** a year |
| Length of benefits | **14 to 45 weeks** |

EI benefits are **taxable**, and federal and provincial taxes are deducted from your payments.

## Who qualifies

You can get EI regular benefits if:

- your job ended **for reasons outside your control**, for example a layoff or a shortage of work;
- you have had **at least 7 straight days with no work and no earnings** in the last 52 weeks;
- you worked enough **insurable hours** in your qualifying period;
- you are **ready, willing and able to work** each day, and **actively looking for work**.

## How many hours you need

You need **420 to 700 insurable hours**, usually in the last **52 weeks**. The number depends on the unemployment rate where you live:

| Regional unemployment rate | Hours needed |
|---|---|
| 6% and under | 700 |
| 6.1% to 7% | 665 |
| 7.1% to 8% | 630 |
| 8.1% to 9% | 595 |
| 9.1% to 10% | 560 |
| 10.1% to 11% | 525 |
| 11.1% to 12% | 490 |
| 12.1% to 13% | 455 |
| Over 13% | 420 |

## When you may not qualify

- You **quit without just cause**.
- You were **fired for misconduct**.
- You are out of work because you are directly taking part in a **labour dispute**.

## While you receive EI

- Submit your **EI reports every 2 weeks**, online or by phone. Missing them can mean losing benefits.
- Keep a **written record** of the employers you contact and the dates.

## Tips for newcomers

- **Apply as soon as you stop working**, even if you are still waiting for paperwork from your employer.
- **Keep your pay stubs** to show your hours and earnings.
- Think about EI before you **resign**. Leaving without just cause usually means no benefits.
