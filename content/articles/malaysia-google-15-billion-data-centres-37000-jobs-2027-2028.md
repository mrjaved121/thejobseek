Big tech investment means more **Malaysia jobs**. **Google** will spend **$15 billion** on data centres and cloud operations in Malaysia during **2027 and 2028**, and says the construction phase will support **more than 37,000 local jobs a year**. Once running, the finished data centres should keep **over 9,000 people in work each year**.

## What has Google announced?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Investment | $15 billion |
| Timing | 2027 and 2028 |
| Focus | New computing hubs, cloud infrastructure, water security, power, training |
| Jobs during construction | 37,000+ a year |
| Long-term jobs | 9,000+ a year |
| Boost to economy (construction) | $1.1 billion |

Alphabet's chief investment officer **Ruth Porat** announced the plan in Kuala Lumpur, with Prime Minister **Anwar Ibrahim** attending. Earlier the same week, Google also announced **$160 million** for shared water infrastructure in **Negeri Sembilan**.

## Which jobs will be in demand?

Google says the long-term roles include:

- Data centre technicians
- Mechanical engineers
- Electrical engineers
- Logistics managers
- Security managers

The construction phase will also need large numbers of builders, electricians, project managers and engineers, typically hired through contractors.

## Why is Malaysia attracting data centres?

Malaysia has become **Southeast Asia's main data centre hub**, thanks to relatively low costs, reliable infrastructure and its closeness to Singapore. Oracle, Amazon, Alibaba and ByteDance are also expanding there.

## How do I apply?

Google's own roles are posted on its official **[careers website](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/)** (filter by Malaysia). Construction jobs will usually be advertised by Google's building contractors and through Malaysia's official MYFutureJobs portal.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Electrical and mechanical skills are in high demand** across Malaysia's data centre boom.
- **Construction jobs come first** (2027–2028), then long-term operations roles.
- **Training is part of the plan**, opening doors for new entrants.
