Paid annual leave is a legal right for private-sector workers in the UAE. Here is what the law allows, in plain terms.

## How many days you get

| Length of service | Annual leave |
|---|---|
| More than 6 months, less than 1 year | 2 days for each month worked |
| 1 year or more | 30 days, fully paid |
| Job ends before leave is used | Leave for the part of the year worked |

**Part-time workers** earn leave based on the hours in their contract.

## Public holidays during leave

If a public holiday falls during your annual leave, it **counts as part of your leave**, unless your contract or company policy is more generous.

## Who decides the dates

- Your employer can set leave dates according to work needs, by agreement with you, or by rotating leave among staff.
- The employer must tell you your leave dates **at least one month in advance**.

## Carrying leave over

- Leave should normally be taken in the year it is earned.
- You can carry unused leave into the next year **only with your employer's agreement** and in line with company rules.
- An employer **cannot stop you taking your leave for more than two years in a row**, unless you choose to carry it over or take cash instead.

## Leave pay when you leave the job

When your job ends, your employer must pay you for:

- **all unused annual leave**, however many days, and
- leave earned for the **part of the final year** you worked.

This is calculated on your **basic salary**, and it is part of the final settlement that must be paid within **14 days** of the job ending, together with gratuity.

## Tips

- **Keep a record** of the leave you take and any approval emails.
- **Check your final settlement** includes unused leave days.
- If leave pay is missing, raise it with HR in writing, then complain to **MoHRE** if it is not fixed.
