Building professionals have a new employer to watch for **Hong Kong jobs**. The **Building Maintenance Support and Services Council**, due to be set up by the end of 2026, plans to recruit **more than 90 staff** by **mid-2027**, Secretary for Development **Bernadette Linn** said on 8 November 2026.

## What will the new council do?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Body | Building Maintenance Support and Services Council |
| Set up | By end of 2026 |
| Staff to recruit | 90+ |
| Recruitment complete | Mid-2027 |
| Main tasks | Tendering, bid evaluation, monitoring repair work |
| Partner | Urban Renewal Authority (about 270 building maintenance staff) |

It will hire **building inspection consultants** on owners' behalf and check the quality of their work, working alongside the URA to give owners two layers of protection.

## Why is it being created?

It is part of a reform package introduced after the **Wang Fuk Court fire**. Other measures include:

- Surprise inspections by the **Buildings Department** during building checks
- Video evidence required from inspection consultants
- Unannounced site visits at least every six months during works
- Six-monthly progress reports from contractors

The government is also considering requiring owners to set aside money for maintenance after major repairs to keep subsidy eligibility.

## Which roles are likely?

A body doing this work typically needs:

- Building surveyors and engineers
- Procurement and tendering officers
- Contract and project managers
- Quantity surveyors
- Inspectors and admin support

These are typical for this kind of work, not an official vacancy list.

## How do I apply?

Watch for recruitment notices once the council is formally established, and follow announcements from Hong Kong's Development Bureau.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Building safety is a growth area** in Hong Kong after recent reforms.
- **Surveyors and engineers** with local experience will be well placed.
- **Inspection and compliance skills** are increasingly in demand.
