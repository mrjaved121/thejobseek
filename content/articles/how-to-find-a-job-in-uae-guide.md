Finding a job in the UAE is very possible, but the process has rules that many first-time job seekers don't know. This guide walks you through each step and links to our detailed, source-checked guides.

## Step 1: Choose how you will search

| Option | Good for | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Search from home** | Most people | Apply online first. Many employers interview by video. |
| **Visit visa** | Meeting employers in person | You can attend interviews, but you **cannot work**. |
| **Job seeker visa** | Graduates and skilled professionals | No sponsor needed; **60, 90 or 120 days**, single entry. |

Read: [UAE job seeker visa: who qualifies](/uae/uae-jobseeker-visit-visa-requirements/) and [can you work on a visit visa?](/uae/can-you-work-on-visit-visa-in-uae/)

Some nationalities resident in the US, UK, EU and other countries can now get a **visa on arrival**. Read: [UAE visa on arrival for six more nationalities](/uae/uae-visa-on-arrival-six-nationalities-residence-permit/)

## Step 2: Prepare your documents

- An up-to-date CV in PDF or Word format
- Passport copy and a colour photo
- **Attested** degree certificate. Attestation can take weeks, so start early.
- Experience letters from past employers

## Step 3: Apply in the right places

**Government jobs.** Each government has its own portal, and some portals are only for UAE nationals:

- **Federal:** the FAHR federal jobs portal
- **Dubai:** Dubai Careers
- **Abu Dhabi and Sharjah:** expats apply on each entity's own careers page

Read: [UAE government job portals for every emirate](/uae/uae-government-jobs-portals-federal-dubai-abu-dhabi-sharjah/)

**Airlines.** Emirates and Etihad hire cabin crew all year. Read: [Emirates cabin crew requirements](/uae/emirates-cabin-crew-requirements-how-to-apply-2026/) and [Etihad cabin crew requirements and recruitment days](/uae/etihad-cabin-crew-requirements-recruitment-days-2026/)

**Healthcare.** Doctors, nurses and allied health workers need a licence. In Dubai, that usually means passing a DHA assessment. Read: [DHA licence exam rules](/uae/dha-licence-exam-dubai-assessment-attempts-rules/)

## Step 4: Check the offer before you accept

A real offer clearly states the job title, basic salary, allowances, working hours and contract type. Before you accept:

- Confirm the company exists by calling its **official** phone number yourself.
- Make sure the **basic salary** is clear. Gratuity, overtime and leave pay are calculated on it.
- Never pay a "visa fee" or "processing fee". The employer pays for your work permit.

Read: [8 warning signs of a fake UAE job offer](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/)

## Step 5: Your work permit and visa

1. Your employer applies for a **MoHRE work permit**. There are 13 types, depending on your situation.
2. You sign the offer letter and employment contract.
3. Your visa status is changed, or you enter on an entry permit.
4. You pass the **medical fitness test** and give fingerprints for your **Emirates ID**.
5. Once the residence visa is stamped or issued, your first working day can begin.

Read: [UAE work permit types explained](/uae/uae-work-permit-types-mohre-13-explained/)

## Step 6: Know your rights from day one

Once you start, learn the basics: probation of up to 6 months, salary due on the 1st of each month, 30 days' annual leave, and gratuity when you leave. Sign up for **ILOE** job-loss insurance, which is mandatory.

Read: [UAE Labour Law: the complete guide](/uae/uae-labour-law-guide-private-sector-workers/) and [ILOE insurance guide](/uae/uae-iloe-unemployment-insurance-guide/)

> Rules and fees change from time to time. Always check the latest requirements on official UAE government websites before you travel or sign.
