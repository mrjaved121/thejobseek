**Amazon** has promised **30,000 entry-level jobs** to young people in the UK between **2026 and 2030**. The plan, called the **Amazon Youth Promise**, was launched on **20 October 2026**. It focuses on young people in areas where jobs are hardest to find.

Applicants do **not need a CV** or any previous work experience.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Jobs | 30,000 entry-level roles |
| Period | 2026 to 2030 |
| Starting pay | Above £31,000 a year (pro rata) |
| Benefits | Private medical insurance and pension from day one |
| Type of work | Operations roles such as picking, packing and shipping |
| Locations | Amazon's 100+ UK sites |
| Work experience | 10,000+ placements by 2030 |

## Why Amazon is doing this

Roughly **one million young Britons** are currently NEET (out of work and out of education or training), a ten-year high. Amazon says that last year almost half of the people who joined its operations teams came directly from school, college or unemployment.

## Work experience and training

Amazon plans to grow its work experience programme from about **900 places in 2026** to **more than 3,000 a year by 2030**. Options include:

- **One-week placements** for school pupils and young people out of work
- **Three-week online T Level projects**
- **45-day T Level industry placements**, nine weeks inside Amazon teams

More than a third of last year's eligible T Level students went on to become Amazon apprentices.

## Extra support

- With the charity **Barnardo's**, Amazon will support **2,000 young people** through its JOBS Project from 2027 to 2030.
- The **DFN Project SEARCH** programme, for young people with learning disabilities and autism, will grow to support **over 1,000 young people at more than 40 sites** by 2030.
- Amazon has joined the government's **Youth Guarantee** and will work with the **Department for Work and Pensions (DWP)**.
- In 2027, new recruitment events will be held for young people in **Merseyside, Stoke-on-Trent, Greater Manchester and South Yorkshire**, with DWP Youth Hubs.

Applicants aged **18 to 24** who are unsuccessful will receive a follow-up email with advice, free job-search support and a link to apply again.

## How to apply

Visit Amazon's official page at [jobsatamazon.co.uk/youthpromise](https://www.jobsatamazon.co.uk/youthpromise) for videos, tips and links to current openings. You can also ask your local Jobcentre about upcoming Amazon hiring events.

## What it means for job seekers

- **No CV is not an excuse to skip preparation.** Read about the role, shift patterns and site location before you apply.
- **Check the commute.** Many fulfilment centres are outside town centres, so make sure you can reach the site for early or late shifts.
- **Use it as a first step.** Operations jobs can lead to apprenticeships, team leader roles and training.
- **Competition will be strong.** UK vacancies are at a 12-year low, so apply early.
