Emirati job seekers interested in banking and finance have a big opportunity this week. The **Ethraa Career Fair 2026** opened at **Expo Centre Sharjah** on **Thursday, 22 October 2026**, with **more than 70 financial institutions** offering **over 1,350 jobs** to **UAE nationals**.

The fair was opened by **Sheikh Fahim bin Sultan Al Qasimi**, Member of the Sharjah Executive Council and Chairman of the Department of Government Relations.

## Key facts at a glance

| Detail | Information |

| Event | Ethraa Career Fair 2026 |
| Venue | Expo Centre Sharjah |

| Employers | 70+ banks, insurers and finance firms |
| Who can apply | UAE nationals |
| Education level | High school graduates to university degree holders |

## Who is behind the fair

The event is run by the **Emirates Institute of Finance (EIF)**. It works together with the **Central Bank of the UAE**, the federal Emiratisation programme **Nafis**, the **Sharjah Chamber of Commerce and Industry** and Expo Centre Sharjah.

Ethraa is a long-running national effort to bring more Emiratis into the financial sector. Its current goal is to create **more than 10,000 jobs for UAE nationals by 2027**.

## What kinds of jobs are available

Exhibitors come from three main areas:

- **Banking** – branch, operations, customer service and specialist roles
- **Insurance** – sales, underwriting, claims and support roles
- **Other financial services** – finance companies and related institutions

Organisers have not published a full list of job titles. Because the fair welcomes candidates from **high school level upwards**, there should be entry-level openings as well as roles for graduates.

The Sharjah Department of Human Resources also has a stand showing its own training and recruitment programmes.

## Why it matters

Banks and insurers in the UAE must meet Emiratisation targets, and the financial sector is one of the main areas where national hiring is pushed. Events like Ethraa let job seekers meet recruiters face to face, ask what each role needs, and learn which skills are most in demand.

## How to apply

Go to the fair at Expo Centre Sharjah and bring several printed copies of your CV, your Emirates ID and copies of your certificates. For updates on Ethraa programmes, follow the official **Emirates Institute of Finance** website at [eif.gov.ae](https://eif.gov.ae). You can also register on the **Nafis** platform at [nafis.gov.ae](https://nafis.gov.ae) to find more private-sector jobs for Emiratis.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Prepare a short pitch.** Recruiters see hundreds of people. Explain in 30 seconds what you studied and which role you want.
- **Do not skip if you only have a high school certificate.** The fair is open to you, and some banks offer training-based entry roles.
- **Ask about training programmes.** Many banks hire Emirati graduates into structured trainee schemes.
- **Follow up.** Note the names of recruiters you meet and apply online to the same company within a few days.
- **Expats:** these roles are for UAE nationals only. Look instead at private-sector job portals and company careers pages.
