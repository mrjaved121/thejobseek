Job hunters in Central Florida have a fresh chance to meet employers. **CareerSource Central Florida's** annual **Paychecks for Patriots** job fair takes place on **Tuesday 10 November 2026** in **Orlando**, with **more than 20 employers**. It is organised in honour of veterans but is **open to all job seekers**.

## When and where is the fair?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Date | Tuesday, 10 November 2026 |
| Time | 10am – 1pm |
| Venue | Orange Technical College, South Campus, 2900 W. Oak Ridge Road, Building 1600, Orlando |
| Employers | 20+ |
| Who can attend | Everyone; veterans, service members and spouses especially welcome |

## Which employers are coming?

Employers include the **Orange County Sheriff's Office**, and organisers say others will offer jobs across a range of industries and positions.

## Who is it for?

The fair is aimed at veterans living in **Lake, Osceola, Orange, Seminole and Sumter counties**, as well as people currently leaving the military. The event also links attendees with community resources and helps veterans translate military skills and leadership into civilian careers.

## How should I prepare?

- Bring **several printed copies** of your CV.
- Be ready to **network and interview** on the day.
- Research the participating employers on CareerSource Central Florida's website.

## How do I apply?

Attend the fair in person. Event details and updates are on CareerSource Central Florida's official website.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Public-sector jobs are on offer**, including law enforcement roles.
- **Veterans get extra support** turning service experience into civilian CV language.
- **Everyone is welcome**, so non-veterans should attend too.
