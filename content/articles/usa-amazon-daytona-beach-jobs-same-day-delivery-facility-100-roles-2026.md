**Amazon Daytona Beach jobs just grew: the company has launched a Same-Day Delivery site there with more than 100 full-time roles starting at $20 an hour or more.** It arrives during Amazon's holiday hiring drive, which includes over 2,000 positions in Central Florida. You apply on Amazon's own website, hiring.amazon.com, and it is free.

## What has opened in Daytona Beach?

Local stations [WFTV](https://www.wftv.com/news/local/volusia-county/amazon-opens-daytona-beach-facility-with-faster-delivery-100-plus-jobs/ITAEKKCXU5CYDJMGWRA2W2XGEQ/) and FOX 35 Orlando reported the opening on 12 November 2026, based on Amazon's announcement.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Site | Amazon Same-Day Delivery facility, Daytona Beach, Florida |
| New jobs | More than 100, full-time |
| Starting pay | At least $20 an hour |
| Benefits | Health, vision and dental from day one |
| Wider hiring | 2,000+ roles in Central Florida; 21,000+ across Florida |

A Same-Day site is smaller than a regional warehouse. Stock is stored, picked, sorted and sent out for delivery from one building, so orders can reach customers within hours. The reports disagree on whether this is Amazon's fifth or sixth building in Volusia County; either way, the company is now among the county's biggest employers, according to Team Volusia.

## Which roles are open?

Amazon has not released a list of job titles for this building. At a site that picks, sorts and dispatches orders, warehouse associate work is typical: picking items, packing, sorting parcels and loading delivery vehicles. Treat that as a guide, not a confirmed list. The live openings shown on Amazon's site are the only reliable source.

The holiday drive adds seasonal, part-time and full-time roles. WFTV notes that some seasonal workers may get the chance to move into full-time positions.

## How to apply — step by step

Amazon sets out its process on its [hiring process page](https://hiring.amazon.com/hiring-process):

1. **Open Amazon's own hiring website:** [hiring.amazon.com](https://hiring.amazon.com/). The address amazon.com/localjobs, given in the news reports, sends you to the same place.
2. **Search for Daytona Beach** (or your ZIP code) and open a role. Compare the shift times and pay shown.
3. **Complete the job application** online. Have your ID details, Social Security number, phone and email ready.
4. **Attend a pre-hire appointment.** Bring original identity and work-authorization documents.
5. **Track your contingent offer.** Amazon runs a background check and a drug test, and you can check the status online.
6. **Accept the formal offer** once you pass, and complete the employment paperwork.
7. **Before day one,** order safety shoes (Amazon gives a credit of up to $110) and set up your account in the Amazon A to Z app.

Amazon's hiring site advertises that you can "skip the interview" for these hourly roles, so shifts can fill quickly.

There is no fee at any stage. Amazon hires directly, so you do not need a staffing agent or a paid "job placement" service. Many look-alike websites copy Amazon job adverts; only apply on addresses ending in amazon.com, and never pay for a job, a background check or equipment.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Apply early in the day and check back often.** New shifts are posted and taken fast in November.
- **Be flexible on shifts.** Nights, early mornings and weekends usually have more openings.
- **Bring the right documents** to the pre-hire appointment. A missing ID is the most common delay.
- **Be ready for the drug test and background check** listed in Amazon's process.
- **Tell them about warehouse, retail or delivery experience** if the form asks, but it is not stated as a requirement.
- **Finish every step in one go.** A half-finished application does not hold a shift for you.

Quick checklist: government photo ID, work-authorization document, Social Security number, email address, phone, and your available hours written down.
