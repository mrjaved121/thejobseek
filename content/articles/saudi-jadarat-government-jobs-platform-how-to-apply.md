**Jadarat** is Saudi Arabia's official job platform for both **government and private sector** vacancies. It is run by the **Human Resources Development Fund (HRDF)** and replaced the older Jadara system. Most Saudi public sector jobs are advertised here.

## Who can apply

Jadarat job seekers **must be Saudi nationals**, and the jobs advertised on it are reserved for Saudi citizens. Expat workers cannot apply through it.

Three groups use the platform:

| User | What they do |
|---|---|
| Saudi job seekers | Search and apply for jobs |
| Government and semi-government bodies | Advertise public sector jobs |
| Private companies, non-profits and foreign investors | Advertise private sector jobs |

If you already work:

- **Public sector employees** can apply for higher-grade government jobs they qualify for, and for any private sector job.
- **Private sector employees** can apply for jobs in either sector.

## How to register

1. Open Jadarat and choose **New User**.
2. Log in through **Nafath**, the national single sign-on, using your national ID.
3. Complete your profile. Some details are filled in automatically from government records, Saudi Post and HRDF services.
4. Verify your **email and mobile number**.

If you had a Jadara account, your data moves over to your new profile.

## Finding and applying for jobs

- You can **browse jobs without logging in**, by city, job title, date posted and employer.
- For **government jobs**, you must first **verify your qualifications** through the platform before applying.
- A complete profile helps employers find you, and HRDF says job matching will be added in a later update.

## Help

HRDF's unified support number is **8001222030**, or use "Contact Us" after logging in.

## Tips

- Keep your qualifications and certificates up to date in your profile, especially for government roles.
- Check the closing date on each job. Some government programmes have short windows, as with the [4,291 healthcare vacancies across Saudi health clusters](/saudi-arabia/saudi-health-holding-4291-healthcare-jobs-20-clusters/).
- Never pay anyone to "submit" an application. Applying on Jadarat is free.
