**US hiring 2026** in hospitality is strong in Northern California. **Thunder Valley Casino Resort** is holding job fairs every Tuesday and Thursday through November, to fill **over 100 full- and part-time positions** and **on-the-spot interviews**.

## Which jobs are open?

Thunder Valley is hiring across many departments, including:

| Department | Roles |
|---|---|
| Gaming | Table games dealers, cage cashiers |
| Security and safety | Security officers, EMTs |
| Food and beverage | Cooks, kitchen workers, food servers, beverage servers, bus persons, beverage porters, food court attendants |
| Hotel and facilities | Housekeepers, housepersons, janitorial porters |
| Guest services | Front desk agents, call centre agents, VIP drivers |

## When are the job fairs?

All fairs run from **10am to 2pm** at the **Employment Center, 1200 Athenas Avenue**, with parking in the FF lot:

- Tuesday 3 and Thursday 5 November
- Tuesday 10 and Thursday 12 November
- Tuesday 17 and Thursday 19 November
- Tuesday 24 and Thursday 26 November

## Who can apply?

- Minimum age **18**; some roles require you to be **21 or older**
- Must pass a **drug test and background check**
- Must be free to work **weekends and public holidays**

Thunder Valley says full-time employees receive a benefits package.

## How do I apply?

Go to any of the November job fairs with your ID and CV for an on-the-spot interview, or check Thunder Valley Casino Resort's official careers page for current listings.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Hospitality hiring peaks before the holidays.** Casinos and resorts staff up for busy weekends.
- **Entry-level roles are available.** Many positions need little or no experience.
- **Bring documents.** On-the-spot interviews move fast, so carry ID and proof of work authorisation.
