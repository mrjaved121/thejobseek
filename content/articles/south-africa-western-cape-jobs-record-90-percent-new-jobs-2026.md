If you are searching for **South Africa jobs**, the Western Cape is the province to watch. Premier **Alan Winde** told the Tygerberg Business Chamber's annual dinner in Durbanville that **nearly 90% of all net new jobs** added across South Africa in the last five years were in the **Western Cape**.

## What did the Premier say?

Winde was presenting the province's **2025-'26 Record of Delivery**, a review of the provincial government's work over the past financial year. He said the province's "growth for jobs" strategy is delivering results.

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Share of South Africa's net new jobs (5 years) | Nearly 90% |
| Western Cape unemployment rate | Below 20% |
| Business confidence 2025/26 | Up 5 points (only province with gains) |
| Investment commitments 2025/26 | R23.13 billion |
| Businesses supported 2025/26 | 574 |

## Where is the investment coming from?

The **R23.13 billion** in investment commitments was recorded through **Wesgro**, the province's trade and investment agency, and the **Atlantis Special Economic Zone**, a green-technology manufacturing hub north of Cape Town. New investment usually means new jobs in construction, manufacturing, logistics and services.

## Who is getting support?

Of the 574 businesses that received provincial support in 2025/26:

- 279 were black-owned
- 167 were women-owned
- 71 were youth-owned
- 7 were owned by persons with disabilities

Supporting small businesses matters for job seekers because small and medium firms are among the biggest employers in South Africa.

## Which sectors are hiring in the Western Cape?

The Premier did not list specific vacancies. Sectors that typically drive hiring in and around Cape Town include:

- Tourism and hospitality
- Business process outsourcing (call centres and customer service)
- Technology and digital services
- Green energy and manufacturing (including the Atlantis SEZ)
- Agriculture and agro-processing

## How do I apply?

There is no single recruitment drive linked to this announcement. Search employers' official careers pages, the Western Cape Government's own vacancies page and national job portals, and be wary of anyone asking for money to secure a job.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Cape Town is a jobs hotspot.** Lower unemployment means more opportunities than in many other provinces.
- **Skills still matter.** Digital, customer service and technical skills are in demand.
- **Foreign workers** need a valid South African work visa before starting a job.
