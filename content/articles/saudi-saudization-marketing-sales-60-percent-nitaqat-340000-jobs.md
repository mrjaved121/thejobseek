Saudi Arabia has made several big Saudization moves in 2026 that change which private sector jobs expats can get. The **Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)** has set a **60% quota in marketing and sales**, completed the **100% localization of 69 admin jobs**, and launched a **new Nitaqat phase** to localize more than **340,000** jobs.

## The three changes at a glance

| Rule | Saudi share | Applies to | In force since |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marketing and sales (18 professions) | 60% | Firms with 3+ workers in these jobs | 19 April 2026 |
| Administrative support (69 professions) | 100% | Firms with 1+ worker in these jobs | 5 April 2026 (19 jobs), 4 October 2026 (50 jobs) |
| New Nitaqat phase | Varies by sector and size | Private sector | 2026, for three years |

## 60% in marketing and sales

HRSD announced the quota on **19 January 2026** and gave employers three months to comply. It started on **19 April 2026**.

| Marketing (10) | Sales (8) |
|---|---|
| Marketing manager | Sales manager |
| Advertising agent | Retail sales representative |
| Advertising manager | Wholesale sales representative |
| Graphic designer | Sales representative |
| Advertising designer | ICT sales specialist |
| Public relations specialist | Sales specialist |
| Advertising specialist | Commercial specialist |
| Marketing specialist | Commodity broker |
| Public relations manager | |
| Photographer | |

A Saudi employee in a **marketing** job counts toward the quota only if paid at least **SAR 5,500 a month**.

## 69 admin jobs now fully Saudized

The 100% rule covers secretarial, writing, translation, data entry and admin support roles. The first 19 of these jobs were reserved for Saudis from 5 April 2026; the other 50 joined them on **4 October 2026**, once a six-month transition ended. Read our earlier report on the [69 administrative professions](/saudi-arabia/saudi-saudization-69-administrative-professions/).

## Nitaqat: more than 340,000 jobs

On **22 January 2026**, HRSD launched a new phase of the **Developed Nitaqat** programme. It runs for **three years** and aims to localize **more than 340,000 additional** private sector jobs, with targets by sector and company size. HRSD says the first phase, launched in 2021, helped employ more than **550,000** Saudis. Some reports say the target is "by 2029", but HRSD has not given an end year.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Office, admin and translation jobs are closed.** Expats should not apply for secretarial, data entry or translation roles in Saudi Arabia.
- **Marketing and sales jobs are shrinking.** Up to 40% can still go to expats, so senior or specialist candidates have a better chance.
- **Technical and skilled trades remain open.** Engineering, healthcare, construction and energy roles are still hiring expats.
