German defence company **Rheinmetall** has opened a new gun barrel factory in **Telford, Shropshire**, bringing new manufacturing jobs to the West Midlands. The **UK Gun Hall** was officially opened on **22 October 2026** and has created **35 new jobs** so far. That number is expected to grow to **around 100** as production speeds up.

The factory adds to the **685 people** Rheinmetall UK already employs in Telford.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Location | Hadley Castle, Telford, Shropshire |
| New jobs now | 35 |
| New jobs at full production | About 100 |
| Existing Rheinmetall staff in Telford | 685 |
| Site size | 123,780 sq ft |
| Products | 120mm and 155mm barrels and breeches |
| Expected boost to UK economy | £400 million |

## What the factory will make

The site is a renovated former GKN warehouse. At full capacity it can make up to **245 barrels a year** in two sizes, plus more than **50 breeches**. Products include barrels for the British Army's new **Challenger 3** tank and for future artillery systems. It is the first time in about two decades that tank barrels will be made in the UK.

The first staff were trained at Rheinmetall's plant in Unterlüß, Germany, and production started on schedule.

## Why it matters for jobs

Defence Secretary **Wes Streeting** said defence in the West Midlands is worth **£1.8 billion** and supports about **8,000 jobs**. Rheinmetall has invested more than **£200 million** in its UK businesses over the past five years. New factories like this usually create extra work for local suppliers, engineering firms and service companies too.

## What kinds of jobs to expect

Rheinmetall has not listed specific roles. Factories of this type typically need:

- **CNC machinists and machine operators**
- **Mechanical and manufacturing engineers**
- **Quality inspectors and metrology technicians**
- **Maintenance technicians**
- **Logistics and stores staff**

Search for current UK vacancies on Rheinmetall's official careers website at [rheinmetall.com/en/career](https://www.rheinmetall.com/en/career/career-overview) and open the job listings to look for Telford and other UK roles.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Engineering skills pay off.** Machining, welding and quality control experience are valuable in defence manufacturing.
- **Check security rules.** Defence jobs often need security clearance, which can take time and may have nationality requirements.
- **Look at the supply chain.** Local suppliers around Telford may also hire as the factory grows.
- **Young people:** ask about apprenticeships at defence and engineering firms in the area.
