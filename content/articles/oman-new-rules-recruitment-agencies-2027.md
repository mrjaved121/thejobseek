Oman's Ministry of Labour has issued new rules for agencies that recruit expatriate workers. **Ministerial Decision No. 390/2026** takes effect on **1 January 2027** and includes important protections for workers coming to Oman.

## Key protections for workers

- **No recruitment fees:** agencies may **not charge workers** any recruitment fees.
- **Job offer first:** a job offer stating the **wage and type of work** must exist before a worker is recruited.
- **Accommodation and care:** agencies must provide suitable accommodation and care while workers are in their custody.
- **Approved contracts:** only contracts and forms approved by the ministry may be used.
- **Complaints:** disputes go through the ministry, with set time limits.

## Rules for agencies

- Only offices **licensed by the Ministry of Labour** may recruit, and they must be Omani-owned and managed.
- Each agency must lodge a **RO 5,000 financial guarantee** with the ministry.
- Recruitment must be completed within **45 days**, with a two-year guarantee on the service.

## Fines

Agencies breaking the rules face fines, including:

- RO 100 per worker for recruiting domestic workers through unlicensed channels
- RO 50 per contract for not using approved contracts or job offers
- RO 200 per worker for skipping required medical examinations
- RO 500 if a non-Omani is allowed to manage the office

## What this means for you

If you are applying for a job in Oman through an agency:

1. Ask for the agency's **Ministry of Labour licence** and check it.
2. Make sure you receive a **written job offer** with the salary before you travel.
3. **Do not pay recruitment fees.** From 2027, charging workers is against the rules.

Even before 2027, paying an agent for a job abroad is risky. Read our [guide to spotting fake job offers](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/).
