**Etihad Airways**, the UAE's national airline, plans to add about **7,000 employees by 2035**, taking its total workforce to **22,000**. The plan was outlined by **Dr Nadia Bastaki**, Etihad's chief people, government and corporate affairs officer, as reported by Khaleej Times.

## The hiring plan

| Item | Figure |
|---|---|
| Expected headcount by end of 2026 | About **15,000** |
| New hires needed in 2026 | More than **3,000** |
| Hired in 2026 so far (by mid-September) | About **1,340** |
| In the pipeline | About **600**, plus around **300** more expected by year-end |
| Workforce target for 2035 | **22,000** |

Most of the 2026 hiring is for **frontline roles**, especially **pilots, technicians and engineers**. Etihad's operations are growing by about **20%** a year.

Hiring continued during the six months of regional disruption, when about **700** new staff were onboarded, mostly online.

## How Etihad recruits

- **Monthly recruitment campaigns** inside and outside the UAE. A European campaign has just finished, and campaigns in **Africa and China** are planned.
- Hiring through **LinkedIn** and international roadshows.
- **AI screening:** Etihad's system sorts and ranks over **50,000 CVs within three days**. Cabin crew roles alone draw over **100,000 applications**.

## Opportunities for Emiratis

More than **300 UAE nationals** are expected to join this year through Etihad's **cadet, airport and technician programmes**, and some core grades are reserved for UAE nationals.

## Career growth

Cabin crew can be promoted to **purser within 18 months**, according to the airline.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Pilots, aircraft technicians and engineers** are the biggest need. Keep your licences and type ratings up to date.
- **Cabin crew applicants:** with over 100,000 applications, a clear, keyword-rich CV matters, because AI screens it first.
- Apply only through **Etihad's official careers site**. Etihad does not charge for applications.
