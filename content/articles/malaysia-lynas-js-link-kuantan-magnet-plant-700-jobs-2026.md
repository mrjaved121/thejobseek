New **Malaysia jobs** are on the way in Pahang. A **RM1 billion** partnership between Australia's **Lynas Rare Earths** and South Korea's **JS Link** will build a **permanent magnet manufacturing plant in Kuantan**, expected to create **up to 700 jobs**. Malaysia's Natural Resources and Environmental Sustainability Ministry (NRES) shared the details on 2 November 2026.

## What is being built in Kuantan?

The factory will make **neodymium-iron-boron (NdFeB) permanent magnets**, powerful magnets used in electric vehicle motors, wind turbines and many electronic devices.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Partners | Lynas Rare Earths Ltd and JS Link Inc (South Korea) |
| Location | Kuantan, Pahang, Malaysia |
| Value | About RM1 billion long-term cooperation agreement |
| Jobs | Up to 700 |
| Product | NdFeB permanent magnets |
| Capacity | 3,000 tonnes a year |
| Extra investment | Lynas to take A$50 million (about RM144 million) equity in JS Link |

## How did the deal come together?

The two companies first signed a memorandum of understanding in **July 2025**. It was upgraded to a long-term cooperation agreement signed on **7 July 2026**. Lynas will also supply rare earth materials for magnet production in both Malaysia and South Korea.

## Why does this matter for Malaysia?

NRES says the project moves Malaysia beyond simply processing raw rare earths into making **high-value downstream products**. It is part of the government's plan to build a complete rare earth industry, from exploration and mining to processing and manufacturing.

## What jobs could be available?

A magnet manufacturing plant typically needs:

- Production operators and technicians
- Materials, process and quality engineers
- Maintenance and electrical technicians
- Laboratory analysts
- Supply chain, safety and admin staff

These are typical roles for this kind of plant, not a confirmed vacancy list. Hiring timelines have not yet been announced.

## How do I apply?

No recruitment details have been published yet. Watch the official careers pages of Lynas Rare Earths and JS Link, and Malaysia's MYFutureJobs portal, for openings in Kuantan.

- **Engineering and technical skills pay off.** Advanced materials jobs usually pay above average.
