People looking for **USA jobs for foreigners** and local factory work in the Midwest have a new employer in Indiana's RV heartland. **Boundless Recreation**, a start-up recreational vehicle maker, plans to create **up to 115 jobs** at its plant in **Bristol, Elkhart County**, by the end of **2028**, Indiana Governor **Mike Braun** announced on 28 October 2026.

The company has already hired **45 people** and began building RVs in **October 2026**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Boundless Recreation |
| Location | Bristol, Elkhart County, Indiana |
| Investment | $2 million |
| Plant size | 104,000 sq ft |
| Jobs planned | Up to 115 by end of 2028 |
| Already hired | 45 |
| Products | Fifth-wheel RVs now, travel trailers later |

## About the company

Boundless Recreation was set up in **2025** by people with long experience in the RV industry. Founder and president **Nate Nickell** leads the business. Indiana likes to call itself the world's **RV capital**, and Elkhart County sits at its centre, with many of America's biggest RV brands and suppliers based nearby, so the area has a deep pool of skilled workers and parts makers.

## Which jobs to expect

The company has not listed job titles, but RV plants usually hire:

- **Production line workers** and assemblers
- **Carpenters, framers and cabinet builders**
- **Electricians and plumbers** for RV wiring and water systems
- **Welders** for chassis and frames
- **Quality inspectors**
- **Supervisors** and purchasing staff

No wage figures were confirmed in the state announcement. Local reports said average pay would be above the county average, but this has not been officially stated in the release we checked.

## Can foreigners apply?

These are factory jobs, and RV makers rarely sponsor work visas for production roles. Applicants normally need the right to work in the US, such as citizenship, a green card or another valid work permit.

## How to apply

No official careers page has been published yet. Interested workers can:

1. Watch Indiana job boards and the state's official job service for Bristol and Elkhart County listings.
2. Contact the plant directly if you have RV or woodworking experience.
3. Keep your CV ready with any assembly, carpentry or electrical skills.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Start-ups hire in waves.** With 45 hired and 115 planned, about 70 more jobs are still to come.
- **RV experience counts.** Workers from other Elkhart plants can move across quickly.
- **Trade skills pay off.** Carpentry, wiring and plumbing are all used in RV building.
- **Growth through 2028** means openings over the next two years.
