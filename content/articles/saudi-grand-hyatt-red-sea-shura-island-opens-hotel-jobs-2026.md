New **Saudi jobs** in luxury hospitality are opening on the Red Sea coast. **Hyatt** has welcomed its first guests at **Grand Hyatt The Red Sea**, a **430-room** resort on **Shura Island**, its second resort at the Red Sea destination and its **eighth hotel in Saudi Arabia**. More restaurants will open in later phases, pointing to continued hiring.

## What has opened?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Hotel | Grand Hyatt The Red Sea |
| Location | Shura Island, Red Sea, Saudi Arabia |
| Rooms | 430 rooms, suites and villas |
| Dining | 9 venues (7 opening in later phases) |
| Pools | 5 (3 outdoor, 2 indoor) |
| Wellness | 8,070 sq ft spa, 5,380 sq ft fitness centre |
| Events | 5 meeting suites, a junior ballroom and a Grand Ballroom |

**Stephen Ansell**, who runs Hyatt in the Middle East and Africa, noted that it follows only a few months after **Miraval The Red Sea** welcomed its first guests. General Manager **Jean-Francois Durand** leads the new resort.

## Which jobs does a resort like this need?

A 430-key resort with nine restaurants and large event spaces typically employs staff in:

- Front office, guest relations and concierge
- Housekeeping and laundry
- Food and beverage: chefs, cooks, servers, baristas, stewards
- Spa therapists and fitness instructors
- Events, sales and marketing
- Engineering, security and finance

These are typical hotel departments, not a confirmed vacancy list. Because seven dining venues open in later phases, more food and beverage roles are likely over the coming months.

## How do I apply?

Search for Grand Hyatt The Red Sea vacancies on Hyatt's official **[careers website](https://careers.hyatt.com)** and filter by Saudi Arabia. Never pay a fee to an agent promising a Hyatt job.

## What it means for job seekers

- **The Red Sea is a hospitality hotspot.** Several luxury resorts have opened there in the past two years.
- **Saudi nationals are prioritised** under Saudization rules for many hotel roles.
- **Experienced expats** in culinary, spa and management roles are often recruited internationally.
