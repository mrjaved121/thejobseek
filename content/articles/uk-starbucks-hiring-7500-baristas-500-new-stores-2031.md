Young people looking for a first job have a big new opening. **Starbucks** plans to hire **7,500 new baristas** across the **UK** over the next **five years** to staff **500 new coffee shops** by 2031, with most of the growth **outside London**. Roles will appear on Starbucks' official careers site.

## What has Starbucks announced?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| New baristas | 7,500 over five years |
| New coffee shops | 500 by 2031 |
| Focus areas | Midlands, north of England, Wales, Scotland |
| Current UK staff | 18,000+ |

The plan follows a global restructuring last year that closed dozens of weaker UK outlets. Starbucks UK boss **Darren King** said the company wants to help more young people into work by keeping entry-level roles easy to access.

## Why does this matter for young people?

Early this year, more than **1 million** young people in the UK were classed as **NEET**, meaning outside work, education and training. At the same time, the latest KPMG and REC jobs report showed permanent hiring fell in October for the first time in four months, so a large youth-friendly hirer is welcome news.

## Which roles will be available?

New coffee shops typically need:

- Baristas (the main role announced)
- Shift supervisors
- Assistant store managers
- Store managers

Only barista numbers have been announced; the other roles are typical for new stores.

## Who can apply?

Barista roles usually need no formal qualifications and suit school leavers, students and people returning to work. Applicants must have the right to work in the UK.

## How do I apply?

Search UK locations on Starbucks' official **[careers site](https://careers.starbucks.com/)** and set alerts for new stores near you.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Entry-level hospitality is hiring** in northern England, Wales and Scotland.
- **Steady five-year pipeline**, so new openings will keep appearing.
- **Barista experience** can lead to supervisor and manager roles.
