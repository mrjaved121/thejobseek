New **Huddersfield jobs** are on the horizon in West Yorkshire. Bradford-based developer **Nano Park Company** is building a business park on the site of the former **Wappy Spring Inn**, on Lindley Moor Road beside the **M62**, and expects it to create **120 jobs** once businesses move in.

## What is being built?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Developer | Nano Park Company (Bradford) |
| Location | Lindley Moor Road, Huddersfield, next to the M62 |
| Units | 13 industrial units with offices |
| Layout | Warehousing on the ground floor, offices above |
| Expected jobs | 120 |
| Letting agents | Knight Frank and Eddisons |

The pub closed in summer 2022, and the empty site had suffered fly-tipping, arson and vandalism. Construction is now under way, with the steel frame for the second building being put up.

## Who will the units suit?

Knight Frank's **Max Vause** said the Nano Park format works well for **small and young companies** and for **satellite operations of larger firms**, offering storage and office space in one place. He said the developer's earlier parks in Bradford, Wakefield and Armley are fully let.

## What jobs could appear?

Jobs will depend on which businesses lease the units. Business parks of this type usually bring roles such as:

- Warehouse and stock staff
- Delivery and van drivers
- Office administration and customer service
- Trade counter and light manufacturing staff
- Sales and management roles

These are typical for this kind of development, not confirmed vacancies.

## How do I apply?

There is no single recruitment drive. Watch for tenant announcements and local job adverts in Huddersfield and the Kirklees area as units are let, and check employers' own careers pages once they are named.

## What it means for job seekers

- **M62 access attracts logistics firms.** Warehouse and driving roles are likely.
- **Small business growth means varied jobs.** Expect a mix of office and practical roles.
- **Jobs will arrive gradually** as each unit is let.
