New **US hiring 2026** is under way in western Maryland. Building work has begun in **Hagerstown** on a **355,000 sq ft factory** for **Jamison Door Company**, owned by **BMP Group**, and the project is expected to bring **250 new jobs by 2030**.

## What is being built?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employers | Jamison Door Company and parent BMP Group |
| Location | Hagerstown, Washington County, Maryland |
| Plant size | 355,000 sq ft |
| Campus | First phase of a planned 58-acre site |
| Jobs | 250 by 2030 |
| Builder | ARCO Design/Build |
| Public grants | $2m from the Senator George C. Edwards Fund and $2m from Washington County (Glass Magazine) |

Automated production, R&D and product testing will all happen in the same building.

## What will the plant make?

Besides expanding Jamison Door's own production, the plant will make several **European BMP Group brands** in the US for the first time, including:

- Salco Industrial Doors
- Champion Door
- Campisa dock levelers

These products have previously been made overseas. Jamison Door was founded in **1906** and has long roots in the region.

## Which jobs are likely?

A modern door and dock-equipment factory typically employs:

- Production and assembly workers
- Machine operators and welders
- Maintenance and automation technicians
- Engineers and product testers
- Warehouse, shipping and logistics staff

Jamison has not listed specific vacancies yet, so treat these as examples; hiring will ramp up gradually to 2030.

## How do I apply?

Check vacancies on Jamison Door's official **[careers page](https://www.jamisondoor.com/careers)**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Reshoring is creating US factory jobs**, as European products move production to Maryland.
