Supermarket chain **Lidl** is planning a big jobs boost in the south of Ireland. On **23 October 2026**, the company announced it will spend **€420 million across Munster**, creating **more than 500 permanent jobs** through new stores, store upgrades and a large new distribution centre in Cork.

About **250** of the new roles will be in **County Cork**. Lidl's Munster workforce is expected to grow over the longer term from about **1,300 to 1,800** people.

## A new warehouse for Cork

The biggest single project is a **regional distribution centre at Little Island**, costing around **€200 million**. It will be Lidl's fourth in the Republic, after Charleville, Mullingar and Newbridge, and is expected to be up and running **by 2030**. Distribution centres need large teams of warehouse operatives, forklift drivers, HGV drivers, maintenance technicians and supervisors.

## Part of a national plan

The Munster spending is one part of a wider **€600 million, five-year programme** across the Republic, which includes up to **35 new stores**. Lidl already employs more than **6,300 people** in Ireland across **192 stores**, its Tallaght head office and its distribution centres.

The company also highlighted its links with local food producers, saying it buys from 120 Cork suppliers and spent €136 million with them last year.

Taoiseach **Micheál Martin** called the plan a significant commitment to Ireland. Lidl Ireland and Northern Ireland CEO **Robert Ryan** said it showed the company's long-term confidence in Cork and Munster.

## What roles to expect

Lidl has not yet listed the exact jobs or store locations. Based on how its stores and warehouses are staffed, likely roles include:

- Customer assistants and shift managers in stores
- Store managers and deputy managers
- Warehouse operatives and team leaders
- HGV and transport staff
- Facilities and maintenance technicians

Store locations and opening dates have not been published, so the hiring will come in stages over the next few years.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Watch Lidl Ireland's careers channels.** New stores are usually advertised a few months before opening.
- **Retail experience helps** but Lidl also hires people for entry-level store roles and trains them.
- **Logistics workers in Cork** should keep an eye on the Little Island project, as warehouse hiring will ramp up nearer to 2030.
- **Construction trades** can benefit sooner, with around 4,500 building jobs linked to the 25 projects.
