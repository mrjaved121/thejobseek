Canada is about to announce its **Immigration Levels Plan for 2027 to 2029**. The plan sets how many permanent residents, foreign workers and students the country will admit. Under the **Immigration and Refugee Protection Act**, the minister must table it with the annual report to Parliament **on or before 1 November**, or within 30 sitting days after that if Parliament is not sitting. Last year's plan came out on **4 November 2025**.

As of **21 October 2026**, the new plan had not been released. Immigration Minister **Lena Diab** said in mid-October that it will come in **November**, and she hinted that some levels could go up.

## Current targets (2026-2028 plan)

| Category | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 |
|---|---|---|---|
| New permanent residents | 380,000 | 380,000 | 380,000 |
| All temporary resident arrivals | 385,000 | 370,000 | 370,000 |
| Temporary workers | 230,000 | 220,000 | 220,000 |
| – International Mobility Program | 170,000 | 170,000 | 170,000 |
| – Temporary Foreign Worker Program | 60,000 | 50,000 | 50,000 |
| Students | 155,000 | 150,000 | 150,000 |

The 2027 and 2028 numbers are notional and will be updated in the new plan. TFWP targets exclude seasonal workers.

## Fewer foreign workers are arriving

The target for **temporary worker arrivals in 2025 was 367,750**. IRCC told a parliamentary committee in March 2026 that worker arrivals in 2025 fell **47%**, or 184,340, compared with 2024. By our calculation, that leaves 2025 arrivals well over **100,000 below** the target. In early 2026, worker arrivals were down **74%** on the same period of 2024.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Wait for the plan before paying for anything.** The numbers for 2027 will decide how many work permits are available.
- **Employer-backed routes matter most.** The TFWP needs an LMIA, so start with an employer offer.
- **Express Entry is changing too.** From 3 November 2026, high-wage jobs can earn extra points.
