Retail **jobs in Ireland** are growing in Cork city centre. **Lidl Ireland** opened a new store at **Merchants Quay Shopping Centre** on Patrick's Street on **29 October 2026**, creating **23 new permanent jobs**, TheCork.ie reported.

The company spent **€4.5 million** on the store, which fills a unit that had been empty for almost four years.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Lidl Ireland |
| Location | Merchants Quay Shopping Centre, Patrick's Street, Cork |
| New jobs | 23 permanent |
| Investment | €4.5 million |
| Opened | 29 October 2026 |
| Store size | 1,530 sq m sales floor |
| Lidl stores in Cork | 23 |

## About the store

It is Lidl's **second store in Cork city centre** and its **23rd in the county**. The shop has 16 tills, 14 of them self-service (including Lidl Ireland's 200th self-checkout), and an in-store bakery.

Regional managing director **Tara O'Connor** said the company was pleased to open in the heart of the city.

## More Lidl jobs on the way

Lidl Ireland now has **193 stores** and more than **6,300 staff**. In Cork, it has recently won planning permission for new stores at **Mahon Point** and **Carrigtwohill**, which should mean more hiring in the area once they are built. Lidl also bought €136m worth of goods from 120 Cork food suppliers in 2025.

## Typical Lidl store roles

The company did not list the 23 roles, but Lidl stores usually hire:

- **Customer assistants** (tills, shelves and bakery)
- **Shift managers / deputy store managers**
- **Store managers**
- **Cleaners and stock staff**

Pay rates were not given in the announcement.

## How to apply

Apply through the careers section of Lidl Ireland's official website, **lidl.ie**. Search for Cork vacancies and set up alerts so you hear about the Mahon Point and Carrigtwohill stores when recruitment starts. Applying is free, and genuine Lidl adverts are posted only on its own site.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Permanent retail work is available** without a degree, which suits students, parents returning to work and newcomers to Ireland.
- **City-centre jobs are easy to reach** by bus or on foot.
- **Retail can lead to management.** Lidl promotes many managers from shop-floor roles.
- **Non-EU nationals** will need permission to work in Ireland; retail jobs are rarely eligible for new employment permits.
