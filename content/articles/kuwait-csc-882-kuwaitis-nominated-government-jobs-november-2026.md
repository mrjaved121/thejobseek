New **Kuwait government jobs** are being filled this week. The **Civil Service Commission (CSC)** has nominated **882 Kuwaiti men and women** for positions in government institutions, as part of the national employment plan. Nominees were asked to report to their assigned employers from **Tuesday 3 November 2026**.

## Who has been nominated?

The 882 nominees are all registered on the CSC's **central employment system**. According to the commission, this batch includes three groups:

| Group | Description |
|---|---|
| First-time nominees | Registered job seekers being nominated for the first time |
| Re-nominated applicants | People previously rejected by the entity they were sent to |
| Returning applicants | People who declined an earlier job placement |

The nominations follow a decision of the **Civil Service Council**.

## How were jobs allocated?

The CSC said it used its approved **priority criteria**. Placements across job categories took into account each government body's staffing needs, especially the **academic qualifications and specialisations** required for open posts.

## What should nominees do now?

Nominees must go to the government entity named in their nomination, starting Tuesday 3 November, and complete their employment paperwork within the set schedule. Missing the deadline can affect a nomination, so act quickly.

## How do I apply?

Kuwaiti citizens who want a government job must register on the CSC's central employment system and keep their qualifications and contact details up to date. The CSC announces new nomination batches regularly through official channels and the KUNA news agency.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Government hiring continues in batches.** Keep your CSC registration active.
- **Qualifications drive placement.** Your degree and specialisation decide which entities you are matched to.
- **A second chance is possible.** Applicants who were rejected or declined earlier offers were included again.
