Canada is having its **biggest Express Entry year on record**. According to the official rounds of invitations published by **Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC)**, the department held **62 draws** and sent **143,467 invitations to apply** for permanent residence between **5 January and 16 October 2026**.

## How 2026 compares

| Year | Invitations to apply |
|---|---|
| **2026 (to 16 October)** | **143,467** |
| 2025 | 113,998 |
| 2024 | 98,903 |
| 2023 | 110,266 |
| 2022 | 46,539 |
| 2021 | 114,224 (previous record) |

*Figures added up from IRCC's published draw records.*

With more than two months still to go, 2026 has already passed every earlier full year since the system launched in 2015.

## Canadian Experience Class draws

People already working in Canada have done well this year. IRCC has held **18 Canadian Experience Class (CEC) rounds** in 2026, inviting **57,750** candidates. The minimum score has stayed in a narrow range:

| Detail | 2026 |
|---|---|
| CEC rounds | 18 |
| CEC invitations | 57,750 |
| Lowest cut-off | 507 (March) |
| Highest cut-off | 523 (August) |
| Latest CEC draw | 15 October: 2,500 invitations, cut-off 519 |

## The latest draws

| Date | Draw type | Invitations | Cut-off |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 October | French-language proficiency | 5,000 | 435 |
| 15 October | Canadian Experience Class | 2,500 | 519 |
| 13 October | Provincial Nominee Program | 752 | 772 |
| 1 October | Trades occupations | 3,500 | 476 |
| 29 September | Canadian Experience Class | 2,000 | 518 |
| 28 September | Provincial Nominee Program | 733 | 725 |
| 16 September | Senior managers with Canadian work experience | 250 | 389 |

Provincial nominee cut-offs look high because a provincial nomination adds 600 points to a candidate's score.

## What it means if you are in the Gulf

- **Category draws are your best chance.** French speakers and skilled trades workers have been invited with much lower scores than general draws. Recent French draws cut off at 435 and trades at 476.
- **Language scores move the needle.** A stronger IELTS General, CELPIP-General or PTE Core result can add many points. See: [which English tests count for Express Entry](/canada/canada-express-entry-language-tests-ielts-celpip-pte-core/).
- **Check your occupation's TEER level** before building a profile. See: [Canada NOC and TEER explained](/canada/canada-noc-teer-categories-explained/).
- **More changes are coming.** From 3 November, high-wage job offers can earn more points. See: [Express Entry changes on 3 November](/canada/canada-express-entry-changes-november-2026-high-wage-occupations/).

> Draw sizes and cut-offs change every round. Check IRCC's official rounds page before making decisions.
