US defence giant **Lockheed Martin** says a big push to make more missiles and munitions will create **6,000 jobs across the United States**, including about **2,000 in Texas**. The company announced the plan on **14 October 2026**.

At the centre of the expansion is a **$300 million investment** at the company's **Grand Prairie, Texas**, plant. This is part of a wider **$8–9 billion** commitment through **2030** to modernise or grow **more than 20 US sites**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| New US jobs | About 6,000 |
| Jobs in Texas | About 2,000 |
| Grand Prairie investment | $300 million |
| Wider investment plan | $8–9 billion through 2030 |
| Sites to be upgraded | 20+ across the US |
| Current Grand Prairie production staff | Nearly 800 |

Lockheed Martin has not given a hiring timetable, and it has not said exactly how many of the Texas jobs will be at Grand Prairie.

## What the Grand Prairie site does

The Grand Prairie plant makes parts for several missile programmes, including **PAC-3 MSE**, **THAAD**, **PrSM** and **JATM**. These parts are then sent to final assembly plants in **Camden, Arkansas**, and **Troy, Alabama**.

The site will grow to **more than 735,000 square feet**. That includes 100,000 sq ft of new space and 85,000 sq ft of offices being turned into factory floor. More than 30 high-precision machines are already working there, and the company expects to have **nearly 100 advanced machines** by the end of 2026. An automated machining cell from supplier Hadrian is due to start in early 2027.

## What kinds of jobs to expect

Lockheed Martin has not listed the roles. For a manufacturing expansion of this type, demand is usually strongest for:

- **CNC machinists and machine operators**
- **Manufacturing, quality and industrial engineers**
- **Maintenance technicians**
- **Supply chain and logistics staff**
- **Inspectors and production supervisors**

## How to apply

Lockheed Martin posts all openings on its official careers site, [lockheedmartinjobs.com](https://www.lockheedmartinjobs.com). Search by location, for example "Grand Prairie" or "Texas", and set up job alerts.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Skilled trades are in demand.** If you have machining, welding or maintenance experience, this is a good time to apply.
- **Check citizenship rules.** Many defence jobs require US citizenship and a security clearance. Read each job advert carefully.
- **Think about suppliers too.** Big defence expansions also create jobs at local parts makers and contractors.
- **Set alerts early.** Openings will be posted over several years, so a saved search on the careers site is the easiest way to catch them.
