**Omanisation jobs** are growing steadily. Oman's **Ministry of Labour** says it delivered **53,525 job and on-the-job training opportunities** for Omanis by the end of September 2026, reaching **89%** of its target of **60,000** for the year under the **National Employment Plan**.

## How many opportunities has Oman created?

| Category | Achieved | Target | Completion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jobs and replacement opportunities | 41,024 | — | 95% |
| On-the-job training places | 12,501 | — | 74% |
| **Total** | **53,525** | **60,000** | **89%** |

"Replacement" opportunities are jobs where Omanis replace expatriate workers as part of localisation.

## How did the public and private sectors perform?

| Sector | Jobs created | Job target | Training places | Training target |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Government | 14,235 | 10,000 | 2,370 | 2,000 |
| Private sector | 26,789 (first-time jobs) | 33,000 | 10,131 | 15,000 |

The **government sector beat both of its targets**. The private sector is behind on both but still created most of the jobs.

The ministry also said the total number of placement, replacement and training opportunities provided reached **95,323**, of which **41,798** went to people with previous work experience.

## Why does on-the-job training matter?

On-the-job training links job seekers with real workplaces and trains them for roles that match their qualifications. The ministry says this improves long-term job stability and makes national workers more efficient.

## How do I apply?

Omani job seekers should register with the Ministry of Labour's national employment services and keep their qualifications up to date. Watch for vacancies and training programmes announced by the ministry and the **National Employment Program**, and apply through official channels only.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Hiring for Omanis is on track** for a strong 2026.
- **Private sector training places are still available**; the target has not yet been reached.
- **Government jobs exceeded targets**, so competition for remaining posts may rise.
