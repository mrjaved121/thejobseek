UAE nationals looking for a healthcare career were offered **347 job opportunities** at **Nabd Al Seha 2026**, the Emirati healthcare career fair held at **Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC)**. The event was organised by Dubai's **Emirati Human Resources Development Council (EHRDC)** together with the **Dubai Healthcare City Authority (DHCA)**. Emiratis who missed it can follow up directly with the participating hospitals and clinics.

## Who organised the fair?

EHRDC and DHCA partnered with three government bodies: **MoHRE** (the Emiratisation ministry), **MOHESR** (the higher education ministry) and the **DHA**, Dubai's health regulator. The venue was DHCC's **Mohammed Bin Rashid Academic Medical Center**.

## Which roles were on offer?

Private healthcare providers based in DHCC brought openings in four broad areas:

- **Clinical** jobs, such as nursing and allied health
- **Administrative** jobs, including patient services and HR
- **Technical** roles, for example lab, imaging and health IT
- **Support** functions across hospital operations

Visitors could also learn about training and development programmes that prepare new graduates for hospital work.

## Why is this part of Emiratisation?

EHRDC deputy chairman **Abdulla Ali bin Zayed Al Falasi** said the fair supports the **Dubai Economic Agenda (D33)** by building national talent in a strategic sector. DHCA chief executive **Issam Galadari** said it gives Emiratis a direct route into supportive workplaces. Healthcare is one of the sectors where the UAE wants a larger share of citizens in the private workforce.

## How do I apply now?

The organisers did not publish a single application link. Emiratis can:

1. Contact the careers teams of hospitals and clinics located in DHCC.
2. Register on the **Nafis** programme for private-sector support and salary top-ups.
3. Watch EHRDC channels for the next Emirati career event.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Health graduates are in demand:** clinics are actively seeking Emirati nurses, technicians and administrators.
- **Non-clinical paths exist:** business, IT and HR graduates can also work in hospitals.
- **Keep your file ready:** have your DHA licence (if clinical), CV and certificates updated.
