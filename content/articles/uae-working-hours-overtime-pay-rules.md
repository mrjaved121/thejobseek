How long can your employer ask you to work in the UAE, and how much extra should you be paid for overtime? These are the rules for private-sector workers under Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021.

## Normal working hours

- **Maximum 8 hours a day or 48 hours a week.**
- Some sectors and job types can have different hours under the law's executive regulations.
- **Ramadan:** daily hours are cut by **2 hours**.
- Time spent travelling to and from work does not count as working time, except for some categories.

## Breaks

After **5 hours in a row**, you are entitled to a break or breaks totalling **at least 1 hour**. Breaks are not counted as working hours.

## Summer midday break

From **15 June to 15 September**, work in the sun and in open areas is banned between **12:30 pm and 3 pm**.

## Overtime limits

Your employer can ask you to work overtime, but **no more than 2 extra hours a day**.

## How overtime is paid

Overtime pay is calculated on your **basic salary**:

| When you work | Overtime pay |
|---|---|
| Normal overtime | Hourly basic wage **+ 25%** |
| Between 10 pm and 4 am | Hourly basic wage **+ 50%** |
| On your rest day | A replacement day off, **or** hourly basic wage + 50% |

**Example:** basic salary AED 4,800 a month. Hourly basic wage = 4,800 ÷ 30 ÷ 8 = AED 20. One hour of normal overtime = AED 20 + 25% = **AED 25**. One hour at night = **AED 30**.

**Exception:** the overtime pay rule does **not** apply to shift workers.

## Other points

- If you work for more than one employer (for example on a part-time permit), you cannot be made to work more hours than your contract says unless you agree in writing.
- Employers with remote staff must set clear working hours for them.

## If you are not paid for overtime

1. Keep a record of the hours you work, such as time sheets or messages.
2. Raise it with your employer in writing.
3. If unresolved, file a complaint with **MoHRE**.
