Saudi Arabia has opened a **status correction window for Yemeni nationals** who are living or working in the kingdom without valid papers. The programme started on **Sunday, 11 October 2026**, follows a royal directive, and is planned to last **about two months**.

It is run by the **General Directorate of Passports (Jawazat)** with other agencies, while the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development handles job transfers on **Qiwa** and **Musaned**. For Yemeni workers, it offers a way to clear an expired iqama or an absence report and work legally again.

## The programme in one table

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Who it covers | Yemeni nationals in Saudi Arabia with irregular status |
| Start date | 11 October 2026 |
| Duration | About two months (no exact end date published) |
| Run by | Jawazat, with HRSD platforms for job transfers |
| Online platforms | Absher (appointments), Qiwa (private-sector workers), Musaned (domestic workers) |
| Embassy step | Free preliminary online registration with the Yemeni Embassy in Riyadh |
| Levy relief | Old work permit levies cancelled on transfer for workers reported absent before 16 June 2026 (per Qiwa) |

## Who can do what

| Your situation | What the programme allows | Main condition |
|---|---|---|
| Iqama has expired | Move to a new employer online, or take a final exit visa | Valid travel document |
| Reported absent (huroob) | Cancel the report and transfer to another employer | Valid travel document |
| Expired visit visa or visitor ID | Six-month extension done online, no office visit | Valid passport and biometrics already on file |
| No passport or ID at all | Free six-month visit visa | Book on Absher, attend a correction centre with proof of Yemeni nationality |
| Holder of an exceptional visit visa | Can obtain residency if hired while the visa is valid | Valid passport and a medical check |

People who choose to leave on a final exit visa are not barred from coming back later, the reports said.

## What changes for employers

Qiwa says employers can book a virtual appointment through a dedicated service for correcting the status of absent Yemeni workers. When such a worker joins a new firm, **earlier unpaid work permit levies are written off** and charges begin only from the transfer date. This applies to professional workers reported absent **before 16 June 2026**, including those who never received an iqama.

The reports did not publish fee amounts for the transfer itself. Only the visit visa for undocumented Yemenis is described as free.

## What it means for job seekers

- **A route back to legal work.** Yemenis stuck with an absence report or an old iqama can now be hired openly instead of working informally.
- **Lower cost for the hiring firm.** Because old levies are cancelled, a new employer is less likely to refuse you over debts left by your previous sponsor.
- **Time is short.** Two months passes quickly, and appointments at correction centres may fill up. Start the process now.
- **This is for Yemenis only.** Workers of other nationalities need the general HRSD procedures, such as the [employer work permit correction window that ends with 2026](/saudi-arabia/saudi-work-permit-correction-deadline-end-2026/).

## Practical steps

1. **Check your passport.** Most options need a valid Yemeni passport or embassy travel document. Renew it first if it has expired.
2. **Register with the embassy** online if you need a document. It is free and needs no agent.
3. **Find an employer** willing to take your transfer and ask them to use the Qiwa service (or Musaned for domestic workers).
4. **Book on Absher** if you need to visit a Jawazat correction centre. You do not need an Absher account to book this appointment.
5. **Get the medical test done** where required, and keep copies of every receipt and confirmation.
6. **Avoid middlemen.** Anyone charging money to "register" you is not part of the official process.
