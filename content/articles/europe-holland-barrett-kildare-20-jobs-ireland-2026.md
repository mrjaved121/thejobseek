Health and wellness retailer **Holland & Barrett** is bringing new **jobs in Ireland** to County Kildare. The company announced on **28 October 2026** a **€17.5 million** investment in its Irish business over three years, which will create around **20 new roles** in **Kildare town**, plus 6 to 10 indirect jobs in the area.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Holland & Barrett (with partner Road Truck Services) |
| Location | Kildare town, Co. Kildare |
| Total investment | €17.5 million over 3 years |
| Direct new roles | About 20 |
| Indirect roles | 6–10 |
| Start | Irish online orders fulfilled locally from November 2026 |

## What is changing

Until now, Irish customers' online orders have been packed and shipped from the UK. From **November 2026**, they will be handled in Ireland. The investment is split into:

- **€8.2 million** of new spending on online order fulfilment
- **€9.3 million** on store fulfilment, which supplies Holland & Barrett's Irish shops

The work is being run with **Road Truck Services (RTS)**, a logistics firm based in Kildare. The retailer will also hold about **€1.5 million** more stock in Ireland and add **34 Irish brands** to its online range.

## Likely roles

Because this is a fulfilment operation, most jobs are expected in:

- Warehouse picking and packing
- Goods-in and stock control
- Dispatch and delivery
- Team leader and supervisor posts

The new roles appear to sit with the logistics partner RTS rather than directly with Holland & Barrett.

## A boost for Kildare

Kildare town sits on the M7 motorway, making it a natural base for distribution across Ireland. Moving fulfilment from the UK to Ireland also means faster deliveries for Irish customers, which could lead to more stock, more orders and, over time, more jobs at the site.

## How to apply

No official vacancy page for these roles was published. Job seekers in Kildare should watch the official channels of Holland & Barrett Ireland and Road Truck Services, and check Ireland's public employment service, Intreo, for local warehouse vacancies.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Entry-level warehouse work** is coming to a smaller Irish town, not only Dublin.
- **Peak season is near,** so hiring may move fast before Christmas.
- **Forklift tickets help** for stock and goods-in roles.
- **EU/EEA citizens** can work in Ireland freely; others need a valid permit.
