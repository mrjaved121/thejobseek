**OPmobility Riverside Missouri jobs are on the way: the French automotive supplier said on 10 November 2026 that it will create more than 100 jobs at a new facility in Riverside, in the Kansas City region.** The company is leasing a 213,000-square-foot building for the operation. Hiring dates are not out yet, but vacancies will go on the [OPmobility careers site](https://careers.opmobility.com/?locale=en_US).

## What has OPmobility announced?

The news came from the [Missouri Department of Economic Development](https://ded.mo.gov/press-room/opmobility-establish-automotive-facility-riverside-creating-more-100-new-jobs) and from a [company press release](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/opmobility-create-more-100-jobs-162700972.html) issued the same day.

OPmobility said the Riverside site enlarges its North American manufacturing network and puts it closer to customers in the Midwest. Christophe Marceau, who leads the group's Exterior & Lighting business, called the investment an important step for the company's growth on the continent.

## What will the jobs pay?

Exact wages were not published. The state's release says average pay in the new positions will be well above what is normal for the county. The company's own statement expects about $27.5 million in new payroll from the project.

Neither source explains what period that payroll figure covers, so it should not be divided by the number of jobs to guess a salary.

## Which roles are likely?

No job titles have been released. A plant that makes exterior parts and lighting for vehicles would normally need:

- production operators and assemblers
- injection moulding and paint line technicians
- maintenance technicians
- quality and logistics staff
- engineers, team leaders and managers

These are typical for this kind of factory and are not confirmed openings.

## Who is OPmobility?

OPmobility was founded in 1946 and supplies large car makers around the world. It works in four areas: vehicle exteriors and lighting, complex modules, energy storage, and electrification using batteries and hydrogen. It also has a software development activity.

The group reports 38,100 employees, 152 plants and 40 research and development centres, and economic revenue of €11.5 billion for 2025.

## Why the Kansas City region?

The area is already a car-making centre. The announcement describes a $5.3 billion regional automotive industry with more than 20,000 jobs, plants run by Ford and General Motors, and over 100 suppliers. Trucks leaving Kansas City can reach 90% of the mainland United States within two days.

Missouri is supporting the project through its Missouri Works programme, which rewards job creation with tax credits or retained withholding taxes. The estimated benefit of $2,149,143 depends on the company meeting the programme's conditions.

The release also says OPmobility may get help from Missouri One Start, the state's workforce division, which assists eligible employers with recruitment and training. That often means local hiring events once a plant is close to opening.

## How do I apply?

There is no Riverside application window yet. To be ready:

1. Visit the [OPmobility careers website](https://careers.opmobility.com/?locale=en_US) and search for United States or Missouri roles.
2. Create a candidate profile and switch on job alerts.
3. Follow the Missouri Department of Economic Development and the City of Riverside for news of hiring events.

Do not pay anyone for a job offer. Real applications go through the company's own site.

- More than 100 manufacturing jobs are coming to Riverside, with pay promised above the local average.
