**Morocco has more than 22,500 public sector jobs open in November 2026, with teaching and health making up most of them.** The Ministry of National Education is filling **19,712 places** through the regional teacher training centres (CRMEF), and the Ministry of Health and Social Protection is recruiting **2,094 nurses and health technicians**, according to a round-up by FesNews Media on 9 November. Most competitions close between **10 and 30 November**, and official notices are gathered on the government's [public employment portal](https://www.emploi-public.ma/fr/).

## Which ministries are hiring?

| Employer | Posts | Closing date |
|---|---|---|
| Ministry of National Education (CRMEF) | 19,712 (17,953 teaching, 1,759 support) | 25 November 2026, 4:30pm |
| Ministry of Health – nurses and technicians | 2,094 | 19 November 2026 |
| Tangier-Tetouan-Al Hoceima health grouping (GST) | 328 | 25 November 2026 |
| ONDA (airports) | 79 | 13 November 2026 |
| OFPPT (vocational training) | 39 competitions | 19 November 2026 |

Figures are as reported by FesNews Media. Some French-language outlets gave a lower count of teaching exam places earlier in the month, so check the final number on the ministry's own notice before applying.

## What teaching jobs are on offer?

The education intake covers primary teaching (including Amazigh-language tracks) and middle and secondary school teaching, plus administrative, teaching-support and social support roles. Applicants register and upload documents only through the ministry's online platform. The written exam is set for **Saturday 28 November 2026**. Moroccan media also reported this month that the age limit for the teaching competition has been raised to **40**.

## Which health roles are open?

The national health competition is for first-grade nurses and health technicians in a wide range of specialities:

- general (polyvalent) nursing
- anaesthesia and intensive care, and emergency care
- midwifery
- radiology and laboratory analysis
- speech therapy, psychomotor therapy and mental health

Exams take place on **29 November**. Separately, the Tangier-Tetouan-Al Hoceima regional health grouping wants doctors, pharmacists, dentists, nurses, engineers, administrators and technicians.

## Are there other public jobs?

Yes. Universities in Fez, Meknes, El Jadida and Rabat are recruiting administrators, engineers, technicians and assistant professors, with deadlines from 14 to 23 November. ONDA is looking for 65 technicians in firefighting and air navigation safety, along with 14 engineering and finance executives. Local councils across the country also have smaller competitions for engineers, technicians and medical staff.

## How do I apply?

Start at Morocco's official [public employment portal (emploi-public.ma)](https://www.emploi-public.ma/fr/), which lists civil service competitions and links to each ministry's registration platform. Read each notice carefully for diploma requirements, age limits and the documents you must upload. Avoid unofficial sites asking for fees.

## What it means for job seekers

- Deadlines are close: the health competition shuts on 19 November and teaching on 25 November.
- Graduates with nursing or health technician diplomas have one of the biggest intakes in years.
- The higher age limit for teaching gives career changers up to 40 a chance.
- Most posts require Moroccan nationality, as is usual for civil service roles.
