Bahrain has made it easier for companies to share workers, with new protections for the workers involved. The Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA) announced **Resolution No. 2 of 2026** at the end of September 2026.

## What changes

The resolution allows two new arrangements for foreign workers who **already hold a valid work permit in Bahrain**:

1. **Within the same group:** an employer can assign a worker to another establishment it owns, or to a company in the same corporate group.
2. **To a different employer:** with LMRA approval, a worker can work for **another employer for up to three months**.

## Conditions

- The worker must give **written consent**. An employer cannot move you without your agreement.
- Both the original and the receiving employer must agree.
- Bahrainization requirements must be met. Extra fees may apply if the receiving employer has a lower Bahrainization rate.
- Both employers share **joint responsibility** for the worker's rights during the assignment.
- LMRA registers each approved arrangement and notifies everyone involved.

## What it does not do

The new rules **do not create a new way to recruit workers from abroad**. They only apply to people already authorised to work in Bahrain.

## What this means for workers

- A temporary assignment can mean extra experience or work during a quiet period with your own employer.
- **Do not sign consent until you understand** where you will work, for how long, and who pays your salary.
- Keep a copy of the LMRA approval. Without it, working for another employer could still break the rules.
