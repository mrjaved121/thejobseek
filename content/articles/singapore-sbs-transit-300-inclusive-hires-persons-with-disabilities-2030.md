**Singapore jobs** are opening up for persons with disabilities (PwDs). Public transport operator **SBS Transit** has pledged **300 inclusive hires by 2030**, counting both its own staff and people hired by the companies that provide services to it. The pledge was announced at a partnership signing with **SG Enable** on **29 October 2026**.

## What did SBS Transit announce?

SBS Transit and SG Enable, Singapore's agency for disability and inclusion, signed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). **Shawn Huang**, Senior Parliamentary Secretary for Manpower, praised the pledge at the signing event held at Taman Jurong Community Club.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | SBS Transit |
| Partner | SG Enable |
| Hiring commitment | 300 cumulative inclusive hires |
| Deadline | 2030 |
| MOU signed | 29 October 2026 |
| Focus areas | Inclusive employment, staff training, public education |

## What does the partnership cover?

The MOU covers three areas:

- **Inclusive employment** – SBS Transit will step up hiring of persons with disabilities.
- **Staff training** – employees will learn how to better support colleagues and commuters with disabilities.
- **Public education** – SG Enable will use SBS Transit's buses, trains and stations to raise awareness about inclusion.

## How is Singapore doing on disability employment?

Singapore's **Enabling Masterplan 2030** aims to raise the employment rate of resident PwDs to **40% by 2030**.

| Year | PwD employment rate |
|---|---|
| 2022 (masterplan launch) | 31.4% |
| 2025 | 34.7% |
| 2030 target | 40% |

The government is also strengthening hiring support, including enhancements to the **Enabling Employment Credit** and a new **Supported-Enterprise Enabling Grant**, to encourage more employers to hire inclusively and help workers stay and progress in their jobs.

## How do I apply?

Visit the official **[SBS Transit careers page](https://www.sbstransit.com.sg/careers)** for current vacancies. Jobseekers with disabilities can also contact **SG Enable**, which offers job matching and employment support in Singapore.

## What it means for job seekers

- **More doors are opening.** Large employers like SBS Transit are setting public hiring targets for PwDs.
- **Support is available.** SG Enable can help with job matching, training and workplace adjustments.
- **Employers get help too.** Government credits and grants make it easier for companies to hire inclusively.
