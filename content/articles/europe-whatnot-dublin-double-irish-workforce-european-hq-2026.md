**Whatnot**, the live video shopping marketplace, has opened its **European headquarters in Dublin** and plans to **double its Irish workforce** within **18 months**. The company already has **more than 130 staff** in Ireland, so the plan points to well over **130 new jobs**. IDA Ireland announced the move on **20 October 2026**.

Whatnot lets sellers run live video streams where buyers bid on and buy items in real time, from trading cards and collectables to fashion and electronics.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Whatnot |
| Current Irish staff | 130+ |
| Plan | Double headcount in 18 months |
| New office | European HQ, Sir John Rogerson's Quay, Silicon Docks, Dublin |
| Platform launch for Irish sellers | 2027 |
| Support | IDA Ireland |

## Which roles are being filled

| Team | Examples of work |
|---|---|
| Engineering and AI | Building the app, recommendation and live-stream systems |
| Operations | Running the marketplace day to day |
| Strategy | Planning growth across Europe |
| Trust and safety / trust and risk | Fraud checks, seller standards, buyer protection |
| Customer experience | Supporting buyers and sellers |

Trust and safety and customer experience teams are often open to people from many backgrounds, including language skills, retail or customer service experience. Engineering and AI roles will usually ask for technical degrees or strong project work.

## Why Dublin

Chief executive **Grant LaFontaine** credited Irish universities for the depth of engineering and AI talent the company has found. Ireland's Minister of State **Alan Dillon** and IDA Ireland's **Mary Buckley** both welcomed the expansion as a sign of confidence in Ireland's tech sector.

## How to apply

Open roles are listed on Whatnot's official careers page: [whatnot.com/careers](https://www.whatnot.com/careers). Filter by Dublin to see European jobs.

## What it means for job seekers

- **E-commerce know-how counts.** Experience in online marketplaces, payments or seller support is directly relevant.
- **Languages help in trust and support teams.** A European hub often needs staff who can work in several languages.
- **AI engineers are wanted across Dublin.** Whatnot joins other firms hiring for AI in the city this month.
- **Apply early.** The 18-month timeline means steady hiring rather than one big intake.
