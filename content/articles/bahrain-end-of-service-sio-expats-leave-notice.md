Bahrain changed how expat workers receive their end-of-service benefit. Instead of the employer paying a lump sum when the job ends, employers now pay **monthly contributions** into a fund run by the government's **Social Insurance Organization (SIO)**. Here is how it works, along with the main leave and notice rules.

## The SIO end-of-service fund

| Item | Details |
|---|---|
| Who it covers | **Non-Bahraini** workers in the **private sector** |
| Started | Contributions began in **March 2024** |
| Legal basis | **Law No. 14 of 2022**, which set up the fund |
| Who pays | **The employer**, monthly |
| Rate, first 3 years | **4.2%** of wages |
| Rate after 3 years | **8.4%** of wages |

The aim is to protect workers if an employer fails to pay at the end of the job, which was a common complaint.

## How much you are entitled to

The entitlement itself is the same as under the Labour Law:

- **Half a month's wage** for each of the first **3 years**
- **One full month's wage** for each year after that
- Part-years count pro rata

**Example:** wage BHD 400 a month, 5 years' service:
- First 3 years: 3 × 200 = BHD 600
- Next 2 years: 2 × 400 = BHD 800
- **Total: BHD 1,400**

If a worker dies, the benefit goes to their family in their home country.

## Annual leave

- At least **30 days** of paid leave after **one year** of service
- Leave builds up at **2.5 days a month**

## Sick leave

| Period | Pay |
|---|---|
| First 15 days | Full pay |
| Next 20 days | Half pay |
| Next 20 days | Unpaid |

## Probation and notice

- **Probation:** up to **3 months** for open-ended contracts.
- **Notice:** either side can end an open-ended contract with **30 days' notice**. A contract can require longer notice from the employer, but not from the worker.
- If the employer doesn't give the required notice, you are paid for the notice period.

## Tips

- **Check your SIO record** to confirm contributions are being paid for you.
- Keep your **contract and payslips** up to date.
- For work permit questions, contact the **Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA)**.
