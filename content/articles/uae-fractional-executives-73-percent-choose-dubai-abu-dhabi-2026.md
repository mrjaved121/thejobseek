The UAE is the top choice for senior leaders looking for flexible work abroad. New figures from **Fractional**, a network of part-time C-suite executives, show that **73%** of members who applied to work in another country chose **Dubai or Abu Dhabi**. Khaleej Times reported the data on **21 October 2026**.

The numbers cover applications to Fractional's 64 city hubs between **1 August and 13 October 2026**.

## The key numbers

| Measure | Result |
|---|---|
| Cross-border applicants choosing the UAE | 73% |
| Lowest monthly share for the UAE | 70% |
| Likelihood vs other hubs | Almost 3 times more likely |
| Dubai/Abu Dhabi applicants based abroad | 27% (8% for all other hubs) |
| Countries non-resident applicants came from | 27 |
| From Europe / South Asia | 36% / 24% |
| Applicants with 15+ years' experience | More than half |
| Would relocate to the UAE | 31% |
| Would serve UAE firms through visits | About 67% |

Notably, none of the executives already living in the UAE applied to move to another country's hub.

## What "fractional" work means

A fractional executive is a part-time senior leader. Instead of hiring a full-time chief financial officer or chief marketing officer, a growing company pays for a few days a week of expert help. One executive may serve two or three firms at once.

This model suits the UAE, which has thousands of small and mid-sized companies, start-ups and family businesses that are expanding but are not ready to pay a full senior salary.

## Why it matters for the job market

The data adds to other signs that the UAE is still pulling in experienced talent. Hiring in the country rose in the third quarter, and employers are still competing for senior finance, technology, sales and operations skills.

Fractional's managing director Rhys Holding said senior talent continues to pick the UAE. The data suggests that demand is strong enough for many leaders to test the market through regular visits before deciding to move.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Senior professionals have options beyond full-time roles.** Part-time and project-based leadership work is a real route into the UAE market.
- **Specialist skills sell best.** Finance, fundraising, digital marketing, tech strategy and operations are the areas growing firms most often buy in.
- **Mid-career staff benefit too.** Companies that bring in fractional leaders often go on to build teams under them.
- **Expect competition.** With applicants from 27 countries, a clear track record and UAE or GCC experience help you stand out.
- **Check the rules before working.** If you are not a UAE resident, make sure any work arrangement is set up properly with the right permit.
