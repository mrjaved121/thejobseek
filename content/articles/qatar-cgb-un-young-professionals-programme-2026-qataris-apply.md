Young Qataris looking beyond **Qatar government jobs** to an international career have a chance to join the United Nations. The **Civil Service and Government Development Bureau (CGB)**, working with the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs**, has invited Qatari nationals to apply for the **UN Young Professionals Programme (YPP) 2026**, The Peninsula reported. Applications are open through **October and November 2026**.

## What is the YPP?

The Young Professionals Programme is the UN's main entry route for young graduates. Successful candidates are offered entry-level professional posts in the UN Secretariat, often after a placement exam. Qatar is among the countries invited to take part this year, which means Qatari applicants can sit the exam.

## Key details

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Programme | UN Young Professionals Programme 2026 |
| Exam area | Development and Sustainability |
| Nationality | Qatari |
| Age | Born on or after 1 January 1994 (32 or under by end of 2026) |
| Education | Bachelor's degree in a relevant field |
| Languages | Fluent English or French |
| Application window | October–November 2026 |
| Selection | Screening, written exam, interviews |

## Who should apply

The "Development and Sustainability" stream suits graduates in:

- Economics and development studies
- Environmental science and climate policy
- International relations and public policy
- Social sciences and statistics

Selection takes several months, so this is a long-term career step rather than a quick job.

## How to apply

Applications are made online through the official [UN Careers portal](https://careers.un.org/), where the YPP 2026 job opening for the Development and Sustainability family is published. Check the exact closing date there, create your profile early and upload your degree certificate. CGB is promoting the programme, so it is also worth following its official channels for updates or info sessions.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Start now** – the online form is detailed and needs a full education and work history.
- **Prepare for the exam** – it tests subject knowledge and writing skills, so review development topics and practise essays.
- **Polish your language skills** – fluency in English or French is required, and both is a bonus.
- **Think about mobility** – UN posts can be based anywhere in the world.
