There is fresh news for Omanis seeking **Oman government jobs**. On **Sunday 1 November 2026**, the **Ministry of Labour**, working with **Royal Court Affairs**, announced a set of vacancies for Omani citizens in **Muscat** and **Salalah**. Applications are open for two weeks, until **Saturday 14 November 2026**.

Royal Court Affairs is one of Oman's most prestigious public employers, so openings there usually attract strong interest.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Royal Court Affairs |
| Announced by | Ministry of Labour, Oman |
| Locations | Muscat and Salalah |
| Open to | Omani citizens |
| Apply by | SMS to 80057 |
| Application period | 1–14 November 2026 |

The exact number of jobs and the list of job titles were published in the ministry's official announcement graphics. Check the ministry's channels for the opportunity numbers before you apply.

## Eligibility conditions

- Omani nationality
- Registered as a job seeker with the **Ministry of Labour**
- Good conduct and behaviour
- Not currently employed in any government job, including military and security posts
- Has not resigned from a government body or government-owned company in the past year
- Some posts are reserved for **residents of Dhofar Governorate**
- Apply for **one job only**

## Part of a wider push

The Ministry of Labour regularly publishes public sector vacancies in partnership with government bodies, as part of Oman's drive to find work for registered national job seekers. Announcements like this often come in batches, so candidates who miss out should keep checking for the next round.

## How to apply

The ministry has asked applicants to apply by text message:

1. Find the **opportunity number** of the job you want in the Ministry of Labour announcement.
2. Send an SMS to **80057** in this format: *opportunity number # civil number AT*.
3. Send your application before the end of **14 November 2026**.

Make sure your job seeker profile on the Ministry of Labour system is up to date before applying, as this is how your eligibility is checked.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Act fast.** The window is only two weeks.
- **Choose carefully.** You may apply for just one post, so pick the one that best matches your qualification.
- **Salalah candidates have an edge** for posts reserved for Dhofar residents.
- **Keep your registration active** to be considered for future national vacancies.
