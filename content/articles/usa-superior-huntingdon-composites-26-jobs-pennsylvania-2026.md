Good news for **Pennsylvania manufacturing jobs**: **Superior Huntingdon Composites** will put **$4.4 million** into its plant in **Huntingdon** to bring a long-idle production line back to life, creating **26 new full-time jobs** over three years. The state's Department of Community and Economic Development (DCED) announced the project on **28 October 2026**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Superior Huntingdon Composites LLC |
| Location | 1200 Susquehanna Ave, Huntingdon, PA |
| Investment | $4.4 million |
| New jobs | 26 full-time over 3 years |
| Jobs kept | 74 |
| State offer | $1,687,757 PIDA loan + $200,000 PA First grant |

## What is happening

The company makes **fibreglass mat and veil**, thin reinforcing materials used inside composite products. Its customers are in **defence, energy, building materials, transport and marine** industries. The plant has two lines running; the third has been shut since **2012**. Restarting it will raise output to meet growing demand.

The state support is a funding offer made up of a low-interest Pennsylvania Industrial Development Authority (PIDA) loan and a PA First grant.

## Likely roles

Restarting a production line usually means hiring:

- Machine and line operators
- Maintenance technicians and electricians
- Quality control staff
- Material handlers and warehouse workers
- Shift leads

## Why it matters

Restarting a line that has been silent for more than a decade is a clear sign of rising demand for composite materials. For a small town like Huntingdon, 26 new jobs plus 74 secured jobs is a meaningful boost to the local economy, supporting shops and services around the plant.

## How to apply

No official careers page for these posts was included in the announcement. Job seekers in Huntingdon County should watch the company's own channels and the state's official job service, PA CareerLink, where local manufacturing vacancies are listed.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Rural Pennsylvania is hiring.** Small towns are seeing new factory jobs tied to defence and energy demand.
- **Entry-level options exist.** Operator and handler roles often train on the job.
- **Shift work is likely,** so check rotas and shift pay when you apply.
- **Composites skills transfer** to aerospace, boats and wind energy.
