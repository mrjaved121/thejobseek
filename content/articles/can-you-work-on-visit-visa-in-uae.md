Coming to Dubai on a visit visa to search for a job is very common, especially for job seekers from Pakistan and India. But many people misunderstand what a visit visa actually allows, and some end up in serious trouble.

## The short answer: no, you cannot work on a visit visa

A UAE visit visa allows you to visit, attend interviews and meet employers. It **does not** allow you to start working. Working in the UAE requires a valid work permit and residence visa arranged by your employer (or an approved freelance or self-employment permit).

## Looking for a job on a visit visa is allowed

You can use your visit to:

- Attend interviews and walk-in hiring events
- Meet recruiters and register with licensed recruitment agencies
- Take assessments or trade tests requested by employers

What you cannot do is start the job — not even a "trial period" — until your work permit is issued.

## What happens when you get a job offer

1. The employer applies for your work permit through the UAE's official channels (for most private sector jobs this is MOHRE).
2. You sign the official offer letter and employment contract.
3. Your status is changed from visitor to resident, or you exit and re-enter on an entry permit, depending on the process the employer uses.
4. You complete the medical test and Emirates ID biometrics.
5. Your residence visa is issued and you can legally work.

The employer should cover the costs of the work permit and visa process.

## Risks of working illegally

People who work without a permit, and employers who hire them, can face heavy fines. Workers risk deportation and entry bans, and have no legal protection if they are not paid — a problem many unregistered workers face.

## Practical tips

- Plan your visit duration and budget realistically; finding a job can take longer than expected.
- Keep your visa validity in mind to avoid overstay fines.
- Ask every employer clearly how and when your work permit will be processed.
- Never pay a recruiter for a job. See our guide on [warning signs of fake UAE job offers](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs).

Rules and fees change from time to time, so always confirm current requirements on official UAE government websites before you travel.
