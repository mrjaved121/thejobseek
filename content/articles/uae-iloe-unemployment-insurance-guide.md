The UAE's **Involuntary Loss of Employment (ILOE)** scheme gives workers a temporary income if they lose their job through no fault of their own. Subscription is **mandatory** for private-sector and federal government employees, so it is worth knowing how it works.

## How much it costs and pays

| Category | Basic salary | Premium | Maximum monthly payout |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Up to AED 16,000 | AED 5 a month (AED 60 a year) | AED 10,000 |
| 2 | Above AED 16,000 | AED 10 a month (AED 120 a year) | AED 20,000 |

- The payout is **60% of your average basic salary** over the 6 months before you lost your job, up to the cap.
- It is paid for **up to 3 months per claim**.
- You can pay premiums **monthly, quarterly, every 6 months or yearly**.

## Who can claim

You can claim if **all** of these are true:

- You have been subscribed for **at least 12 months in a row**, with no gap of more than 3 months.
- You have paid your premiums on time.
- You **did not resign**, and you were **not dismissed for disciplinary reasons**.
- There is no absconding case against you.
- You are **legally in the UAE** when you claim.
- You claim **within 30 days** of losing your job, or of a labour court case being settled.

## How to subscribe

Use the ILOE website (iloe.ae) or app, or pay through bank apps, exchange houses, kiosks, business service centres, or SMS and telecom bills.

## How to claim

1. Make sure your job loss is recorded and that your status is clear with MoHRE.
2. Submit the claim on the ILOE website or app, or through the call centre (**600 599 555**), within **30 days**.
3. Keep your termination letter and any documents your employer gave you.

## Don't let your policy lapse

If premiums are **more than 3 months overdue**, the policy is cancelled and a fine applies under the law. You would then need to take out a new policy, and your 12-month waiting period would start again.

> Set up automatic yearly payment so you never miss a premium. At AED 60 a year for most workers, it is one of the cheapest safety nets available.
