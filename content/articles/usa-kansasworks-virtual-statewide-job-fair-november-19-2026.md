Looking for **Kansas jobs** without leaving home? **KANSASWORKS** is hosting a free **Virtual Statewide Job Fair** on **Thursday 19 November 2026**, from **8:30am to 4:30pm**, where job seekers can apply, chat live and **interview virtually** with Kansas employers, the Kansas Department of Commerce announced on 5 November.

## What is the KANSASWORKS virtual job fair?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Organiser | KANSASWORKS (Kansas Department of Commerce) |
| Date | Thursday, 19 November 2026 |
| Time | 8:30am – 4:30pm |
| Format | Online, on any device |
| Cost | Free |
| Registration | Required for every fair |

The event portal includes a **training video for job seekers**, a **list of participating employers** and links to register and log in.

## What can I do at the fair?

- Fill out job applications
- Chat live with employers
- Take part in **virtual interviews**

Lieutenant Governor and Secretary of Commerce **David Toland** said the monthly fairs help connect active job seekers with employers keen to hire talent in Kansas.

## How should I prepare?

- **Register in advance** through the KANSASWORKS virtual job fair portal.
- **Dress professionally**, as you may be invited to interview.
- Check the employer list and prepare a short introduction for each company you want to speak to.
- Test your camera and microphone before the event.
- Need accommodation? Call **(877) 509-6757** before the fair.

## How do I apply?

Register for the event through the KANSASWORKS website and the link on the Kansas Department of Commerce announcement. You will need to register even if you joined a previous fair.

## What it means for job seekers

- **No travel needed.** You can job hunt from anywhere in Kansas.
- **Monthly events** mean more chances if you miss this one.
- **Interviews can happen on the day**, so treat it like a real interview.
