Saudi Arabia's foreign workforce has **shrunk for the first time in more than two years**. According to official quarterly data reported by **Semafor** on **6 October 2026**, the number of foreign workers, **excluding domestic workers**, fell by **16,000** in the three months to **June 2026**.

## What is behind the fall

| Factor | Details |
|---|---|
| **Construction** | The main driver, with fewer construction jobs |
| **State-linked projects** | Some trimmed staff or did not renew contracts after budget cuts |
| **NEOM** | Significantly scaled back |
| **Regional conflict** | Hurt the region's image as a safe place to work |

Another sign: the number of **medical insurance policies for expats did not grow** in the first half of 2026, suggesting fewer new arrivals.

## What has not changed

- The **unemployment rate for Saudi citizens** was unchanged.
- The economy is **still creating jobs**, but more slowly.

## Signs of recovery

Purchasing managers' surveys point to **private-sector hiring picking up**. The **Cooper Fitch Gulf Employment Index** showed Saudi hiring up **1.5%** in Q3 2026, as companies grew sales and technical teams. Energy was the fastest-growing sector across the Gulf. ## What it means for job seekers

- **Construction workers:** expect fewer openings and more competition on Saudi projects for now.
- **Skilled professionals:** demand continues in energy, technical and sales roles, and skilled workers can now bring family sooner. Read: [Saudi family visas alongside work visas](/saudi-arabia/saudi-skilled-expats-family-visas-alongside-work-visa/).
- **Check every offer carefully.** In a slower market, fake job offers increase. Never pay for a visa.
