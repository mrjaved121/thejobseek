Here is solid **Canada hiring 2026** news from the public sector. Canada's **Department of National Defence (DND)** is recruiting **dozens of civilian workers** for **5th Canadian Support Base Gagetown** in New Brunswick. Its recent job fair at The Cultural Centre in downtown **Fredericton** drew so many people that the line of applicants reached the parking lot, CBC News reported on 28 October.

## Why the base is hiring

Gagetown is growing. Ottawa is spending **more than $1 billion** to upgrade the base's training area, firing ranges and defence systems, and has also set money aside for housing nearby. A DND spokesperson said the fair was meant to show people the civilian careers available as the government rebuilds the Canadian Armed Forces. DND did not give a headcount or pay rates.

## Roles on offer

The event flyer listed a wide mix of jobs:

| Area | Example roles |
|---|---|
| Office | Administration, customer service, finance, project coordination |
| Health | Registered and licensed practical nurses, nurse practitioners, doctors, psychologists, social workers |
| Operations | Kitchen and food services, warehouse, procurement, forestry |
| Technical | IT, engineering |
| Trades | Skilled trades, heavy equipment operators, mechanics |
| Emergency | Firefighters |
| Education | Language teachers |
| Early career | Student jobs and apprenticeships |

## Who can apply

These are federal public service jobs. **Canadian citizens and permanent residents get preference**, so newcomers without PR status will find it hard to be selected. Some posts may also need a security clearance.

## How to apply

Go to the official [DND civilian job opportunities page](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/corporate/job-opportunities/civilian-jobs/civilian-job-opportunities.html). It links to the Government of Canada job search filtered to National Defence. Search for "Gagetown" or "Oromocto" to find local postings, and check DND's apprenticeship programmes page if you are starting out in a trade.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Set a job alert** on the federal job site for Gagetown and Oromocto.
- **Follow the screening questions closely** – federal applications are often screened out for missing details.
- **Health workers** are in particular demand on defence bases; have your licence details ready.
- **Students and apprentices** have a real entry route into stable government work here.

Federal-style recruitment events are popping up elsewhere too, like the [Edmonton hiring events with 24 employers](/canada/canada-edmonton-24-employers-hiring-november-2026-walmart-alberta-health/), and tradespeople can also look at the [Red Seal completion bonus](/canada/canada-red-seal-completion-bonus-5000-trades-november-16-2026/).
