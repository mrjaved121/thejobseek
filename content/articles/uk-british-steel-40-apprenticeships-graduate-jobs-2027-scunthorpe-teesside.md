**UK graduate jobs** and apprenticeships are open at one of Britain's biggest industrial employers. **British Steel** said on **2 November 2026** that it is recruiting **40 apprentices** and a new group of graduates, all starting in **early September 2027**.

The jobs are at its Scunthorpe works in Lincolnshire and its Northern Mills in Teesside and Skinningrove.

## Apprenticeships at a glance

| Site | Electrical | Mechanical | Total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scunthorpe | 12 | 12 | 24 |
| Teesside and Skinningrove | 8 | 8 | 16 |
| **Total** | **20** | **20** | **40** |

Training is fully funded, and apprentices are paid while they learn.

## Graduate roles

The first graduate openings are in:

- **Manufacturing operations**
- **Mechanical engineering**
- **Electrical engineering**
- **Materials engineering** (quality and process)

British Steel says more graduate roles in other functions will be added in the coming weeks. It has not said how many graduates it will take on.

## Benefits

Both schemes come with:

- 35 days' holiday, including bank holidays
- A company pension
- A health cash plan and an Employee Assistance Programme
- Safety clothing and local discounts

Salary figures were not published.

## Who can apply

Apprentice applicants need **at least three GCSEs at grade 4/C**, including **Maths, English and Science**, plus a positive attitude and willingness to learn. Graduate requirements were not listed in the announcement, but engineering roles normally ask for a relevant degree.

HR director **Nick Ashton** said investing in future talent is key to the company's long-term success.

## Closing dates

| Programme | Closing date |
|---|---|
| Graduate scheme | 26 February 2027 |
| Apprenticeships | 8 March 2027 |

## How to apply

Go to the official [British Steel vacancies page](https://www.britishsteel.co.uk/join-our-team/current-vacancies/). It links to the company's careers website, where you can find the apprentice and graduate listings and register for job alerts. British Steel does not accept speculative CVs, so apply to a listed role.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Apply early.** Closing dates are months away, but places are limited and popular schemes can fill fast.
- **Show practical interest.** For apprenticeships, mention hands-on projects, DIY, cars or anything that shows mechanical or electrical curiosity.
- **Graduates should watch for new roles.** More functions are being added soon.
- **Check travel.** Scunthorpe, Teesside and Skinningrove are industrial sites, so plan how you would get to work.
