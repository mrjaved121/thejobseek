Saudi Arabia's state-owned **Health Holding Company** has announced **4,291 new healthcare vacancies** spread across its **20 health clusters**. The company runs public hospitals and clinics through these regional clusters around the Kingdom. The announcement was made on **19 October 2026**.

## Key facts

| Detail | What we know |
|---|---|
| New vacancies | 4,291 |
| Where | All 20 health clusters across Saudi Arabia |
| Fields | Several medical and healthcare specialties |
| Aimed at | Saudi practitioners |
| How to apply | Online, through each health cluster's recruitment portal |
| Deadline | 15 days from the date each cluster publishes its vacancies |

Including recruitment already completed and approved training contracts, the company says its total healthcare hiring for 2026 reaches **19,610**.

## Who these jobs are for

The vacancies are aimed at **Saudi healthcare practitioners**, in line with the Kingdom's push to employ more citizens in the health sector. Requirements differ by specialty and cluster, so candidates should read each cluster's listing carefully.

## How to apply

1. Find the **recruitment portal of the health cluster** in your region.
2. Check the specialties advertised and the conditions for each post.
3. Apply online **within 15 days** of the cluster's announcement. Each cluster sets its own closing date, so do not wait.

## What it means for expat healthcare workers

These particular openings target Saudi nationals. For expat doctors, nurses and allied health staff, it is a sign of strong overall hiring in Saudi healthcare, where many specialist and private-sector roles remain open to foreign professionals. Expats should:

- apply only through **official hospital or employer portals**, or recruiters licensed in their home country;
- make sure their **Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS) licensing** route is clear before accepting an offer;
- **never pay for a job offer**, which is a common scam in Gulf healthcare recruitment.
