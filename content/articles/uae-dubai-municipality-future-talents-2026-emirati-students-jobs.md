Emirati students hoping for **UAE government jobs** can now apply for **Dubai Municipality's "Future Talents" programme**. The scheme, reopened for 2026 according to Emarat Al Youm on **29 October**, sponsors university students in key technical subjects and gives them a path to a job at the municipality once they graduate.

## What the programme offers

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Dubai Municipality |
| Programme | Future Talents (Mawaheb Al Mustaqbal) |
| Benefit | Monthly allowance until graduation |
| After graduation | Chance of employment with Dubai Municipality |
| Who can apply | Emirati students in year 3 or 4 |
| Reported deadline | 6 November 2026 |

The amount of the allowance and the number of places were not published.

## Eligible fields of study

The municipality is targeting 12 specialisations that match its future needs:

- Cybersecurity
- Artificial intelligence
- Data analytics
- Digital forensics
- Geographic information systems (GIS)
- Environmental science
- Physics and chemistry
- Geology
- Engineering
- Food technology and food safety
- Applied nutrition

## Requirements

- UAE nationality
- Studying in the **third or fourth year** of a university degree
- GPA of **3 or higher**
- Not holding any other scholarship at the same time
- Male applicants must have completed **national service**

## Why it matters

Dubai Municipality looks after planning, buildings, environment, food safety and public health in the emirate. Its work is becoming more digital, which is why AI, data and cybersecurity sit next to traditional science subjects on the list. Students who join now can help shape those services as future employees.

## How to apply

Apply on Dubai Municipality's official online form: [dm.gov.ae – Future Talents application](https://www.dm.gov.ae/scholarship-application-form-future-talents/). Have ready a copy of your UAE passport, Emirates ID, your latest transcript showing your GPA, and (for men) your national service certificate.

The official form does not show a closing date, but Emarat Al Youm reported **6 November 2026**, so do not leave it late.

## What it means for job seekers

- **A job lined up before graduation.** Sponsorship schemes are one of the surest routes into Dubai government work.
- **Tech and science students are in demand.** AI, cyber and data are top of the list.
- **Keep your grades up.** The GPA of 3 is a firm cut-off.
- **First and second-year students** should note the scheme for next year.
