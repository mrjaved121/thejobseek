Kuwait's **Civil Service Commission (CSC)** has nominated **5,201 Kuwaiti citizens** for jobs in the public sector. The latest batch of men and women will be placed at **33 government entities**, according to a CSC statement carried by the state news agency **KUNA** and published by Kuwait Times on **8 October 2026**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Kuwaitis nominated | 5,201 |
| Government entities | 33 |
| System used | Central Employment System |
| Basis | Each entity's approved staffing needs |
| Announced by | CSC Undersecretary Salah Al-Saqabi |

## How the nominations work

Kuwaitis looking for a government job register on the CSC's **Central Employment System**. When ministries and public bodies have approved vacancies, the CSC matches registered candidates to those posts based on their **specialisation and qualifications**.

The announcement came from CSC Undersecretary **Salah Al-Saqabi**, following directions from Deputy Prime Minister **Shareeda Al-Maousherji**. The CSC did not publish a breakdown by specialisation or by entity.

## What nominees must do now

Nominees were told to contact their assigned government entity from the Sunday after the announcement to complete the hiring procedures. These steps must be finished **within the deadlines set by the CSC**. Anyone who misses the deadline risks losing the nomination.

The CSC statement did not list the paperwork, so ask your entity what it needs. It helps to have these ready:

- Civil ID
- Educational certificates (attested where required)
- Recent photos
- Any professional licences linked to the job

## Why it matters

Government jobs remain the first choice for many Kuwaiti graduates, thanks to stable pay and benefits. Large nomination rounds like this help reduce the waiting list of registered job seekers. At the same time, the government is also pushing more citizens into private-sector and oil-sector roles. For example, Kuwaitis now make up more than 93% of staff at the state refiner KNPC.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Check your status.** If you are registered, log in to the CSC's official system or check its announcements to see if your name is on the list.
- **Act fast.** Contact your assigned entity as soon as possible and keep copies of everything you submit.
- **Not nominated?** Keep your registration and qualifications up to date, as the CSC releases new batches through the year.
- **Expats:** these posts are for Kuwaiti citizens. Expat job seekers should focus on private-sector employers.
