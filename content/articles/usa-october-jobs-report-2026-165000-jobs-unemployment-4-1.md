The latest **US jobs report** is good news for job seekers. American employers added **165,000 jobs in October 2026**, more than double what forecasters expected, and the **unemployment rate dipped to 4.1%** from 4.2%, according to the **Bureau of Labor Statistics** report published on 6 November.

## What were the key numbers?

| Measure | October 2026 |
|---|---|
| Jobs added | 165,000 |
| Forecast | About 60,000–65,000 |
| Unemployment rate | 4.1% (from 4.2%) |
| Annual wage growth | 3.1% |
| Three-month average gain | About 137,000 a month |

Rice University economist **John Diamond** told ABC13 that the three-month average shows a healthy labour market.

## Which sectors are hiring?

| Sector | October change |
|---|---|
| Professional and business services | +70,000 |
| Health care | +55,000 |
| Transport and warehousing | Gains |
| Manufacturing | Gains |
| Government | Gains |
| Information, construction, leisure and hospitality | Losses |

Professional services alone made up almost half of the month's new jobs.

## Who benefited most?

Unemployment fell fastest for **teenagers** and **Black workers**, according to ABC15's report on the Labor Department figures. Economists watch Black unemployment closely as an early recession signal, so the drop was welcomed.

## Are wages keeping up?

Not yet. Pay rose **3.1%** over the year, while inflation is expected to run near **3.7%**. That means price rises have wiped out pay gains for seven months in a row.

## How can job seekers use this?

- **Target growing sectors**: business services, health care, logistics and manufacturing.
- **Check public-sector openings** on USAJOBS and state job boards.
- **Negotiate pay** with inflation in mind.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Hiring is picking up**, giving more bargaining power.
- **Health care stays a reliable hirer** month after month.
- **Hospitality is weaker**, so widen your search if you work in that field.
