South Africa's **Department of Basic Education (DBE)** will hire **more than 20,000 young people** as **literacy and numeracy education assistants** in public primary schools. The posts form a new pilot of the **Basic Education Employment Initiative (BEEI)** running from **January to March 2027** in all nine provinces. Applications are expected to open **later in November 2026** on the **SA Mobi App**.

## What is the BEEI literacy and numeracy pilot?

The BEEI started in 2020 under the Presidential Employment Stimulus and has grown into one of the country's biggest youth job schemes. This new phase is known as the **FLN pilot** (Foundation Phase Literacy and Numeracy). Trained assistants will sit with teachers in Grades R to 3 and help children with reading, writing and maths. According to an earlier DBE announcement, it covers **15 education districts** and is backed by **R319 million** from National Treasury.

## What will education assistants do?

- **Literacy:** build reading fluency, vocabulary, comprehension and early writing.
- **Numeracy:** strengthen mental maths and number confidence using practical and visual activities.
- Give structured support that follows the school curriculum, with mentoring at school level.

DBE spokesperson **Terence Khala** described the pilot as a move to tie youth employment to better learning results.

## Who can apply?

- Unemployed South African citizens with a valid ID
- Aged **22 to 39**
- Hold a **Bachelor of Education (BEd)** for the Foundation or Intermediate Phase, **or** be a 3rd- or 4th-year student on such a degree
- Be cleared on the **National Child Protection Register**, and submit an application to be checked on the **National Sex Offenders Register**
- Provide a **police clearance certificate** no older than three months

Unlike earlier BEEI rounds, general school assistants without teaching studies are not part of this pilot.

## How do I apply?

Download the **SA Mobi App** now, create your profile and wait for the DBE advert later this month. Get your police clearance early, because it must be recent. Do not pay anyone who offers to secure a post for you; applications are free.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Ideal for BEd students and new teachers** looking for paid classroom experience.
- **Short contract:** three months, but useful for a CV and teaching references.
- **Prepare documents now:** ID, academic record and police clearance.
