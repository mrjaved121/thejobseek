Aviation is opening new **Kuwait jobs**. Low-cost airline **Jazeera Airways** has started doing its **own ground handling** at **Terminal 5, Kuwait International Airport**, replacing an outsourced service, and says the move will **create new jobs** and build local aviation skills. Vacancies are posted on the airline's official careers site.

## What has changed?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Jazeera Airways |
| Location | Jazeera Terminal 5, Kuwait International Airport |
| Change | Ground handling moved from outsourced to in-house |
| First flight handled | Arrival from Madinah, departing for Beirut |
| Years operating | 21 |

Chief executive **Barathan Pasupathi** said the change gives the airline more control over turnaround times and service, and helps develop local aviation talent.

## Why is Jazeera expanding?

The airline is entering a growth phase backed by an order for **26 Airbus jets**:

| Aircraft | Number |
|---|---|
| A320neo | 18 |
| A321neo | 8 |

The first A320neo has arrived and another is due before the end of 2026. Jazeera already runs its own **aircraft maintenance** and **aviation training**.

## Which jobs does in-house ground handling need?

Airline ground handling teams typically include:

- Ramp agents and baggage handlers
- Aircraft loading and turnaround coordinators
- Check-in and boarding agents
- Ground equipment operators and drivers
- Supervisors and duty managers

These are typical ground handling roles, not a confirmed vacancy list.
