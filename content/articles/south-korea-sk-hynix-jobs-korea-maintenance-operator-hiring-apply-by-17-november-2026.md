**SK hynix jobs in Korea are open right now: the memory chipmaker is hiring entry-level Maintenance and Operator staff for its Icheon, Yongin and Cheongju plants, and the application window shuts at 3pm Korea time on Tuesday 17 November 2026.** There is no degree or diploma requirement in the advert. You apply free of charge on SK Careers, the SK Group's own recruitment site, and people who are selected start work from February 2027.

## What is SK hynix hiring for?

The round is called "[November] Talent hy-way (Maintenance/Operator)". According to [BigGo Finance](https://finance.biggo.com/news/2e62a47f-81d5-40fb-ac7e-5f09ecbbeba0), the notice went up on 10 November and covers full-time production work, including staff for the company's first fab in Yongin, where cleanroom operations are due to begin in February. The outlet adds that SK hynix scrapped its old education limits for these jobs in June.

The [official posting on SK Careers](https://www.skcareers.com/Recruit/Detail/R262381) splits the work into four areas:

| Track | Area | What the job involves |
|---|---|---|
| Maintenance | Fab equipment | Preventive checks, upkeep and fixing faults on chip-making tools |
| Maintenance | Fab utility | Running gas, chemical and other supply plant safely |
| Maintenance | Fab safety | Inspecting processes, spotting hazards, first response on site |
| Operator | Fab operation | Running the production process and managing output and quality |

All four are listed as entry-level, permanent positions.

## Who can apply?

The advert gives four conditions:

- you can start work from February 2027;
- you can work a four-crew, three-shift rota;
- you are able to wear a cleanroom suit;
- you have completed or are exempt from military service and face no bar on overseas travel. Applicants still serving must be discharged before 1 February 2027.

Certificates or knowledge in mechanical, electrical, control, chemical or safety fields are listed as a plus, as are data and automation skills. None is compulsory.

One honest note for readers outside Korea: the advert, the form and the test are all in Korean, and the posting says nothing about visa sponsorship. In practice this round suits people who already have the right to work in South Korea and can handle Korean at work.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Find the posting on SK's own site.** Head to the [SK Careers posting](https://www.skcareers.com/Recruit/Detail/R262381). SK hynix's own Talent Hub links to the same page.
2. **Create an SK Careers account or log in,** then press the apply button on the posting.
3. **Download the education guide.** The advert attaches a PDF explaining how to enter your schooling. Follow it exactly.
4. **Enter your full record.** SK hynix asks for every school, including high school attendance and grades, plus any course you are still taking.
5. **Pick Maintenance or Operator and a preferred site.** The company warns that the final workplace may differ from your choice.
6. **Write your own self-introduction.** Copied text or false details can lead to rejection, even after an offer.
7. **Submit before 15:00 KST on 17 November.** To correct something after submitting, withdraw the application, edit it and submit again before the cut-off.

## What happens after I apply?

| Stage | Date given in the advert |
|---|---|
| Document screening | Applications 10–17 November |
| SKCT online test | One day, 28 or 29 November |
| Interview | During December |
| Health check | January 2027 |
| Start of work | From February 2027 |

The SKCT is SK's online aptitude test covering reasoning, situational judgement and attitude. If you sat it within the past four months, that earlier score is used and you cannot retake it. The company notes that dates may shift.

## Do I need an agent?

No. SK hynix recruits directly through SK Careers, and applying costs nothing. Be wary of anyone selling a "guaranteed" place or test answers. The company also bans applying to more than one SK member firm at once, so never let a third party submit forms in your name.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Practise for the SKCT early.** The test comes less than two weeks after the deadline.
- **Show shift readiness.** Mention any rota, night or factory work you have done.
- **List relevant certificates** in electrical, mechanical, safety or data fields, since the advert names them as preferred.
- **Common mistake:** leaving the form until the last hour. Edits are impossible once the window closes.
