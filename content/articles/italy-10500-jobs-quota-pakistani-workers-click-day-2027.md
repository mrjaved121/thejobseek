Italy has given Pakistan a dedicated quota of **10,500 jobs over three years**, according to Pakistan's Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis and Human Resource Development. Italy is the first European country to give Pakistan its own labour quota.

## How the quota is split

| | Per year | Over 3 years |
|---|---|---|
| Seasonal jobs | 1,500 | 4,500 |
| Non-seasonal jobs | 2,000 | 6,000 |
| **Total** | **3,500** | **10,500** |

Seasonal jobs are mostly in **farming and tourism** and last a few months. Non-seasonal jobs are longer-term work in other sectors.

In June 2026, Italy's ambassador in Islamabad confirmed the quota and said Italy has started **pre-departure training programmes** for workers going from Pakistan.

## Italy's 2027 hiring decree (Decreto Flussi)

Italy admits non-EU workers through yearly quotas under its **Decreto Flussi**. For 2027, it has approved **165,850 entries**:

| Type of work | Places |
|---|---|
| Seasonal (agriculture and tourism) | 89,000 |
| Non-seasonal | 76,200 (of which 14,000 for home care / family assistance) |
| Self-employed | 650 |

## Click day dates for 2027

Applications are sent online on fixed "click days". Places go quickly, so timing matters.

| Date | Type of job |
|---|---|
| **12 January 2027** | Seasonal – agriculture |
| **9 February 2027** | Seasonal – tourism |
| **16 February 2027** | Non-seasonal |
| **18 February 2027** | Non-seasonal – home care |

Before click day, applications can be **pre-filled from 23 October to 7 December 2026**, and completed between **9 and 13 December 2026**, on the Italian Interior Ministry's portal.

## Important: the employer applies, not you

Under the Decreto Flussi, **the Italian employer** (or an authorised representative) submits the application for a work permit (*nulla osta*). A worker cannot apply alone. You need a **job offer from a real employer in Italy** first.

Once the permit is approved, you apply for the work visa at the Italian embassy or consulate.

## Avoid visa scams

- Nobody can "guarantee" you an Italy click-day slot.
- Do not pay agents for an "Italy work visa" without a written job offer you can verify.
- Use only **licensed overseas employment promoters** registered with Pakistan's Bureau of Emigration, and check updates from the **Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis**.
