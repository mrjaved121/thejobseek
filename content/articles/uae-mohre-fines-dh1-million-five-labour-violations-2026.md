The UAE **Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE)** has warned companies that five labour law violations each carry a fine of **at least Dh100,000 and up to Dh1 million**. The reminder, reported by Gulf News and Emarat Al Youm on **23 October 2026**, targets practices that often leave workers stranded.

## The five violations

| # | Violation | Fine |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Employing someone without a MoHRE work permit | Dh100,000 – Dh1 million |
| 2 | Recruiting or employing a worker but not giving them any work | Dh100,000 – Dh1 million |
| 3 | Using work permits for purposes other than the one issued | Dh100,000 – Dh1 million |
| 4 | Closing or suspending a business without settling workers' status | Dh100,000 – Dh1 million |
| 5 | Employing minors in breach of the rules, even with a parent's consent | Dh100,000 – Dh1 million |

## Why this matters to job seekers

Several of these violations describe common traps for new arrivals:

- **Visa trading.** Some companies issue visas, collect fees, and never provide a job. That is violation number 2.
- **Working "on the side".** Being put to work for another firm without the right permit can fall under violations 1 and 3.
- **Sudden shutdowns.** When a company closes and leaves staff without cancelled visas or final pay, that is violation number 4.

The ministry urged employers to check legal requirements before taking anyone on.

## How to protect yourself

1. **Check your work permit.** You can confirm your permit and contract on the [MoHRE website](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/) or app.
2. **Never pay for a job.** Recruitment fees charged to workers are illegal in the UAE.
3. **Report problems.** MoHRE's call centre is **600 590 000**.
4. **Keep your documents.** Hold copies of your offer letter, contract and salary slips.

## What it means for job seekers

- **A real job comes with a permit.** If an employer says you can start before the permit is issued, be careful.
- **Visa-only offers are a red flag.** A company that sells you a visa without a job is breaking the law.
- **You have support.** MoHRE can help settle your status if your employer closes.
