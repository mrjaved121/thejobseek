Online marketplace **eBay** is cutting about **1,000 jobs**, around **9%** of its workforce, in its **second round of layoffs in 2026**. Reuters first reported the move on **21 October 2026**, based on an internal memo from chief executive **Jamie Iannone**, and an eBay spokesperson confirmed it.

Affected employees are due to be told **in the coming days**.

## Key facts

| Earlier 2026 cuts | About 800 (around 6%) in February |
| Staff at end of 2025 | About 12,300 worldwide |
| Of whom in the US | About 7,200 |
| Headquarters | San Jose, California |

## Why eBay is cutting jobs

In his memo, Iannone said the company's strategy is working but that eBay must choose carefully where to put its money and people. The company said the aim is to simplify how it operates and focus on its main growth areas.

Those areas include **luxury goods, collectibles and refurbished items**. In July, eBay also completed its roughly **$1.4 billion** purchase of the resale app **Depop** from Etsy.

The memo did not say which departments, countries or offices will be hit, and there are no public details yet on severance pay. eBay is due to report its third-quarter results next week, which may give more detail on the cost of the restructuring.

Together with February's round, the two cuts add up to roughly **1,800 roles** removed in 2026, a large share of a company that employed just over 12,000 people at the start of the year.

## Part of a wider wave of US layoffs

eBay joins a long list of large employers trimming staff in 2026 even while their businesses are growing. Many tech firms are shifting budgets towards AI and away from older teams. In finance, [Barclays has announced 5,000 job cuts](/uk/uk-barclays-5000-job-cuts-ai-2026/) linked to AI efficiency.

Not all the news is negative: on the operations side, [Amazon plans to hire 300,000 holiday workers](/usa/usa-amazon-300000-holiday-jobs-2026/) across the US.

## What it means for job seekers

- **If you work at eBay,** watch for your notification, read your severance terms carefully and ask about benefits continuation.
- **Laid-off tech workers** should update LinkedIn quickly; many recruiters track layoff lists for experienced e-commerce talent.
- **E-commerce skills are still in demand** at retailers, marketplaces and payment firms, especially in trust and safety, data and product roles.
- **Check unemployment benefits** in your state as soon as your last day is confirmed, so there is no gap in income.
