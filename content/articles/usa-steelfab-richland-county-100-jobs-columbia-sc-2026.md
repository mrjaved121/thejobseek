**SteelFab**, one of the largest structural steel fabricators in the US, is bringing **100 new manufacturing jobs** to **Richland County, South Carolina**. The company is investing **$30 million** in a new facility in the Columbia area, WIS News 10 reported on **27 October 2026**.

## About the project

SteelFab is based in **Charlotte, North Carolina**. It designs, makes and supplies fabricated structural steel, the beams and frames used in offices, hospitals, stadiums, warehouses and factories.

The company runs **18 facilities**, and **four** of them are already in South Carolina. The new site will be at **1030 Metso Way** in Richland County, and SteelFab expects to start operations there in **late 2026**.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | SteelFab |
| Location | 1030 Metso Way, Richland County, SC |
| Investment | $30 million |
| New jobs | 100 |
| Start of operations | Late 2026 |

Governor **Henry McMaster** praised the decision, saying the 100 jobs were good news for the state's workforce and economy.

## Jobs you can apply for now

SteelFab has not published a full list of roles for the new plant. However, its official careers page already shows openings in South Carolina, including:

- **1st Shift Maintenance Mechanic** – Columbia, SC
- **1st Shift Fitter** – Rock Hill, SC
- **2nd Shift Fitter** and **2nd Shift Welder** – York, SC
- **Structural Connection Design Engineer** – Charleston, SC
- **Miscellaneous and Specialty Metals Detailer** – Rock Hill, SC

Steel fabrication plants usually hire fitters, welders, crane and saw operators, painters, maintenance staff, quality inspectors, detailers and shipping workers, so expect similar roles in Richland County.

## How to apply

Go to SteelFab's official careers page at [steelfab-inc.com/careers](https://steelfab-inc.com/careers). Each job has a "Read More" link with full details, and there is an "Apply Now" option with a general application form. Check back regularly, as roles for the new site should appear as the opening date gets closer.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Welders and fitters are in demand.** If you can read blueprints and use hand and power tools, you are a strong fit.
- **Shift work is common.** Be clear about which shifts you can work when you apply.
- **Apply early.** With operations planned for late 2026, hiring could start within weeks.
- **Build skills locally.** Welding and industrial maintenance courses at technical colleges in the Midlands can help you qualify.
