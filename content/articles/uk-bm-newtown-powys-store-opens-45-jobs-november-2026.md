Shoppers in mid-Wales have a new store and local people have new **Wales jobs**. Discount retailer **B&M** has opened in **Newtown, Powys**, creating **45 jobs**. Future vacancies at the store will appear on B&M's official careers site.

## What has opened?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | B&M European Value Retail |
| Location | Newtown, Powys, Wales |
| Site | Former Focus Do It All building |
| Jobs created | 45 |
| Opened | November 2026 |

The opening drew a queue of shoppers, the County Times reported.

## What did the mayor say?

**Margaret Lewington**, mayor of Newtown and Llanllwchaiarn Town Council, said a new retailer investing in the town was encouraging. She singled out the **45 local jobs** and said Newtown must keep attracting new businesses to support its economy.

## Which jobs do B&M stores usually have?

A B&M store of this size typically employs:

- Customer service and till staff
- Stock replenishment and warehouse workers
- Supervisors and team leaders
- Assistant and store managers

These are standard store roles, not a confirmed breakdown for Newtown.

## Are retail jobs a good start?

Yes. Retail roles often offer flexible hours, need little previous experience and can lead to supervisor and management posts.

## How do I apply?

Search for Newtown or nearby stores on B&M's official **[careers site](https://careers.bmstores.co.uk/)**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Local retail jobs** are arriving in smaller Welsh towns.
- **Seasonal hiring** may add extra shifts before Christmas.
- **Entry-level roles** suit school leavers and people returning to work.
