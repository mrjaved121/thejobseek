**De Havilland Aircraft of Canada** will hire new staff across five Canadian cities after signing a financing deal worth up to **$2.5 billion** to **double production** of its DHC-515 firefighting aircraft, the company announced on 30 October 2026. It is one of the biggest **Canada hiring 2026** stories in aerospace, with the company saying the expansion will support thousands of jobs.

## The deal

The **Government of Canada** and **Export Development Canada (EDC)** will provide the financing. De Havilland will use it to add production capacity for the **DHC-515**, also called the **De Havilland Canadair 515**, an amphibious aircraft that skims lakes to scoop up water and drop it on wildfires.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Financing | Up to $2.5 billion |
| Lenders | Government of Canada and EDC |
| Goal | Double DHC-515 production |
| Hiring sites | Victoria, Calgary, Toronto, Kitchener, Fort Erie |
| Deal closing | Expected by Q1 2027 |
| Extra production slots | From 2030 |
| Current orders | 38 aircraft, with slots booked to 2034 |

## Where the jobs will be

De Havilland says the expansion will create **thousands of jobs in Canada**, both at its own sites and among suppliers stretching from **Newfoundland to British Columbia**. It has not given a precise number.

Separately, the company has been building **De Havilland Field**, a new aircraft campus east of Calgary, since May 2026. That project is not part of this financing, but it adds to the long-term hiring outlook in Alberta.

## Types of roles

De Havilland's careers site lists openings in fields including:

- **Production and manufacturing** (assemblers, structures technicians, machinists)
- **Engineering** (design, stress, systems, manufacturing engineers)
- **Supply chain** and procurement
- **Finance** and **administrative support**

## How to apply

Current vacancies are on De Havilland Canada's official careers page: **[dehavilland.com/careers](https://dehavilland.com/careers/)**. From there, the "View Current Openings" link opens the company's job portal, where you can filter by city.
