Scottish hospitality operator **Signature Group** is recruiting **more than 90 staff** for two new venues in **Edinburgh's Old Town**. Named **Ember & Fellows**, the bars will share a historic building on **Forrest Road** and are due to open in **early 2027**. Applications are open now through the group's careers website or by emailing a CV.

## What is opening on Forrest Road?

The site is a short walk from Greyfriars Bobby and the National Museum of Scotland. The Victorian building dates from 1872–1874 and was first used as a hall by the Oddfellows, a friendly society that helped members in hard times. Signature will turn it into two separate concepts:

- **Ember** – an upmarket Scottish pub cooking seasonal produce over charcoal
- **Fellows** – a sports bar and entertainment venue

Both will be run by the same team and trade from daytime into late evening.

## Why does this matter for Edinburgh hospitality?

This is the group's first brand-new venue since 2020, when McLarens on the Corner opened in Morningside. Director Nic Wood said government policy had made growth harder in recent years, so the move signals fresh confidence. Signature already runs over 20 Scottish venues, from bars and restaurants to hotels, and has its own brewery.

## Which roles are open?

The group has said it is hiring across a range of hospitality positions. For two food-led bars of this size, typical openings include:

- Chefs, sous chefs and kitchen porters
- Bartenders and waiting staff
- Supervisors and assistant managers
- Hosts and front-of-house team members

These are common roles for such venues, not a confirmed list.

## What are the staff perks?

Signature's careers page lists a **25%–50% staff discount** in all its venues, an extra day off for your birthday, development programmes and an employee assistance programme.

## How do I apply?

Search live vacancies on the [Signature Pubs careers site](https://careers.signaturepubs.co.uk/) or email your CV to recruitment@signaturepubs.co.uk. Mention the Forrest Road venues so your CV reaches the right hiring team.

- **Early applicants win:** opening teams are usually built months ahead, so apply now.
- **Career growth:** new sites need supervisors, which can mean quick promotion.
- **Central location:** Forrest Road is easy to reach by bus and on foot from the city centre.
