**The USPS job fair in Yemassee, South Carolina, is on Tuesday 17 November 2026 at the Yemassee Community Center, and the Postal Service wants to fill rural carrier associate and assistant rural carrier jobs straight away at $21.89 an hour.** You can go in person or apply online through the official USPS careers site. Either way, it is free.

## Job fair details

Local station [WTOC reported the event on 12 November](https://www.wtoc.com/2026/11/12/usps-host-job-fair-yemassee-nov-17/), quoting a USPS release.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Date | Tuesday 17 November 2026 |
| Venue | Yemassee Community Center |
| Address | 10 Mixson St., Yemassee, SC 29945 |
| Roles | Rural carrier associate; assistant rural carrier |
| Pay | $21.89 per hour, paid biweekly |

One gap to know about: the report does not state what time the fair opens or closes. Phone your local post office or the community center on Monday to confirm the hours, or simply apply online first so you lose nothing if the timing does not suit you.

## Which jobs are open?

| Role | Schedule | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| Rural carrier associate (RCA) | Full-time; may include weekends and holidays | Deliver mail and packages. Health benefits are offered. |
| Assistant rural carrier (ARC) | Part-time; includes weekends and holidays | Deliver packages. |

USPS says career employees can earn a wider package: a choice of health and life insurance plans, a pension, a Thrift Savings Plan (the federal version of a 401(k)), paid vacation and sick days. It also says it promotes mostly from inside the organization and runs training from entry level up to management.

## Who can apply?

The release lists four conditions:

- **Age:** 18 or older when appointed, or 16 with a high school diploma.
- **Status:** US citizen or permanent resident.
- **Checks:** you must clear a criminal background check and a drug screen.
- **Driving:** a valid state driver's license and a safe driving record if the job involves driving. Carrier jobs do.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Start online at the official site:** [about.usps.com/careers](https://about.usps.com/careers/welcome.htm). Choose the option to search and apply.
2. **Use a laptop or desktop.** USPS advises this and asks you to allow pop-up windows, because the application opens in a new window.
3. **Search for the role.** Look for "Rural Carrier Associate" or "Assistant Rural Carrier" postings in South Carolina, near Yemassee.
4. **Create a candidate profile** and complete the application for the posting you want.
5. **Attend the fair on 17 November** with photo ID and your driver's license. Staff can answer questions about routes, hours and the hiring steps.
6. **Watch your email afterward.** Check your inbox and spam folder daily for any follow-up. The release says candidates must pass the background check and drug screen, so expect those before you start.

Applying costs nothing. Websites and ads that sell "postal job registration" or promise hiring for a fee are not the Postal Service. Use only web addresses ending in usps.com.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Check your driving record first.** A safe record is a stated requirement for driving jobs, so sort out any problems before you apply.
- **Be honest about weekends.** Both roles include weekend and holiday work. Say clearly that you are available.
- **List delivery or driving work.** Courier, rideshare, school bus, farm or warehouse experience is relevant.
- **Finish every online step.** A half-completed application cannot be considered.
- **Know the area.** If you already drive the rural roads around Yemassee, say so.

Quick checklist: photo ID, driver's license, your recent work history, a working email address and a phone number.

## What it means for job seekers

- $21.89 an hour is on offer for work that does not ask for a degree.
- The ARC role suits people who want part-time income; the RCA role is the better route toward a career position, though that is not guaranteed.
- USPS calls these immediate openings, so applying online before the fair is the smart move.
