**The ABQ RIDE hiring event takes place on Tuesday 17 November 2026 at America's Job Center Albuquerque, where the city's transit department will interview people for bus driver jobs and may hand out conditional job offers the same day.** You do not need a commercial driver's licence (CDL) to apply, training is paid for, and new Motorcoach Operators can receive a hiring bonus of up to $5,000. Successful candidates could start work as early as 30 November.

## What is on offer at the event?

The [City of Albuquerque's announcement](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/news/abq-ride-hiring-bus-drivers) describes a fast-track process: staff help with the application, interview you on site and can make a conditional offer before you leave. The city's [event page](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/abq-ride-hiring-event) names two jobs being filled: Motorcoach Operators, who drive the city buses, and Sun Van Chauffeurs, who drive the paratransit service.

## What benefits does the city list?

According to the announcement, the package includes union membership, paid CDL training and testing, a pension and chances to move up. Transit Director Leslie Keener said the department needs a steady supply of drivers because a redesigned route network is being rolled out over the next several years, with more service planned. That points to continued demand for drivers, not a one-off intake.

The city has not put hourly pay rates in the announcement. Each job posting on the city's official jobs portal shows the pay range, so read it before the event.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Read the job descriptions.** Open the [ABQ RIDE hiring event page](https://www.cabq.gov/transit/abq-ride-hiring-event) and follow its link to the City of Albuquerque jobs portal, filtered to the Transit department.
2. **Apply online before the day.** The city recommends this because it shortens the time you spend at the event. Creating an account and applying costs nothing.
3. **RSVP on the event page.** It is optional, but it tells staff to expect you.
4. **Go to 501 Mountain NE on 17 November.** Doors open at 9am. Because the two official pages give different closing times, aim to arrive in the morning and no later than midday.
5. **Bring a valid form of identification.** This is the one item the city says every applicant must have.
6. **Interview on site.** If it goes well you may leave with a conditional offer. A conditional offer still depends on the city's remaining checks, so it is not a final contract.

Getting there is simple: the city says ABQ RIDE routes 5 and 16 stop near the job centre, the buses are free to ride, and the Transit app can plan the trip.

## Do I need an agent or have to pay?

No. This is a city government employer hiring directly. Apply only through cabq.gov and the city's own jobs portal it links to. Nobody can sell you an interview slot, and a real city job never asks for a payment, a deposit or your bank PIN. Ignore anyone on social media who offers to "arrange" a place for a fee.

## Tips that improve your chances

- **Have your work history ready.** Dates, employer names and phone numbers for the last few jobs make the on-site application quicker.
- **Show customer service experience.** Drivers deal with the public all day, so retail, delivery, care or security work is worth describing clearly.
- **Be honest about your driving record.** Driving jobs involve record checks, and hiding a problem usually causes more harm than the problem itself.
- **Ask about the training timeline.** If you have no CDL, ask how long paid training lasts and when the first bonus instalment is paid.
- **Common mistake:** arriving near closing time. Interviews take a while, so late arrivals risk missing out.

Checklist: valid ID, online application done, list of past employers.
