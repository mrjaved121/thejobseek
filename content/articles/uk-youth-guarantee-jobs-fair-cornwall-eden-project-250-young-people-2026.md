Young people across Britain have a new route to work through **Youth Guarantee jobs fairs**. The first one in Cornwall, held at the **Eden Project**, drew **more than 500 young people**, and **over 250** left with a job opportunity, the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) said on **30 October 2026**.

The fair is part of the government's **£2.5 billion Youth Guarantee**, which promises every under-25 a route into work or training.

## Who was hiring

More than 45 employers and training providers took part. They included:

- **Cornwall Council**
- **Cornwall College**
- **The Royal Navy**
- **Royal Cornwall Hospitals NHS Trust**
- **Cornish Lithium**
- **Danish Crown**
- **The Eden Project**

Sectors on show included renewable energy, engineering and critical minerals, which are growing fast in Cornwall.

## Extra help on the day

Visitors could get help with CVs, applications and interviews. Google ran a workshop on using AI tools to improve CVs and prepare for interviews.

Work and Pensions Secretary **Pat McFadden** said every young person deserves the chance to reach their potential.

## What the Youth Guarantee offers

The scheme aims to support almost **1 million young people** and create **500,000 opportunities**. Every under-25 should be offered one of these:

- A job
- Education or training
- **An apprenticeship**
- A traineeship
- Work experience
- A sector-based work academy (SWAP) place

## How to take part

DWP plans **at least one fair in every region** of Great Britain but has not yet published dates. If you are under 25, ask your local Jobcentre or work coach about the next Youth Guarantee fair near you and about SWAP courses, which often end with a guaranteed interview. These fairs are free; never pay anyone to attend.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Bring several CVs** and a short pitch about yourself.
- **Talk to apprenticeship providers,** not just employers. Many offers come through training routes.
- **Look at growth sectors** such as renewables, engineering and health.
- **Follow up within a week** with anyone you met.
