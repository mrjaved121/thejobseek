Teenagers thinking about engineering careers have a new route in. **Siemens Mobility** has launched **Tech Experience Launchpad**, a **UK work experience** programme that will create **more than 3,300 placements a year** for **14 to 16-year-olds**, reaching **10,000 young people over three years**. Schools and families should watch Siemens' UK channels for how places are offered.

## What does the programme offer?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Organiser | Siemens Mobility UK |
| Age group | 14 to 16 |
| Places | 3,300+ a year; 10,000 over three years |
| Format | Online learning plus hands-on experiences at Siemens sites |
| Topics | Rail engineering, advanced manufacturing, AI and digital technology |

Participants will meet working engineers and tackle real problems from Siemens' UK operations.

## Why is Siemens doing this now?

Britain's railways have an ageing workforce. Just **6.8%** of rail workers are **25 or younger**, according to the National Skills Academy for Rail. The scheme also supports the government's goal of **200,000 extra work experience places** for 14 to 16-year-olds.

Prime Minister **Andy Burnham** joined pupils for a national online science lesson on launch day and urged other businesses to open their doors to young people.

## Where is Siemens investing?

| Site | Investment |
|---|---|
| Goole, Yorkshire (train factory) | Up to £240 million |
| Chippenham, Wiltshire (Rail Technology Centre) | £100 million |
| UK research and development | £20 million |

Together that is **£360 million**. Since 2015, Siemens' **National Training Academy for Rail** and its education team have trained or educated **more than 28,000 people**.

## What comes after work experience?

Work experience is often the first step towards an **apprenticeship** or engineering degree. Large rail employers typically recruit apprentices in:

- Electrical and mechanical engineering
- Signalling and rail systems
- Manufacturing and production
- Software and digital technology

These are common pathways, not confirmed Siemens vacancies.

## How do I get involved?

Ask your school's careers adviser about Siemens Tech Experience Launchpad, and check Siemens' official **[jobs portal](https://jobs.siemens.com/en_US/externaljobs)** for apprenticeships and graduate roles.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Early experience matters**: placements help win apprenticeships later.
- **Rail needs young talent**, so engineering skills will be in demand for years.
- **Suppliers may offer places too**, as Siemens asks its 2,100 partners to join in.
