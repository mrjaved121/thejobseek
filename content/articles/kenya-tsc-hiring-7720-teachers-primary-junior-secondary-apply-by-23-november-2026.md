**Kenya's Teachers Service Commission (TSC) is hiring 7,720 teachers for public primary, junior and secondary schools, with applications open from 10 November until midnight on 23 November 2026.** The posts are permanent and pensionable. Only applications made through the [TSC online portal](https://teachersonline.tsc.go.ke/) will be accepted.

## How many posts are there?

The commission says the new teachers will replace staff who have retired or left for other reasons.

| School level | Vacancies |
|---|---|
| Primary schools | 5,609 |
| Junior schools | 25 |
| Secondary schools | 2,086 |
| **Total** | **7,720** |

A list showing the posts in each county and sub-county is on the TSC website, so you can see where the openings are before you choose.

## Who can apply?

- You must be a **Kenyan citizen**.
- You must be **registered as a teacher with TSC**.
- **Primary school posts:** at least a Primary Teacher Education (P1) certificate.
- **Junior and secondary school posts:** at least a Diploma in Education.

TSC will give preference to candidates it has never employed before. People with disabilities are encouraged to apply.

## What is the deadline?

| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Advert published and portal opens | Tuesday 10 November 2026 |
| Closing time | Midnight, Monday 23 November 2026 |

## How do I apply?

1. Check the county and sub-county vacancy list on the TSC website.
2. Log in to the [TSC online recruitment portal](https://teachersonline.tsc.go.ke/).
3. Submit **one** application. Chief executive Evaleen Mitei has warned that paper applications will not be looked at.
4. If shortlisted, bring your original academic and professional certificates to the interview.

Do not fill in more than one employment form. TSC says doing so leads to disqualification. The commission does not charge for applications, so ignore anyone who asks for money to "secure" a post.

## What if I am not picked this time?

The merit lists drawn up in this exercise will be used again for other recruitment during the 2026/2027 financial year. That means a good score now could still lead to a job later, without a fresh advert.

## What it means for job seekers

- This is one of the larger public hiring rounds in East Africa this month, and it offers secure, pensionable work.
- Most posts (about 73%) are in primary schools, so P1 holders have the best odds.
- The window is short: under two weeks. Apply early in case the portal slows down near the deadline.
- These posts are for Kenyan citizens only; foreign teachers cannot apply.
- Keep scanned copies of your certificates and TSC registration details ready before you log in, so the form can be finished in one sitting.
