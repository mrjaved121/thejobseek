The number of Omanis looking for work in **Musandam** is falling fast. The Ministry of Labour's office in the governorate says **741 jobseekers** were placed in jobs between **January and the end of September 2026**, the Times of Oman reported on **24 October 2026**.

As a result, the jobseeker list dropped to **1,115 people** at the end of September, a fall of **9.7%** in one year.

## Jobseekers in Musandam over three years

| Year | Registered jobseekers |
|---|---|
| 2024 | 1,770 |
| 2025 | 1,235 |
| End of September 2026 | 1,115 |

That is a drop of about **37%** compared with 2024.

## How people were helped into work

The Musandam Labour Department, working with the Office of the Governor of Musandam, used several routes:

| Programme | People reached |
|---|---|
| Part-time employment initiative (men and women) | 573 |
| Training linked to a job (technical, technological, electrical) | About 50 |
| On-the-job training | 40 |
| Electronic nomination (job matching) | Ongoing |

The **electronic nomination system** matches registered jobseekers with vacancies posted by companies, which is how many Omanis are put forward for private-sector roles.

## Where the jobs are

Officials named the sectors expected to create current and future openings in the governorate:

- **Tourism and hospitality**, including hotels and tour operators
- **Logistics and maritime transport**, especially port work
- **Fisheries**
- **Services**
- **Large investment projects** planned in the area

The work is part of the System of Governorates and **Oman Vision 2040**, which aim to spread jobs and development beyond Muscat. Hussein bin Khamis Al Naqbi, Director of the Labour Department in Musandam, and Fatima bint Ali Al Shehhi of the Governor's Office both spoke about the results.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Register with the Ministry of Labour.** Being on the national jobseeker list is how you get nominated for vacancies.
- **Take the training offers.** Job-linked training often leads directly to a contract with the partner company.
- **Do not dismiss part-time work.** Hundreds of people in Musandam started with part-time roles, which build experience for full-time jobs.
- **Look at tourism and ports.** These sectors are growing in Musandam and often need Arabic and English speakers, drivers, technicians and front-line staff.
