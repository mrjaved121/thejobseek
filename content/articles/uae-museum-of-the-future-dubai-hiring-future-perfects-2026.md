Dubai's **Museum of the Future** has launched a recruitment drive for its visitor team, and it is open to people with **no degree and no work experience**. The campaign, called **Future Perfects**, was reported on **21 October 2026**, and applications close on **8 November 2026**.

People who are hired will carry the title "Future Perfects" and work directly with visitors.

## What the job involves

The roles sit in the museum's **visitor experience team**. Day to day, the work includes:

- **Guiding** visitors through the exhibitions
- **Storytelling** about the ideas and displays in the building
- **Talking with guests** from many different countries and backgrounds

These are **full-time** positions. New staff are expected to start in **November 2026**, so they can be trained before the museum's planned **reopening in 2027**.

## Who can apply

The museum is not asking for certificates. Instead, it is looking for personal qualities:

| What the museum wants | What it does not require |
|---|---|
| Imagination and curiosity | A university degree |
| Emotional intelligence | Previous work experience |
| Clear communication skills | Museum experience |
| Adaptability | Customer service experience |

The announcement does not say how many people will be hired, which nationalities are eligible, or what the salary will be. Check these details on the official portal before you apply.

## How the selection works

Instead of a normal interview, candidates go through **auditions**:

1. Submit an application on the official portal.
2. Shortlisted applicants attend **live assessments**.
3. The final stage is an **audition in front of a panel of judges**.

## How to apply

Apply only through the official Future Perfects portal: [futureperfects.museumofthefuture.ae](https://futureperfects.museumofthefuture.ae). The deadline is **8 November 2026**. Genuine employers do not ask for money to process a job application, so be wary of any agent or social media page that does. - **A rare entry-level opening** at one of Dubai's best-known landmarks; good for fresh graduates or anyone changing careers.
- **Prepare for a performance-style audition.** Practise explaining an idea or telling a short story clearly and with energy.
- **Show languages.** Visitors come from all over the world, so mention every language you speak.
