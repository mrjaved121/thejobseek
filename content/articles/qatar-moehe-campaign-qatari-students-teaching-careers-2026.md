Qatari students interested in **Qatar government jobs** are being encouraged to look at teaching. The **Ministry of Education and Higher Education (MoEHE)** has launched the first phase of a campaign urging Qatari students to become teachers, presenting it as a stable, well-paid career with clear promotion paths.

## What is the campaign?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Organiser | MoEHE – Academic and Career Guidance Section |
| Campaign name | "The Teacher… The Maker of the Future" |
| Audience | Grade 11 and 12 students |
| First phase | 3–4 November 2026, four government secondary schools |
| Programmes presented | Tamouh programme; Qatar University College of Education |

The first day covered Al Ab (girls) and Mohammed bin Abdulaziz Al Mana (boys) secondary schools; the second covered Al Sailiya (girls) and Hamad bin Abdullah bin Jassim (boys).

## Why should Qataris consider teaching?

The ministry set out the main benefits of the profession:

- **Competitive income**
- **Job stability**
- **Continuous professional development**
- **A clear career ladder**, from teacher to school leadership roles

Qatari principals and teachers shared their own career stories, and current education students explained what studying teaching involves. Education Minister **Lolwa bint Rashid Al Khater** has described teachers as the foundation of any nation's progress.

## How does this link to Qatar National Vision 2030?

The campaign highlighted education achievements under **Qatar National Vision 2030**, which puts developing national talent at the centre of the country's plans.

## How do I start a teaching career in Qatar?

- Speak to your school's academic and career guidance team about the **Tamouh** programme.
- Explore education degrees at **Qatar University's College of Education**.
- Watch MoEHE announcements for teacher recruitment and scholarship opportunities.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Teaching is a growing path for Qatari nationals**, with government support.
- **Start planning in Grade 11–12** to choose the right university route.
- **Stability and progression** make teaching attractive compared with many private-sector jobs.
