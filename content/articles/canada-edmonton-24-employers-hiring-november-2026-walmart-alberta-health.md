If you are looking for **Canada jobs for foreigners** or local roles in Alberta, Edmonton has a lot on offer this autumn. A round-up published on **28 October 2026** found **24 employers** in and around the city with **hundreds of open jobs**, from retail and coffee shops to engineering, healthcare and government.

## The biggest recruiters

| Employer | Openings (approx.) | Example roles |
|---|---|---|
| Walmart | 210+ | Opticians, department managers, store associates |
| Starbucks | ~120 | Baristas, entry-level and professional roles |
| Stantec | ~120 | Engineers, architects, admin support |
| Government of Canada | 110+ | Financial analysts, account reps, supervisors |
| Loblaw | ~100 | Pharmacy assistants, butchers, sales reps |
| Government of Alberta | 90+ | Policy analysts, legal assistants, inventory specialists |
| AgeCare | ~80 | Health care aides, RNs, LPNs, housekeeping |
| Alberta Health Services | Hundreds | Nurses, health technicians |

## Other employers on the list

Smaller but still useful openings are available at:

- **City of Edmonton** – about 40 roles, including analysts and project coordinators
- **West Edmonton Mall** – nearly 40 jobs
- **Canadian Tire** – around 35 roles, such as automotive technicians and cashiers
- **Edmonton Oilers Entertainment Group** – 20+ full- and part-time jobs at the arena
- **ATB** – 19 roles, some remote
- **NAIT** – 17 in-office and hybrid positions
- **EPCOR** – 14 roles for engineers, analysts and project managers
- **Strathcona County**, **ATCO**, **Enbridge**, **Precision Drilling**, **Champion Petfoods**, **The Brick**, **WestJet**, **City of Leduc** and **City of Spruce Grove**

## How to apply

Every employer recruits through its own careers site. Useful official pages include [Walmart Canada careers](https://careers.walmart.ca/), [Alberta Health Services careers](https://www.albertahealthservices.ca/careers/Page12899.aspx), [Government of Alberta job postings](https://jobpostings.alberta.ca/go/All-Jobs-GoA/2617217/), [City of Edmonton recruitment](https://recruitment.edmonton.ca/search-results) and [Stantec jobs](https://stantec.jobs/jobs/?location=Edmonton%2C%20AB). Federal roles are listed on the official GC Jobs portal.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Healthcare is the standout:** AHS and AgeCare need nurses and aides. Internationally trained nurses should start the Alberta registration process early.
- **Retail is busiest now:** holiday hiring at Walmart, Loblaw, Canadian Tire and Starbucks peaks in November.
- **Government jobs take longer:** public-sector hiring can take weeks, so apply now for roles starting in the new year.
- **Engineers are wanted:** Stantec, EPCOR, ATCO and Enbridge all have technical openings.
- **Check your status:** make sure your work permit covers the job, and read each posting for citizenship or residency rules.
