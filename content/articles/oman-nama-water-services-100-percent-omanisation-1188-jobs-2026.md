Young Omanis looking for **Omanisation jobs** have a strong employer to watch in the water sector. **Nama Water Services** says its own workforce is now **100% Omani**, and its training-linked hiring with contractor firms has produced **1,188 permanent jobs for Omani nationals**. Selection for the latest batch of **303 jobs** is under way now.

## How were the 1,188 jobs offered?

The roles were released in three rounds through programmes that pair hiring with specialist training:

| Phase | Jobs for Omanis |
|---|---|
| Phase 1 | 479 |
| Phase 2 | 406 |
| Phase 3 (selection under way) | 303 |
| **Total** | **1,188** |

The jobs sit mainly with the **supporting companies** that hold operation and maintenance contracts for Nama's water and wastewater network. Omani staff in those contracts have risen from roughly **55% to 90%**, the company says.

## Which kinds of work are involved?

Nama links each opening to a training path. Its operations chief, **Abdullah bin Mohammed Al-Nuaimi**, highlighted three areas where Omanis are now replacing expatriate skills:

- Running water and wastewater facilities
- Managing pipe and distribution networks
- Maintaining infrastructure and equipment

## What programmes can graduates and students join?

| Programme | Who it is for | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Ta'zeez | Fresh graduates | One year of practical training with an allowance |
| Bridges | College and university students | Workplace training; 355 students joined in summer 2026, from more than 300 a year across 18 institutions |
| Progress | Omani staff | Leadership training in decisions, projects and teamwork |

Nama has also run specialist courses with Chinese firm **HCIG** for **100 employees**, covering project management, design, planning, AI and customer service.

## Who can apply?

These schemes are for **Omani nationals**. Graduates in engineering, technical and business fields are the best fit for the water and wastewater sector.

## How do I apply?

Watch Nama Water Services' official announcements and Oman's national employment channels for new phases and graduate intakes. Students should ask their college placement office about the Bridges programme.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Utilities are a growing source** of stable jobs for Omanis.
- **Technical training counts**: plant operation and maintenance skills are in demand.
- **Graduate schemes are a door in**, so apply when Ta'zeez intakes open.
