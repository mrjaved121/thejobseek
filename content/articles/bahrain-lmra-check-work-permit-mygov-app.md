Workers in Bahrain can now check their **work permit details more easily**. The **Labour Market Regulatory Authority (LMRA)** has upgraded its service for viewing a registered worker's permit, according to an announcement reported by the Bahrain News Agency on **5 October 2026**.

## How to check your work permit

1. Open the **MyGov app**.
2. Log in with **eKey 2.0**, Bahrain's national digital identity.
3. Open the LMRA **work permit details** service.
4. Enter your **ID card number** and **passport number**, and select your **country**.
5. View your permit's **status and validity**.

## What's new

- A **simpler interface** and easier steps.
- **Unified service information** across all LMRA channels.
- **Direct access** for workers to their own permit details, according to LMRA's Executive Vice President for Services and Business, **Ahmed Ibrahim Al Arabi**.

The upgrade is part of a wider government programme. More than **1,300 government services** have been documented and published, and **800** are being redeveloped.

## Why you should check your permit

- **Confirm it is valid** before it expires, so your employer can renew it in time.
- **Check your employer details** are correct.
- **Spot problems early.** If your permit has been cancelled or isn't what you were told, contact the LMRA.
