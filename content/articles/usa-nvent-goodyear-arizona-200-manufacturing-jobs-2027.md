Electrical products maker **nVent** has picked the Phoenix area for its next big factory, a fresh boost for **US hiring 2026**. The company said on **27 October 2026** that it has leased a **400,000 square foot** manufacturing site in **Goodyear, Arizona**, which will employ **more than 200 people** once fully operational.

## What the plant will build

The Goodyear site will make **engineered buildings** – large, factory-built structures that house electrical equipment. A key product is the **e-house**, a prefabricated electrical building delivered to a site ready to install.

nVent says demand is coming from three main groups of customers:

- **Power utilities** upgrading the grid
- **Data centres**, which are expanding fast to support AI and cloud computing
- **Industrial** customers building or upgrading plants

The company also broke ground in August on a 58,000 sq ft expansion at an existing plant in south-east Kansas, which it says should add up to **60 skilled trade jobs** over the next year.

## What kinds of jobs to expect

nVent describes the new roles as **manufacturing jobs**. It has not published a list of positions yet. Building large steel-framed electrical enclosures usually calls for:

- Welders, fabricators and sheet-metal workers
- Electricians and electrical assemblers who wire panels and switchgear
- Machine operators and production associates
- Quality inspectors and test technicians
- Production supervisors, planners and engineers
- Warehouse, shipping and logistics staff

## How to apply

nVent has not yet posted a dedicated hiring page for Goodyear. Check the careers section of nVent's official website regularly and search for Goodyear or Phoenix locations. Hiring will usually start with supervisors and engineers before production roles open closer to the 2027 launch.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Arizona's factory boom continues.** Data-centre demand is driving new manufacturing jobs across the Phoenix area.
- **Trade skills pay off.** Welding, electrical and fabrication certificates will put you near the front of the queue.
- **Start now.** Community college courses in welding or industrial electrical work take months, so begin before hiring peaks.
- **Use official channels only.** Never pay anyone who promises you a job at a new factory.
