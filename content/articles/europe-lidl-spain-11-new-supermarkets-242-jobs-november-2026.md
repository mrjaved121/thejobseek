Supermarket chain **Lidl** is adding **242 jobs in Spain** this month. It is opening **11 new stores** across **five regions** in November 2026, backed by more than **€66 million** of investment. Vacancies are posted on Lidl Spain's official jobs site.

## Where are the new Lidl stores?

| Region | Store | Opening date |
|---|---|---|
| Madrid | Valdebebas | 5 November |
| Madrid | Aranjuez (Alerce Park) | 20 November |
| Madrid | Latina district | 26 November |
| Valencia | Gandía | 5 November |
| Valencia | Patraix, Valencia city | 12 November |
| Valencia | Orihuela (Alicante) | 19 November |
| Basque Country | San Sebastián | 19 November |
| Basque Country | Bergara | 26 November |
| Catalonia | Amposta (Tarragona) | 5 November |
| Catalonia | Barceloneta, Barcelona (modernised and enlarged) | 6 November |
| Asturias | Villaviciosa | 26 November |

Together the stores add more than **12,000 m²** of shop floor.

## How big is Lidl's Spain expansion?

These openings are part of a wider plan. In its current financial year, which runs from March 2026 to February 2027, Lidl expects to spend **over €440 million** in Spain on **more than 60 projects**, a mix of new stores and refurbishments, with a focus on the islands and large cities. The company is also putting money into its logistics network.

## Which jobs do new supermarkets need?

Large discount supermarkets usually recruit:

- Shop assistants and cashiers
- Shelf fillers and stock staff
- Bakery and fresh-food staff
- Shift leaders and store managers
- Warehouse and logistics workers

These are typical store roles, not a confirmed list for each opening.

## Can foreigners apply?

EU citizens can work in Spain without a permit. Non-EU applicants need the right to work in Spain before they can be hired. Basic Spanish is usually expected for customer-facing roles.

## How do I apply?

Search by city on Lidl Spain's official **[jobs site](https://empleo.lidl.es/)**. Apply early, as stores staff up before opening day.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Retail is hiring across Spain**, especially in Madrid and Valencia.
- **Entry-level roles** are a good first job or a way back into work.
- **Logistics growth** may bring warehouse openings too.
