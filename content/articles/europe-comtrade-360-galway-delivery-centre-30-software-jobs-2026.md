There are new **jobs in Ireland** for software engineers in the west of the country. **COMTRADE 360**, a global software engineering and AI company, has opened a **new delivery centre in Galway**, with the announcement on 28 October pointing to **30 new jobs**. The company says it has already hired almost 20 people in Galway this year and expects the centre to support **about 50 highly skilled roles** over time.

## About the company

COMTRADE 360 builds software and AI solutions for large organisations. The Galway office becomes part of its network of delivery centres across **Europe and the United States**. Teams in these centres work on projects for clients in banking, telecoms, aviation, medical technology and government.

## What was said

Education Minister Hildegarde Naughton welcomed the new centre, calling it a positive addition to Ireland's tech scene and pointing to the close ties between industry and universities in Galway. Yvonne Brewer, the company's Business Development Director for Ireland and the UK, said choosing Galway was easy because of the engineering talent and strong universities in the area.

## Who they are looking for

The firm has not published a full list of roles, salaries or requirements. Based on its work, likely openings include:

- Software engineers (back-end, front-end and full-stack)
- AI and data engineers
- Cloud and DevOps specialists
- Test and quality engineers
- Graduate engineers from local universities

More hiring is planned over the coming months.

## How to apply

No dedicated Galway job page has been published yet. Check the careers section on COMTRADE 360's official website and the company's official LinkedIn page, and apply only through those channels.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Galway is a growing tech hub** – medtech and software firms there often hire graduates from the University of Galway and ATU.
- **Highlight sector experience** – finance, telecoms or medtech project work can set you apart.
- **Non-EU applicants** usually need an Irish employment permit; ask whether the employer can support one.
- **Apply early** while the team is still small and growing.
