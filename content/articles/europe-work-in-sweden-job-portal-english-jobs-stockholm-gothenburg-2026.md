**The Work in Sweden job portal is a new official website that gathers jobs in Sweden advertised in English and needing no Swedish; it showed 1,294 adverts when we checked on 14 November 2026.** It is run by the Swedish Institute, a government agency, and every advert sends you to the employer's own application page. You do not need an agent, and searching costs nothing.

## What has Sweden launched?

The [Swedish Institute announced](https://si.se/en/job-portal-in-english-to-make-swedish-job-opportunities-more-visible-internationally/) the portal in a notice first published on 11 November 2026 and updated on the 13th. It pulls adverts automatically from Platsbanken, the job bank of the Swedish Public Employment Service. An advert appears only if it meets three tests:

- it does not ask for Swedish language skills;
- the job is located in Sweden;
- the occupation counts as highly qualified under the Swedish Migration Agency's definition.

The agency says employers in technology, science, engineering and mathematics struggle to find specialists, and that information for foreign applicants has been too scattered. The portal is meant to be one front door.

## How to use it — step by step

1. **Open the official portal:** [jobs.workinsweden.com](https://jobs.workinsweden.com/).
2. **Search** by job title, company or place, or filter by municipality, region, category, permanent or temporary contract, and publishing date.
3. **Sort by deadline** if you want the adverts that close soonest. Each one shows the employer, location, hours and closing date.
4. **Open an advert and read all of it.** One we opened, an AI Platform Engineer role in Gothenburg, was published on 13 November and closes on 4 December 2026.
5. **Click "Apply for job".** The button takes you to the company's own website. Some adverts ask you to quote a reference, so copy it.
6. **Apply in the way the employer asks.** The advert we read said email applications are not accepted.
7. **Check work permit rules yourself.** The portal links to Sweden's official guidance on whether you need a permit; an advert being listed does not mean the employer will sponsor one.

The Swedish Institute notes that each employer is responsible for its own advert. A real Swedish employer will not ask you for money to be considered, so walk away from anyone who does.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Write the CV in English, one or two pages,** with skills and results first. Mirror the wording of the advert.
- **Send a short, specific cover letter.** Say why this company and this role, not Sweden in general.
- **Apply early.** Some employers say they review applications as they arrive.
- **Be ready for checks.** The Gothenburg advert mentioned background checks and drug and alcohol testing as part of hiring.
- **State your permit position clearly:** EU citizen, existing right to work, or permit needed.
- **Common mistake:** sending one generic application to dozens of adverts. Ten tailored applications beat a hundred copies.

Quick checklist: English CV, tailored letter, degree certificates, reference contacts, passport details and a list of adverts with their deadlines.

## What it means for job seekers

- Skilled English speakers now have one official, free place to find Swedish vacancies without language barriers.
- Being listed is not a job offer or a visa promise. Your skills, a strong application and the permit rules decide the outcome.
- Stockholm and Gothenburg dominate, but smaller industrial towns such as Västerås and Ludvika have dozens of adverts too.
