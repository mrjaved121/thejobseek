New **Tunisia jobs** are coming in car-parts manufacturing. German supplier **Marquardt Group** is expanding its operations around **Tunis**, a move expected to create **more than 400 jobs** over the coming years. The World Bank Group's private-investment arm, **IFC**, is providing up to **€52 million** to back the plan, announced on 3 November 2026.

## What is Marquardt expanding?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Marquardt Group (founded 1925, headquartered in Germany) |
| Location | Three factories in the greater Tunis area |
| New jobs | 400+ over the coming years |
| Financing | Up to €52 million from IFC |
| Products | Automotive mechatronic components and electrical switches |
| In Tunisia since | 1991 |

The money will expand production of higher-value automotive components, support working capital, and fund **solar panels and energy-efficiency** upgrades.

## Why is this good news for women?

About **65%** of Marquardt's workforce in Tunisia are women. IFC will also advise the company on helping more women move into **managerial and technical** positions.

## How big is Tunisia's auto parts sector?

Tunisia's automotive components industry has close to **300 companies** that directly employ an estimated **90,000 people**. Exports reached about **TND 10 billion in 2025**, more than 5% of GDP. World Bank country manager **Alex Kremer** said Tunisia has the talent and industrial base to create more and better jobs.

## What jobs could be available?

Car-parts plants like Marquardt's usually need people for:

- Production and assembly operators
- Quality control technicians
- Electrical and mechatronics engineers
- Maintenance technicians
- Supply chain and logistics staff

Marquardt has not published specific openings yet; these are common roles in the industry.

## How do I apply?

Check the careers section of Marquardt's official website for openings in Tunisia, and watch for local recruitment announcements in the Tunis area.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Manufacturing is a strong job source** in Tunisia, especially for women.
- **Technical skills** in electronics and mechatronics will stand out.
- **More growth is likely** as Tunisia moves into higher-value car parts.
