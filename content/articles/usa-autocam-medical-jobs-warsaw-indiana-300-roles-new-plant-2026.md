**Autocam Medical jobs in Warsaw, Indiana are available now: the medical device contract manufacturer opened a $70 million plant in the Warsaw Technology Park on Monday 9 November 2026 and expects its workforce there to grow to 300 or more within a few years.** The company says it is already recruiting, and candidates apply through the current openings link on its official careers page.

## What has opened in Warsaw?

[News Now Warsaw](https://www.newsnowwarsaw.com/autocam-medical-opens-70-million-orthopedic-manufacturing-plant-in-warsaw/) reports that the Michigan-based firm has moved into a new 100,000-square-foot building along US 30, at the west end of the tech park. Until now it had been working out of space at the nearby Medtronic site, and it has started shifting new high-tech machining equipment into its own facility.

Chief executive John Kennedy said the company intends to stay for the long term and praised the quality of workers it has been able to attract locally. He also told the outlet that Autocam Medical holds an option on the lot next door and hopes to begin a second building within four years. That suggests hiring will continue well beyond the first intake.

One number needs care. The report puts the eventual headcount above 300, while Warsaw's mayor, Jeff Grose, referred to an expected 250. Either way, the jobs will be filled in stages as production grows, not all at once.

## Which roles are likely?

The company has not released a list of Warsaw positions in the news coverage. Its [official careers page](https://www.autocam-medical.com/careers/) says it is regularly looking for people in engineering, manufacturing and quality assurance, and that it frequently has openings for:

- CNC machinists and programmers
- Technicians
- Mechanical and manufacturing engineers
- Manufacturing quality engineers
- Controls engineers

Treat these as the kinds of jobs Autocam Medical usually fills, not a confirmed list for Warsaw. The page describes the work as CNC milling, turning, cutter grinding and metal finishing. Pay rates are not published on the careers page or in the news report, so look for them in each individual posting.

The careers page also describes the Advanced Manufacturing Partnership (AMP), under which the company works with selected universities so employees can train on the job and earn a certificate or degree in a manufacturing field. It tells candidates to ask about the programme.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Go to the official careers page.** Open [autocam-medical.com/careers](https://www.autocam-medical.com/careers/).
2. **Open the current openings.** Near the bottom of the page a link leads to the company's online job listings.
3. **Look for Warsaw, Indiana postings.** The company also has plants in Michigan, Massachusetts and Tennessee, so check the location on each job before applying.
4. **Prepare your resume.** For shop-floor roles, list the machines, controls and materials you have worked with. For engineering roles, list projects and quality systems.
5. **Apply online and keep the confirmation.** Note the job title and date so you can follow up.
6. **Ask about AMP.** If you want to study while working, raise the training partnership at interview. The careers page gives 616-541-8080 as the number for AMP information.
7. **Recheck regularly.** Because hiring is phased, new Warsaw postings should appear over the coming months. If none fits today, look again every couple of weeks.

## Is there a fee or a middleman?

No. Autocam Medical recruits through its own website. You should never pay to apply, to be interviewed or to "reserve" a position, and you do not need a staffing agent to be considered. Treat unsolicited offers that ask for money or banking details as scams.

## Tips that improve your chances

- **Be specific about machining.** Name the milling, turning or grinding work you have done and the tolerances you held. Medical parts demand precision.
- **Stress quality and traceability.** Experience with inspection, documentation or regulated manufacturing is a strong plus for implant work.
- **Common mistake:** applying to the wrong site. Double-check that the posting is for Warsaw.
- **Keep expectations realistic.** Nothing in the coverage mentions visa sponsorship, and no employer guarantees an offer. A resume matched to the posting gives you the best odds.
