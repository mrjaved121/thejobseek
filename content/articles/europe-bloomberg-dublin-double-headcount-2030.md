Financial data giant **Bloomberg** plans to **double its workforce in Ireland** by the end of the decade, after already doubling its Dublin team over the past 12 months. The plan, backed by **IDA Ireland**, was announced on **20 October 2026**.

Dublin is now Bloomberg's **largest office in the EU**, with about **190 staff**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Bloomberg |
| Location | Dublin, Ireland |
| Current staff | About 190 |
| Plan | Double Irish headcount by 2030 |
| Main hiring areas | Software engineering, product, data operations |
| Platform | DL+ enterprise data delivery |
| Support | IDA Ireland |
| New leaders | Amanda Brady (director for Ireland), Richard Norton (head of engineering) |

## What the Dublin team does

Bloomberg's Dublin staff work on **DL+**, a platform that delivers large volumes of financial data to banks, asset managers and other big clients. The work spans engineering, product development and data management. Both new Irish leaders will also act as global leaders for DL+, which shows how central Dublin has become to that product.

The company moved into a **purpose-built office** in Dublin last November to make room for growth.

## Roles to expect

| Area | Example roles |
|---|---|
| Software engineering | Back-end, data and cloud engineers, site reliability engineers |
| Product | Product managers, product owners, UX designers |
| Data operations | Data analysts, data quality and onboarding specialists |
| Engineering leadership | Team leads and engineering managers |

Bloomberg did not give an exact number of new jobs. Doubling a team of about 190 points to a large hiring programme over the next few years.

## How to apply

Bloomberg did not publish a dedicated application link with this news. Look for Dublin roles on Bloomberg's own careers site and filter by location. Graduates should also check Bloomberg's early-careers and internship programmes, which are a common route into its engineering teams.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Data and engineering skills are in demand.** Python, Java, C++, cloud platforms and large-scale data pipelines are core to this kind of work.
- **Finance knowledge helps.** Understanding markets data, reference data or trading is a plus for product and data roles.
- **It is a long hiring window.** With growth planned to 2030, keep checking for new roles rather than applying once.
- **Ireland's tech scene is busy.** Several firms are adding Dublin jobs this month, so compare offers.
