**Kitchen Food Co Hopkinsville jobs are open today: the Australian ready-meal maker started production at its first US factory on 19 October 2026 and its official job board lists 11 roles at the Kentucky plant.** You apply online, straight to the company, through its US careers page. No recruiter is needed and applying costs nothing.

## What is the news?

[Christian County Now reported on 12 November](https://christiancountynow.com/news/267762-kitchen-food-co-begins-operations-in-hopkinsville-to-produce-1-million-chilled-meals-a-week/) that the plant is now running and still recruiting. Key facts from that report:

- **Investment:** $69 million.
- **Size:** about 100,000 square feet.
- **Output:** up to one million fresh, never-frozen meals a week for US retailers such as ALDI.
- **Jobs target:** up to 925 as the site grows. Governor Andy Beshear unveiled the project in December 2024.
- **Location:** Frank Yost Lane, just outside Pembroke.

The local outlet adds that the city's grant deal with the firm includes safeguards tied to the company reaching 350 jobs.

## What does a Food Production Associate do?

This is the main entry-level job and is posted as hourly and full-time. According to the advert, the work includes:

- running food production machines under good manufacturing practice and FDA rules
- preparing ingredients to a recipe
- checking product quality and consistency
- packing and labelling finished meals correctly
- keeping tools and the work area clean
- reporting equipment faults or safety hazards at once

The advert we read does not print an hourly wage. Ask about pay, shift times and benefits at interview.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Start on the company's US careers page:** [kitchenfoodcompany.com/careers-usa](https://www.kitchenfoodcompany.com/careers-usa/).
2. **Click "Explore current opportunities".** This opens the company's own job board, hosted on Rippling.
3. **Choose one role** and read the full description.
4. **Fill in the form.** It asks for your first and last name, email, phone number, city and a resume file. A cover letter is optional.
5. **Note the AI notice.** The form says software helps review applications, that people make the final hiring decisions, and that you may opt out of the AI review.
6. **Watch your email and phone** for the next step. The company has not published a timeline.

No suitable role today? The careers page has a "Can't find the right role?" option where you can leave your details for future openings.

Only apply through the company website. A real employer will never ask you to pay for an application, a uniform or a "guaranteed" slot. If someone does, walk away.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Lead with food or factory experience.** Restaurant kitchens, meat or dairy plants, warehouses and packing lines are all relevant.
- **Use the advert's words.** If you have worked under GMP, HACCP or FDA rules, say so plainly on your resume.
- **Show reliability.** The careers page says effort, reliability and teamwork matter, so mention attendance records or long service.
- **Keep the resume simple.** One or two pages, saved as a PDF, with a phone number that works.
- **Common mistake:** sending a blank or outdated resume. The form requires one, so update it first.

Quick checklist: current resume, working email, phone number, your city and the shift times you can work.

## What it means for job seekers

- A brand-new plant usually hires in waves, so new roles may appear over the coming months. Nothing is promised, but checking weekly helps.
- Production and machine operator roles suit people without a degree.
- Technical staff in maintenance, food safety and supply chain are also wanted.
- You must be able to work on site in Christian County; these are not remote jobs.
