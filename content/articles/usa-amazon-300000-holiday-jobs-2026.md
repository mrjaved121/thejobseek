**Amazon** plans to hire **more than 300,000 workers** across the United States for the **2026 holiday season**. That is **20% more** than last year and the company's **largest holiday hiring target on record**, GeekWire reported on **20 October 2026**.

The hiring covers **seasonal, part-time and full-time** roles in Amazon's operations network.

## Where the jobs are

The roles are mainly in:

- **Fulfilment centres**, where orders are picked, packed and shipped
- **Delivery stations**, where parcels are sorted for the last mile

Amazon said part of the increase is because **new sites** are opening as its network grows. At the same time, it is shrinking its office workforce: it cut **16,000 corporate jobs** in January.

## Pay and benefits

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Seasonal pay | About $20 an hour on average |
| Full-time operations starting pay | At least $20 an hour |
| Average across operations staff | Nearly $24 an hour |
| Health cover (from 1 January) | Day one for seasonal staff working 30+ hours a week |

The new day-one health cover starts on **1 January**, so it does not apply during this peak season. However, holiday hires who are still in a qualifying role on that date will be covered from then. Before the change, seasonal staff waited **90 days**.

## The rest of retail is holding back

Amazon's plan stands out. Retailers hired the **fewest seasonal workers since 2008** last year, and outplacement firm **Challenger, Gray & Christmas** expects even fewer this year, pointing to automation and cautious spending as tariffs keep prices high.

- **UPS** plans to hire more than **100,000** seasonal workers, about **9% fewer** than last year.
- **Target** and **Walmart** have not announced national holiday hiring goals.

For a wider look at US hiring, see our report on [the September 2026 US jobs report](/usa/us-jobs-report-september-2026-healthcare-hiring/).

## How to apply

Apply only through Amazon's official jobs site, [hiring.amazon.com](https://hiring.amazon.com), where you can search by city and shift. Be careful with third-party sites or agents that ask for payment to get you a warehouse job.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Apply early.** The best shifts and locations fill first.
- **Ask about staying on.** Seasonal roles can turn into permanent jobs, and staying past 1 January now brings health cover.
- **Be ready for physical work.** Warehouse roles involve standing, lifting and walking for long shifts.
- **Compare offers.** With other retailers hiring fewer people, Amazon and UPS are the biggest seasonal employers this year.
