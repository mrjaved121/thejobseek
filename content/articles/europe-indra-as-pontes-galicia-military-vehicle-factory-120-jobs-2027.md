Spain's defence industry is growing, bringing new **Europe jobs for foreigners** and local workers with technical skills. On **28 October 2026**, defence and technology group **Indra** presented its new military vehicle factory in **As Pontes**, in the province of A Coruña, Galicia. The plant will create **70 to 120 direct, highly skilled jobs** and start production in **2027**.

## Factory at a glance

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Indra |
| Location | As Pontes, A Coruña, Galicia, Spain |
| Investment | €18 million to adapt the site |
| Site size | 52,872 m² (13,730 m² built) |
| Direct jobs | 70 to 120 |
| Capacity | Up to 80 vehicles a year |
| Production start | 2027 |

## What the plant will build

Inside, the factory will have **three parallel assembly lines**, storage and electronics workshops. Outside, there will be a paint and test area, a test track of almost 15,000 m² and a large yard for finished vehicles. Indra says it will use digital tools and artificial intelligence on the production lines.

The work plan is set out in stages:

1. **2027:** bridge-launching vehicles for the Spanish Army, with 32 systems planned.
2. **Next phase:** 34 amphibious infantry combat vehicles for the Spanish Marines, based on an 8x8 platform. The first units will be built in Italy before production moves to Spain.
3. **2029:** 186 wheeled self-propelled howitzers in several versions.

## A wider hiring push

As Pontes is part of a bigger plan. Indra has a **€200 million** industrial investment programme in Spain that foresees around **1,000 industrial jobs and 1,400 engineering posts**. Galicia's regional president, **Alfonso Rueda**, said the region's defence strategy aims to mobilise €900 million, with support for smaller local firms too.

## Jobs likely to be needed

- Mechanical and electrical assembly technicians
- Electronics and systems integration engineers
- Welders and metal workers
- Painters and surface treatment specialists
- Test drivers and quality inspectors
- Production planners and logistics staff

## How to apply

The announcement did not include a job page. Indra recruits through the careers section of its official corporate website, where you can filter jobs by country and city. Search for Galicia or A Coruña as hiring for the plant begins.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Language matters:** most roles at a Spanish factory will need good Spanish. Galician is a plus.
- **EU citizens** can work in Spain without a permit. Non-EU workers will need an employer willing to support a work permit.
- **Technical training counts:** vocational qualifications in mechanics, electronics or welding fit these roles well.
- **Defence is hiring across Europe:** similar plants are opening in Poland, Germany and the UK.
