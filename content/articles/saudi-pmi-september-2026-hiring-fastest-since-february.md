Saudi Arabia's non-oil private sector hired more staff in **September 2026**, according to the **Riyad Bank Saudi Arabia PMI** released on **5 October**. The headline index climbed to **55.3** from **53.8** in August, the best result since February and the sixth monthly improvement in a row.

A reading above **50** means business conditions are improving.

## Key numbers

| Indicator | September 2026 |
|---|---|
| Headline PMI | 55.3 (August: 53.8) |
| Employment | Rising, faster than in August |
| New orders | Fastest growth since February |
| Export orders | Still falling, but less sharply |
| Business activity | Growth eased to a five-month low |
| Confidence | Weaker, due to regional tensions |

## Who is being hired

Survey respondents said they took on more **sales staff** and **technical workers** to support new investments. Riyad Bank chief economist **Naif Al-Ghaith** said the hiring appears linked to firms **building long-term capacity**, not just meeting short-term orders, which is a better sign for job security.

He also said the jump in new orders points to more activity in the months ahead.

## The cautions

- **Exports are weak.** Orders from foreign clients fell for a seventh month, though the drop was smaller.
- **Confidence slipped.** Firms were less upbeat about the year ahead because of the regional conflict and supply chain disruption.
- **Output growth slowed** even as orders rose, so backlogs started to build.

## How this compares with the UAE

In the UAE, the September PMI held at **55.3**, but hiring there was only **modest**. Saudi firms are currently adding staff more confidently. See [our report on UAE hiring in September](/uae/uae-pmi-september-2026-hiring-returns-prices-rise/).

## What it means for job seekers

- **Sales roles are in demand.** Experience in B2B sales or account management is useful.
- **Technical staff are wanted.** Engineers, technicians and IT staff fit firms' expansion plans.
- **Know the Saudization rules.** Many sales and admin roles now have high Saudi quotas, so expats should target specialist jobs.
- **Use official platforms.** Saudi nationals can find openings on [Jadarat](/saudi-arabia/saudi-jadarat-government-jobs-platform-how-to-apply/).
