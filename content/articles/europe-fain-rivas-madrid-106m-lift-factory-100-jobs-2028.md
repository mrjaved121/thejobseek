Engineers and technicians looking for **Europe jobs for foreigners** can add Madrid to their list. Family-owned Spanish lift maker **Fain Ascensores** will put **€106 million** into a new industrial, logistics and technology complex in **Rivas-Vaciamadrid**, just east of Madrid, creating **more than 100 skilled jobs**, Spanish media reported on 27 October 2026.

The new site will replace Fain's current plants in **Vallecas** and **San Fernando** and become the base for the group's growth in Europe.

## What will be built

- A **7,500 m² technology campus** for engineering and research
- **Two 10,000 m² industrial buildings** for manufacturing and logistics
- A **50-metre test tower** to try out new lifts
- Fully **robotised logistics**, solar power and a goal of net-zero energy use

Fain says the plant could raise output from about **1,500 lifts a year** to as many as **20,000**.

## Which jobs to expect

The complex will bring together manufacturing, corporate engineering, R&D, training, a remote service centre and central purchasing. That points to openings for:

- **Mechanical, electrical and electronic engineers**
- **R&D and software specialists** for lift control systems
- **Production and robotics technicians**
- **Logistics and purchasing staff**
- **Lift installation and service trainers**

Fain has not published salaries or a hiring timetable. Most new roles are likely to be filled as the buildings open from 2028.

## Working in Spain as a foreigner

EU and EEA citizens can work in Spain without a permit. People from outside the EU usually need a job offer from an employer willing to support their work authorisation, so focus on highly skilled roles where you bring hard-to-find experience. Spanish is very useful for most posts.

## How to apply

Fain posts vacancies on its official [Work with us page](https://fain.es/en/who-we-are/work-with-us/). You can apply for current roles now and send your CV so you are on file when the Rivas hiring begins.

## What it means for job seekers

- **This is a long-term project.** The biggest hiring wave will come closer to 2028–2031.
- **Lift and escalator skills travel well.** Experience here is valued across Europe and the Gulf.
- **Engineers with automation knowledge** will be in demand at a robotised site.
- **Learn Spanish now** if you want to stand out.
