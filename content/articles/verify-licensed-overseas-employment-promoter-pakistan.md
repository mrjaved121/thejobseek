Every year a large number of Pakistanis go abroad for work, mostly to Saudi Arabia, the UAE and other Gulf countries. Most go through Overseas Employment Promoters (OEPs), commonly called agents. Licensed promoters are legal and regulated. Unlicensed "agents" are behind many of the job scams that cost families their savings.

## What is a licensed Overseas Employment Promoter?

In Pakistan, recruiting workers for jobs abroad is regulated by the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment (BE&OE). Only promoters holding a valid licence are allowed to recruit for overseas jobs.

## Why using a licensed promoter matters

- **Legal protection:** complaints against licensed promoters can be filed with the authorities.
- **Proper process:** your emigration and protector process is done correctly.
- **Lower scam risk:** unlicensed agents can disappear after taking your money.

## How to check a promoter

1. Ask the agent for their **licence number** and the exact registered name of the company.
2. Check the promoter on the **official Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment website** linked above.
3. Visit the registered office address yourself.
4. Make sure the job, salary and employer details are written in the official documents.

## Red flags

- The agent works only from WhatsApp or a Facebook page with no office
- They refuse to share a licence number
- They promise a job "on a visit visa" in Dubai — working on a visit visa is not allowed in the UAE
- They ask for large cash payments with no official receipt

## If you are cheated

Keep all receipts, chats, bank transfer records and the agent's details, and file a complaint with the Bureau of Emigration's Protector of Emigrants office and the relevant authorities.
