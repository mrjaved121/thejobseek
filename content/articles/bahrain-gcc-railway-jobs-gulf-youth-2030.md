A rail line across the Gulf could open new **Bahrain jobs** for young people. Bahraini lawmakers say the **1,768km Gulf railway**, now more than **55% complete** and due to run from **December 2030**, will need **engineers, train drivers, maintenance teams and station workers**, News of Bahrain reported on 8 November 2026.

## What is the Gulf railway?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Length | About 1,768km |
| Countries | All six GCC states |
| Progress | Over 55% complete |
| Start of operations | December 2030 |
| Passenger speed | Up to 200km/h |
| Freight speed | 80–120km/h |

GCC states recently signed an agreement to move the project forward.

## Which jobs did lawmakers mention?

According to MP **Jameel Mulla Hassan**, young Gulf citizens will find work on the line first while it is built and then once it runs and needs upkeep. He listed:

- Rail engineers
- Train drivers
- Maintenance crews
- Staff at stations and freight centres

MP **Maryam Al Sayegh** said shops, hotels and businesses could grow around stations and freight hubs. **Dr Hassan BuKhammas** expects the line to widen job options for young nationals.

## When will hiring start?

Construction is under way now, so engineering and building roles exist today. Operations jobs such as drivers and station staff are likely closer to the 2030 launch, after training.

## What skills will be useful?

- Civil, electrical and mechanical engineering
- Signalling and rail systems
- Logistics and freight handling
- Customer service for station roles

## How can I prepare?

Watch announcements from the GCC Railway authority and Bahrain's transport ministry, and look for rail engineering courses and Tamkeen-supported training.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Long-term jobs pipeline** to 2030 and beyond.
- **Rail skills are new to the region**, so early training pays off.
- **Logistics jobs** may grow around freight hubs.
