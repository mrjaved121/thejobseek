Online parts manufacturer **SendCutSend** is coming to north-eastern Pennsylvania with **309 new jobs**. The company will invest **$63.2 million** in a new plant in **Pittston, Luzerne County**, Governor Josh Shapiro announced on **20 October 2026**.

It will be the firm's first site in Pennsylvania. The state says it won the project against competition from Ohio.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | SendCutSend, Inc. |
| Location | 535 Research Drive, Pittston, PA |
| Investment | $63.2 million |
| New jobs | 309 over three years |
| Site size | About 200,000 sq ft (leased and upgraded) |
| Work | CNC machining and laser cutting |
| State support | $3 million Pennsylvania First grant |

## About the company

SendCutSend, based in **Reno, Nevada**, lets customers upload a design online and receive custom-cut metal or wood parts within days. It already runs sites in Reno, **Paris, Kentucky** and **Arlington, Texas**. The Pittston plant is meant to offer one-day shipping to more customers in the north-east. CEO Jim Belosic summed it up in the announcement: the company is hiring and machines are arriving.

## Jobs likely to be needed

The state did not publish a list of roles. Based on the work the plant will do, likely openings include:

| Area | Example roles |
|---|---|
| Production | Laser operators, CNC machinists, press brake operators |
| Finishing | Deburring, powder coating and tapping technicians |
| Quality | Inspectors, quality technicians |
| Maintenance | Machine maintenance and electrical technicians |
| Logistics | Packers, shippers, material handlers |
| Support | Supervisors, engineers, customer service, HR |

Many fabrication jobs offer on-the-job training, so not every role will need years of experience.

## How to apply

Visit SendCutSend's official careers page at [sendcutsend.com/careers](https://sendcutsend.com/careers/) and click **View Openings**. At the time of writing, the page listed Reno, Paris and Arlington, with no Pittston roles yet. You can still submit a resume to the company's talent community so recruiters can contact you when Pennsylvania roles open.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Get in early.** The 309 jobs will be filled over three years, and first hires often move into lead and supervisor roles.
- **Trade skills pay off.** Machining, welding, fabrication or maintenance training from local colleges is directly useful.
- **Entry-level roles are likely.** Packing, finishing and material handling are good ways in for people new to manufacturing.
- **Commuting area.** Pittston sits between Wilkes-Barre and Scranton, so the plant should draw from across the region.
