**Scotland's Wild tour guide jobs are open now: the tour operator Experience Scotland's Wild is hiring guides based in Edinburgh and Glasgow on a full-time salary of £28,600, with tips on top.** Part-time posts are available too. You need category D1 or D on a UK driving licence, and you apply directly to the company by email. No closing date has been published.

## What is the job?

The [Daily Record](https://www.dailyrecord.co.uk/scotland-now/unique-job-opportunity-offers-chance-37846183) reported the vacancy on 8 November 2026, and the role is still advertised on the company's [official careers page](https://www.scotlandswild.com/careers-with-scotlands-wild), which we opened on 15 November.

This is a driver-guide job. You drive a small group through the Highlands, Lowlands and islands, tell the stories behind the places and lead short walks. The company runs day tours and multi-day tours, so some trips mean nights away from home.

## Who can apply?

The careers page lists these as must-haves:

- a full UK driving licence that includes category D1 or D
- a driving licence held for at least three years
- the ability to start tours from Edinburgh or Glasgow
- being comfortable with overnight stays on multi-day tours
- fitness for roughly two hours of walking daily on mixed terrain
- a high standard of customer care

Previous guiding is described as useful, not essential. The company says minibus driving, hospitality, customer service, public speaking and extra languages all carry over well. It also invites people living near Inverness to get in touch, because it plans to start tours from there.

## What is the salary and what are the benefits?

The full-time wage is £28,600. In its own FAQ the company states that gratuities are not part of that sum, so anything guests give you is extra. It does not say how much guides typically make in tips.

Listed benefits include ongoing training, first aid training, staff tours, discounted family tours, a uniform and staff social events. Time in lieu built up in busy months is given back in the quieter season.

## How to apply — step by step

1. Read the [official careers page](https://www.scotlandswild.com/careers-with-scotlands-wild) in full and check you meet every essential listed above.
2. Check your photocard licence for D1 or D. If the category is missing, you cannot be considered for this role yet.
3. Write a short, personal application explaining your background, your reasons for wanting the job and what a group would gain from you.
4. Attach an up-to-date CV and email it to **gfm@scotlandswild.com** with the subject line **Scotland Tour Guide Application**.
5. Say which base you want (Edinburgh or Glasgow) and whether you want full-time or part-time work.
6. Wait for a reply. The company has not published interview dates or a timeline.

One warning: use the email address shown on the company's own website. The address printed in some news coverage is spelled slightly differently, so copy it from the official page. Applying is free. No agent is needed, and you should never pay anyone for this job, an interview or a "guaranteed" place.

## Tips to improve your chances

- **Lead with the licence.** Put "Full UK licence with D1, held since [year]" in the first two lines of your CV. It is the first thing they will look for.
- **Show you are good with people.** The company says personality counts as much as knowledge. Give a real example of handling a group, a complaint or a change of plan.
- **Do not fake history knowledge.** They say nobody is expected to know every date and battle. Show curiosity and a favourite place or story instead.
- **Accept the long days.** The company itself says days can be long and the weather unpredictable.
- **Common mistakes:** applying with a car-only licence, sending a CV with no covering message, or leaving out the requested subject line.

**Quick checklist:** D1 or D licence · three years of driving · CV · personal message · preferred base.

## What it means for job seekers

- This is a small company hiring a limited number of guides, not a large recruitment drive. Apply soon, as the advert may be taken down without notice.
- The page says nothing about visa sponsorship, and a UK licence is required, so assume you need the right to work in the UK.
- No result is promised; a personal application that meets every essential improves your odds.
