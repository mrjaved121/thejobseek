Whether you are resigning or your employer is ending your contract, UAE Labour Law sets clear rules on notice and compensation. Here is a plain-English summary for private-sector workers.

## Notice period

- **Minimum 30 days, maximum 90 days**, as agreed in your contract.
- Notice must be given **in writing**, and you keep working and get **full pay** during it.
- The notice period should be the **same for both sides**, unless it is better for the worker.
- Both sides can agree to shorten or waive it.
- Whoever fails to give notice must pay the other side **pay in lieu of notice**.

## When a contract can end

Common reasons include: mutual written agreement; the contract term ending without renewal; either side ending it with the agreed notice; the company closing permanently or going bankrupt; and the worker not meeting work permit renewal conditions for reasons outside the employer's control.

## When an employer can dismiss without notice

Only for serious reasons set out in the law, for example: using forged documents, causing serious losses, breaking safety rules, disclosing confidential information, being drunk or on drugs at work, assaulting someone at work, or being absent without a valid reason for **more than 20 separate days in a year** or **more than 7 days in a row**. The employer must investigate in writing and give a written, reasoned dismissal.

## When you can leave without notice and keep your rights

You can leave without notice, and still keep your end-of-service benefits, if:

1. **Your employer breaks the contract or the law** (for example, unpaid wages). You must inform MoHRE at least **14 working days** before leaving, and the employer fails to fix the problem.
2. **You are assaulted or harassed** by the employer at work. Report it to the authorities and MoHRE within **5 working days**.
3. You are given **fundamentally different work** without your written consent.
4. There is a **serious danger** to your safety or health that the employer does not remove.

## Arbitrary dismissal

If you are dismissed for an unlawful reason, for example because you filed a genuine complaint, a court can award compensation of up to **3 months' wages**, based on your last salary. You can also claim your gratuity, notice pay and other unpaid dues.

## Your final settlement

Your gratuity, last salary and other entitlements must be paid within **14 days** of the contract ending. See: [How UAE gratuity is calculated](/uae/uae-end-of-service-gratuity-calculation-guide/).
