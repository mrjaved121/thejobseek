Fresh law graduates looking for **Kuwait government jobs** have a clear opening this month. Kuwait's **Ministry of Justice** is accepting applications for the post of **Junior Legal Researcher**, a training route into the public prosecution. The application window opened on **Sunday 1 November 2026** and closes on **Monday 30 November 2026**, Arab Times reported on 31 October.

Successful candidates are trained at the **Kuwait Institute for Judicial and Legal Studies** and can then be appointed as an **assistant public prosecutor (Grade C)**. The ministry has not published how many posts are available.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Ministry of Justice, Kuwait |
| Job title | Junior Legal Researcher |
| Career path | Assistant Public Prosecutor (Grade C) |
| Open to | Kuwaiti nationals by origin, men and women |
| Graduation year | 2025/2026 |
| Applications | 1 November – 30 November 2026 |
| Number of posts | Not announced |

## Who can apply

The ministry's conditions are strict, so check each one before you start:

- **Kuwaiti by origin**, male or female
- A **law degree (LLB)** earned at Kuwait University, or a qualification the Ministry of Higher Education accepts as equal to it
- Graduated in the **2025/2026** academic year
- A GPA of at least **2.67 out of 4**, or **80%**
- **Aged 26 or under** when the degree was awarded
- Has **not applied** for this post before
- Has not worked in the public or private sector using the degree, and is **not registered** with the Public Institution for Social Security
- Good conduct, no disciplinary penalty or conviction for a dishonesty offence, and medically fit

Shortlisted applicants sit a **written exam** first. Only those who pass go on to a **personal interview**.

## Documents you need

All papers must be uploaded as **PDF files**. Missing documents mean the application is rejected.

1. Personal photo
2. Civil ID copy
3. Birth certificate
4. Nationality certificate
5. A recent certificate from the Public Institution for Social Security
6. Good conduct certificate
7. Degree certificate showing the GPA, plus the transcript
8. For foreign degrees, an equivalency certificate from the Ministry of Higher Education

## Salary

No figure was published. The ministry says appointees receive the financial benefits and allowances set for the position.

## How to apply

Go to the Ministry of Justice website at **moj.gov.kw**, open **Employment Services**, read the requirements, then complete the job application form and upload your documents. The ministry also listed phone numbers for questions: 22486739, 22486717 and 22486741. Do not wait for the final day, as late applications will not be considered.

## What it means for job seekers

- **This is for this year's graduates only.** If you graduated earlier or have worked with your degree, you are not eligible.
- **Prepare your PDFs now.** Getting a fresh social security and good conduct certificate can take time.
- **Revise for the exam.** The written test decides who reaches the interview.
- **It is a long-term career.** The role leads into the prosecution service, one of the most respected legal careers in Kuwait.
