One of the most famous **seasonal jobs USA** opportunities is open again. The **Masters Tournament** is accepting applications for paid roles at the **2027 Masters** in Augusta, Georgia, in **concessions, hospitality and merchandise**. Apply at **jobs.masters.com** by **1 December 2026**, or meet the team at a public **job expo on 12 November**.

## Which Masters jobs are open?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Masters Tournament, Augusta National Golf Club |
| Roles | Concessions, hospitality, merchandise |
| Pay | Paid; rate varies by position |
| Applications close | 1 December 2026 |
| Offers sent | From mid-December 2026 to March 2027 |

## Which dates must I work?

Availability for **all** dates is required:

- **3 April 2027** – Augusta National Women's Amateur
- **4 April 2027** – Drive, Chip and Putt National Finals
- **5–11 April 2027** – Masters Tournament week

## Who can apply?

- At least **16 years old** by the first day of work
- **Eligible to work in the United States**
- Able to pass a **background check**
- Fit for long days: most roles involve **10–14 hours a day** of standing, walking, bending and lifting

You can apply for **one position only**. Shortlisted applicants are emailed an invitation to an online interview.

## When is the job expo?

- **Date:** Thursday, 12 November 2026
- **Time:** 3pm to 6pm
- **Place:** HUB for Community Innovation, 631 Chafee Ave., Augusta

Attendees can talk to the Masters team and learn about the roles available.

## How do I apply?

Submit your application on the official **[Masters jobs site](https://jobs.masters.com)** before **1 December 2026**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Apply early.** Places are limited and only one application is allowed per person.
- **Clear your calendar.** Missing any of the required April dates rules you out.
- **Great for students and locals** wanting a short, high-profile seasonal job.
