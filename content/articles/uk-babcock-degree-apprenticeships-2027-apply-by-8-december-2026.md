**Babcock degree apprenticeships for 2027 are open now: the defence company is taking applications for Degree Apprenticeships in England and Graduate Apprenticeships in Scotland, and the listings we checked close on 8 December 2026.** You apply directly on Babcock's own careers site, the programmes start in September 2027, and you earn a salary while the company pays for your degree.

## What is the news?

This week [The Manufacturer reported](https://www.themanufacturer.com/articles/babcock-welcomes-more-than-800-early-careers-recruits-to-strengthen-uk-defence-skills/) that Babcock has taken on more than 800 apprentices, graduates and undergraduates in its 2026 intake. The roles range from welding and fabrication to software, data and cyber security. The same report notes Babcock now ranks 12th on the Department for Education's list of top apprenticeship employers.

The next intake is already recruiting. [Babcock's apprenticeships page](https://careers.babcockinternational.com/emerging-talent/apprenticeships/) says degree-level schemes are live today, while the lower levels (Intermediate, Advanced and Higher in England, Modern Apprenticeships in Scotland) start taking applications in December.

## What is the pay, and what grades do I need?

Each listing sets its own terms. Two examples we read in full:

| Detail | Electro-Mechanical Engineering, Devonport | Project Management, Rosyth |
|---|---|---|
| Level | Level 6 (BEng) | SCQF Level 10 |
| Length | 5 years | 4 years |
| Starting pay | £27,381 | £18,819 (year 4: £32,261) |
| Entry | 5 GCSEs at C/4 including Maths and English, plus A-levels with an A in Maths and a physical science, or listed equivalents | SQA Highers BBBB including English, or A-levels BBC, or listed equivalents |
| Closing date | 8 December 2026 | 8 December 2026 |

The Devonport listing adds that apprentices spend four weeks a year at the University of Warwick and can expect a salary above £42,000 once qualified. Always read the entry rules on the exact programme you want, because they differ.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Go to the official page:** [careers.babcockinternational.com/emerging-talent/apprenticeships](https://careers.babcockinternational.com/emerging-talent/apprenticeships/) and open the programme list.
2. **Choose one programme only.** Babcock reviews a single application per cycle. If you send several, only the first counts.
3. **Create a candidate profile** and upload your CV as a PDF or DOC file. Babcock asks for simple formatting with no graphics or tables.
4. **Complete the online assessment** as soon as the link arrives. The listings say applications are reviewed as they come in.
5. **Attend an assessment centre** if invited. Expect a presentation, a group activity and an interview.
6. **Offer and checks.** Successful candidates receive a formal offer, then pre-employment and security checks before the September 2027 start.

You do not need an agent, a paid course or a "CV service" to apply. Babcock does not charge applicants, and anybody asking for money to secure an apprenticeship is not acting for the company.

## Who can apply? The security rules matter

Babcock works on submarines, warships and nuclear sites, so vetting is strict. Its nationality and security page says every employee must pass at least a Baseline Personnel Security Standard check. In most cases that means having the right to work in the UK and at least three years of UK residency. Higher clearances ask for five or ten years. The company says many unsuccessful applicants fall at this stage, so check it before you spend time on the form.

You must also live within reach of the site by your start date. The Rosyth listing says the role is on site and you may need to relocate.

## Tips that improve your chances

- **Apply early.** Do not wait for 8 December; popular schemes can shut sooner.
- **List your subjects and units.** Listings ask BTEC and Access students to name the units they are taking.
- **Show interest in engineering,** such as a school project, cadets, a part-time job using tools or a coding hobby.
- **Practise a short presentation** and group tasks before the assessment centre.

Quick checklist: predicted or achieved grades, simple CV, an email address you check, proof of UK residency history, one chosen programme.

## What it means for job seekers

- A paid route to a degree with no tuition fees is rare; competition will be strong, and no place is guaranteed.
- School leavers in Plymouth, Bristol, Fife and Argyll have the widest choice.
- If degree level is not right for you, wait for the other levels to open in December.
