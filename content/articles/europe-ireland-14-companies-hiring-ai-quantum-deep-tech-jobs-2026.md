Ireland remains one of the best places for **Europe jobs for foreigners** with tech skills. On **30 October 2026**, Silicon Republic listed **14 Ireland-based companies** recruiting in **AI, quantum computing and deep tech**, from global consultancies to a Dublin quantum chip start-up.

Most roles are in Dublin, but there are also openings in Cork, Galway, Letterkenny and Dún Laoghaire.

## The skills in demand

Three skill groups come up again and again:

- **AI and large language models** – architects and engineers who can build and run AI systems at scale
- **Data** – data engineers, data scientists and analysts
- **Quantum and physics** – rare, specialist roles at Equal1 and Horizon Quantum

Pharma firms Amgen and MSD are also hiring engineers and lab specialists, a reminder that Ireland's life sciences sector uses more and more automation and data.

## Can foreigners apply?

EU and EEA citizens can work in Ireland freely. Non-EU applicants usually need an employment permit. Many senior software, data and AI roles fall under Ireland's **Critical Skills Employment Permit**, but the employer must agree to support your application. None of the roles above promise sponsorship, so read each advert carefully.

## How to apply

Each company hires through its own official careers site. Search the employer's careers page for the role title and "Ireland". Avoid third-party sites that charge fees to "submit" your CV.

## What it means for job seekers

- **AI architecture is the hottest skill.** Four of the big consultancies are hunting for it.
- **Students should look at internships** at Fidelity and Horizon Quantum.
- **Regional jobs are a real option.** Letterkenny has roles at both Optum and TCS.
- **Show real projects.** A portfolio on GitHub often matters more than a long CV.
