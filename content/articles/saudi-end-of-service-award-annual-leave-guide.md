If you work in Saudi Arabia's private sector, you are entitled to an **end-of-service award** when your job ends. How much you get depends on your years of service and whether you resigned or the employer ended the contract.

## The basic formula (Article 84)

The award is calculated on your **last wage**:

| Years of service | Award per year |
|---|---|
| First 5 years | **Half a month's wage** |
| Each year after 5 | **One month's wage** |

**Example:** last wage SAR 8,000 a month, 8 years of service, contract ended by the employer:
- First 5 years: 5 × 0.5 × 8,000 = SAR 20,000
- Next 3 years: 3 × 8,000 = SAR 24,000
- **Total: SAR 44,000**

## If you resign (Article 85)

Resigning reduces the award, depending on your service:

| Service when you resign | Share of the award |
|---|---|
| Less than 2 years | None |
| 2 to 5 years | **One third** |
| More than 5 and less than 10 years | **Two thirds** |
| 10 years or more | **Full award** |

## When you get the full award even if you leave (Article 87)

- You leave because of **force majeure** beyond your control.
- A female worker ends her contract within **6 months of marriage** or **3 months of giving birth**.

## Annual leave and leave pay

- At least **21 days** of paid annual leave a year (Article 109).
- This rises to at least **30 days** after **5 consecutive years** with the same employer.
- If you leave with unused leave, you must be **paid for those days**, plus leave pay for the part of the year you worked (Article 111).

## Recent amendments to know

The amended Labor Law, set out in an HRSD guide, includes:

- **Probation**: a maximum of **180 days** in total.
- **Notice for indefinite contracts (monthly paid)**: **30 days** from the worker, **60 days** from the employer.
- **Maternity leave**: **12 weeks** fully paid.
- **Resignation**: treated as accepted if the employer doesn't respond within 30 days, and it can be withdrawn within 7 days unless already accepted.

## Tips

- Check that your **"last wage"** figure in the settlement matches your contract.
- Keep your **Qiwa contract** and payslips.
- If your dues are not paid, file a complaint through HRSD's official channels.
