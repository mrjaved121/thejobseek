Bakery group **Roberts Bakery** is hiring around **50 people** at its **Little Treats Bakery** site in **Winsford, Cheshire**, trade publication Just Food reported on 28 October 2026. The jobs span **engineering, operations and production**, and include openings that could suit people looking for **UK graduate jobs** in food manufacturing as well as experienced factory staff.

## What is happening in Winsford

A **£500,000** investment from **Boparan Private Office**, the investment arm that took over Roberts Bakery last year, is paying for a new production line. The line will make **chocolate clusters** and **high-protein clusters**, taking the Winsford site beyond its main biscuit business.

The Little Treats unit already turns out about **100 million biscuits a year**. The site has been rebuilding its operations after a fire in 2023, and site director **James Rostron** leads the plant.

## The jobs on offer

| Area | Typical roles | Who it suits |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | Maintenance engineers, line engineers, technicians | Engineering graduates, apprentices, experienced fitters |
| Operations | Shift managers, team leaders, planners | Graduates in food science or operations, supervisors |
| Production | Line operatives, packers, machine operators | Entry-level workers, people with factory experience |

Exact job titles and pay have not been published. The company says it is **already recruiting** for the new positions.

## Why food manufacturing is worth a look

Food factories run around the clock, so they need people on every shift. Engineering and operations roles in the sector often come with structured training and a clear route into management. For new graduates, a site that is adding a brand new line offers the chance to learn how a production process is set up from the start.

## How to apply

No direct link to the Winsford vacancies was given in the announcement. Search the **Roberts Bakery careers section** on its official website, and look for jobs listed under Winsford or Little Treats Bakery. Apply only through the company's own site or adverts it links to, and never pay a fee to apply.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Engineers are in demand.** Multi-skilled maintenance engineers with mechanical and electrical skills are hard to find, so expect good interest in your CV.
- **Be shift-ready.** Food lines often run days, nights and weekends; flexibility strengthens your application.
- **Graduates should pitch problem-solving.** Mention projects on process improvement, quality or automation.
- **Food hygiene counts.** A Level 2 food safety certificate can give production applicants an edge.
