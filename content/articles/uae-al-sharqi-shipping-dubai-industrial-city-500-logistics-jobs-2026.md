**Logistics jobs in Dubai** are getting a boost. UAE logistics company **Al Sharqi Shipping** has opened a new multi-temperature logistics and distribution hub at **Dubai Industrial City**, and says the site is expected to create **around 500 new jobs**. The opening was announced on 31 October 2026.

## What has Al Sharqi Shipping opened?

The new hub is a large integrated warehouse and distribution centre built for both ambient and frozen goods. It sits inside Dubai Industrial City, which is part of Tecom Group, and is close to **Jebel Ali Port** and **Al Maktoum International Airport**.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Al Sharqi Shipping |
| Location | Dubai Industrial City, Dubai |
| Expected new jobs | About 500 |
| Built-up storage | 280,000 sq ft |
| Open yard | 200,000 sq ft |
| Temperature range | Ambient down to -18°C |
| Loading lanes | 38 |
| Truck capacity | Up to 178 trucks a day |

## Which sectors will the hub serve?

The facility is designed for local demand and for distribution across the region. Its main client sectors are:

- Food and beverage
- FMCG (fast-moving consumer goods)
- Retail
- Manufacturing
- Hospitality
- Quick-commerce (fast home delivery)

Chief Operating Officer **Asif Rafiq** said the site will support the company's next phase of growth, especially in temperature-controlled logistics, regional distribution and full supply-chain services.

## What jobs could open?

The company has not yet listed specific roles. Based on how hubs of this size usually operate, likely openings include:

- Warehouse operatives and pickers/packers
- Forklift, reach-truck and heavy truck drivers
- Inventory and stock controllers
- Cold-room and quality checkers
- Shift supervisors and warehouse managers
- Dispatch, transport planning and customer service staff
- Supply-chain, IT and admin roles

Treat this list as a guide only. Exact roles, numbers and salaries will be confirmed by the employer when hiring starts.

## How do I apply?

Al Sharqi Shipping has not announced a separate recruitment drive or careers page for the new hub. Follow the company's official website and LinkedIn page for openings, and apply only through those channels. Never pay a fee to anyone who promises you a job at the hub.

- **Jebel Ali is a busy area.** Many logistics firms recruit around Jebel Ali and Dubai South, so this hub is one more option in an active job market.
