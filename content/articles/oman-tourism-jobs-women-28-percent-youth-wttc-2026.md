Tourism is one of the most open sectors for **Oman jobs** seekers, especially women and young people. A **World Travel & Tourism Council (WTTC)** briefing paper shows women hold **28% of travel and tourism jobs** in Oman, far above the **18% Middle East average**, while young people hold **13%** of tourism jobs.

## What do the WTTC figures show?

| Measure | Oman | Comparison |
|---|---|---|
| Women's share of tourism jobs | 28% | 18% Middle East average |
| Youth share of tourism jobs | 13% | Under 9% across Oman's whole economy |

The WTTC pointed to Oman as an example for the region in bringing women into tourism work.

## Where were the figures presented?

Oman's Minister of Heritage and Tourism, **Sayyid Ibrahim bin Said al Busaidi**, met WTTC President **Gloria Guevara Manzo** at **World Travel Market (WTM)** in London, where Oman was promoting its tourism sector. The data comes from the WTTC's "Power, Efficiency and Opportunity" indicators.

## What is the "Power, Efficiency and Opportunity" framework?

- **Power:** tourism's contribution to GDP, jobs and investment
- **Efficiency:** environmental performance and use of energy, water and resources
- **Opportunity:** employment and the participation of women and young people

## Which tourism jobs are common in Oman?

The tourism and hospitality sector typically employs people in:

- Hotel front office, guest relations and reservations
- Food and beverage service and kitchens
- Tour guiding and travel agency work
- Events, marketing and sales
- Heritage sites, museums and visitor centres

These are typical tourism roles, not a confirmed vacancy list.

## How do I find tourism jobs in Oman?

Omanis interested in tourism work can sign up for the Labour Ministry's job-matching services and also approach hotels, tour companies and heritage sites directly. Training programmes linked to Omanisation often include hospitality.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Tourism welcomes women and youth** more than many other sectors.
- **Hospitality training pays off** as Oman grows its tourism offer.
- **Languages help.** English and other foreign languages are valuable in guest-facing roles.
