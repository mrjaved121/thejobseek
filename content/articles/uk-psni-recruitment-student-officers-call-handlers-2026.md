The **Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI)** is recruiting **student officers**, **call handlers** and **administrative staff** in a campaign launched to mark its **25th anniversary**. New officers start on around **£35,000**, and the student officer application window closes at **3pm on Monday 30 November 2026**.

The campaign was launched by Chief Constable **Jon Boutcher** on **12 October 2026** and is being backed by a seven-week advertising push across TV, radio, print and social media.

## Roles and key dates

| Role | Status | Key date |
|---|---|---|
| Student officers | Open now | Closes 3pm, 30 November 2026 |
| Transferee Scheme (serving officers from other forces) | Open now | Runs alongside student officer campaign |
| Call handlers | Open | Opened 19 October 2026 |
| Administrative Support Officers | Opening soon | Opens 9 November 2026 |

## Pay and training

The PSNI says recruits get a **competitive starting salary of about £35k**, full training and ongoing development. Student officers can also work towards a **degree in criminology and policing from Ulster University** while they earn, which means you can gain a qualification without paying fees out of your own pocket.

## Who can apply

For student officers, the official PSNI site lists these main rules:

- Age **17 to 57** on 30 November 2026
- **5 GCSEs (or equivalent) at grades A\* to C**, including **English Language**
- **Eligible to work permanently in the UK**
- Meet the **physical and medical standards**
- No criminal convictions that automatically bar you, and no offensive tattoos

The PSNI and the Northern Ireland Policing Board are encouraging people from **all communities and backgrounds** to apply. Justice Minister **Naomi Long** linked the drive to a workforce recovery plan to raise police numbers.

## How to apply

Apply online through the official PSNI recruitment site at [joinpsni.co.uk](https://www.joinpsni.co.uk/police-officer/apply). The steps are:

1. Check you meet the criteria on the "Can I Join?" page.
2. Read the Guidance for Applicants and the Candidate Charter.
3. Click "Start Application" and fill in the form.
4. Submit before 3pm on 30 November 2026.

Call handler and administrative support officer vacancies are also advertised through the PSNI's official recruitment channels.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Do not leave it late.** Online forms can take time, and the deadline is firm.
- **Prepare for fitness tests.** Start training now if you are not used to running.
- **Check your GCSE certificates.** You will need proof of English Language.
- **Consider civilian roles.** Call handling and admin are good entry points if you are not ready for frontline policing.
