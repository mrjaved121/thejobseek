Job seekers in Virginia can meet the **US Postal Service** in person next week. USPS is holding a **job fair in Fredericksburg** on **Friday 13 November 2026** to fill **immediate full- and part-time openings**, making it a good option for **seasonal jobs USA** and longer-term careers.

## When and where is the job fair?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Date | Friday, 13 November 2026 |
| Time | 10am – 3pm |
| Venue | Virginia Career Works – Fredericksburg |
| Address | 600 Princess Anne St., Fredericksburg, VA 22401 |
| Openings | Full-time and part-time |

USPS staff will be on hand to explain the open positions and answer questions.

## What does USPS offer?

According to USPS:

- **Competitive wages and benefits**
- **Career advancement opportunities**
- **Training and on-the-job support** for new employees under its 10-year plan
- **Seasonal, part-time and full-time** options

USPS expects package volumes to keep growing as online shopping increases, which is driving its hiring.

## Who can apply?

Each USPS job posting lists its own requirements. Postal jobs generally require applicants to pass a background check and drug screening and to be eligible to work in the US. Check the listing for age, driving and other requirements before applying.

## How do I apply?

Visit the fair, or search and apply online on the official **[USPS careers website](https://www.usps.com/careers)**, which is updated frequently.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Holiday hiring is in full swing** at USPS.
- **Full-time and part-time roles** suit different needs.
