**US hiring 2026** is getting a boost in West Tennessee. **MAT Industries** will create **85 new jobs** at its factory in **Jackson**, Madison County, Tennessee Governor **Bill Lee** and the state's economic development department announced on **29 October 2026**.

The jobs come with a **new production line**, added because demand for the company's products keeps growing.

## About the company

MAT Holdings was founded in 1984 and is based in Long Grove, Illinois. It runs more than **40 factories** and employs about **14,000 people** across three continents. Its brands are common in US hardware stores and workshops.

This is the second expansion in Jackson in little more than a year. In **June 2025**, the company added **30 jobs** and invested **$9 million** at the same plant. The state did not give an investment figure for the new phase.

Vice president of operations **Robbie Hite** said the company is proud to keep growing in Jackson.

## Jobs likely to be needed

A new production line in a compressor and tool factory usually needs:

- **Production and assembly workers**
- **Machine operators and welders**
- **Maintenance technicians**
- **Quality inspectors**
- **Shipping and warehouse staff**
- **Supervisors and engineers**

Wages and start dates were not published.

## How to apply

The announcement did not name a hiring page. Watch MAT Holdings' official website for job postings at the Jackson plant, and check the Tennessee state job service, Jobs4TN, for local factory openings. Never pay anyone for a factory job.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Steady manufacturing work** is coming to West Tennessee, with a company that keeps investing in the same site.
- **Entry-level roles are likely** on a new production line, so this is a good chance for people new to factory work.
- **Mechanical skills stand out.** Welding, machining or maintenance experience can lead to higher-paid roles.
- **Overseas job seekers** should note that most US factory jobs are filled locally and rarely come with visa sponsorship.
