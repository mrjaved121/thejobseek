Northeast Tennessee is getting a major jobs boost. Nuclear firm **BWXT** will create **181 new jobs** and invest **$714.5 million** at its site in **Jonesborough, Washington County**, more than doubling its local workforce, Tennessee Governor **Bill Lee** announced on 6 November 2026. Openings will be listed on BWXT's official careers site.

## What is BWXT building?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | BWXT (BWXT Ordnance Tennessee) |
| Location | Jonesborough, Washington County, Tennessee |
| New jobs | 181 |
| Investment | $714.5 million |
| Product | High purity depleted uranium (HPDU) metal |
| Customer | US National Nuclear Security Administration (NNSA) |

The NNSA gave BWXT a **10-year, $1.6 billion contract** in September 2025 to build the HPDU plant. When finished, Jonesborough will be the only active US site able to perform this process. A state economic development grant supports the project.

## Is this BWXT's first Tennessee expansion?

No. It is BWXT's third project backed by Tennessee's **Nuclear Energy Fund**:

| Location | Jobs | Investment |
|---|---|---|
| Oak Ridge (2025) | 60 | $45 million |
| Erwin (2025) | 198 | $122 million |
| Jonesborough (2026) | 181 | $714.5 million |

Across all 12 projects, the fund has been linked to nearly **4,000 jobs** and more than **$10 billion** of investment.

## Which jobs are likely?

A specialist nuclear materials plant typically hires:

- Process and production operators
- Chemical, nuclear and mechanical engineers
- Maintenance and instrument technicians
- Quality, safety and radiation protection staff
- Security personnel

These are typical roles for this kind of site, not a confirmed vacancy list. Defence nuclear work usually requires **US citizenship** and a **security clearance**.
