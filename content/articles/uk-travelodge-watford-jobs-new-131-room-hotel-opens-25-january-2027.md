**Travelodge Watford jobs are being filled now: the chain's new 131-room hotel at Watford Riverwell opens on 25 January 2027, and it is recruiting staff ahead of that date, including housekeepers and front desk receptionists.** It will be the company's second hotel in the town. You apply directly on Travelodge's official careers site, where the Vacancies section lets you search by location.

## What do we know about the new hotel?

The [Watford Observer](https://www.watfordobserver.co.uk/news/26722347.new-watford-travelodge-hotels-opening-date-confirmed/) confirmed the opening date on 12 November 2026 and said hiring is under way. The hotel stands on Shrodells Road, close to Watford General Hospital, and forms a centrepiece of the Riverwell regeneration scheme, which is also bringing around 1,000 homes and new shops to the area.

Travelodge's [own announcement of the project](https://www.travelodge.co.uk/our-business/media/press-release/travelodge-to-open-new-hotel-as-part-of-watford-riverwell-regeneration-scheme/) adds detail. The four-storey building will have the brand's newer room design and an 85 Bar Cafe restaurant, with 78 parking spaces set aside in the neighbouring multi-storey car park. The company has signed a 30-year lease, so these are jobs in a hotel built to stay. Vicarage Road stadium and the town centre are both nearby.

## Which roles does Travelodge recruit for?

The company's careers site describes three kinds of hotel job:

- **Hotel team members**, who may greet guests at reception, help housekeeping or serve in the bar, depending on the hotel.
- **Housekeeping**, which Travelodge calls its biggest team. It says the work is hard but hours can be flexible.
- **Managers**, who lead the team and are measured on guest review scores, cost control and food and drink sales.

Because the Watford hotel has a Bar Cafe, kitchen and bar shifts are a reasonable expectation, but only housekeeping and reception have been reported so far. Check the live adverts for exact titles, hours and pay, as Travelodge had not published wage figures for this site in the sources we read.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Visit the official careers site** at [travelodge.co.uk/careers](https://www.travelodge.co.uk/careers/).
2. **Choose "Vacancies" or "Hotel Jobs".** Search for Watford. Two hotels may appear, so check the advert names the new Riverwell site if that is the one you want.
3. **Read the job description.** Note the hours, shift pattern and duties before you start the form.
4. **Apply online with an up-to-date CV.** Travelodge asks for clear contact details, months and years for each job, and a reason for any gaps in work.
5. **Prepare for interview.** The company suggests the STAR method: describe the Situation, the Task, the Action you took and the Result.
6. **Ask about next steps.** Travelodge itself tells candidates to ask this at the end of the interview. With the opening set for late January, expect training before the doors open, though no schedule has been published.

## Is an agency needed?

No. Travelodge takes applications itself through its careers site and does not charge candidates. If a person or website wants money to "arrange" a hotel job or an interview slot, it is not coming from the company. Stick to the official page.

## Tips that help you stand out

- **Tailor the CV to the role.** Travelodge's own guidance says to match your skills to the duties in the advert.
- **Bring real examples.** Think of a time you dealt with an unhappy customer or kept standards high under pressure.
- **Be ready for "Why do you want to work here?"** The careers site lists it as a likely question. Visiting an existing Travelodge first gives you something specific to say.
- **Arrive ten minutes early** and check the address, as the company advises.
- **Common mistakes:** one-word answers and exaggerating experience. Travelodge asks candidates to stick to the facts.

## What it means for job seekers

- Hospitality work is opening in west Watford at a quiet time of year for hiring.
- Benefits listed by Travelodge include half-price UK rooms and Bar Café meals, a £50 anniversary voucher, retail discounts, life assurance and automatic pension enrolment.
- There is a route upwards: the company runs a management development programme called ASPIRE, although it has no graduate scheme.
- The adverts do not mention visa sponsorship, so assume UK right to work is required.
- Applying early matters because a new hotel recruits its opening team in one go. An application is still no guarantee of an offer.
