**The McAllen job fair 2026, called Hiring Red, White & You!, is on Tuesday 17 November, and over 190 employers will be recruiting inside the McAllen Convention Center.** Veterans go in at 8:30am and everyone else from 9:30am to 1pm. It costs nothing and you do not have to sign up first.

Texas Border Business published the organiser's announcement on 13 November 2026. We checked the details against the event page of Workforce Solutions (WFS), the workforce board for Hidalgo, Starr and Willacy counties.

## Who can attend?

Hiring Red, White & You! is a statewide Texas hiring event built to connect veterans, service members and military spouses with employers. That is why the first hour is reserved for them. After 9:30am the doors open to any job seeker in the Rio Grande Valley.

WFS runs the McAllen fair with five partners: the Texas Workforce Commission, Hidalgo County, the City of McAllen, the city's Chamber of Commerce and its Economic Development Corporation.

## Which employers will be there?

The announcement gives the number, more than 190, but does not print the names in the article. WFS says the employer list is out as a video on its social media pages, and suggests pausing the video as it scrolls so you can note the companies you want. We could not find a written list on the WFS website, so we are not naming employers here.

## How to attend — step by step

1. **Confirm the details** on the official [WFS event page](https://www.wfsolutions.org/my-wfs/calendar-of-events/4261/-/hiring-red-white-you.html) the evening before.
2. **Watch the employer list video** on the Workforce Solutions social media pages and write down five to ten target employers.
3. **Print your résumé.** The organiser asks for multiple copies. Twenty is a safe number for a fair this size.
4. **Dress professionally,** as WFS advises. Recruiters may hold short interviews at the booth.
5. **Arrive at the right time.** Veterans, military members and spouses: 8:30am, with proof of service if you have it. General public: 9:30am. Coming early matters because the fair closes at 1pm.
6. **After the fair,** complete any online application the recruiter mentions the same day, and send a short thank-you email.

Entry is free and there is no middleman. You deal with employers face to face. Nobody should charge you for a place in line, a "guaranteed" job or résumé access at this event.

## Can I get help before the fair?

Yes. WFS says it provides job postings, résumé assistance and counselling for job seekers at no cost. Its website lists offices in McAllen (2101 S. 2nd St.), Edinburg, Mission, Weslaco, Rio Grande City and Raymondville, and you can call (888) 261-3286 to book a visit.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Lead with what you can do.** Open your résumé with a three-line summary: job title you want, years of experience, top skills.
- **Veterans:** translate military roles into civilian terms. "Managed a team of 12 and equipment worth $2 million" works better than a unit code.
- **Prepare one answer** to "Tell me about yourself" that lasts under a minute.
- **Bring a pen and a folder** so collected business cards and flyers stay in order.
- **Common mistakes:** one generic résumé with typos, arriving at noon, and leaving without the recruiter's name.

Checklist: 20 résumés, photo ID, list of target employers, references sheet, professional outfit, phone charged.

## What it means for job seekers

- With 190-plus employers in one hall, you can cover in a morning what would take weeks of separate applications.
- Attending does not guarantee an offer, but meeting a recruiter in person usually beats a cold online application.
