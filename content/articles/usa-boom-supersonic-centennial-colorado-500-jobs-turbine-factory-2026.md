Looking at **USA jobs for foreigners** and skilled locals in advanced manufacturing? **Boom Supersonic**, the Denver-area company best known for its supersonic jet plans, announced on 22 October 2026 a **$150 million manufacturing campus in Centennial, Colorado** that will create **up to 500 jobs**. Colorado Governor Jared Polis took part in the announcement, and US Energy Secretary Chris Wright attended.

## What Boom is building

The new "Superfactory" will produce Boom's **Superpower**, a **42-megawatt gas turbine** designed to supply electricity to AI data centres. Key facts:

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Location | Centennial, south of Denver |
| Investment | $150 million |
| Size | Nearly 200,000 sq ft, two buildings |
| Jobs | Up to 500 |
| Special feature | Foundry for single-crystal turbine blades |
| Fully operational | Early 2027 |
| First customer turbines | Mid-2027 |
| Output target | 25 turbines a year by 2028, 50 by 2029 |

Parts production has already begun on site while construction continues. For the Denver area, it is one of the larger factory hiring announcements of the year, and it adds to Colorado's growing cluster of aerospace and energy employers.

## Which roles are needed

Boom says the jobs will cover:

- **Design engineers**
- **Manufacturing engineers**
- **Machinists** and machine tool operators
- **Technicians**, including quality roles

The in-house blade foundry means there may also be openings in metallurgy, casting and inspection. CEO Blake Scholl said the company is bringing all manufacturing in-house so it can move faster.

## Can foreigners apply?

Anyone can submit an application, but be realistic. Aerospace and energy hardware firms often need staff who already have the right to work in the US, and some roles may fall under export-control rules. Read each job listing carefully for eligibility notes before applying.

## How to apply

Open roles are posted on Boom's official [manufacturing careers page](https://boomsupersonic.com/careers/manufacturing). The main careers page at boomsupersonic.com/careers lists engineering and office jobs too.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Machinists are in demand** – CNC, 5-axis and precision measurement skills fit this type of factory well.
- **Engineers with turbine, casting or power-generation experience** should apply early while teams are being built.
- **Timing matters** – most hiring is likely in the run-up to full operations in early 2027.
- **Tailor your CV** to energy and data-centre power, not only aviation.
