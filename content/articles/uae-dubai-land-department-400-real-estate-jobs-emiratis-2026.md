Emirati job seekers looking for **Dubai jobs** in property have a strong new route in. The **Dubai Land Department (DLD)** brought together more than **80 real estate companies** offering around **400 jobs** for UAE nationals at its fourth **Real Estate Day for Emirati Employment**, held on **Thursday 29 October 2026** at the Address Sky View hotel, Khaleej Times reported on 30 October.

Candidates met recruiters face to face and many were interviewed on the spot. Big names such as **Emaar**, **Danube** and **Dirham Real Estate** were among the employers.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Organiser | Dubai Land Department |
| Date | 29 October 2026 |
| Companies | 80+ developers, brokers and property managers |
| Jobs on offer | About 400 |
| Who could apply | UAE nationals (students and job seekers) |
| Partners | MoHRE, Nafis, Emirati Human Resources Development Council in Dubai |

## Which jobs were on offer

The openings went well beyond sales. Employers were looking for people in:

- Real estate brokerage and sales
- Property and facilities management
- Valuation and consulting
- Accounting and finance
- Some part-time posts for students
- A smaller number of senior and executive roles

No salary figures were published, but private sector jobs taken by Emiratis can be supported by the federal **Nafis** programme.

## A growing pipeline for Emiratis

This was not a one-off. DLD says more than **2,100 Emiratis** have joined the Dubai property sector since 2023. Its **Tamkeen Real Estate Programme** set a goal of 500 placements a year and has been reaching more than **800**. The first group on DLD's Real Estate Diploma has **103 students**, far above the target of 40.

## How to apply

The employment day itself is over, and no single official online application page was published for it. Emiratis who missed it should:

1. Register on the **Nafis** platform and keep the profile updated.
2. Follow DLD's official channels for the next Real Estate Day and Tamkeen Real Estate intake.
3. Apply directly on the careers pages of large developers such as Emaar and Danube.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Property is hiring nationals.** With strong Emiratisation targets, real estate firms are actively seeking UAE talent.
- **You don't need sales experience.** Accounting, valuation and management roles were all on the list.
- **Get licensed.** A RERA broker qualification or the DLD diploma makes you far more attractive.
- **Students can start early** with part-time roles while studying.
