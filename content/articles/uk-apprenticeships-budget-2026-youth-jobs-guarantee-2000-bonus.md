**UK apprenticeships** and youth jobs were a key theme of the **Budget on 28 October 2026**. The Chancellor announced new cash for employers who hire young apprentices, a wider jobs guarantee for young people on benefits, and rules that will force big public building projects to train apprentices.

For school leavers and young adults, that should mean more firms willing to take on beginners in 2027 and 2028.

## The main measures

| Measure | What it means |
|---|---|
| Jobs Guarantee | Extended to 22–24-year-olds on Universal Credit |
| Youth Jobs Grant | £3,000 for businesses hiring eligible young unemployed people (confirmed) |
| Apprentice incentive | £2,000 per young apprentice for non-levy employers |
| Public projects | At least 3% of the workforce on large government construction and infrastructure schemes must be apprentices |
| Apprenticeship brokerage | £100m over 2027/28–2028/29, pilots grow from 7 to 14 mayoral areas |
| Union Learning Fund | Returns at £15m a year from 2027/28 |
| Plymouth | £3m to help young people not in work or study into defence jobs |

## Why it matters

The **£2,000 payment** targets small and medium-sized firms, which take on most apprentices but often say the cost of training a young starter is a barrier. The **3% quota** on major projects means roads, rail, energy and housing schemes funded by the state will need to recruit and train new people, especially in construction trades.

The **brokerage pilots** help match young people with local employers offering apprenticeships, and will now run in twice as many city regions.

## How to apply

In England, apprenticeships are advertised on the government's official service: [Find an apprenticeship](https://www.gov.uk/apply-apprenticeship). Create an account, set alerts for your area and trade, and apply directly. Young people on Universal Credit should ask their Jobcentre work coach about the Jobs Guarantee.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Construction trades will be in demand.** Look at bricklaying, electrical, plumbing and civil engineering apprenticeships.
- **Small employers may be more willing to hire,** thanks to the new £2,000 payment.
- **Aged 22–24 and on Universal Credit?** You now qualify for Jobs Guarantee support.
- **Plymouth residents** should watch for defence-sector training routes.
