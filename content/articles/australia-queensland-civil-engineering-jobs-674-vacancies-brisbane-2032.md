If you are a civil engineer thinking about a move, Queensland is where the work is. The state advertised **674 civil engineering vacancies in August 2026**, almost **30% more** than a year earlier and the **highest in Australia**, according to the federal **Internet Vacancy Index**. The surge is driven by building work for the **Brisbane 2032 Olympic and Paralympic Games** and a wider infrastructure boom; most jobs are advertised by contractors, consultancies and government agencies.

## How many engineering jobs are there?

| State | Civil engineering vacancies (August 2026) |
|---|---|
| Queensland | 674 |
| New South Wales | 639 |
| Victoria | 369 |

Queensland topped the table even though NSW has a much larger population. The figures come from Jobs and Skills Australia data, highlighted by **Engineers Australia**.

## Which roles are in demand?

The civil engineering group covers more than 20 professional occupations. Fields named in the data include:

- Geotechnical engineers
- Rail engineers
- Structural engineers
- Transport engineers
- Water engineers

Typical related openings on big projects also include site engineers, project engineers, design engineers and graduate engineers, though these were not counted separately.

## What is driving the boom?

The Queensland Major Contractors Association says the state's major infrastructure pipeline has reached **$139.5 billion**. Games-linked work alone is estimated at around **$11 billion**, including a proposed **63,000-seat stadium at Victoria Park**, the **National Aquatic Centre** and upgrades at the **Chandler Sports Precinct**. Hospitals, schools, roads and homes are also on the list. Late last year the Queensland Audit Office said the state could be short of about **50,000 workers** in 2026-27 as construction ramps up, and a fresh audit report is expected soon.

Engineers Australia chief engineer **Katherine Richards** described Queensland as the place to be for engineers right now, especially those willing to relocate. She also noted that close to **70,000 engineers** are set to retire nationally over 15 years, which creates room for young graduates.

## What salaries do civil engineers earn?

No pay figures were published with the vacancy data. As a general guide, graduate civil engineers in Australia often start on salaries in the $70,000s to $80,000s, and experienced project and structural engineers can earn well into six figures. Strong demand usually pushes offers up, so candidates have room to negotiate.

## Who can apply?

Most roles ask for an engineering degree or diploma and, for senior posts, experience on major projects. Overseas engineers usually need a skills assessment from Engineers Australia and a valid visa; some employers sponsor hard-to-fill roles.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Target Brisbane and South East Queensland**, where Games and transport projects are clustered.
- **Rail, structural and water skills** are especially useful.
- **Graduates** should apply to big contractors' graduate programmes early.
- **Overseas engineers:** start the skills assessment before job hunting.
