Saudi Arabia's **Qiwa** labour platform, which belongs to the **Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)**, groups private-sector employers into **seven size categories** based purely on headcount. The breakdown was reported by the Saudi daily **Okaz** on **21 October 2026**.

## The 7 categories

| Category | Number of workers |
|---|---|
| Small A | 5 or fewer |
| Small B | 6 to 49 |
| Medium A | 50 to 99 |
| Medium B | 100 to 199 |
| Medium C | 200 to 499 |
| Large | 500 to 2,999 |
| Giant | 3,000 or more |

You can see your employer's details and its classification on Qiwa.

## Why it matters

### Nitaqat Saudization targets

Under **Nitaqat**, Saudi Arabia's Saudization programme, the share of Saudi staff a company must reach depends on several factors, including its **economic activity**, its **size** and how many Saudis it already employs. A company's Nitaqat status then affects services such as issuing and renewing work visas.

Read more: [Saudization 2026 and the 340,000-job Nitaqat target](/saudi-arabia/saudi-saudization-marketing-sales-60-percent-nitaqat-340000-jobs/).

### Training duties for firms with 50+ staff

A ministerial decision from **February 2026** requires employers with **50 or more workers** to offer on-the-job training places to **Saudi graduates and job seekers** equal to at least **2% of their workforce** each year, subject to conditions.

## Qiwa vs Monsha'at

Don't confuse Qiwa's bands with the categories used by **Monsha'at**, the Small and Medium Enterprises authority. Monsha'at looks at both **full-time staff** and **annual revenue**:

| Monsha'at category | Full-time staff | Annual revenue |
|---|---|---|
| Micro | 1 to 5 | Up to SAR 3 million |
| Small | 6 to 49 | Over SAR 3 million, up to SAR 40 million |
| Medium | 50 to 249 | Over SAR 40 million, up to SAR 200 million |
| Large | 250 or more | Or revenue over SAR 200 million |

When staff numbers and revenue point to different categories, Monsha'at uses the higher one.

## What it means for expat workers

- **Check your employer's Nitaqat status** on Qiwa. A company in a low Nitaqat band may struggle to renew visas, which can affect your residency.
- **Bigger employers face more Saudization pressure** in many sectors, so expats in roles being localised should keep their skills and certificates current.
- Before accepting a Saudi job offer, ask about the company's size and Saudization standing.
