**The Uline Reno hiring event takes place on Thursday 12 November 2026, from 4 p.m. to 6 p.m., at the company's branch at 8800 Military Road in Reno, Nevada.** Uline is looking for full-time warehouse associates for day and night shifts, with starting pay between $27 and $34 an hour. You can register ahead or walk in, and you can also apply online at [uline.jobs](https://www.uline.jobs/).

## What is the Uline Reno hiring event?

Uline sells shipping, packaging and industrial supplies and runs a large distribution branch in north Reno. According to a [report by KTVN 2 News Nevada](https://www.2news.com/news/local/uline-hosting-reno-hiring-event-for-full-time-warehouse-careers/article_1f2da892-0565-4891-814e-0b200ddd3702.html), the company is holding a two-hour session this week so local people can meet the team and be considered for warehouse work.

Uline's own [locations page](https://www.uline.com/Corporate/About_Locations) confirms the Reno address and currently marks the branch as "Now Hiring".

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Uline |
| Date | Thursday 12 November 2026 |
| Time | 4 p.m. – 6 p.m. |
| Place | 8800 Military Road, Reno, NV 89506 |
| Roles | Warehouse associates, day and night shift |
| Contract | Full-time, year-round |
| Entry | Registration encouraged; walk-ins welcome |

The company has not said how many people it plans to hire.

## What is the pay?

Starting rates depend on the shift and the exact position:

| Shift | Starting pay per hour |
|---|---|
| Day shift | $27 – $32 |
| Night shift | $29 – $34 |

Night work pays about $2 an hour more than days at both ends of the range.

## What benefits come with the job?

The benefits Uline listed for this hiring round include:

- health coverage
- a 401(k) retirement plan with a 6% company match that begins on your first day
- bonus programs
- profit-sharing for eligible staff once they have three years of service
- tuition assistance if you keep studying
- a fitness center and a café on site

## Are these holiday jobs?

No. Many employers take on short-term staff in November and December, but Uline says these posts are permanent. Nevada branch manager Brian Wayer said the company is looking for people who want a lasting career rather than a few weeks of seasonal work.

Uline also says its employees stay for seven years on average and that there is room to move up.

## Who can apply?

No list of requirements was published with the announcement. For warehouse associate work in the United States, employers normally expect you to be an adult with the legal right to work in the country, and to be able to stand, walk and lift for a full shift. Check the job advert on the careers site for the exact conditions.

One practical point: Uline says translation services will not be offered at the event, so be ready to speak with recruiters in English.

## How do I apply?

1. **Go in person:** arrive at 8800 Military Road between 4 p.m. and 6 p.m. on Thursday 12 November. Registering first is recommended, but you will not be turned away without it.
2. **Apply online:** look for Reno warehouse openings on [uline.jobs](https://www.uline.jobs/), the company's careers site, and send your application there.

Take a current résumé and photo identification. You should never be asked to pay to attend or to apply.

## What it means for job seekers

- This is a short event: only two hours on one evening. If you cannot make it, apply through the careers site instead.
- A starting wage of $27 or more is high for entry-level warehouse work, so expect plenty of interest. Arrive early.
- Decide before you go whether day or night shift suits you; recruiters are filling both.
- The 401(k) match from day one and tuition support add real value on top of the hourly rate.
- These are local, on-site jobs in Reno. Nothing in the announcement mentions visa sponsorship or relocation help.
