Drone delivery is bringing new **Fort Worth jobs**. **Wing Aviation**, the delivery-drone firm that flies for Walmart, will invest **$125 million** over two years in a factory in **Fort Worth, Texas**, creating **up to 100 manufacturing jobs**, the company announced on 6 November 2026. Openings are posted on Wing's official careers page.

## What is Wing building?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Wing Aviation LLC |
| Location | Fort Worth, Texas |
| Investment | $125 million over two years |
| Facility | 15,000 sq ft |
| Jobs | Up to 100 manufacturing roles |
| Output | Tens of thousands of drones a year |

The site will build Wing's **next-generation** delivery aircraft.

## Why Fort Worth?

Wing started delivering in North Texas in **June 2024**, from a Walmart on North Beach Street, and now flies from **19 local Walmart stores**. Chief executive **Adam Woodworth** said the US is Wing's main growth market.

## How fast is Wing growing?

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Walmart stores served now | About 70 |
| Planned Walmart stores | More than 270 |
| Home deliveries this year | Over 1 million |
| New markets | San Diego, Memphis, New Orleans, Philadelphia, Phoenix, Salt Lake City, San Francisco Bay Area |

Wing aims to reach **40 million US residents** with fast drone delivery.

## Which jobs are likely?

A drone factory typically hires:

- Assembly and production technicians
- Electronics and avionics technicians
- Quality inspectors
- Manufacturing and test engineers
- Supply chain and warehouse staff

Wing has not published a breakdown yet, so treat this list as a guide only.

## How do I apply?

Check Wing's official **[careers page](https://wing.com/careers)** for Fort Worth roles.
