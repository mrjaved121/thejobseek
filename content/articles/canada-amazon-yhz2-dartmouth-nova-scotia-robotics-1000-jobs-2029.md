Amazon will need **more than 1,000 full-time workers** for a new robotics fulfilment centre in **Dartmouth, Nova Scotia**. Construction of the site, called **YHZ2**, began with a groundbreaking on 4 November, and Amazon says it should open in **2029**. Recruitment will run through Amazon's official Canadian hiring website as the opening date gets closer.

## What is Amazon building in Nova Scotia?

YHZ2 is the company's first robotics fulfilment centre in the province. It will cover more than **2.2 million square feet** in the Halifax region and use Amazon's "Generation 14" robotics, its newest warehouse technology. In this design, robots bring shelves of goods to staff at fixed stations, so workers walk less and lift less than in an older warehouse.

Nova Scotia Premier Tim Houston and Halifax Mayor Andy Fillmore attended the groundbreaking. Amazon also gave C$50,000 to Upward Mobility Kitchens, a local social enterprise, to pay for 10,000 meals.

## What is the pay and what are the benefits?

Amazon lifted its average base wage for Canadian operations staff by C$1 to **C$26.27 an hour** in September 2026. Amazon Canada's head of economic development, Brooks Barnett, told reporters that Dartmouth wages would start at about **C$26**. Full-time staff get health, dental and vision cover plus a Registered Retirement Savings Plan with a company match, all from day one.

## Which roles will be available?

Amazon has not yet listed job titles. Robotics fulfilment centres typically hire:

- **Fulfilment associates** for picking, packing and stowing
- **Robotics and maintenance technicians**
- **Area managers** and operations leaders
- **Safety, HR and IT** staff

These are typical roles for this type of site, not a confirmed list.

## Will it be hard to fill 1,000 jobs?

Atlantic Canada's labour market is tight. Statistics Canada data show transportation and warehousing vacancies were up almost 30% year on year in August 2026, and Nova Scotia's overall vacancy rate was 3.0%. Amazon says it is confident it will find the staff it needs, which should give local candidates bargaining power.

## How do I apply?

No Dartmouth adverts are live yet because the building is still at an early stage. Create a profile on Amazon's official Canadian hiring site and set job alerts for Nova Scotia. Never pay anyone for an Amazon job offer.
