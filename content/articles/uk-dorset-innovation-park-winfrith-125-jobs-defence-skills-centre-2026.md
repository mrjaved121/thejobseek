Defence and engineering are growing **Dorset jobs**. **Dorset Innovation Park** at the former nuclear site in **Winfrith** has added **125 full-time equivalent jobs**, and it plans an **Advanced Engineering and Defence Skills Centre** that would offer apprenticeships and technical training, the Dorset Echo reported on 7 November 2026.

## What has changed at the park?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Site | Dorset Innovation Park, Winfrith (former nuclear site) |
| Owner | Dorset Council |
| Operator | Dorset Innovation Park Ltd (full management since July 2026) |
| New jobs | 125 full-time equivalent |
| Savings | More than £500,000 of operational efficiencies |

Most of the new jobs came from **firms already on site growing**, with help from the operator and the council. Councillor **Richard Biggs** said rising employment shows the work is paying off.

## What is the proposed skills centre?

A partnership between **Yeovil College** and **Dorset Council**, the centre would provide:

- Full-time technical education
- Apprenticeships
- Adult training
- Courses designed with employers

The college wants to put roughly **£2.4 million** of its Defence Technical Excellence College capital into the project, with a further **£800,000** for specialist kit. Both sums are still waiting for sign-off by the **Ministry of Defence** and the **Department for Education**.

## What else is planned?

- **Cyber security:** Bournemouth University's Security Operations Centre of the Future, at the BattleLab facility.
- **Drones and autonomy:** a bid for a £20 million Global Autonomy Cluster led by Plymouth University, possibly with a drone corridor to Portland.
- **Defence prospectus:** due by the end of 2026. The county's defence sector is already worth £437 million.

## Which skills will be in demand?

Parks focused on defence and autonomy typically recruit:

- Mechanical and electronics engineers
- Software and cyber security specialists
- Drone and autonomous systems technicians
- Engineering apprentices
- Test and trials staff

These are typical for the sector, not a list of open jobs.

## How do I find jobs here?

Look at the careers pages of companies based at the park, and ask Yeovil College about engineering apprenticeships and courses.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Defence tech is hiring** in the south-west.
- **Apprenticeships may grow** if the skills centre is approved.
- **Cyber and drone skills** are increasingly valuable.
