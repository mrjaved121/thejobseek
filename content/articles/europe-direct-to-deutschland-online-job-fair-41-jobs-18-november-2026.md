Looking for **jobs in Germany for foreigners**? The EU's job mobility network **EURES** is hosting an online recruitment event called **"Direct to Deutschland"** on **18 November 2026**. **20 employers** will offer **41 permanent jobs** in Germany, and registration is already open.

Because it is online, you can take part from anywhere with an internet connection.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Event | Direct to Deutschland (European Job Days) |
| Date | 18 November 2026 |
| Main programme | 13:00–16:00 CET |
| Format | Online |
| Employers | 20 |
| Jobs | 41 permanent positions |
| Eligibility | EU / EEA job seekers |
| Cost | Free |

## Which jobs are on offer

The employers are recruiting in these areas:

- **Manufacturing**
- **IT, engineering and life sciences**
- **Sales**
- **HR and purchasing**
- **Healthcare and finance**
- **Truck and bus driving**
- **Skilled trades**
- **Logistics**

All jobs are permanent professional placements in Germany. Exact job titles and salaries are shown in the job listings on the event page.

## Help with moving to Germany

The programme includes guidance from Germany's **Federal Employment Agency** (Bundesagentur für Arbeit) and **GVP**, the German association of recruitment and staffing firms. Expect advice on job searching, recognition of qualifications and settling in.

## Who can take part

The event is listed as **EU/EEA only**. That makes it ideal for EU citizens who want to move to Germany and do not need a work visa. Non-EU job seekers should look at Germany's own official channels, such as the Make it in Germany portal, instead.

## How to apply

1. Go to the official [Direct to Deutschland event page](https://europeanjobdays.eu/en/event/direct-deutschland).
2. Register as a job seeker and create your profile.
3. Upload a CV, ideally in German or English.
4. Apply to the jobs listed and book online interviews where offered.
5. Join the live programme on 18 November.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Register early.** Employers often screen CVs before the event and pick who to interview.
- **German helps.** Even basic German (A2–B1) improves your chances, especially in healthcare and driving jobs.
- **Get your papers ready.** Drivers need their licence and driver qualification card; nurses need to start recognition of their qualifications.
- **Use a Europass CV.** It is easy for German employers to read.
