The Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) recruits for a large number of government posts in Punjab, from lecturers and doctors to assistants and inspectors. Applications are made online through the official PPSC website. This guide walks you through the process.

## Before you start

Keep these ready:

- Your CNIC (and its number)
- A recent passport-size photograph in digital form
- Educational certificates and degrees
- Domicile certificate
- An active mobile number and email address

## Step 1: Read the advertisement carefully

Every PPSC advertisement lists the post, number of vacancies, age limit, required qualification, quota and last date. Check that you meet **all** eligibility conditions before applying. Read age relaxation rules and the closing date in the official advertisement.

## Step 2: Create your account on the PPSC website

Go to the official PPSC website and register with your CNIC number, email and mobile number. Use details that match your CNIC exactly.

## Step 3: Fill in the online application

Select the advertised post using its case number, then enter your personal details, qualification and experience accurately. Mistakes here can lead to rejection later during document verification.

## Step 4: Pay the application fee

Pay the fee using the method given in the advertisement and keep the payment receipt. Your application is generally not complete until the fee is paid as instructed.

## Step 5: Submit and save everything

Submit the application before the last date and download or print a copy. Save your login details, application number and fee receipt in a safe place.

## After applying

- **Test or interview:** depending on the post, PPSC holds a written test, interview, or both. Schedules are announced on the official website.
- **Admit card:** download it from your PPSC account when it is released.
- **Results:** published on the official website.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Applying at the last minute when the website is busy
- Entering marks or dates that do not match certificates
- Missing the fee payment
- Relying on unofficial pages for dates and results
