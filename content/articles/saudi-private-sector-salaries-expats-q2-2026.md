Saudi Arabia's private sector kept growing in the year to June 2026, but the pay gap between Saudi and foreign workers remains very wide. The **National Labor Observatory's** report for the **second quarter of 2026**, reported by EnterpriseAM on **14 October 2026**, shows non-Saudis earn **about SAR 2,700 a month** on average, against **about SAR 9,500** for Saudis.

## Saudi private sector at a glance (end of Q2 2026)

| Measure | Figure | Change on a year earlier |
|---|---|---|
| Total private sector workers | 13.6 million | +7% |
| Non-Saudi workers | 10.9 million (80.2%) | +6.8% |
| Saudi workers | 2.689 million (19.8%) | +8.2% |
| Non-Saudi women | 523,000 | +16.7% |
| Average monthly pay, non-Saudis | about SAR 2,700 | not stated |
| Average monthly pay, Saudis | about SAR 9,500 | +5.4% |

**87.7%** of non-Saudi workers earn **under SAR 4,000** a month. Average pay is higher at bigger companies, from about SAR 5,400 at micro firms to about SAR 12,100 at large firms (all workers).

## Where the jobs are growing

| Sector | Growth in workers, year on year |
|---|---|
| Real estate | +29.3% |
| ICT | +28.4% |
| Health and social work | +0.3% |

Micro-businesses added the most jobs, up **14.1%** to almost **4.8 million** workers. Large firms grew only **2.9%**. Riyadh has **6.2 million** private sector workers, the most of any region.

## Where Saudization is strongest

Saudis now fill **51% of high-skill jobs**, up from 47% in Q2 2023, but only **23% of mid-skill jobs**. They hold **92% of clerical** and **64% of managerial** posts. By sector, Saudization is highest in finance and insurance (84%), mining and quarrying (70%) and ICT (59%).

## What it means for job seekers

- **The market is still growing for expats.** The number of non-Saudi workers rose by 6.8% in a year, even though a separate official count showed a small drop between March and June. We covered that in our report on the [Saudi expat workforce in Q2 2026](/saudi-arabia/saudi-expat-workforce-shrinks-q2-2026/).
- **Check pay against the averages.** Most expats are in manual and elementary jobs, which pull the average down. Skilled roles pay well above SAR 2,700, so negotiate based on your profession, not the national average.
- **Office and finance jobs are mostly for Saudis.** Clerical, finance and many admin roles are heavily Saudized. Read which [69 admin jobs are now reserved for Saudis](/saudi-arabia/saudi-saudization-69-administrative-professions/).
- **Look at growth sectors.** Real estate and ICT added workers fastest.
