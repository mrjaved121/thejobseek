Good news for **New Zealand jobs** in banking. **Bank of New Zealand (BNZ)** chief executive **Dan Huggins** says the bank intends to keep expanding and expects to **add staff in New Zealand** as its customer base grows. BNZ added **112 full-time-equivalent (FTE) staff** in the year to September, taking its New Zealand workforce to **5,476**.

## Is BNZ following NAB's job cuts?

No, according to Huggins. Its Australian parent, **National Australia Bank (NAB)**, intends to shed as many as 2,500 Australian roles. Huggins said those changes relate only to NAB's Australian business and that BNZ is not planning comparable cuts or moves of work offshore in New Zealand, where market conditions are different.

## Which bank jobs are common at BNZ?

Large retail banks typically hire for:

- Customer service and branch roles
- Contact centre staff
- Home loan and business banking specialists
- Risk, compliance and fraud teams
- Technology, data and digital roles

These are typical banking roles, not a confirmed vacancy list.
