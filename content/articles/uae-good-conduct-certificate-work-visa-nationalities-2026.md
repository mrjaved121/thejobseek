Some foreign workers now need a **certificate of good conduct** (police clearance) to get a **new UAE employment visa**. The **Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP)** lists it as a required document for a work entry permit, but only "according to the announced categories". It is **not** a rule for every nationality.

The clearest public list comes from the **Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ)**, which relayed an ICP update on **17 July 2026**.

## The rule at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Authority | ICP (federal) |
| Applies to | New employment and investor visas |
| Nationalities covered | 24, according to RAKEZ |
| Latest additions | 10 countries, from 20 July 2026 |
| Outside the UAE or on a tourist visa | Home-country certificate, attested by the UAE embassy |
| In the UAE with a cancelled visa | Certificate from UAE police |

## Which nationalities are covered

| Added from 20 July 2026 | Already covered |
|---|---|
| Ghana, Lebanon, Lithuania, Morocco, Mozambique, Pakistan, Senegal, Syria, Tonga, Zambia | Afghanistan, Algeria, Bhutan, Bulgaria, Cameroon, Cuba, Egypt, Iraq, Kenya, Mexico, Nepal, Nigeria, Sri Lanka, Uganda |

Some business-setup and agent websites publish longer lists, with India, Bangladesh, the Philippines and China, but no government source has confirmed these. Ghana's foreign ministry said in August that the rule came from a UAE Cabinet decision, but there has been no public UAE statement listing nationalities.

**Ghana's dates:** according to Ghana's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, as reported by Khaleej Times on 5 August 2026, the requirement applied first to Ghanaians applying for UAE employment visas from Ghana (from **30 March 2026**), then to all Ghanaian workers coming to the UAE (from **20 July 2026**). Ghanaians already in the UAE on a visit visa who want to switch to an employment visa were given **45 days** to meet the new requirement.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Start early.** Getting a police certificate and embassy attestation at home can take weeks. If your country is on the list, apply as soon as you get a job offer.
- **Ask your employer or free zone.** They submit the visa and can see the current ICP requirements for your nationality.
- **Renewals and job changes** have generally been reported as not affected, but this has not been officially confirmed.
- **Don't buy a "certificate".** Only your home police authority or UAE police can issue one. A fake certificate can get you banned.
