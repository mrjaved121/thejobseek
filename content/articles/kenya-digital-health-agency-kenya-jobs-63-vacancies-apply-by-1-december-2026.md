**Digital Health Agency Kenya jobs are open: the state agency has advertised 63 vacancies across 50 positions, and applications close at 5pm on Tuesday 1 December 2026.** The posts run from director level down to drivers and office assistants. Candidates apply online through the [DHA careers page](https://dha.go.ke/careers).

## Who is hiring?

The Digital Health Agency (DHA) was set up under the Digital Health Act No. 15 of 2023. Its job is to run Kenya's national health information system and to act as custodian of the country's health data. [The Star](https://www.the-star.co.ke/news/2026-11-10-digital-health-agency-advertises-63-vacancies) reports that the recruitment is intended to build up the agency's own staff so it can deliver digital health services better.

## Which roles are open?

The advert covers corporate services, digital health informatics, cyber security, legal, finance, human resources, supply chain and ICT. The table groups the titles reported by The Star by level.

| Level | Positions advertised |
|---|---|
| Director | Director of Corporate Services |
| Deputy Director | Digital Health Informatics; Standards and Compliance; Certification; Enterprise Infrastructure; Cyber Security; Point of Care Services; Capacity Building; Health Information Management; HR Management and Administration; Internal Audit; Corporate Communications; Planning and Resource Mobilisation; Systems Support and Maintenance |
| Assistant Director | Administration; HR Management and Development |
| Principal Officer | Supply Chain Management; Internal Audit; Research and Innovation; Legal Affairs; Digital Health; Finance; Corporate Communications |
| Senior Officer | Senior Digital Health Officer; Senior ICT Officer; Senior Supply Chain Management Officer; Senior Planning Officer; Senior Accountant |
| Officer | ICT Officer I; Finance Officer I; Accountant I; Digital Health Officer I; Assistant Supply Chain Management Officer I; HR Management and Development Officer I; Legal Officer II |
| Support | Digital Health Assistant; Customer Care Assistant; Senior Driver; Driver; Office Assistant |

Some titles have more than one opening, which is how 50 positions add up to 63 vacancies. The number of posts per title, plus the qualifications and years of experience for each, are in the detailed job descriptions.

## Who can apply?

The agency asked for applications from qualified, competent and results-driven Kenyans. It describes itself as an equal opportunity employer and says people with disabilities and applicants from marginalised and minority communities are encouraged to apply.

Two warnings from the advert matter. Canvassing leads to automatic disqualification, and only shortlisted candidates will hear back.

## Which documents do I need?

| Document | Issued by |
|---|---|
| Curriculum vitae and application letter | You |
| National identity card | Government of Kenya |
| Academic and professional certificates, testimonials | Your institutions and employers |
| Certificate of Good Conduct | Directorate of Criminal Investigations |
| Tax Compliance Certificate | Kenya Revenue Authority |
| Clearance certificate | Higher Education Loans Board |
| Clearance | Ethics and Anti-Corruption Commission |
| Credit report | An approved Credit Reference Bureau |

The last five items show compliance with Chapter Six of the Constitution, which deals with leadership and integrity. Several of them take days to arrive, so request them now.

## How do I apply?

1. Go to the careers page at [dha.go.ke/careers](https://dha.go.ke/careers).
2. Open the job description for the post you want and note its title and reference.
3. Address your application letter to the Chief Executive Officer, Digital Health Agency, P.O. Box 29409–00100, Nairobi.
4. Upload the letter, CV and all the documents listed above.
5. Submit before 5pm on 1 December 2026.

One practical note: when we checked the careers page on 11 November, it said the newly gazetted vacancies were still being uploaded, with full role details and application links to follow shortly. If the links are not there yet, check again over the next few days instead of sending documents to unofficial sites. The advert does not mention any application fee, so treat requests for payment as a red flag.

## What it means for job seekers

- This is a rare chance for ICT, cyber security and health informatics specialists to join a young public agency while its structure is still being filled.
- It is not only for tech staff. Accountants, lawyers, HR officers, supply chain officers, auditors and communications staff are all wanted.
- Entry and support posts such as Digital Health Assistant, Customer Care Assistant, Driver and Office Assistant suit people early in their careers.
- With about three weeks to the deadline, the clearance certificates are the part most likely to hold you up. Start there.
- Apply for the one role that best fits your record. A focused application reads better than several loosely matched ones.
