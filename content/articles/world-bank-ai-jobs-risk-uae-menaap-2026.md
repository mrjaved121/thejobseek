The **World Bank** says the **UAE and Jordan** have the largest share of jobs at risk of being replaced by artificial intelligence in the region. The finding comes from its **October 2026 Economic Update** for the Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan and Pakistan (MENAAP), as reported by Khaleej Times on 11 October.

## The key findings

| Finding | Figure |
|---|---|
| Jobs at risk of AI substitution, region-wide | **No more than about 10%** |
| Jobs that could become more productive with AI | **13% to 20%** |
| Countries with the largest share at risk | **UAE and Jordan** |
| Countries with the smallest share at risk | **Afghanistan and Pakistan** |

The bank says richer economies are more exposed because more of their jobs are in **cognitive, office-based work** that generative AI handles well. It also warns that the productivity gains are **"far from guaranteed"**.

## AI is already widely used in the UAE

- In the UAE, AI use is higher than average in **9 out of 22 job groups**, including **management, sales, arts and media**, and some food preparation and personal care roles.
- The value of work done or assisted by AI tools, relative to the size of the economy, is highest in the **Gulf**. The UAE's level is close to Germany's and the UK's.

To estimate AI use, the bank analysed a published sample of **one million conversations with Anthropic's Claude AI assistant**. It notes that these users may be more educated than the general population, which could overstate the gains.

## Since ChatGPT launched

Compared with the least exposed skills, jobs that rely most on AI-replaceable skills have:

- lost **9.8%** of their value, and
- seen **12.1% fewer** new workers entering them.

## What it means for workers in the UAE

- **Most jobs are not at risk of being fully replaced.** The bigger change is in *how* work is done.
- **Learning to use AI tools** in your field can make you more productive and more employable.
- Human skills still count. Communication, judgement and customer-facing work are harder to automate.
