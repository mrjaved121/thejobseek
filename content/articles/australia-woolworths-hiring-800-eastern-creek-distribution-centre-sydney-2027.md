**Woolworths has started hiring 800 people for its new chilled and fresh food distribution centre in Eastern Creek, Western Sydney, which is due to open in early 2027.** The A$900 million building will be the largest refrigerated distribution centre in the Southern Hemisphere, the company says. Jobs are advertised on the [Woolworths Group careers site](https://careers.woolworthsgroup.com.au/).

## What is Woolworths building?

The Sydney Chilled and Fresh Distribution Centre sits at Eastern Creek, close to where the M4 and M7 motorways cross. From there it will send fruit, vegetables, milk and other chilled food to around 300 Woolworths supermarkets in Sydney and regional New South Wales.

It will be operated by Primary Connect, the group's supply chain arm. The site has been designed with spare room so volumes can grow for the next 20 years.

## Which roles are open?

The announcement gives the total number of jobs but does not break it down by job title. It does say that **45 per cent of people hired so far are women**.

Large refrigerated warehouses normally recruit for:

- order pickers and store-loading staff
- forklift and reach-truck operators
- inventory and receiving clerks
- shift team leaders and operations managers
- maintenance and automation technicians
- safety, HR and admin staff

Treat this list as typical for this kind of site rather than a confirmed list from Woolworths. The centre uses a lot of automation, such as machines that pack milk crates, so technical skills will be useful.

## Who can apply?

No special eligibility rules were announced. As with most Australian warehouse work, you will need the right to work in Australia. A forklift licence and experience in cold storage or food handling often help. Chief executive Amanda Bardwell said the company sees these roles as the start of long careers, not short-term work.

## How do I apply?

1. Go to the [Woolworths Group careers website](https://careers.woolworthsgroup.com.au/).
2. Search for supply chain or Primary Connect jobs and filter by Eastern Creek or Western Sydney.
3. Create a profile and submit your application online.

Only apply through the official website. Woolworths does not charge any fee to apply.
