People looking for **UK jobs for foreigners** and local warehouse work in the Midlands have a big new employer to watch. **Marks & Spencer** has launched the first recruitment drive for its new **fashion distribution centre in Lichfield, Staffordshire**, with **more than 70 vacancies** already live, trade news site Retail Technology Innovation Hub reported on 29 October 2026. Logistics Manager also confirmed the plans.

The **437,000 sq ft** site will be highly automated and is due to open in **2027**. When it is running at full strength, it is expected to employ about **600 people**.

## Which roles are open first

M&S says the first phase is about building the leadership and technical team before the site goes live. Roles include:

- **Planners** to organise stock flows and labour
- **Team managers** and shift leaders
- **Technicians** to keep the automated systems running
- Other **operational and specialist** posts

M&S says more roles will be advertised over the coming months as the opening gets closer. Salaries were not published; adverts on the M&S site mention competitive pay and benefits.

## A new food store too

M&S is also opening a **15,800 sq ft food store** on St John's Grange, Baker Way, in Lichfield next March. It will create about **70 local jobs**, such as customer assistants and shift leads.

## About the right to work

These are UK-based jobs, and most warehouse and store roles do not come with visa sponsorship. Applicants need the right to work in the UK, which includes people on many family, graduate and other visas. Technical and management posts may be different, so read each advert and ask the recruiter if sponsorship is needed.

## How to apply

Search for "Lichfield" on the official [M&S careers job search](https://jobs.marksandspencer.com/job-search) and set up job alerts. Some early adverts have short closing dates, so apply as soon as a matching role appears.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Automation needs skills.** Engineers and technicians with experience of conveyors, robotics or PLC systems are in a strong position.
- **Getting in early helps.** People hired before opening often train the later intake and move up faster.
- **Retail fans have two routes.** Choose between the distribution centre and the new food store.
- **Keep checking.** With 600 jobs planned and only 70 open now, many more roles are still to come.
