The holiday shopping season is creating plenty of **Canada hiring 2026** opportunities, and fashion brand **Aritzia** is one of the busiest recruiters. The Vancouver-based retailer has **more than 200 positions** open across the country, from shop-floor roles in its boutiques to jobs in offices, distribution centres and its concierge service. Both **full-time and seasonal** jobs are available.

## Openings at a glance

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Aritzia |
| Openings | 200+ |
| Job types | Full-time and seasonal |
| Workplaces | Boutiques, support offices, distribution centres, concierge |
| Cities | Vancouver, Richmond, Delta, Calgary, Toronto, Mississauga, Vaughan, Oshawa, Halifax |
| Perks | Benefits, career development, staff product discount |

## Which roles are open

Aritzia is recruiting in a wide range of areas, so you do not need a fashion background for every job:

- **Retail:** store associates and boutique managers
- **Supply chain:** distribution centre and logistics staff
- **Technology:** digital and tech positions at support offices
- **Finance and strategy:** office-based roles in accounting, planning and business strategy
- **Food and beverage:** hospitality roles such as chefs and baristas
- **Concierge:** client service roles helping shoppers online and by phone

## Pay and benefits

Aritzia says it offers **industry-leading pay** and full benefits, although it has not published salary figures. Staff also get career development support and a discount on the brand's clothing, which is a nice extra for retail workers.

## How to apply

Visit the official [Aritzia careers page](https://www.aritzia.com/en/aritzia/corporate-hub/careers/careers-landing-page), choose the type of role you want (retail, support office or distribution), and filter the live listings by city. Seasonal roles tend to fill quickly in November, so apply as soon as you see a match.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Seasonal can lead to permanent:** retailers often keep strong holiday staff on after January.
- **Show your customer skills:** for boutique roles, highlight any experience in sales, hospitality or client service.
- **Look beyond stores:** warehouse and office roles often offer more regular hours than the shop floor.
- **Newcomers:** if you are new to Canada, make sure your work permit allows the hours and type of job you apply for.
- **Be ready for group interviews:** fashion retailers often run them, so prepare a short introduction about yourself.
