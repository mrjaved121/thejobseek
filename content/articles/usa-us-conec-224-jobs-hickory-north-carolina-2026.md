Fibre optic parts maker **US Conec** will create **224 jobs** in **Hickory, North Carolina**, Governor Josh Stein announced on **22 October 2026**. The company is investing more than **$149.7 million** in a new **200,000 sq ft** facility in **Catawba County**.

The new jobs carry an **average salary of $56,982**, a little above the county average.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | US Conec Ltd. |
| Location | Hickory, Catawba County, NC |
| New jobs | 224 |
| Average salary | $56,982 |
| County average salary | $56,937 |
| Investment | More than $149.7 million |
| Building | 200,000 sq ft |
| Uses | Manufacturing, R&D, administration, distribution |
| Yearly payroll impact | More than $12.7 million |

## About US Conec

US Conec has been based in Hickory since **1992**. It designs and makes **high-density optical interconnects**, the small but critical connectors that join fibre optic cables. These parts are used in networks such as data centres, where more connections have to fit into less space.

## Jobs likely to be needed

The state did not publish a role list, but the four uses of the new building point to these areas:

| Area | Example roles |
|---|---|
| Manufacturing | Production operators, moulding and polishing technicians, machine setters |
| Quality | Inspectors, metrology and quality engineers |
| Research and development | Optical, mechanical and process engineers |
| Distribution | Warehouse staff, shipping and logistics coordinators |
| Administration | Finance, HR, IT and customer service |

## Training support

**Catawba Valley Community College** is named as a partner on the project. Community colleges in North Carolina often run free or low-cost training linked to new employers, so it is worth asking the college about advanced manufacturing courses.

## How to apply

North Carolina Commerce did not publish a hiring date or application link. Check US Conec's own website for its careers section and current openings, and watch for hiring events with the Catawba County Economic Development Corporation and the community college.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Pay is solid for the area.** An average near $57,000 is competitive for manufacturing jobs in Catawba County.
- **Precision skills are valued.** Attention to detail, clean-room habits and experience with small parts help.
- **Engineers have options.** R&D and process engineering roles are part of this expansion.
- **The grant is tied to hiring.** The state only pays once targets are met, which encourages the company to fill the roles.
