**Emirates is hiring pilots in Dubai: the airline's careers site is advertising First Officer, Accelerated Command and Direct Entry Captain jobs, with tax-free packages worth AED 900,000 to AED 1,185,000 a year.** The adverts state no closing date. You apply online, for free, at [emiratesgroupcareers.com/pilots](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/pilots/).

Gulf News reported on 23 November 2026 that the Emirates Group has over 120 vacancies across flying, engineering, IT and airport roles. When we checked the official site on 24 November 2026, it showed 127 open jobs. This article covers only the pilot roles, using the airline's own job adverts.

## Which pilot jobs are open?

All three experienced-pilot roles are based in Dubai. The figures below come from Emirates' own adverts and role pages, checked on 24 November 2026.

| Role | Minimum flying time | Licence and English | Total yearly package | Monthly take-home cash |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First Officer (B777, A350, A380) | 2,000 hours | ICAO ATPL, Class 1 medical, ICAO English 4+ | Up to AED 900,000 | AED 32,100 |
| First Officer – enhanced package | 4,000 hours (2,000 on jets over 50 tonnes) | ICAO English 5+ | AED 935,000 | AED 34,600 |
| Accelerated Command | 5,000 hours, 2,000 in command | ICAO ATPL, Class 1 medical, ICAO English 5+ | Up to AED 970,000 | AED 37,100 |
| Direct Entry Captain (A350/A380) | 7,000 hours, 3,000 as captain on Airbus FBW | ICAO ATPL, Class 1 medical, ICAO English 5+ | Up to AED 1,185,000 | AED 48,000 |

Monthly cash is Emirates' own figure for basic pay plus flying pay at an average of 85 hours. The total package also counts housing, schooling, insurance, leave tickets and the pension contribution, worked out for a pilot with a spouse and two children.

## Requirements: who can apply?

Each advert lists its own minimums. In plain terms:

**First Officer.** You need 2,000 hours on jets of 20 tonnes or more for the Boeing 777, or on jets of 50 tonnes or more for the A350 and A380. Training (P3), helicopter and piston time does not count. You also need 1,000 hours on the Boeing or Airbus fly-by-wire type you are applying for, you must be flying that type now, and you need 150 hours on it in the last 12 months.

**Frozen ATPL.** First Officer applicants with a frozen ATPL (a CPL plus passed ATPL theory exams) can be invited to assessment, but only if they agree to Emirates' terms for frozen-licence holders.

**Accelerated Command.** This is for experienced narrow-body captains. You need 5,000 hours in total, 2,000 hours in command on Boeing or Airbus fly-by-wire jets, and 1,000 of those in command on Airbus (the A220 does not count). You join as an Airbus First Officer on a higher package. After at least 700 hours in the right-hand seat and two recurrent training modules, you can be assessed for an upgrade to captain.

**Direct Entry Captain.** You need 7,000 hours and must be flying as an Airbus captain now. For the A380, Emirates wants 3,000 hours as pilot in command on Airbus wide-bodies (A330, A340, A350 or A380). For the A350, 3,000 hours in command on any Airbus fly-by-wire type is enough.

Your Class 1 medical must have no limitations other than glasses or lenses.

## Pay and benefits Emirates lists

The adverts promise company housing with utilities (a three or four-bedroom villa in an Emirates community), school fees of AED 52,250 a year for primary and AED 79,750 for secondary education for up to three children, and 42 days of annual leave. They also list medical, life and accident insurance, a share of profits when the company does well, and a provident fund. Emirates pays 12% of basic salary into that fund and the pilot pays 5%, starting after six months.

Captains get leave tickets in Business Class that can be upgraded to First. First Officers get leave tickets that can be upgraded to Business.

## How to apply for Emirates pilot jobs

1. Open the official pilots page at [emiratesgroupcareers.com/pilots](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/pilots/) and choose your role, or go straight to the adverts for [First Officer](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/search-and-apply/5), [Accelerated Command](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/search-and-apply/3) or [Direct Entry Captain](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/search-and-apply/186).
2. Check your logbook against the minimums above. Count only the hours each advert accepts.
3. Prepare scans of the four documents Emirates asks for: your CV, your passport, your ATPL showing the type rating you fly now (or your CPL and ATPL exam results if your ATPL is frozen), and your Class 1 medical certificate.
4. Press Apply, create a candidate account and upload the documents.
5. Watch your email for a reply from an @emirates.com address. Emirates also holds pilot roadshows: the pilots page lists dates in Johannesburg and Cape Town (1–5 December), London and Manchester (8–14 January), and later in Brussels, Macau and Taipei. You must register for these on the same page.

**Deadline:** no closing date is published on the pilot adverts. Re-check the advert on the careers site before you apply.

## Tips for applicants from Pakistan and India

**Licence and logbook first.** Emirates asks for an ICAO ATPL with a matching Class 1 medical from the same authority. Make sure your licence, type rating and medical are all valid on the day you apply.

**Attestation.** Degree or school certificates from home may need a UAE stamp. Per the [MOFA attestation service](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Services/Attestation), the ministry confirms seals and signatures on documents issued inside or outside the UAE, together with UAE missions. If Emirates asks for attested certificates after an offer, start early.

**Police clearance.** In India, a Police Clearance Certificate is requested on the [Passport Seva](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/) portal. In Pakistan, ask your provincial police service, and only get one if the employer asks for it.

**Medicals.** Your aviation Class 1 medical is part of the application. Any UAE residence-visa medical comes after an offer, and Emirates will tell you where to go.

**India: eMigrate, ECR and ECNR.** The eMigrate FAQ, checked on 24 November 2026, lists the UAE among 18 notified (ECR) countries. If your passport is marked ECR, you need emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants through [emigrate.gov.in](https://emigrate.gov.in) before you fly for the job. The FAQ ties this step to ECR passports, so look at your passport first to see whether it is ECR or ECNR.

**Pakistan: BE&OE and the Protector of Emigrants.** Pakistani workers taking a job abroad register with the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment, which the [Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis](https://ophrd.gov.pk/) lists as one of its departments. The bureau's own site, beoe.gov.pk, did not open from our checks on 24 November 2026, so confirm the current document list and fees there or at your nearest Protectorate office before booking a flight.

**Scam warning.** Emirates' [recruitment fraud page](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/recruitment-fraud/) says it never asks applicants for money, never asks for payment towards travel, visas or permits, and only posts jobs on its official careers site. Real emails come from @emirates.com or @dnata.com. Do not hand any agent money for an Emirates interview, job offer or visa: the real process is free.
