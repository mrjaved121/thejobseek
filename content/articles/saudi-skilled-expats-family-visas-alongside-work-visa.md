Saudi Arabia has made it easier for **highly skilled foreign workers** to move with their families. The **Ministry of Interior** announced on **6 October 2026** that eligible workers can now start their **family visa** process **while their own work visa is still being processed**.

The change was introduced together with the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs** and the **Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)**.

## What has changed

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| Workers had to finish their own work visa before starting the family visa process | **Family entry visas can be issued at the same time as the worker's employment visa** |

After the family arrives, their **residence permits** are processed under the normal rules.

## Who can come

| Family member | Eligible |
|---|---|
| Spouse | Yes |
| Sons | Under **25** |
| Daughters | **Unmarried** |

## Who qualifies

The rule applies to **highly skilled expatriate workers**. The announcement did **not publish a detailed definition** of "highly skilled", a list of occupations, fees or processing times.

Saudi Arabia already checks the qualifications of skilled workers before they arrive, focusing on managers, professionals and technicians. Read: [Saudi Professional Verification explained](/saudi-arabia/saudi-professional-verification-expat-qualifications-160-countries/).

## Why it matters

Moving alone first and waiting months for family visas has been one of the main hurdles for professionals considering Saudi jobs. The ministry says the change is meant to improve quality of life and simplify procedures for skilled expats.

## What to do if you have a Saudi job offer

1. **Ask your employer** whether your role counts as highly skilled under the new rule.
2. **Prepare family documents early**, including attested marriage and birth certificates.
3. Apply only through **official Saudi visa channels** and your employer.
