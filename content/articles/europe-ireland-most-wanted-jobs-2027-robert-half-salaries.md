Want to know which **jobs in Ireland** will be most sought after in 2027? Recruitment firm **Robert Half's 2027 Ireland Salary Guide** names **15 jobs** it expects employers to chase hardest, led by **AI engineers, data scientists and senior finance staff**, and finds many employers are paying **above their original salary budgets** to secure them.

## Which jobs are most in demand in Ireland?

| Area | Roles in high demand |
|---|---|
| Technology | AI engineers, data scientists, CTOs, lead developers, IT support managers |
| Finance | Financial controllers, finance business partners, financial modellers, financial planning analysts |
| HR and business support | HR directors and business partners, HR admins, executive assistants, office managers |

## Are salaries rising?

Overall pay growth is slowing, but employers are paying more for hard-to-fill roles:

- Almost half (**48%**) of surveyed hiring managers in Europe and the UK are paying **more than they first budgeted**, and most of them point to a lack of specialist skills.
- **36%** pay more to staff who can **use AI** to boost efficiency.
- **41%** of European and UK employers plan to raise wages next year to attract and keep staff.

The guide gives starting salaries for more than 140 roles. For example, a **finance business partner in Dublin** could start on **€84,500 to €99,000**.

## What does Robert Half say?

**Matt Weston**, who leads Robert Half in the UK and Ireland, said demand remains strong for specialist talent, especially people who help companies use AI, improve productivity and drive change. He added that employers are choosy about where they raise pay, but will go beyond budget when key skills are scarce.

## How can I use this to find a job?

- **Target the listed roles** if you have matching skills.
- **Show AI skills on your CV** with concrete examples of time or money saved.
- **Use salary benchmarks** when negotiating an offer.
- **Non-EU applicants** should check whether the role qualifies for an Irish employment permit.

## What it means for job seekers

- **AI and data skills command premium pay** in Ireland.
- **Finance and HR professionals** are also in strong demand, not just tech.
- **Employers will stretch budgets** for the right candidate, so negotiate.
