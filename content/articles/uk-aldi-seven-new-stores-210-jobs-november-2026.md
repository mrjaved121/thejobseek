Supermarket **Aldi UK** is opening **seven new stores in England in November 2026**, creating **up to 30 jobs at each site** and up to **210 jobs in total**, Retail Times reported on **26 October 2026**. Two of the stores are in London.

## Opening dates and locations

| Store | Area | Opening date | Jobs (up to) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basildon | Essex | 12 November | 30 |
| Newport | Isle of Wight | 19 November | 30 |
| Matlock | Derbyshire | 19 November | 30 |
| Stepney Green | East London | 19 November | 30 |
| Willesden | North-west London | 26 November | 30 |
| Orrell | Wigan | 26 November | 30 |
| Waterlooville | Hampshire | 26 November | 30 |

## Part of a bigger expansion

The new shops are part of a **£370 million investment** in Aldi's UK stores this year. The discounter is working towards a long-term target of **1,500 UK stores**.

Aldi is now Britain's fourth-largest supermarket, with a 10.5% share of the grocery market according to Worldpanel by Numerator. **Jonathan Neale**, Aldi UK's managing director of national real estate, said demand for new Aldi stores remains strong.

## Which jobs are on offer

Aldi has not published a breakdown, but a new Aldi store is normally staffed with:

- **Store assistants**, the largest group, who work on tills, stock shelves and keep the store clean
- **Team leaders** who run shifts
- **Assistant store managers** and **store managers**

Pay for these specific stores has not been stated, so check the hourly rate on each job advert.

## Why apply now

Stores opening in mid to late November will want staff hired and trained before the doors open. That means recruitment for these sites is likely to be happening right now. The roles are also permanent, unlike most Christmas temp jobs.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Apply early.** With openings from 12 November, interviews are likely to take place in the coming days.
- **Check Aldi's official UK careers site** and search by the store's town. Avoid third-party sites that ask for fees.
- **Be flexible on shifts.** Supermarkets need early-morning, evening and weekend workers, and flexibility makes your application stronger.
- **No experience needed** for many store assistant roles; reliability and customer service count most.
- **Consider nearby stores too.** If you miss these openings, Aldi says many more stores are planned.
