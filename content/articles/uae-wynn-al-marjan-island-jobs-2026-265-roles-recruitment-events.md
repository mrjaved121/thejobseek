**Wynn Al Marjan Island, the integrated resort opening in Ras Al Khaimah in 2027, had 265 open roles on its official careers site on 26 November 2026, and it is also running invitation-only recruitment events in Dubai and Abu Dhabi for restaurant, bar and kitchen staff.** No single closing date is published for the whole drive. You apply free of charge at [wynnalmarjanisland.com/careers/open-roles](https://wynnalmarjanisland.com/careers/open-roles).

According to the resort's job adverts, it will have 1,530 rooms and suites, 22 venues for food and drink, a theatre and a beach club. Most openings are in rooms, food and drink, and gaming.

## Who can apply? Requirements from the adverts

Requirements differ by job. These are the ones stated in the adverts we opened:

- **F&B Attendant:** at least one year of customer service in a busy five-star hotel or a similar restaurant is preferred; high school education or above; fluent English plus one more language; well groomed and physically fit.
- **Driver – Offsite Warehouse:** at least two years of professional driving, ideally in logistics or hospitality; high school diploma; a valid UAE light or heavy licence (Category 3 or 4), with refrigerated-truck experience preferred; a UAE forklift licence is a plus; conversational English.
- **Recruitment events:** "experienced" professionals, with roles at several levels; pre-screening answers decide who is invited.
- **Theatre cast:** the resort's entertainment page asks for strong multi-disciplinary dancers and performers with outstanding voices, for roles from their 20s to their 60s. The National, which reported the casting call on 26 November 2026, says it is aimed at performers based in the UAE.

One point to weigh before applying: every event advert states that the resort includes gaming operations and that candidates should be comfortable working in that setting.

## How do I apply?

1. Open [wynnalmarjanisland.com/careers/open-roles](https://wynnalmarjanisland.com/careers/open-roles) and search "Recruitment Event" or filter by department.
2. Read the full advert and check the experience, licence and language requirements.
3. Prepare an up-to-date CV in English, a passport copy and, for drivers, your UAE licence details. Chefs and bar staff should list each hotel or restaurant and its star rating.
4. Create a profile, apply and answer every pre-screening question honestly.
5. Wait for an invitation email with your interview slot. Do not go to any venue without one.
6. Performers apply differently: send a CV plus a headshot or showreel by email to casting@wynnalmarjanisland.com by 4 December 2026, as stated on the [Wynn Al Marjan Island entertainment page](https://wynnalmarjanisland.com/entertainment).

## Tips for applicants from Pakistan and India

**Where Wynn is recruiting abroad.** The events listed on 26 November were in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, plus overseas events for engineering and gaming staff in Kathmandu, Colombo, Manila and Batumi (Georgia). None was in Pakistan or India. You can still apply online for any listed role from home.

**Attestation.** A job offer may come with a request to attest your degree, diploma or trade certificate. The UAE foreign ministry handles this through its [attestation service](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Services/Attestation), which covers papers from your home country as well as UAE ones.

**Police clearance and medicals.** Both come after an offer: get a police certificate only if Wynn asks, and the UAE visa medical is arranged through the employer.

**India: check whether your passport is ECR.** India's Ministry of External Affairs runs [emigrate.gov.in](https://emigrate.gov.in). When we read it on 26 November 2026, it named the UAE among 18 destinations where an ECR (Emigration Check Required) passport needs emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants before you leave for work. A worker hired straight by a foreign company, as Wynn hires, fills in the emigrant registration form on the portal and uploads the documents. With an ECNR passport, registration is approved automatically, and joining the PBBY accident insurance is optional.

**Pakistan: register with BE&OE before travel.** Pakistani workers leaving for a foreign job go through the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment, a department of the [Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis](https://ophrd.gov.pk/), and its Protector of Emigrants offices. On 26 November 2026, beoe.gov.pk was stuck behind a bot-check screen for us, so we could not read its current rules. Ask the bureau or your nearest Protectorate office which papers and fees apply to a UAE hotel contract once you hold a written offer.

**Scam warning.** Wynn's own process is online and free. Never pay an agent for a Wynn job, an event invitation, an interview or a visa. More red flags are listed in our article on [fake UAE job offers](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/).
