The **Eastern Oklahoma VA Health Care System** is holding a **hiring fair in Tulsa on Friday 6 November 2026**, with **on-the-spot interviews for every open job type**. The event supports staffing at the new **James Mountain Inhofe VA Medical Center** in downtown Tulsa, Tulsa Today reported on **21 October 2026**.

## Event details

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Date | Friday 6 November 2026 |
| Time | 11am – 6pm (Central Time) |
| Venue | Lobby, James Mountain Inhofe VA Medical Center |
| Address | 400 S. Houston Ave., Tulsa, OK 74127 |
| What happens | Recruiters on site, interviews for all open occupations |

The medical center is in the **OSU Academic Medical District**, next to the new Oklahoma Psychiatric Care Center.

## Jobs on offer

The VA has listed a wide range of clinical and support roles, so there are openings for people with and without medical degrees:

**Clinical roles**
- Registered nurses (Emergency, ICU, Operating Room, Medical-Surgical) and RN assistant managers
- Physicians, hospitalists and a non-interventional cardiologist
- Clinical pharmacists and pharmacy technicians
- Diagnostic radiology technologists and a supervisory radiologic technologist
- Lab health technicians, EKG technicians and nursing assistants
- Senior social workers

**Support and admin roles**
- Medical support assistants, advanced medical support assistants and medical administration specialists
- Health benefits assistants, program support assistants and medical records technicians
- Accounting and budget technicians, education and training specialists
- Housekeeping aides, leads and supervisors
- Cooks and food service workers
- Medical supply and sterile processing technicians
- Transportation assistants and motor vehicle operators
- Police officers and security assistants

Health system specialists are also welcome to attend.

## How to apply

VA jobs are federal jobs, so the formal application is made online. Start at the official VA careers site, [vacareers.va.gov](https://www.vacareers.va.gov/), which links to current openings on USAJOBS. Read each vacancy announcement carefully: it lists the eligibility rules, qualifications and documents you need.

For questions about the event, the Eastern Oklahoma VA HR team can be reached on **(918) 577-3600**.

Note that attending the fair does not guarantee an interview or a job offer. Openings depend on approved vacancies.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Bring copies of your résumé** and any licences or certificates relevant to the role.
- **Check USAJOBS first** so you know which posts are open and can apply before or after meeting recruiters.
- **Veterans may get preference** in federal hiring, so bring proof of service if it applies to you.
- **Non-clinical workers count too.** Housekeeping, food service and admin roles are a good way into a stable federal job.

Healthcare remains one of the strongest areas of US hiring, as shown in the [September jobs report](/usa/us-jobs-report-september-2026-healthcare-hiring/).
