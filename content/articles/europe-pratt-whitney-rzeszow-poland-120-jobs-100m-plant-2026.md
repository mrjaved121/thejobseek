Poland's aerospace sector keeps growing, bringing new **jobs in Poland** for engineers and skilled machinists. US engine maker **Pratt & Whitney** has started building a **$100 million** facility in **Rzeszów**, south-east Poland, which will create **more than 120 jobs**. The work began on **23 October 2026**, and the plant is due to be up and running in **2028**.

## What the new plant will do

The facility will make **critical rotating engine parts** – the turbine and compressor disks that spin at very high speeds inside a jet engine. These parts are used in:

- **GTF engines**, which power Airbus A320neo and A220 aircraft
- **F135 engines**, used in the F-35 fighter jet
- **F100 engines**, used in fighter aircraft

Pratt & Whitney is also adding a new forging press at its Columbus Forge site in Georgia, USA. Together, the two projects should lift output of these critical parts by about **30%**.

## A major aerospace employer

Rzeszów is already one of Pratt & Whitney's key sites. It builds GTF gear systems, F100 parts and engines for helicopters, business jets and general aviation aircraft. Poland is **RTX's largest employee base outside the United States**, with **more than 9,500 people** working for Pratt & Whitney, Collins Aerospace and Raytheon.

Piotr Owsicki, who runs the Rzeszów site, said the start of construction marks the moment the plan becomes reality.

## Likely roles

The company has not listed the new positions yet. Precision engine-part production usually needs:

- CNC machinists and machine operators
- Manufacturing and process engineers
- Quality inspectors and metrology specialists
- Maintenance technicians
- Production planners and supervisors

There is no dedicated hiring page for the new plant yet. Search for Rzeszów openings on the official RTX and Pratt & Whitney careers websites, and set alerts for Poland as the 2028 start date gets closer.
