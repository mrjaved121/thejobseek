Romania has set a quota of **90,000 work and secondment permits for 2026** for workers from outside the EU, EEA and Switzerland. The limit was set by **Government Decision 1169/2025**, which took effect on 31 December 2025.

## Key facts

| | |
|---|---|
| 2026 quota | **90,000** new non-EU workers |
| 2025 quota | 100,000 |
| Permits issued by 15 July 2026 | 65,040 |
| Non-EU workers already living in Romania (end of 2025) | over 148,000 |

This is the first time since 2022 that the quota has dropped below 100,000. Romania's Labour Minister has said the number can be **revised during the year** depending on the job market.

## Which jobs are in demand

Employers mainly hire foreign workers for:

- **Construction** (builders, welders, especially near shipyards)
- **Industry and factories** (production workers, tailors in textiles)
- **Hotels, restaurants and cafés** (HoReCa)

Most foreign workers in Romania currently come from **Nepal, Sri Lanka and India**.

## New hiring system: WorkinRomania portal

Since **8 August 2026**, all hiring of non-EU workers goes through the official **WorkinRomania.gov.ro** portal, which links the immigration office, employment agency, visa system and tax authority.

What changed:

- Applications are fully **digital**.
- Workers must get a **bilingual employment contract**.
- When an agency is involved, a **three-way contract** is signed by the agency, the employer and the worker.
- Recruitment agencies are now **legally responsible for the welfare** of the workers they bring in, and must hold a financial guarantee of **€75,000**.

Because employers and agencies first had to register on the new portal, the first applications under it were expected from **early October 2026**, so expect slower processing for the rest of the year.

## Update: part of the portal is blocked

In September 2026 the **Bucharest Court of Appeal suspended** the Labour Ministry order that sets the list of shortage occupations (Order 1073/2026). As a result, the WorkinRomania portal says **employer registration, employer authorisation and single applications for D/AM2 visas** (the lower-skilled route) are **deactivated**. The ministry said it would appeal. As of 21 October 2026, Romanian media reported the functions were still blocked, with no date for reactivation.

The new system is based on **Government Emergency Ordinance 32/2026** on foreigners' access to the Romanian labour market, which applies from 27 April 2026. Fines for breaking the rules range from **5,000 to 40,000 lei**.

## Pay and rights

Foreign workers must receive at least Romania's **minimum wage**, and Romanian labour law **does not allow lower pay based on nationality** for the same work.

## How to apply safely

1. Look for a **real job offer from a Romanian employer** or a licensed agency.
2. The employer (or agency) files the application on WorkinRomania; you then apply for a long-stay work visa.
3. Ask to see the **bilingual contract** before paying anything or travelling.
4. Be careful with agents who charge large fees or promise a visa without a contract.
