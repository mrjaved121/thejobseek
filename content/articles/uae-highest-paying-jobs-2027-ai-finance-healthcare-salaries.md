Want to know which **UAE jobs** pay the most in 2027? Recruiter **Michael Page's UAE Salary Guide 2027** identifies **18 sectors** hiring specialist and managerial talent, with the strongest demand in **AI, banking, finance and healthcare**. The most senior roles can pay up to **Dh200,000 a month**, according to figures reported on 4 November 2026.

## Which AI jobs are most in demand?

Employers across the UAE are competing hard for AI and data talent. The guide singles out five roles:

- Agentic AI engineers
- Applied AI engineers
- Data scientists and applied data scientists
- AI and machine-learning platform engineers
- AI product managers

Published salary benchmarks for AI and data leaders include:

| AI / data role | Top monthly pay in the guide |
|---|---|
| Agentic AI engineer | Up to Dh70,000 |
| Agentic AI architect | Up to Dh75,000 |
| Chief data officer | Up to Dh120,000 |

The skills employers value most include building **agentic and multi-agent AI systems**, machine learning, coding with AI assistants, and running AI in production on robust platforms.

## Which finance and banking jobs pay the most?

Banking and financial services has some of the highest salary benchmarks:

- **CFOs in investment and funds businesses:** up to Dh200,000 a month
- **Managing directors in investment banking:** up to Dh180,000 a month

Roles in demand include private banking and wealth managers, private equity and corporate development leaders, fundraising and institutional sales professionals, compliance and money-laundering reporting officers, and corporate relationship managers. Employers value relationship management, financial product knowledge, **AI fluency**, compliance and financial modelling.

## What about consulting and transformation jobs?

Companies going through change are hiring transformation managers, strategy specialists, AI and digital transformation experts, organisational design specialists and change managers. **Management consulting partners** can earn up to **Dh200,000 a month**, according to the guide.

## What skills do UAE employers want most?

The report finds employers increasingly want people who combine **technical knowledge with business sense** and can lead teams and deliver results. Pure technical skills are not enough on their own for the top-paying roles.

## How do I find these jobs?

These roles are advertised on employers' official careers pages and through specialist recruiters. Build a strong LinkedIn profile, keep your CV focused on measurable results, and target companies in AI, banking, healthcare and energy.

## What it means for job seekers

- **AI skills raise your value** in almost every sector, not just tech.
- **Senior pay is very high**, but these roles need many years of experience and leadership.
- **Mix technical and commercial skills** to stand out.
