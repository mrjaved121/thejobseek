The **$100,000 H-1B fee** is still on the books, but it is **not being collected**. In September 2026 the White House **extended** it for another year, yet two federal courts have blocked the agencies from enforcing it.

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|---|---|
| 19 Sep 2025 | Proclamation 10973 requires a $100,000 payment for certain new H-1B petitions |
| 23 Dec 2025 | Washington DC district court upholds the fee (appeal pending) |
| 27 Feb 2026 | New wage-weighted H-1B selection takes effect |
| 8 Jun 2026 | Massachusetts federal court vacates the fee policy nationwide in a case brought by 20 states |
| 24 Jul 2026 | First Circuit refuses to pause that ruling |
| 18 Sep 2026 | Proclamation 11069 extends the restriction to 21 September 2027 |
| 30 Sep 2026 | California federal court blocks USCIS, CBP and State from enforcing the fee |
| 9 Oct 2026 | Government appeals to the Ninth Circuit |

## Who the fee covered

- **Paid by:** the employer, through pay.gov, with the receipt filed with the petition.
- **Applied to:** new petitions filed on or after **21 September 2025** for workers **outside the US**.
- **Exempt:** petitions filed before that date, current H-1B holders, and people inside the US approved for a change of status, extension or change of employer.
- **National interest exceptions** were possible but rare.

The September 2026 proclamation says only about **700 petitions** paid the fee in its first year.

## The new weighted lottery

Since **27 February 2026**, H-1B registrations are weighted by wage level. **Level IV** gets four entries, **Level III** three, **Level II** two and **Level I** one. Higher-paid jobs now have a much better chance of selection.

DHS has also proposed a **$103,265 fee** on new cap-subject petitions through formal rulemaking. Comments closed on 24 September 2026, and no final rule has been issued.

## What it means for job seekers

- **No fee is being charged now**, but the appeals could change that. Employers may stay cautious about hiring from abroad.
- **Higher salaries help.** With the weighted lottery, a Level III or IV offer improves your odds.
- **Never pay a recruiter for an H-1B.** Fees are the employer's responsibility.
