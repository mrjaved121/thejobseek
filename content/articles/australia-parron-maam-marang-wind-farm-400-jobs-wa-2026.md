Building work has officially begun on the **Parron Maam Marang wind farm** in Western Australia, a **470 MW** project that will create **up to 400 jobs at the peak of construction**. The start was announced over the weekend and reported by RenewEconomy on **26 October 2026**.

It is the **largest wind farm in WA** and the biggest project of its kind to start construction anywhere in Australia in **more than two years**.

## Project at a glance

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Project | Parron Maam Marang wind farm |
| Owner | Atmos Renewables |
| Size | 470 MW |
| Turbines | 78 Vestas V162-6.2 MW EnVentus |
| Location | About 170 km north of Perth, mid-west Wheatbelt |
| Power buyer | Synergy (state-owned) |
| Peak jobs | Up to 400 during construction |
| Expected completion | Late 2028 |

## Who will be needed

With about two years of building ahead, a wind farm of this size needs a wide range of workers on site. Typical construction roles include:

- **Civil workers**: plant operators, truck drivers and labourers building access roads and turbine foundations
- **Concreters and steel fixers** for the large turbine bases
- **Crane operators, riggers and doggers** to lift tower sections and blades
- **Electricians and cable jointers** for the internal network and substation
- **Wind turbine technicians** to install and commission the 78 turbines
- **Safety, environment, survey and site admin staff**
- **Camp, catering and cleaning crews** to support a regional workforce

Atmos Renewables chief executive **Nigel Baker** said construction would bring local jobs and investment into the region. WA Energy Minister **Amber-Jade Sanderson** said the project showed momentum behind the state's move to renewable energy.

## More projects in the pipeline

The wind farm is part of a wider push to replace coal in WA. Atmos is also building the **Merredin battery** (100 MW, 400 MWh), and Alinta Energy's **Marri wind project**, at more than 550 MW, is planned to become the state's largest. That means demand for construction and electrical workers in regional WA should stay strong for several years.

## How to apply

No single official careers page has been published for this project yet. Most site jobs on wind farms are hired through the main construction and turbine contractors rather than the owner. To find openings:

- Check the careers pages of **Atmos Renewables** and **Vestas** directly on their own websites.
- Watch for recruitment notices from the project's civil and electrical contractors once they are named.
- Register with official WA government job services if you live in the Wheatbelt.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Get your tickets ready.** A White Card, plus high-risk work licences for cranes, rigging or elevated work platforms, are essential.
- **Be open to FIFO or DIDO.** The site is rural, so many roles will involve rosters and on-site accommodation.
- **Electricians are in demand** across WA's renewable energy build-out.
- **Locals may get priority**, so Wheatbelt and Mid West residents should mention their address when applying.
