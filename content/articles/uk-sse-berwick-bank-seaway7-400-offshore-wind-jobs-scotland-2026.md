A major offshore wind contract is set to bring hundreds of skilled jobs to Scotland. **SSE Renewables** has chosen Aberdeen-based **Seaway7** as preferred supplier for key construction work at the **Berwick Bank B** offshore wind farm, a deal expected to support about **400 skilled offshore jobs in Scotland**, Project Scotland reported on **20 October 2026**.

## The contract

Seaway7 would handle engineering, procurement, construction and installation of the **turbine foundations** and the **subsea inter-array cables** that link the turbines together. The wind farm will sit in the outer **Firth of Forth**, around 38km off the Scottish Borders coast.

## Jobs linked to the project

| Source of jobs | Approximate number |
|---|---|
| Skilled offshore jobs in Scotland | 400 |
| Jobs elsewhere in the UK | 65 |
| Direct roles with Seaway7 (led from Aberdeen) | 160 |
| Indirect UK supply chain roles | 105 |
| Foundation marshalling at a Scottish port (to be named) | About 200 |
| Operations and maintenance base, Montrose Port | About 100 long-term plus 30 construction |

In total, Berwick Bank B is expected to support around **600 UK jobs**, with more than **500 in Scotland**.

## Timing

This is not yet a final contract. The appointment depends on the contract being formally awarded and on SSE's **final investment decision, expected in late 2027**. Offshore construction would follow after that, so most of the direct hiring is a medium-term opportunity.

Scotland's economy secretary **Stephen Flynn** welcomed the jobs and pointed to up to £500 million of Scottish Government support for offshore wind ports and supply chains. Seaway7 has worked on earlier SSE projects including Beatrice, Seagreen and Dogger Bank.

## Jobs likely to be in demand

- Offshore installation technicians and riggers
- Marine coordinators and vessel crew
- Subsea cable engineers and jointers
- Project engineers and planners
- Welders, fabricators and port logistics staff for foundation marshalling
- Health, safety and environment advisers

## How to apply

SSE advertises its vacancies, apprenticeships and graduate schemes on its official careers site: [careers.sse.com](https://careers.sse.com/jobs/search). It currently lists more than 100 roles, including renewables positions in Aberdeen, Montrose, Perth and Inverness. Seaway7 roles are advertised separately by that company.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Get offshore tickets.** Safety certificates such as GWO basic safety training are usually required for wind farm work at sea.
- **Aberdeen's oil and gas skills transfer well.** Many offshore wind roles suit people from the North Sea industry.
- **Look at apprenticeships.** SSE runs apprenticeship and early-careers routes that can lead into renewables.
- **Follow the port decision.** The marshalling port, still to be named, will need around 200 workers.
