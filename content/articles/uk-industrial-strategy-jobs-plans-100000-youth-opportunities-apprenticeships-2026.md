The UK government has published new **Jobs Plans** for two of its biggest growth sectors, setting out **more than 100,000 job and training openings a year** for young people. The plans for **professional and business services** and **construction** came out on **19 October 2026**, with digital and defence plans following the same week.

Work and Pensions Secretary **Pat McFadden** said too many young people had been shut out of good careers for too long.

## The headline numbers

| Sector | What is promised |
|---|---|
| Professional and business services | 22,400 new under-25 apprenticeships a year for five years; 20% more work experience |
| Construction | 40,000 placements a year, part of a 100,000-a-year jobs ambition |
| Defence | At least 14,000 apprenticeships a year by 2035; 25% more routes for young people out of work |
| Small employers | Up to £2,000 for taking on a 16 to 24-year-old apprentice |

Jobs in the government's eight priority sectors pay about **£8,000 a year more** on average than the rest of the economy, according to the announcement.

## Business services: accountancy, law and tech

The professional and business services plan covers **accountancy, law, consultancy, business**, and fast-growing areas such as **AI, cyber security and data analytics**. As well as more apprenticeships, employers have signed up to track how they recruit and support young people who are not working or studying, and to be clearer about early-career pay.

## Construction: trades and professional routes

Construction placements will include hands-on trades like **bricklaying, carpentry, electrical work and plumbing**, plus professional paths in **engineering, surveying, planning and site management**. A new **Construction Apprenticeship Network** will help smaller building firms, which often struggle with the paperwork, take on apprentices.

## Wider support

The plans sit inside a **£2.5 billion** youth employment package that the government says will create close to **one million** chances to earn or learn, including **50,000 extra apprenticeships**. Employers also pay no National Insurance for apprentices under 25.

## How to apply

Apprenticeships in England are advertised on the official [Find an apprenticeship](https://www.findapprenticeship.service.gov.uk/) service. You can also read the sector plans on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/industrial-strategy-jobs-plans/jobs-plans).

## What it means for job seekers

- **Under 25? This is aimed at you.** Most of the new places are reserved for young people.
- **Big accounting and law firms will expand intakes.** Watch their school-leaver and apprentice schemes for 2027 starts.
- **Trades are the fastest route in.** Construction placements do not need a degree and lead to well-paid skilled work.
- **Ask small firms directly.** The £2,000 incentive makes it easier for local employers to say yes.

Young people can also look at [Amazon UK's pledge of 30,000 jobs for young people](/uk/uk-amazon-youth-promise-30000-jobs-young-people-2030/).
