**American Express** is cutting about **3,500 jobs**. The card company announced the plan on **23 October 2026**, the same day it reported strong third-quarter results. The cuts are described as Amex's **first large round of layoffs in around a decade**.

## The job cuts in numbers

| Detail | Figure |
|---|---|
| Jobs to be cut | About 3,500 |
| Share of workforce | Roughly 4.5%–5% |
| Workforce before cuts | About 78,000 worldwide |
| Restructuring charge (Q3) | $336 million |
| Q3 2026 net profit | $3.71 billion (up 28%) |

Different reports put the share at 4.5% or 5%, depending on how the total workforce is counted. A company spokesperson confirmed the 3,500 figure but gave no further detail.

## Why Amex is cutting jobs

Chief executive **Stephen Squeri** told investors the move is about making the company **faster and leaner**, not about weak business. He said that earlier in the year he had asked managers to look for ways to run operations better and make decisions more quickly.

Amex also plans to add more **AI agents** to its platform. Squeri mainly talked about AI for customers, such as personalised restaurant services through Resy, but he also said AI could improve the company's internal work. Rivals such as Capital One and JPMorgan Chase are spending heavily on card rewards, which puts pressure on Amex to control its costs.

## What we do not know yet

- Which **departments** will be affected
- Which **countries or offices** will lose jobs
- The **timeline** for the cuts
- **Severance** terms for affected staff

## The bigger picture

Amex joins a growing list of financial firms trimming staff while investing in AI. In the same week, UK bank Barclays announced 5,000 job cuts. Banks say they still need people for customer-facing, technology and risk roles, but back-office and routine processing jobs are shrinking.

## What it means for job seekers

- **If you work at Amex,** keep your CV up to date and ask HR about internal transfer options before decisions are final.
- **Finance job seekers:** expect more competition for operations and back-office roles. Skills in data, AI tools, fraud prevention and compliance remain in demand.
- **Do not count out the company.** Large firms often keep hiring in growth areas even while cutting elsewhere, so check the official Amex careers site for new openings.
- **Watch for official news.** No details have been given yet about which countries are affected, so rely on company statements rather than social media rumours.
