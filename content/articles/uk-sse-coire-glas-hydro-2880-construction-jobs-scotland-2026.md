Big infrastructure could mean big **UK jobs** opportunities in Scotland. A new study says **SSE Renewables'** proposed **Coire Glas** pumped storage hydro scheme in the Highlands of Scotland would back an estimated **2,880 construction jobs** and add billions to the UK economy. The findings were reported on 2 November 2026.

## What is Coire Glas?

Coire Glas is a planned **1.44GW pumped storage hydroelectric** scheme. Pumped storage works like a giant battery: water is pumped uphill when power is cheap and released through turbines when demand is high.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Developer | SSE Renewables |
| Location | Scottish Highlands |
| Capacity | 1.44GW |
| Construction jobs supported | 2,880 (study estimate) |
| Long-term jobs | Around 60 a year once operating |
| Spending with UK businesses | £1.94 billion |
| UK economic value | £1.5 billion |
| Investment needed | About £3 billion of private capital |

The figures come from an economic study by **BiGGAR Economics**.

## Has the project been approved?

Not yet in full. SSE has already invested **more than £100 million** in development work, including site investigations and a **1.2km exploratory tunnel**. In June 2026, energy regulator **Ofgem** put Coire Glas among 16 long-duration electricity storage projects on its "Minded-to-Decision" list for cap and floor support. SSE says it is still working through many points of detail with Ofgem before the project can go ahead.

SSE Renewables Managing Director **Stephen Wheeler** called it potentially one of the most important UK infrastructure projects in a generation.

## What jobs could it create?

Large hydro and tunnelling projects typically need:

- Tunnellers, miners and drill-and-blast crews
- Civil engineers, geotechnical engineers and site managers
- Plant operators, electricians and mechanical fitters
- Health and safety, logistics and site support staff

These are typical roles for this kind of project, not confirmed vacancies. Jobs would mainly be advertised by SSE and its main construction contractors once work starts.

## How do I apply?

There are no Coire Glas vacancies yet. Watch SSE's official careers website and those of its major contractors for roles in the Highlands.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Big projects take time.** Hiring starts only after final approvals and contracts.
- **Tunnelling and civil skills are in demand** across UK energy projects.
- **Overseas workers** would usually need a UK work visa sponsored by the employer.
