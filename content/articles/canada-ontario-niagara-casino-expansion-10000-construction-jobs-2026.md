Big **Ontario jobs** plans are taking shape in Niagara. Ontario and its gaming agency, the **Ontario Lottery and Gaming Corporation (OLG)**, are inviting casino operators to invest in the Niagara Region through a new **Call for Investment**. Officials expect **more than $3 billion** in casino spending, and say building it will support **upwards of 10,000 construction jobs**.

## What has Ontario announced?

The province is opening Niagara to new casino operators for the first time. The plan is part of the **Destination Niagara Strategy**, which aims to double tourism in the region to more than **25 million visitors a year**.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Who | Government of Ontario and OLG |
| Where | Niagara Region, Ontario |
| Direct gaming investment | More than $3 billion |
| Extra private investment | More than $600 million |
| Construction jobs | 10,000+ |
| Permanent jobs target (whole strategy) | About 46,500 |
| Time frame | Next 20 years |

## How will the new casinos be chosen?

- **Phase one:** experienced, world-class gaming operators are invited to express interest.
- **Phase two:** companies that make the shortlist must then hand in full plans for new casino resorts.
- Up to **three new operators** can enter at first, with possible expansion after a 10-year fixed term.
- Each must invest **at least $1.5 billion** and pay a **$100 million entrance fee**.
- Operators will pay a gaming tax rate of **9.75%**.

Tourism Minister **Zee Hamid** said the plan will attract investment, create good-paying jobs and support Niagara as a year-round destination.

## What jobs could this create?

Over time, integrated casino resorts usually need:

- **Construction:** labourers, carpenters, electricians, plumbers, ironworkers, heavy equipment operators, site managers
- **Hospitality:** hotel, restaurant, kitchen and housekeeping staff
- **Gaming:** dealers, cage cashiers, surveillance and security staff
- **Entertainment and events:** technicians, event staff, marketing

## How do I apply?

There is nothing to apply for yet. Jobs will be advertised by the selected operators and their construction contractors once projects are approved. Watch official announcements from OLG and the Ontario government.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Skilled trades will be needed first.** A recognised trade certificate puts you in a strong position for the construction phase.
- **Hospitality jobs come later.** Permanent casino and hotel roles will follow once resorts are built.
- **This is a long-term plan.** The investment runs over 20 years, so expect a steady pipeline rather than instant openings.
