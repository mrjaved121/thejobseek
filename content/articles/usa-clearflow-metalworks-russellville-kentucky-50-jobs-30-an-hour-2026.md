Well-paid factory work is coming to western Kentucky. **ClearFlow Metalworks** will invest **$28 million** to open its first US plant in **Russellville, Logan County**, creating **50 full-time jobs** at an average of about **$30 an hour** including benefits. Governor **Andy Beshear's** administration announced the project on **30 October 2026**, adding to **US hiring in 2026** across the manufacturing belt.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | ClearFlow Metalworks |
| Location | Shelton Lane Industrial Park, Russellville, KY |
| Investment | $28 million |
| New jobs | 50 full-time |
| Target average pay | $30/hour including benefits |
| Product | Recycled aluminium billet |
| State support | Up to $1,000,500 in tax incentives over 15 years |

## About the plant

The company will move into a newly built speculative building at the industrial park. The first phase is a **secondary aluminium billet casting** operation that melts down recycled aluminium and casts it into logs (billets) used by extrusion companies. Planned capacity is about **100,000 tons a year**. The project will be built in phases; no opening date has been given yet.

The state release linked the project to East Asia Aluminum Company of Vietnam and Singapore-based USVN.

## Likely roles

Casting plants usually hire:

- Furnace and casting operators
- Maintenance mechanics and electricians
- Forklift and material handlers
- Quality and lab technicians
- Shift supervisors and plant engineers

## Why Russellville

Logan County sits in south-central Kentucky, close to the Tennessee border and within reach of Bowling Green and Nashville. The region already has a strong base of automotive and metal suppliers, which gives a new aluminium plant both customers and a pool of experienced factory workers.

- **$30 an hour is above average** for entry to mid-level factory work in the region.
- **Recycling metals is a growth area,** offering steady long-term jobs.
- **Maintenance skills are gold.** Electricians and industrial mechanics are always in demand at melt shops.
- **Be ready early.** Small plants often fill roles quickly once hiring begins.
