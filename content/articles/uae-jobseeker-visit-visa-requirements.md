If you want to come to the UAE to look for work without a sponsor, the **job seeker visit visa** is designed for you. Here is who can get it and how it works.

## Key features

- **No sponsor or host** is needed in the UAE.
- **Single entry**, valid for **60, 90 or 120 days**.
- It is meant for **searching for a job only**. You cannot start working until an employer gets you a proper work permit and residence visa.

## Who qualifies

You must meet the **first** condition and **both** of the others:

1. **Either:**
   - your profession is classified at **skill level 1, 2 or 3** under MoHRE's job levels (generally managerial, professional and technical roles), **or**
   - you graduated within the **last 2 years** from one of the **world's top 500 universities** (as recognised by the UAE Ministry of Education).
2. You hold at least a **bachelor's degree** or equivalent.
3. You can provide the required **financial guarantee**.

## Documents

- A colour photo
- A copy of your passport
- Your **attested** degree certificate

Attestation can take time, so start early.

## Where to apply

- **ICP Smart Services**: "search for job" visa, single entry, 60, 90 or 120 days
- **GDRFA Dubai**: visit visa to explore job opportunities

Check the fee and the financial guarantee amount on the ICP or GDRFA service page before you apply.

## Tips for your job search

- **Line up interviews before you travel**, so you make the most of your visa days.
- **Track your expiry date.** Overstaying leads to daily fines.
- **Never start work on a visit visa.** More on this: [working on a UAE visit visa is not allowed](/uae/can-you-work-on-visit-visa-in-uae/)
- **Avoid agents selling "job + visa packages".** The visa itself doesn't need an agent. See: [Warning signs of a fake UAE job offer](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/)
