Skilled workers searching for **EU jobs with visa sponsorship** may want to look at Austria's rail industry. **Traktionssysteme Austria (TSA)**, which builds electric traction motors for trains, trams and buses, has opened a new **€9.5 million** plant in **Wiener Neudorf**, just south of Vienna, and plans to add **about 100 jobs** by 2030, electrive.net reported on 27 October 2026.

The new building, called **Werk 3a**, was officially opened on **15 October 2026**. It will stamp the electrical steel sheets used inside TSA's motors.

## Why TSA is growing

Until now, TSA made only about a fifth to a quarter of its motor sheets in-house. With the new plant, it will make **all of them** itself. Once the second machine arrives next year, the site should produce around **five million sheets a year**. The company says this will also save about **200 tonnes of CO₂** every year.

The new plant is part of a wider plan known as **"TSA-City"**, with four production halls on the site. Demand for electric rail and bus motors is rising across Europe as cities expand public transport.

## Which jobs to expect

TSA did not list the 100 new posts. Its current careers page shows openings such as **metalworking apprentices** and **quality management** staff. Growth at a motor factory usually means roles like:

- Machine operators and CNC/press technicians
- Electrical and mechanical engineers
- Quality inspectors and quality engineers
- Maintenance technicians
- Apprentices in metal and electrical trades

## Working in Austria from abroad

EU citizens can work in Austria freely. Non-EU skilled workers often use the **Red-White-Red Card**, which needs a job offer and enough points for qualifications, experience, age and language. Shortage trades such as technicians and engineers can qualify. German language skills make a big difference, as most adverts at TSA are in German.

## How to apply

Visit the official [TSA careers page](https://tsa.at/career/), which lists current vacancies, including production jobs and apprenticeships. Send a CV in the Austrian style, with a photo and clear dates, and mention any rail or electric motor experience.
