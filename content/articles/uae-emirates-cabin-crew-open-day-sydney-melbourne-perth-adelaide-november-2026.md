**The Emirates cabin crew open day in Sydney is on Friday 27 November 2026 at Four Points by Sheraton Sydney, Central Park, from 9am.** It is one of four Australian walk-in sessions the Dubai airline lists this month, alongside Perth on 20 November, Adelaide on 22 November and Melbourne on 29 November.

The schedule comes from the airline's own [cabin crew careers page](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/cabin-crew/), read on 15 November 2026.

## Where are the Australian open days?

| City | Day and date | Start | Venue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Perth | Friday 20 November 2026 | 9am | DoubleTree by Hilton Perth Waterfront, 1 Barrack Square, Perth WA 6000 |
| Adelaide | Sunday 22 November 2026 | 9am | InterContinental Adelaide, North Terrace |
| Sydney | Friday 27 November 2026 | 9am | Four Points by Sheraton Sydney, Central Park, 88 Broadway, Chippendale NSW 2008 |
| Melbourne | Sunday 29 November 2026 | 9am | Dorsett Melbourne, 615 Little Lonsdale Street, Melbourne VIC 3000 |

A Sunshine Coast session is also listed for Monday 16 November at the Holiday Inn & Suites in Maroochydore. Dates can move, so reload the page the day before you go.

Cannot make any of them? Emirates lists a live online information session for Australia on **6 December 2026**, with a register link on the same page.

## Who can apply?

Emirates sets out who it is looking for. In short:

| Criterion | Emirates' rule |
|---|---|
| Age | 21 or older |
| Height and reach | Minimum 160 cm, and able to reach 212 cm |
| Language | Fluent English, written and spoken; other languages are a plus |
| Experience | One year or more in hospitality or customer service |
| Education | High school (Grade 12) at least |
| Tattoos | None visible in the cabin crew uniform |
| Visa | Must qualify for a UAE employment visa |

## How much does Emirates pay?

For Grade II (Economy Class) the airline shows a fixed monthly basic of AED 4,980. Each hour flown adds AED 69.6. On a typical 80 to 100 flying hours, it puts the average at roughly AED 11,244 monthly, which it converts to USD 3,061. Emirates labels these as approximate.

On top of pay, the page lists:

- a furnished apartment in Dubai shared with one or two colleagues, with utilities paid
- company transport to and from work
- 30 calendar days of leave a year and one free annual ticket home
- medical and dental cover at Emirates clinics
- discounted staff travel

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Check yourself against the criteria above.** Measure your reach against a wall at home.
2. **Fill in the online application** via Apply on the careers site. Open days are walk-in, so this is not a ticket, but recruiters will already hold your profile.
3. **Load three files onto your phone:** your current English-language CV, your school or college certificate, and a PDF scan of your passport. Emirates asks candidates to have these ready to share.
4. **Carry valid ID.** The airline says you need it to get into the venue.
5. **Be there before 9am.** The day opens with a briefing on the job and on living in Dubai. Group exercises and an online test follow, and candidates who progress finish with an interview.
6. **Afterwards,** wait for an email from Emirates. No results timeline is published, so do not resign from your current job on the strength of a good day.

Attending is free and Emirates recruits Australians directly. No agent is involved for these four cities. Anyone selling "priority passes" or a guaranteed outcome is not acting for the airline.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Follow the dress code.** The page has separate guidance for women and men; read it and arrive polished.
- **Frame hospitality jobs properly.** Cafe, bar, hotel, retail and contact-centre work all count. State how long, and what you handled.
- **Be a good group member.** Listening and bringing quieter people in scores better than dominating.
- **Frequent slips:** turning up late, a CV with no service roles, no documents saved.

Checklist: ID, the three files, online form submitted, business outfit, water and a snack.

## What it means for job seekers

- Four cities in ten days gives most Australians a session within reach.
- Demand is high and many attendees will not be selected. Going well prepared is the only thing you control.
- Successful candidates spend about seven and a half weeks at the Dubai training college before their first flight.
