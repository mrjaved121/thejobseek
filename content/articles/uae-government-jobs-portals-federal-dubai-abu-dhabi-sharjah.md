Government jobs in the UAE are popular for their stability and benefits. But there is no single portal for every emirate, and **some portals are open only to UAE nationals**. Here is where each government advertises jobs and who can apply.

## At a glance

| Government | Where jobs are posted | Open to expats? |
|---|---|---|
| **Federal** | The federal government jobs portal of the Federal Authority for Government Human Resources (FAHR) | Depends on the job |
| **Dubai** | **Dubai Careers** (dubaicareers.ae) | Depends on the job |
| **Abu Dhabi** | **TAMM**'s "Apply to a Job Vacancy" service | **No, Emiratis only** |
| **Abu Dhabi entities** | Each department's own careers page | Yes, for many roles |
| **Sharjah** | Sharjah HR department's job seeker service | **No, Emiratis aged 18 to 64** |
| **Sharjah entities** | Each department's own careers page | Yes, for many roles |
| **Ajman** | **Kawader** portal of the Ajman HR department | Depends on the job |
| **Ras Al Khaimah** | **RAK Government Careers** (careers.rak.ae) | Depends on the job |

## Federal government

The federal portal lists jobs at federal ministries and authorities, such as **Emirates Health Services**, the **Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP)**, the **UAE Space Agency** and the **Federal Tax Authority**. You register with your email, upload your CV and fill in your personal details.

## Dubai

**Dubai Careers** is the Dubai Government's main hiring portal. Have these ready:

- CV in PDF or Word format
- Emirates ID and passport copy
- Educational certificates

## Abu Dhabi

The central TAMM service is for **Emiratis over 18**, using UAE Pass. **Expats** apply directly to individual entities, for example:

- **SEHA** (Abu Dhabi Health Services)
- **Department of Municipalities and Transport**
- **Department of Education and Knowledge (ADEK)**
- **Department of Economic Development**
- **Abu Dhabi Digital Authority**

## Sharjah

As in Abu Dhabi, the central service is for Emiratis. Expats apply on the careers pages of bodies such as **Sharjah Municipality**, **SEWA**, **Sharjah Airport**, **Sharjah Ports** and the **Department of Public Works**.

## Ajman and Ras Al Khaimah

- **Ajman Kawader:** sign in with UAE Pass or an email account, complete your profile (experience, qualifications, training, skills) and apply.
- **RAK Careers:** you need an **Emirates ID number** to register, plus a CV, certificates and passport number. You can set alerts for new jobs.

## Tips for applying

- **Read the eligibility line first.** Many government roles are reserved for UAE nationals under Emiratisation.
- Keep your **degree attested**, since government employers often ask for it.
- Applying is **free**. Anyone charging a fee for a "government job" is likely a scam. See: [Warning signs of a fake UAE job offer](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/).
- Portal addresses can change, so reach them from each government's official website.
