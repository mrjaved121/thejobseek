**Tamkeen jobs** support is coming to Bahrain's tourism sector. The Labour Fund (**Tamkeen**) has agreed to back **Club Hawaii @ Bahrain Surf Park**, the Kingdom's first project of this kind, which aims to create **more than 120 jobs for Bahrainis** in tourism and related industries. The agreement was announced on 3 November 2026 at Gateway Gulf 2026.

## What is Bahrain Surf Park?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Project | Club Hawaii @ Bahrain Surf Park |
| Size | Over 52,000 square metres |
| Main attraction | Surf lake using Wavegarden Cove technology |
| Other features | Club Hawaii Surf Academy, food and beverage outlets, private cabanas, event spaces |
| Jobs | 120+ for Bahrainis |
| Backed by | Tamkeen (Labour Fund) |

The venue will also host events and activities for companies, schools and private groups.

## Which jobs will be available?

Tamkeen says Bahrainis will gain skills and jobs in these areas:

- **Hospitality** – guest services, food and beverage, cabana and reception staff
- **Tourism destination management**
- **Event management** – corporate and school events, private functions
- **Sports tourism services** – surf coaching, lifeguarding and activity staff

The exact job titles and numbers per department have not been published; the examples above are typical for a venue of this kind.

## What did Tamkeen say?

Tamkeen Chief Executive **Maha Abdulhameed Mofeez** said the project fits the strategic investments Tamkeen wants to back, because it directly creates quality jobs for Bahrainis and opens new career paths in growing sectors, using international expertise.

## How do I apply?

Recruitment details have not been announced yet. Watch Bahrain Surf Park's and Tamkeen's official channels, and register with the Ministry of Labour's national employment services so you are ready when hiring opens.

- **Tourism is a growth sector in Bahrain.** New attractions mean new front-line and management roles.
- **Sports and lifeguard certificates help.** Swimming, first aid and coaching qualifications will stand out.
