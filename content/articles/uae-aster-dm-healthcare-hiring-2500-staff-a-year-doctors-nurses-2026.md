Doctors and nurses looking for **nursing jobs in the UAE** have a big employer to target. **Aster DM Healthcare** plans to hire **up to about 2,500 staff a year** as it opens new hospitals, clinics and pharmacies, group CEO **Alisha Moopen** told Khaleej Times on 9 November 2026. Openings are listed on Aster's official careers page.

## How many people will Aster hire?

| Area | Hires per year |
|---|---|
| New hospitals | 1,000 to 2,000 |
| Clinics and pharmacies | 300 to 500 |
| **Total** | **Up to about 2,500** |

Aster already employs about **18,500 people** in the region. A new hospital normally brings **1,000 to 1,500 jobs**, mostly **doctors and nurses**, and the group usually opens one or two hospitals a year, alongside **5–10 new clinics** and **30–40 new pharmacies** each year.

## Which projects are driving the hiring?

| Project | Details |
|---|---|
| Medcare Royal Hills Hospital | 144 beds, Dh350m building (about Dh500m with equipment), due by 2029 |
| Aster Qusais Hospital | 150-bed expansion |
| IMH Hospital | 110 beds added through an acquisition last month |
| Dammam, Saudi Arabia | 100-bed facility, set to double to 200 beds |

Royal Hills will be **Medcare's seventh hospital** and **Aster's 12th in the UAE**, and the group's first with **radiation therapy** for cancer care. Together with Qusais it makes up most of Aster's **Dh1 billion-plus** UAE investment plan.

## Will AI reduce healthcare jobs?

Not at Aster, according to Moopen. The group uses AI and robotics, mostly in surgery, but she said healthcare still depends heavily on people, so automation is not expected to cut headcount.

## Which roles are in demand?

- Registered nurses and specialist nurses
- Doctors and specialists, including oncology
- Pharmacists and pharmacy assistants
- Allied health staff (radiology, lab, therapy)
- Patient services and admin

The main hires are doctors and nurses; the other roles are typical for hospital and pharmacy growth.

## How do I apply?

Search vacancies on Aster DM Healthcare's official **[careers page](https://www.asterdmhealthcare.com/about-us/careers)**. Healthcare roles in the UAE also need a licence from the relevant health authority (DHA, DOH or MOHAP).

## What it means for job seekers

- **Healthcare is one of the UAE's steadiest hirers.**
- **Oncology skills** will be valuable as Royal Hills opens.
- **Get licensed early**, as it speeds up hiring.
