Saudi job seekers in the private sector are set to see better starting pay offers. The **Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development** announced on **25 October 2026** that the monthly salary a Saudi employee must earn to count fully under the **Nitaqat** Saudization programme will rise in two steps, first to **SR5,000** and then to **SR6,000**.

The news was carried by the Saudi Press Agency and reported by Gulf News on 26 October.

## The new timetable

| Phase | Date it starts | Monthly salary needed to count as one full Saudi worker |
|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 | 25 April 2027 | SR5,000 |
| Phase 2 | 25 April 2028 | SR6,000 |

The first step comes six months after the announcement, which the ministry says gives companies time to adjust their pay structures.

## How it works

Under Nitaqat, private companies must employ a set share of Saudi nationals, depending on their size and sector. Each Saudi employee is "counted" towards that share. The salary threshold decides whether a Saudi worker counts as one full employee.

- If a Saudi worker is paid **at or above** the threshold, they count in full.
- If they are paid **below** it, they do not count in full towards the company's Saudization rate.

The ministry was clear that firms are **not automatically required to raise wages**. But in practice, companies that want to keep their Nitaqat band (and the services that come with it) have a strong reason to offer Saudi staff at least SR5,000 from April 2027.

## Why this matters for jobs

Saudi Arabia is pushing hard to move nationals into private-sector work. Recent decisions have raised Saudization rates in several fields, including 60% for marketing and sales roles. A higher counting threshold means those reserved jobs are more likely to come with better pay, rather than low salaries offered only to meet a quota.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Know your number.** From April 2027, an offer below SR5,000 a month means the employer may not get full Nitaqat credit for you, which gives you room to negotiate.
- **Plan for 2028.** Long contracts signed now may need to be reviewed before April 2028, when the bar moves to SR6,000.
- **Target growing sectors.** Marketing, sales, accounting, healthcare and hospitality are all under Saudization pressure and hiring nationals.
- **Use official platforms.** Government and many private jobs are listed on national portals such as Jadarat.
