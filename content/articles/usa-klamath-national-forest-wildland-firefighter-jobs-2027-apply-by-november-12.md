Want to spend 2027 outdoors fighting fires? The **Klamath National Forest** in **Northern California** is hiring **temporary seasonal workers** for its **2027 field season**, including **wildland firefighters, fire lookouts and animal packers**. Applications are made on **USAJOBS** and must be in by **November 12, 2026**, so there are only a few days left.

## Which jobs are open?

The forest is part of the US Forest Service's Fall Fire Hire event. Openings include:

| Role | Type | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Engine crew firefighter | Temporary seasonal (GW-0462/0456, grades 3–5) | Several stations |
| Hand crew firefighter | Temporary seasonal | Physical, team-based work |
| Helitack crew | Temporary seasonal | Helicopter-based fire response |
| Fire lookout | Temporary seasonal | Watches for smoke from towers |
| Animal packer | Temporary (WG-5001-5) | Works with mules and pack stock |

The forest has not given a total number of posts.

## Where are the jobs?

Duty stations are spread across Siskiyou County, in towns such as **Yreka, Fort Jones, Happy Camp, Macdoel, Callahan, Seiad Valley and Klamath River**. Not every role is offered at every station, so check the station list for each job before applying. The forest covers about 1.75 million acres between the Klamath Mountains and Mount Shasta, and is roughly 30 minutes from Medford and Klamath Falls in Oregon. **Government housing** may be available at some locations.

## Who can apply?

Federal jobs on USAJOBS are normally open to **US citizens** only, and firefighting roles also require a medical check and a physical fitness test before you start. Entry-level grades (GS/GW-3) are aimed at people with little or no fire experience, while higher grades ask for previous seasons on a crew. If you are not a citizen, read our guide on [whether non-citizens can apply for federal jobs](/usa/usajobs-can-non-citizens-apply-federal-jobs/).

## What is the work like?

Seasonal fire staff usually start in spring or early summer and work through the fire season, often with long shifts, overtime and time away from home during large fires. Lookouts spend weeks in remote towers, while packers move supplies by mule into roadless country. Pay follows the federal GW and WG pay scales, and hazard pay may apply on active fires.

## How do I apply?

1. Read the Klamath **[Fire Hire page](https://www.fs.usda.gov/r05/klamath/fire/fire-hire)** to see which stations are hiring for each role.
2. Create or update your profile and federal-style resume on **USAJOBS**.
3. Apply to the specific announcements and pick the Klamath duty stations you want.
4. Submit before the **November 12, 2026** deadline.
