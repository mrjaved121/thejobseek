**ADNOC** has opened a pipeline of more than **Dh60 billion** in local manufacturing opportunities running to 2030, and says its supplier programme has already created jobs for **more than 27,500 Emiratis** in the private sector. The news came at the second **Make it with ADNOC Forum** in Abu Dhabi on **25 October 2026**.

For job seekers, the message is simple: more of the equipment and parts the UAE's energy giant buys are meant to be made inside the country, and that means more factory, engineering and supply chain jobs in the UAE.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Organisation | ADNOC (Abu Dhabi National Oil Company) |

## What is being localised

ADNOC grouped the opportunities into several product families. These are the areas where new or bigger UAE factories may appear:

| Product area | Likely job types |
|---|---|
| Rotating equipment and power | Mechanical engineers, fitters, technicians |
| Electrical, control and automation | Electrical and instrumentation engineers, PLC programmers |
| Drilling and completion | Machinists, inspectors, field service staff |
| Chemicals and catalysts | Chemists, process operators, lab technicians |
| Piping, fabrication and heavy equipment | Welders, fabricators, QA/QC inspectors |
| Advanced manufacturing | CNC operators, 3D printing and design engineers |

## How the programme creates jobs

ADNOC's **In-Country Value (ICV)** scheme has run since 2018. Suppliers score better in tenders when they spend in the UAE, build local factories and employ Emiratis. ADNOC says the programme has pushed more than **Dh307 billion** back into the local economy and helped localise over **130 products**.

The newer **Local+** programme goes further. Contractors building ADNOC projects must buy certain items from approved UAE makers, giving those factories steady orders, and steady orders mean steady hiring.

ADNOC is also making it easier to become a supplier. It says the time needed to get ready to bid could fall by up to 80%, paperwork is being halved and many service categories will no longer need prequalification. Smaller UAE firms could win work faster and grow their teams.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Emiratis with technical training are in a strong spot.** ICV rules reward suppliers that hire UAE nationals, so engineering, IT and trade graduates should target ADNOC suppliers, not only ADNOC itself.
- **Look at industrial zones.** KEZAD, ICAD and other Abu Dhabi industrial areas are where new plants usually open. ADNOC signed agreements with ADIO and KEZAD Group at the forum.
- **Experienced expats with manufacturing skills** in machining, fabrication, valves, pumps, cables and chemicals may find new openings as factories scale up.
- **Follow the Local+ list.** Companies on it are the most likely to expand first.
- **Get certified.** Welding, NDT, instrumentation and quality certificates are often what separate shortlisted candidates.

This is a jobs-creation story rather than an open call for applications, so there is no single application link. Watch the careers pages of ADNOC and its main UAE suppliers in the coming months.
