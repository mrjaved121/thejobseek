**British Airways** has opened applications for its **2027 apprenticeships**. The airline is offering paid places in aircraft engineering, business, data science and supply chain at **Heathrow, Gatwick, Glasgow and Cardiff**, with starting pay of up to **£27,611**.

Apprentices earn a salary from day one and gain a recognised qualification, with no student debt.

## The 2027 programmes

| Programme | Location | Length | Starting salary |
|---|---|---|---|
| Data Science | London Heathrow | 3.5 years | £27,611 |
| Business | London Heathrow | 3 years | £19,906 |
| Licensed Aircraft Engineer | Gatwick | 4 years | £17,339 |
| Licensed Aircraft Engineer | Glasgow | 4 years | £17,339 |
| Licensed Aircraft Engineer | Cardiff | 4 years | £17,339 |
| Aircraft Workshop | London Heathrow | 3.5 years | £17,339 |
| Engineering Supply Chain | London Heathrow | 18 months | £17,339 |
| Heathrow Aircraft Maintenance | London Heathrow | 3 years | £16,000 |
| Aircraft Mechanic | London Heathrow | 2 years | £16,000 |
| Wales Aircraft Systems and Components | Cardiff | 3 years | £15,643 |

The Heathrow Aircraft Maintenance scheme has spring and autumn 2027 intakes. Its autumn listing says pay rises to at least **£26,000** in year two.

## Entry requirements

Requirements differ by scheme. In short:

- **Heathrow Aircraft Maintenance:** aged 16 by the start; GCSE grade 5 in Maths and in Combined Science or Physics, plus grade 4 in English Language.
- **Licensed Aircraft Engineer (Gatwick, Glasgow, Cardiff):** A Level Maths and one of Physics, Engineering or Chemistry, or an equivalent Level 3 qualification. Scottish equivalents are accepted.
- **Data Science:** GCSE Maths and English at grade 4+, and 128 UCAS points (ABB) with a B in A Level Maths, held or predicted.
- **Business:** GCSE English and Maths at grade 4 or above are desirable.
- **Aircraft Mechanic and Engineering Supply Chain:** aged 18 by the start.
- **Wales Aircraft Systems:** aged 16 by the start, with grade C in Maths, English and one Science; predicted grades accepted.
- **Aircraft Workshop:** at least grade 5 (B) in Maths, with English and Science desirable; predicted grades accepted.

## Key dates

British Airways does not list one closing date for every programme. The **Heathrow Aircraft Maintenance (autumn 2027)** listing closes on **19 November 2026**. Popular schemes can close early once enough people apply, so do not wait.

## How to apply

Apply only on British Airways' official careers site: [careers.ba.com/apprentices](https://careers.ba.com/apprentices). Pick your programme, check the full requirements and submit the online application. BA never charges a fee to apply.

## What it means for job seekers

- **School and college leavers:** this is a paid route into aviation, a sector where licensed engineers are hard to find.
- **Check your predicted grades now.** Several schemes accept predicted results, so Year 13 students can apply this autumn.
- **Choose the right location.** Many roles involve shift work at the airport, so think about travel.
- **Prepare for assessments.** Expect online tests and an assessment day; brush up on maths and mechanical reasoning.
