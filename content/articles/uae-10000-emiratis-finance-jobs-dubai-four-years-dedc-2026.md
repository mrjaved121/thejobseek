**Emiratisation jobs** in finance are growing fast. More than **10,000 Emiratis** have found work in the UAE's financial sector over the past four years, and about **25,000 Emiratis** now work in the industry. Hadi Badri, CEO of the **Dubai Economic Development Corporation (DEDC)**, shared the figures at the Dubai FinTech Summit on 2 November 2026.

## What did the DEDC chief say?

Badri said Dubai has no shortage of local talent in finance, and that Emirati professionals are learning from experienced international colleagues. The next goal, he said, is to give Emiratis in financial services more exposure to **revenue-generating roles** and **international experience**.

DEDC is the economic development arm of Dubai's Department of Economy and Tourism (DET).

## Why is Dubai's finance sector still hiring?

Badri described Dubai as the "bank of the region" and said finance is a key part of the **Dubai Economic Agenda D33**. Dubai wants to:

- Grow trade finance
- Attract more growth capital, private equity and venture capital
- Help banks lend more to small and medium businesses (SMEs)

He also said **artificial intelligence** will be a major growth engine and could help drive productivity gains linked to around 60% of the D33 agenda.

## Which finance jobs suit Emirati job seekers?

Based on where Dubai is pushing growth, these areas are worth watching:

- **Relationship management and sales** – the revenue-generating roles officials want more Emiratis in
- **Trade finance and corporate banking**
- **Fintech, payments and digital banking**
- **Risk, compliance and regulation**
- **Private equity and venture capital** analyst roles
- **AI and data roles** inside banks and fintech firms

These are typical career paths in the sector, not a list of confirmed vacancies.

## How do I apply?

Emirati job seekers can register on the federal **Nafis** platform, which lists private-sector jobs for UAE nationals, and apply directly on banks' and financial firms' official careers pages. Watch for bank-led Emirati career fairs, which often include on-the-spot interviews.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Finance is a strong path for Emiratis.** Thousands have been hired in recent years.
- **Aim for client-facing and revenue roles.** That is where officials say they want more Emirati talent.
- **Build AI and digital skills.** Banks are investing heavily in AI and fintech.
