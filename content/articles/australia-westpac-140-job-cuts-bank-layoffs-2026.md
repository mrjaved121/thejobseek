**Westpac**, one of Australia's four big banks, is cutting **140 more jobs**. The bank told the **Finance Sector Union (FSU)** about the cuts this week, according to a Reuters report on **22 October 2026**. It comes only about two weeks after Westpac informed the union of **102 other job cuts**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Latest Westpac cuts | 140 roles, across the bank |
| Earlier Westpac cuts | 102 roles in risk, institutional banking and finance |
| Westpac workforce | More than 30,000 |
| NAB | Up to 2,500 job cuts announced this week |
| ANZ | About 3,500 job cuts announced a year ago |
| Bank jobs lost in two years (FSU estimate) | More than 10,000 |

## Why banks are cutting jobs

Several pressures are hitting Australian banks at once:

- **A housing slowdown** has reduced home lending, a key source of bank income.
- **Four interest rate rises** by the Reserve Bank have squeezed borrowers.
- **AI and automation** are replacing some routine work. The FSU says weak regulation of AI is adding to workers' worries.

Westpac says its workforce changes in line with where the group is investing, and that it is still putting money into **bankers, digital delivery and customer-facing roles**.

## The wider bank job picture

**NAB** announced on Tuesday that it will cut up to **2,500 jobs**, one of the biggest bank restructures in Australia this year. **ANZ** said a little over a year ago it would cut around **3,500 jobs**. **Commonwealth Bank** warned in May that more AI could lead to job cuts but did not give a figure.

These cuts come as Australia's unemployment rate sits at **4.7%**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Back-office roles are shrinking.** Risk, finance and operations teams are where most cuts are falling.
- **Customer-facing and tech roles are safer.** Banks still want bankers who deal with clients, plus digital, data and cyber security staff.
- **Learn AI tools.** Staff who can use AI to work faster are more likely to keep their jobs or move into new roles.
- **Look beyond the big four.** Smaller banks, credit unions, fintechs and superannuation funds may still be hiring.
- **Overseas applicants:** expect very few sponsored banking roles while local staff are being cut.
