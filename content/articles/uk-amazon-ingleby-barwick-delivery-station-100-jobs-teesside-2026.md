New **Teesside jobs** are now available in logistics. **Amazon** has opened a **£40 million delivery station** near **Ingleby Barwick** in Stockton-on-Tees, creating **100 jobs**. The site, called **DZM1**, has started sending parcels to homes across Teesside, County Durham and North Yorkshire.

## What has Amazon opened?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Amazon UK |
| Site | DZM1 delivery station |
| Location | Off Queen Elizabeth Way, beside Preston Farm Industrial Estate, near Ingleby Barwick |
| Investment | £40 million |
| Size | 10,800 sq m |
| Jobs created | 100 |
| Area served | Teesside, County Durham, North Yorkshire |

Construction started in early 2025. A new roundabout was added on Queen Elizabeth Way, and staff and lorries enter from **Lockheed Close** on the industrial estate.

## What makes the site different?

DZM1 is Amazon's **first net-zero delivery station in the UK**. It uses lower-carbon steel, timber beams and lower-carbon concrete, over **1,400 sq m of solar panels on the roof**, and all-electric heating and cooling. Amazon expects it to use about **half the energy** of a typical logistics building.

## What jobs does a delivery station offer?

Delivery stations usually employ:

- Warehouse associates who sort and load parcels
- Shift leads and area managers
- Safety and maintenance staff
- Delivery drivers, often through Amazon's local delivery partners

These are typical roles at Amazon delivery stations; check live listings for exact openings.

## How do I apply?

Search for Stockton-on-Tees or Teesside roles on Amazon's official **[jobs website](https://www.amazon.jobs/)**. Delivery driver roles are often advertised by independent delivery service partners working with Amazon.
