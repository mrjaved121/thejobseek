Seasonal **jobs in Spain** are booming ahead of the holidays. Recruitment company **Adecco** has opened **more than 11,200 vacancies** across Spain for the **Christmas and Black Friday** season, in logistics, delivery, hospitality, industry and retail, it announced on 5 November 2026.

## Which jobs are available?

| Sector | Typical roles |
|---|---|
| Logistics | Warehouse workers, forklift operators, order pickers |
| Delivery | Delivery staff |
| Hospitality | Waiters, cooks, chambermaids |
| Retail | Sales assistants, promoters |
| Industry | Production workers |

## Where are the jobs?

There are vacancies in every region of Spain, but the most openings are in:

- Andalusia and the Canary Islands
- Catalonia and the Valencian Community
- Madrid and Castilla-La Mancha

## What types of contract are offered?

Adecco says the jobs include **full-time, part-time, rotating shift** and **weekend-only** contracts. Previous experience is valued, but some roles come **with training** for people seeking their first job in these areas.

The vacancies are being filled through **Adecco Here Now**, the company's platform for quickly matching workers to high-demand roles, launched four years ago.

## Who can apply?

Applicants must have the legal right to work in Spain. EU citizens can work freely; non-EU nationals need a valid Spanish work authorisation. Spanish language skills are usually important for customer-facing roles.

## How do I apply?

Apply through Adecco Spain's official website or its Adecco Here Now platform, searching by region and sector.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Seasonal work is plentiful** in the run-up to Christmas.
- **Weekend and part-time options** suit students and people with other commitments.
- **Beginners have a chance** thanks to training-included roles.
