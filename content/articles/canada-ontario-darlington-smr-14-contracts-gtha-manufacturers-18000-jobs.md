Nuclear power is creating new **Ontario jobs** in local factories. The province has awarded **14 contracts** to manufacturers across the **Greater Toronto and Hamilton Area (GTHA)** to supply the **Darlington small modular reactor (SMR)** project, and it expects building the four reactors to support about **18,000 jobs a year**. Energy and Mines Minister **Stephen Lecce** announced the contracts in Scarborough on 6 November 2026.

## What do the contracts cover?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Contracts | 14, all to GTHA companies |
| Supplies | Transformers, pumps, specialised steel and other parts |
| GDP boost | More than $110 million |
| Spending kept in Ontario | 80% of project spending |
| Example | Armstrong Fluid Technology, Scarborough: $3.9 million for a pumphouse enclosure and pumps |

Armstrong's finance chief, **Robert Dietrich**, said long-term work like this gives the firm confidence to invest in its staff.

## How big is the Darlington project?

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Reactors planned | 4 |
| Cost of first unit (incl. shared infrastructure) | About $7.7 billion |
| Total cost of four units | About $20.9 billion |
| Jobs during construction | About 18,000 a year |
| Jobs once operating | About 2,500 a year |
| Power output | 1,200 MW, enough for about 1.2 million homes |
| First power | By end of 2030 |

The project is at the **Darlington nuclear station in Clarington**, Durham Region, and is the first SMR build in the G7.

## Which jobs could grow?

A nuclear build and its supply chain typically need:

- Welders, pipefitters and millwrights
- Electricians and instrument technicians
- Machinists and fabrication workers
- Quality assurance and inspection staff
- Mechanical, electrical and nuclear engineers

These are common roles for a project of this kind, not specific vacancies.

## How can I find these jobs?

Watch the careers pages of the companies that win contracts, Ontario Power Generation's official job listings, and skilled trades unions in the GTHA and Durham Region.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Skilled trades are in demand** around Toronto, Hamilton and Durham.
- **Nuclear experience pays off** as more units follow the first.
- **Suppliers may hire first**, so target manufacturers named in contracts.
