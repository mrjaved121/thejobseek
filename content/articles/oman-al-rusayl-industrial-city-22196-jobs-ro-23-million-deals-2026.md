Factory investment is adding to **Muscat jobs**. Between January and June 2026, **Al Rusayl Industrial City** agreed **seven new investment contracts** together worth over **RO23 million**, and employment across the city and its logistics zone is expected to reach **22,196 people** this year, with Omanis making up about **38.8%** of the workforce.

## What deals were signed?

| Project | Value |
|---|---|
| Medical equipment and supplies manufacturing | RO15 million |
| Property development | RO3 million |
| International institute | RO1.8 million |
| Bulldozer and tracked undercarriage manufacturing | RO1.5 million |
| Others: marble cutting, property services, blacksmith workshops | Remainder |

The seven projects will use **121,619 square metres** of land. The city received **13 investment applications** in the six months.

## How big is Al Rusayl?

Al Rusayl is part of **Madayn**, Oman's Public Establishment for Industrial Estates. It covers around **11.04 million m²**, of which roughly **7.2 million m²** is developed. Cumulative investment has passed **RO780 million**.

Acting Director-General **Eng Fahad bin Nasser al Abrawi** shared the figures.

## Is there support for small businesses?

Yes. Al Rusayl is building the **Madayn Entrepreneurial Complex** with the SME Development Authority, offering ready-equipped workshops for start-ups. Its **Masar** service centre handles approvals, permits and licences in one place.

## Which jobs could grow?

New factories like these typically need:

- Production and machine operators
- Quality control and lab technicians
- Maintenance technicians and engineers
- Warehouse and logistics staff
- Sales and admin roles

These are typical industrial roles, not a confirmed vacancy list.

## How do I find jobs here?

Follow announcements from **[Madayn](https://madayn.om/)** and the companies based at Al Rusayl, and check Oman's national employment platforms for openings.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Manufacturing is expanding** close to Muscat.
- **Medical equipment production** may bring technical roles.
- **Omanisation at 38.8%** leaves room for more Omani hiring.
