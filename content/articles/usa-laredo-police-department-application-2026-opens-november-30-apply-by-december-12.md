**The Laredo Police Department application for 2026 opens on Monday 30 November and closes on Saturday 12 December, the department announced on 12 November.** New officers earn $56,992 while in the academy, and base pay reaches $82,540 from October 2027. Information and the application link are on the official recruiting site, JoinLaredoPD.com.

## Key dates

The [Laredo Morning Times reported the timetable](https://www.lmtonline.com/local/article/laredo-police-officer-recruitment-application-2026-22527595.php) from the department's announcement.

| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Applications open | 30 November 2026 |
| Applications close | 12 December 2026 |
| Pre-agility (fitness) test | 9–10 January 2027 |
| Written exam | 22 January 2027 |

## What is the salary?

Pay is rising because city leaders and the officers' union, the Laredo Police Officers Association, recently agreed a five-year contract.

| Stage | Pay |
|---|---|
| Academy | $56,992 a year |
| Entry-level patrol, from January 2027 | About $78,000 (up from about $73,500) |
| Base pay, from October 2027 | $82,540 |
| Entry level by 2030 | More than $94,600 |

The contract also adds higher pay for evening and overnight shifts, $1 an hour extra at weekends, a $200-a-month patrol incentive, and larger education, certification and longevity payments.

## What is the fitness test?

To pass the pre-agility stage you must complete:

- a **1.5-mile run in 15 minutes 20 seconds** or less
- **29 push-ups** in one minute
- **31 sit-ups** in one minute
- an **obstacle course** where you climb a six-foot wall and drag a 180 lb dummy

The department's advice is not to wait for the window to open before you start training.

## Who can apply?

The City of Laredo's civil service minimum standards for police, published with its last entrance-exam posting, include:

- **US citizen** living in the United States.
- **Age 21 to 42.** Applicants aged 18 to 20 need 60 college hours, an associate degree, or two years of active US military service with an honorable discharge.
- **A score of 70% or more** on the written exam.
- **Body art rules:** nothing offensive, and no tattoos on the head, face, neck or hands.

That document belongs to the previous cycle, so read the new application package when it is posted in case anything has changed.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Start at the department's recruiting website:** [JoinLaredoPD.com](https://www.joinlaredopd.com/). Its "hiring process" page carries the fitness test video and exam news, and it links to the City of Laredo's online job system where the police entrance exam application is filed.
2. **Before 30 November,** gather your documents: proof of citizenship, driver's license, high school diploma or GED, college transcripts if you are under 21, and military discharge papers (DD-214) if you served.
3. **From 30 November,** open the police entrance exam application through the link on JoinLaredoPD.com and submit it online. The earlier posting was titled "Police Entrance Exam Application".
4. **Submit before 12 December.** Do not leave it to the final evening.
5. **Attend the pre-agility test on 9 or 10 January,** then sit the written exam on 22 January.
6. **Afterward,** candidates who pass are placed on an eligibility list. The news report does not give the dates for later steps, so watch your email and the official site.

Questions? The recruitment office is on the first floor at 4712 Maher Ave., Laredo.

Applying is free and goes straight to the city. You do not need a recruiter or a paid "exam registration" service. Anyone charging a fee to submit your police application is not part of the Laredo Police Department.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Train to beat the minimums, not match them.** Time your 1.5-mile run every week from now.
- **Practise the wall and the drag.** Grip and leg strength matter as much as running.
- **Check the tattoo rules honestly** before you spend time on the form.
- **Order transcripts and your DD-214 now.** Missing paperwork is an easy way to lose a place.
- **Prepare for the written exam.** Brush up on reading comprehension, basic math and report writing; 70% is the pass mark.
- **Keep your record clean.** Traffic tickets and unpaid fines can come up in a background check.

Quick checklist: ID, citizenship proof, diploma, transcripts, DD-214 if relevant, running shoes, and the dates 30 November, 12 December, 9–10 January and 22 January in your phone.

## What it means for job seekers

- The window is short: 13 days.
- The department is hiring to stop losing officers to state and federal agencies, which is why pay has gone up.
- Passing the tests does not guarantee a badge, but fitness and complete paperwork are the two things you fully control.
