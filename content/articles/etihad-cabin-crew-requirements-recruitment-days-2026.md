**Etihad Airways recruits cabin crew all year through one online application, "Cabin Crew Worldwide", on its official careers site.** You must be at least 21, at least 163 cm tall, a Grade 12 graduate and fluent in English. Etihad's advert says crew can earn up to AED 10,000 a month tax-free, and the job is based in Abu Dhabi. No post count or closing date is published: Etihad reviews applications and invites the strongest candidates to an assessment day.

We rewrote this guide on 26 November 2026 using the [Etihad cabin crew careers page](https://careers.etihad.com/teams/cabin-crew) and the live [Cabin Crew Worldwide advert](https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/EtihadAirways5/744000153721915-cabin-crew), both opened that day. For visas and emigration paperwork we checked the [eMigrate portal](https://emigrate.gov.in/) run by India, Pakistan's Overseas Employment Corporation and the [MOFA attestation page](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Services/Attestation) of the UAE.

## Who can apply for Etihad cabin crew?

The careers page and the advert list the same core rules. According to Etihad, you need to be:

- **At least 21** on the day you apply
- **At least 163 cm** tall
- Holding a **Grade 12** certificate or its equivalent (high school completed)
- **Fluent in English**, both written and spoken; other languages are a plus
- Free of **tattoos or piercings that show** while wearing the cabin crew uniform

The careers page adds that make-up or plasters used to hide a tattoo are not accepted. It also asks for people who are comfortable with irregular hours on a roster, who work well in teams of many nationalities and who follow safety procedures strictly.

Etihad does not ask for a set number of years of work experience in either source. Your attitude to guests matters more in its wording. Students of private cabin crew academies may apply, but Etihad says it has no link with any academy. Paying for a course does not give you priority.

Before any event, Etihad asks applicants to read its Style and Image Guidelines. The [cabin crew requirements page](https://careers.etihad.com/legal/cabin-crew-requirements) says they cover business dress, grooming, visible tattoos, scars and birthmarks.

## What is the Etihad cabin crew salary?

Etihad now publishes a pay figure in its worldwide advert, which was posted on 19 November 2026 according to the job page's data. The table below sets out what Etihad states and nothing more.

| Item | What Etihad states |
|---|---|
| Role | Cabin Crew, full-time, Operations & Guest Experience division |
| Base | Abu Dhabi, UAE |
| Posts | Not published (ongoing worldwide recruitment) |
| Salary | Maximum of AED 10,000 monthly, no income tax; flying and layover pay included; varies with your roster |
| Arrival | 30 days of hotel accommodation, then furnished shared housing |
| Transport | Free, between housing, training and the airport |
| Leave | 30 days a year |
| Medical | Global health cover, family included |
| Travel | Free annual tickets plus travel discounts for friends and family |
| Contract | Three years, renewable |
| Closing date | None published; applications reviewed on a rolling basis |

"Up to" matters here. A new crew member with a light roster will earn less than the top figure. Etihad does not split the amount into basic pay and allowances in the advert, so ask about the breakdown if you reach the offer stage.

## How fast can you be promoted?

The advert gives a career path with minimum times. Etihad notes that each move depends on your performance and on the airline's needs.

| Move | Earliest time stated |
|---|---|
| Economy to Business Class | 12 months |
| Business Class to Cabin Senior | 12 months |
| Cabin Senior to Cabin Manager | 24 months |

## How do I apply for Etihad cabin crew? Step by step

1. **Check the basics first.** Confirm your age, height (163 cm without shoes is the safe test) and Grade 12 certificate. Check that no tattoo or piercing would show in uniform.
2. **Read the Style and Image Guidelines.** They are linked from the [cabin crew requirements page](https://careers.etihad.com/legal/cabin-crew-requirements). Plan your interview outfit and grooming from them.
3. **Prepare your files.** Have an up-to-date English CV, a copy of your school certificate and your passport details ready. List every customer-facing job with dates, even part-time work.
4. **Open the official advert.** Go to [Cabin Crew Worldwide on Etihad's careers site](https://jobs.smartrecruiters.com/EtihadAirways5/744000153721915-cabin-crew) and press "I'm interested" to start your application. You can also reach it from the "Apply now" button on [careers.etihad.com](https://careers.etihad.com/teams/cabin-crew).
5. **Track your application.** Etihad's careers site links a "My Profile" area where you can see your applications. Keep checking your email, including spam.
6. **Attend the assessment day if invited.** Expect team exercises, an aptitude test, an English test and, last, an interview on your background and reasons for applying.
7. **Wait for a formal offer.** Etihad says interviews happen face to face, by video or by phone before any offer. An offer without any interview is a warning sign.

**Deadline:** there is no closing date. The worldwide advert stays open and Etihad picks candidates as it plans events, so you can apply at any time.

## Tips to stand out on assessment day

- **Bring short real examples.** Think of a time you helped a difficult guest, and a time you fixed a problem with colleagues. Keep each story under a minute.
- **Show safety thinking.** Etihad stresses strict adherence to procedures. Mention any first aid, fire warden or safety training you have done.
- **Be a team player in group tasks.** Listen, invite quieter people to speak and help the group finish on time.
- **Mention your languages.** Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Punjabi, Malayalam, Tamil or Bengali can all count, since Etihad lists extra languages as an advantage.
- **Read the advert again the night before.** The interview often returns to why you want to live in Abu Dhabi and work rosters.

## Tips for applicants from Pakistan and India

**Attestation.** Etihad's pages do not ask for attested certificates at the application stage. If the airline asks after an offer, the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs attestation page lists a route for documents issued outside the UAE: you sign in and choose "Attestation through UAE Missions Abroad". It says the service runs through selected UAE missions, so check your local UAE embassy or consulate website first.

**Police clearance.** Neither Etihad source lists a police certificate. Apply for one only if Etihad asks in writing, and only through your own government's official service.

**Medicals.** Neither source describes a medical test for applicants. Any UAE residence medical comes after you join. Ignore anyone who says you must pay a "pre-approved" clinic before an offer.

**India: eMigrate, ECR and ECNR.** The [eMigrate portal](https://emigrate.gov.in/) of the Ministry of External Affairs names the United Arab Emirates as one of 18 ECR destinations. Where your passport carries ECR status, you need emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants before you fly out for work. ECNR passport holders can register on eMigrate by choice. The portal also says ECR workers must hold the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana insurance, with INR 10 lakh of cover. The premium is INR 275 (two-year policy) or INR 375 (three-year policy).

**Pakistan: BE&OE and the Protector of Emigrants.** The government's Overseas Employment Corporation explains on its [protector process guide](https://oec.gov.pk/protector-process-guide) that a Protector of Emigrants stamp is compulsory for every Pakistani citizen going abroad for work, including to the Gulf. You visit a Protectorate of Emigrants office, pay the fees by bank challan, give biometrics and collect your stamped passport. The OEC list of papers includes your CNIC, passport, attested job contract, a medical fitness certificate, insurance papers and four photos. The Protectorate offices work under the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment ([beoe.gov.pk](https://beoe.gov.pk/)); that site showed only a security check page when we tried on 26 November 2026, so confirm current fees there or at your nearest office once you hold an offer.

**Scam warning.** Etihad's advert carries a fraud alert. Etihad states it will never request money, your bank details or personal data while recruiting, and that any demand for payment is a scam. Never pay an agent, "academy" or WhatsApp contact for an Etihad job, visa or interview slot. More red flags are in our guide on [how to tell if a UAE job offer is real](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/).
