**Etihad Airways**, the national airline of the UAE, is recruiting cabin crew to be based in **Abu Dhabi**. Its careers site lists **11 recruitment days** between late October and mid-December 2026, including one in Abu Dhabi itself.

## Recruitment days

| Date | City |
|---|---|
| 27 October 2026 | Belgrade, Serbia |
| 29 October 2026 | Sarajevo, Bosnia |
| 1 November 2026 | Vienna, Austria |
| 3 November 2026 | Brussels, Belgium |
| 5 November 2026 | Amsterdam, Netherlands |
| 12 November 2026 | Amman, Jordan |
| 24 November 2026 | Bali, Indonesia |
| **26 November 2026** | **Abu Dhabi, UAE** |
| 8 December 2026 | Porto, Portugal |
| 10 December 2026 | Lisbon, Portugal |
| 15 December 2026 | Bangkok, Thailand |

Each event needs **registration** through Etihad's careers site, and dates can change. You can also apply online at any time through the **"Cabin Crew Worldwide"** application.

## Who can apply

| Requirement | Etihad's rule |
|---|---|
| Age | At least **21** when you apply |
| Height | At least **163 cm** |
| Education | High school graduate (**Grade 12** or equivalent) |
| English | Fluent, written and spoken |
| Tattoos | None visible in uniform. Covering them with make-up or plasters is **not** allowed |

Etihad has no link with cabin crew academies, though their students may still apply.

## How selection works

1. **Online application.** Etihad reviews it and invites strong candidates to an assessment day.
2. **Assessment day** (by invitation), which includes:
   - group activities
   - aptitude and English tests
   - a **final interview** about your experience and why you want the role

## What Etihad offers

- **Tax-free salary** and travel benefits
- **Accommodation in Abu Dhabi**
- Training in hospitality, safety, emergency procedures and aviation security
- **Three-year contracts** that can be renewed

## Tips

- **Register early** for your nearest recruitment day, since places can fill up.
- Prepare a short, honest answer to "Why Etihad?" and an example of great customer service you gave.
- Etihad does not charge for applications or assessments. Ignore anyone asking for money to secure a place.
