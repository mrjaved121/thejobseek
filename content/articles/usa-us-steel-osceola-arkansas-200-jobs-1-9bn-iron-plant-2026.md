Workers following **US hiring 2026** news have a major steel project to watch in the South. **U.S. Steel** broke ground on **Friday 30 October 2026** on a **$1.9 billion direct reduced iron (DRI) plant** at its **Big River Steel Works** in **Osceola, Arkansas**, the company announced. The plant is expected to bring about **200 full-time jobs** and around **2,000 construction jobs** at the busiest point of the build.

DRI is a form of iron made without a traditional blast furnace. The new plant will turn iron ore pellets from U.S. Steel's mines in **Minnesota** into feedstock for the electric arc furnaces at Big River.

## What jobs to expect

The company has not yet published job titles or wages for the plant. A large iron-making site like this usually needs:

- **Process operators** and control room technicians
- **Electrical and mechanical maintenance technicians**
- **Process, metallurgical and reliability engineers**
- **Logistics and rail staff** to handle ore and product
- **Safety, environmental and quality specialists**

During construction, contractors will hire **electricians, welders, pipefitters, ironworkers, crane operators and labourers**. These jobs usually come through the main building contractors and local union halls rather than U.S. Steel itself.

Big River is already a large employer. U.S. Steel opened the **$3 billion Big River 2** mill next door in 2024.

## Hiring now near Pittsburgh

If you want a steel job sooner, U.S. Steel is also holding a **hiring event on Thursday 12 November 2026, 8am to 1pm, in Duquesne, Pennsylvania**, for entry-level **utility person / production worker** roles at its Clairton, Edgar Thomson and Irvin Works, local outlet The Soul Pitt reported. Applicants need a high school diploma or GED, must pass screenings and be ready for rotating shifts. Same-day job offers are possible.

## Can foreigners apply?

Most plant and construction jobs are filled by US citizens and people already allowed to work in the US. Overseas engineers with rare skills can sometimes be sponsored, but there is no sign of that for this project.

## How to apply

Use the official [U.S. Steel careers page](https://www.ussteel.com/careers/careers-in-steel) to search open roles and set alerts for Osceola, Arkansas. Construction workers should watch for contractor hiring in Mississippi County as building ramps up.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Construction work comes first,** with up to 2,000 jobs at peak.
- **Permanent roles follow** once the plant is ready to start up.
- **Manufacturing skills transfer well:** mechanics and electricians from other heavy industries are good fits.
- **Pennsylvania job seekers** can try the 12 November hiring event now.
