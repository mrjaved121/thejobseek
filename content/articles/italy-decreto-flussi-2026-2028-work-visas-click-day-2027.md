Italy has the biggest legal work-migration programme in Europe. Its **decreto flussi** (flows decree), signed on **2 October 2025**, allows **497,550** non-EU workers to enter over three years. The **2027 application season opens on 23 October 2026**.

## Quota by year

| Category | 2026 | 2027 | 2028 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Seasonal (agriculture, tourism) | 88,000 | 89,000 | 90,000 |
| Non-seasonal employed | 76,200 | 76,200 | 76,200 |
| Self-employed | 650 | 650 | 650 |
| **Total** | **164,850** | **165,850** | **166,850** |

Within the non-seasonal quota, places for **domestic and care workers** are 13,600 in 2026, 14,000 in 2027 and 7,000 in 2028.

## 2027 dates

| Step | Date |
|---|---|
| Pre-filling of applications | 23 October 2026 (9:00) to 7 December 2026 (20:00) |
| Editing existing drafts only | 9 to 13 December 2026 |
| Click day: seasonal agriculture | 12 January 2027, 9:00 |
| Click day: seasonal tourism | 9 February 2027, 9:00 |
| Click day: non-seasonal work | 16 February 2027, 9:00 |
| Click day: domestic and care work | 18 February 2027, 9:00 |

After each click day, applications can be sent until **31 December 2027** or until the quota runs out.

## How the process works

1. An **Italian employer** offers you a job.
2. The employer applies online for a **nulla osta** (work authorisation) through the Interior Ministry's **Sportello Unico per l'Immigrazione**, logging in with SPID or CIE.
3. Once the nulla osta is issued, you apply for a **work visa** at the Italian embassy or consulate in your country.
4. After you arrive, you complete your residence permit.

## Scam warning

Fake "Europe work visa" agents are very common. Remember:

- **You cannot apply alone.** Only an employer in Italy can file the application.
- **No one can guarantee a place.** Quotas are filled on click days, first come, first served.
- **Never pay for a "job" or "visa slot".** Ask for the employer's name and check that it is a real Italian company.
