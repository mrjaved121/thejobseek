**Occidental Oman (Oxy Oman) says its Ishaam programme will secure 360 more jobs for Omani nationals between 2026 and 2028, on top of 89 jobs created in 2024 and 2025.** The figures were shared at the company's 2026 local content event in Muscat on 9 November, where it also signed three new agreements with Omani suppliers. Omanis interested in the oil and gas sector can check Occidental's [official careers site](https://www.oxy.com/careers/) and watch for roles at the firm's contractors.

## What is Oxy Oman offering?

Oxy Oman is one of the largest oil and gas producers in the Sultanate. Its **Ishaam** programme, started in 2024, is built to raise Omanisation and grow national talent across the company's supply chain.

| Programme result | Figure |
|---|---|
| Jobs provided (2024–2025) | 89 |
| Extra jobs secured (2026–2028) | 360 |
| People on specialist training | More than 200 |
| Practical training places in contracts | More than 1,100 |
| Omani SMEs supported (Tasharuk) | 17, employing about 450 staff |
| Omanisation in those SMEs | 70% |

## What were the three new deals?

The agreements, signed under the patronage of Eng. Mohsin Hamad Al Hadhrami, Undersecretary of the Ministry of Energy and Minerals, give Omani firms work supplying:

1. wellhead equipment and related services
2. variable frequency drive (VFD) units
3. flanges

More supply work for local companies usually means more jobs at those companies, from technicians to warehouse and sales staff.

## How big is Oxy Oman's local spending?

At the close of Q2 2026, **41.5%** of Oxy Oman's supply-chain spending went to local content, and **19%** went to small and medium enterprises. The company has also helped award **57 contracts** linked to local content, including supplier development and local manufacturing.

## Which roles are likely to open?

Oxy Oman did not list the 360 positions. In oil and gas supply chains, Omanisation jobs often include:

- field operators and well services technicians
- mechanical, electrical and instrument technicians
- HSE (health, safety and environment) officers
- drivers, logistics and warehouse staff
- engineers and graduate trainees

These are typical roles in the sector, not a confirmed list.

## How do I apply?

Search for Oman-based openings on [Occidental's careers site](https://www.oxy.com/careers/). Because many Ishaam jobs sit with contractors, also follow the Omani companies that win Oxy Oman contracts and register with the Ministry of Labour's job platforms.

## What it means for job seekers

- Training places (more than 1,100) can be a route into permanent work, so apply for them too.
- Technical diplomas and HSE certificates are valued by oil and gas contractors.
- Omani SMEs in the energy supply chain are hiring, with high Omanisation rates.
- Local content rules mean more contracts, and more jobs, are going to Omani firms.
