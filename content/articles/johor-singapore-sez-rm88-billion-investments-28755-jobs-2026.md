**Johor jobs** are set to grow as investment pours into the **Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone (JS-SEZ)**. Malaysia's Economy Minister **Akmal Nasrullah Mohd Nasir** said **RM88.83 billion** of investment was approved for the zone from January 2025 to the end of June 2026, enough to support as many as **28,755 jobs**.

## How many jobs are coming?

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Approved investments (Jan 2025 – Jun 2026) | RM88.83 billion |
| Potential jobs from approved investments | 28,755 |
| Investments already realised | RM40.3 billion (45%) |
| Jobs expected from realised projects | 12,796 |

The minister shared the figures after co-chairing the first Johor-Singapore cooperation ministerial committee with Singapore's National Development Minister **Chee Hong Tat**.

## What happens next?

- A **JS-SEZ blueprint** is due to be launched next month by Malaysian Prime Minister **Anwar Ibrahim** and Singapore Prime Minister **Lawrence Wong**.
- Both countries plan a **joint investment forum** in the first quarter of 2027.
- The minister said he expects even stronger investment commitments in 2027 once the blueprint is in place.

Chee said the blueprint sets out the medium- to long-term vision for the region and will need close cooperation between agencies in both countries.

## Which sectors could hire?

Special economic zones like JS-SEZ typically attract investment in:

- Manufacturing and electronics
- Data centres and digital services
- Logistics and warehousing
- Business services and regional offices
- Tourism, healthcare and education

These are typical sectors for such zones, not a confirmed list of employers.

## How do I find JS-SEZ jobs?

There is no single recruitment portal for the zone. Malaysians can search Malaysia's official MYFutureJobs portal and the careers pages of companies setting up in Johor. Singapore-based job seekers can also watch for cross-border roles.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Johor is a growth hotspot.** Billions in approved investment mean steady hiring over the next few years.
- **Cross-border skills help.** Experience working with Singapore-based companies is valuable.
- **Technical and digital skills are in demand,** especially for data centre and manufacturing projects.
