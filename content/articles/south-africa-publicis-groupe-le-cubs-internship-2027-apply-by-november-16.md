**The Publicis Groupe Le Cubs internship 2027 is open: Publicis Groupe Africa is taking applications from unemployed South African graduates for a 12-month workplace internship starting in March 2027 in Johannesburg, Cape Town and Durban.** Applications close on **16 November 2026**, and you apply directly on the company's own Le Cubs page by picking a role and clicking Apply. That leaves only a few days, so start now.

## What is Le Cubs?

Le Cubs is the advertising and media group's main graduate intake in South Africa. [Bizcommunity reports](https://www.bizcommunity.com/article/publicis-groupe-africa-opens-applications-for-le-cubs-2027-295817a) that it lasts 12 months and places graduates in working teams covering creative, strategy, media, data, technology, AI, human resources and business support.

The group hosted 30 interns in 2025 and kept 17 of them on afterwards. A further 29 joined in March 2026.

## Which internships are open?

The official page lists each internship as its own vacancy with a city code. Examples shown when we checked:

| Stream | Example roles on the official page |
|---|---|
| Creative | Art Director Intern, Digital Designer Intern (JHB), Multimedia Designer Intern (JHB, DUR), Social Copywriter Intern (JHB) |
| PR and social | PR Account Assistant Intern (JHB, CPT), Influencer Marketing Intern |
| Strategy and data | Strategic Data Intern (CPT), Data & Reporting Analyst (JHB) |
| Media | Implementation Planner (JHB), E-commerce Specialist (JHB), OOH Traffic Controller (JHB) |
| Business support | Finance, HR, Learning & Development, Project Manager Intern (CPT), Traffic Intern |

The list can change as roles fill, so rely on the live page. The stipend is not published on the page or in the announcement; ask about it if you reach an interview.

## Who can apply?

- South African citizens
- Unemployed graduates holding a relevant degree or diploma
- People who have **not** completed an internship programme before
- Available from March 2027

The company names three study areas as relevant: marketing/creative, data/tech and strategy/business.

## How to apply — step by step

1. Open the [official Le Cubs 2027 page](https://publicisgroupeafrica.com/le_cubs_internship_programme_2027/) on the Publicis Groupe Africa site.
2. Read the role descriptions and choose the one closest to your qualification and city.
3. Get your documents ready (list below) before you click, so you can finish in one sitting.
4. Click **Apply** under that role. It opens the vacancy on Publicis Groupe's careers system, where you complete the online form and upload your files.
5. Submit before the end of **16 November 2026**.

Documents to prepare:

- An updated CV, two pages at most
- A short motivation saying why you want Le Cubs and which stream you prefer
- For creative roles, a link to your portfolio
- Your South African ID, as a certified copy
- Your qualification and academic transcript, also certified

**What happens after you apply:** the company has not published its shortlisting dates or interview format. Keep an eye on the email address you applied with, and check the Le Cubs page for updates. The one fixed date is the March 2027 start.

## Is there a fee, and do I need an agent?

No to both. Apply only through the Publicis Groupe Africa page. A genuine employer does not charge for an internship application, and no third party can book you a place. If anyone asks for a "registration" or "placement" payment for Le Cubs, it is a scam. Do not pay and do not send your ID copy to them.

## How can I improve my chances?

- **Certify early.** Certified copies take time; a police station or post office queue the day before the deadline is the most common reason people miss out.
- **Make the motivation specific.** Name the stream, mention one campaign or piece of work you admire and say what you would like to learn. Generic letters read the same.
- **Creative applicants:** send a portfolio link that opens without a login, with your best three or four pieces first.
- **Avoid these mistakes:** applying for a role that does not match your qualification, uploading uncertified copies, and applying if you have already done an internship programme.

Checklist: right role, two-page CV, motivation, certified scans, tested portfolio link, sent by 16 November.
