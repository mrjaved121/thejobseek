**Jazan University** has opened **13 contract jobs** in its health sector for **Saudi nationals**, Ajel reported on 28 October 2026. These **Saudi government jobs** cover doctors, nurses and technical staff, and applications are taken through the national **Jadarat** platform. The deadline is very close, so anyone interested should apply straight away.

## The 13 roles on offer

| Grade | Specialties | Posts |
|---|---|---|
| Consultant physician | Diagnostic radiology, obstetrics and gynaecology, emergency medicine, internal medicine | 4 |
| Associate physician | Emergency medicine, diagnostic radiology, paediatrics, anaesthesia, obstetrics and gynaecology | 5 |
| Specialist | Nursing, anaesthesia, radiology | 3 |
| Technician | Sterilisation | 1 |

All of the posts are on a contract basis with the university's health services in the Jazan region of south-west Saudi Arabia.

## Who can apply

The university has set the following conditions:

- **Saudi nationality**, medical fitness and good English.
- A diploma or bachelor's degree with a grade of at least **"Good"**.
- A degree from abroad needs an **equivalency certificate** from the Ministry of Education.
- A valid **professional classification and registration** from the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS).
- Shortlisted candidates must pass a **written test and an interview**.

## Documents to prepare

- Degree certificate showing the grade point average and graduation date
- Academic transcript
- SCFHS classification card
- For experienced applicants: a social insurance (GOSI) statement and an experience letter

## Closing date

Ajel says applications close on **Sunday 21/5/1448 AH**. On the Gregorian calendar that is about **1 November 2026**, so there is very little time left. Check the exact deadline shown on the Jadarat listing before you submit.

## How to apply

Log in to **[Jadarat](https://jadarat.sa/)** with your national access (Nafath) details, search for Jazan University in the government jobs section and submit your application with the documents above. If you are new to the platform, our [step-by-step Jadarat guide](/saudi-arabia/saudi-jadarat-government-jobs-platform-how-to-apply/) explains how it works.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Apply today.** With the deadline around 1 November, waiting even one day could mean missing out.
- **Keep your SCFHS registration current.** It is required for every role here and for most health jobs in the Kingdom.
- **Scan your papers in advance.** Have your degree, transcript and GOSI statement ready as PDFs.
- **Watch for bigger rounds.** Larger health hiring drives also appear on Jadarat, such as the [4,291 jobs across 20 health clusters](/saudi-arabia/saudi-health-holding-4291-healthcare-jobs-20-clusters/).
