New **UK jobs** are coming to the Humber. Engineering manufacturer **Thurston Group** is investing millions in a new purpose-built facility at its **Ottringham** site in **East Yorkshire**, which is expected to create **around 55 jobs**. The plant is due to open in the **first half of 2027**.

## What is Thurston Group building?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Thurston Group (owned by GCH Corporation) |
| Location | Ottringham, East Yorkshire |
| New facility | 13,000 sq ft, purpose-built |
| Jobs | Around 55 |
| Opening | First half of 2027 |
| Capacity boost | 158% more specialist manufacturing capacity |

The new site belongs to the company's **Engineered Solutions** division.

## What will the factory make?

- **Blast-resistant buildings** that protect workers on industrial sites
- **Protective shelters** for vital power and energy equipment
- **Cabinets** that house hydrogen and battery storage

Customers include the energy, oil and gas, utilities and data centre sectors, many of which have a big presence around the Humber.

## How did the business get here?

Thurston Group was founded in 1970 and specialises in modular buildings. It also has a site in Wakefield. The company went into administration late last year because of client cutbacks, slow decisions and rising costs. Industrial group **GCH Corporation** bought it in a pre-pack deal on 22 December, saving the Ottringham site. The new investment is part of wider plans to grow Ottringham.

## Which jobs are likely?

Modular and engineered-structure factories typically hire:

- Welders and fabricators
- Electricians and mechanical fitters
- Joiners and assembly operatives
- Engineers, designers and project managers
- Quality and health and safety staff

These are typical roles for this type of factory; the company has not published a vacancy list.

## How do I apply?

Watch Thurston Group's official website and local Hull and East Yorkshire job listings for openings as the 2027 opening date approaches.
