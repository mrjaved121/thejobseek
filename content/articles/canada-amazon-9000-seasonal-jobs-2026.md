Looking for work before the holidays? **Amazon** is hiring about **9,000 seasonal workers across Canada** for the 2026 holiday rush. That is up by more than **25%** from the roughly 7,000 seasonal staff it hired last year, according to reports published on **20–22 October 2026**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Seasonal jobs | About 9,000 |
| Last year | About 7,000 |
| Locations | 55 Amazon sites across Canada |
| Average seasonal pay | Nearly $24 an hour |
| Average permanent pay | More than $26 an hour |
| Where to apply | hiring.amazon.ca |

## What the jobs involve

Seasonal staff will work at Amazon **fulfilment centres, sortation centres and delivery stations**. Typical tasks include:

- Stowing items on shelves
- Picking and packing customer orders
- Sorting parcels
- Getting orders ready to ship

Hours depend on the role and the site. Amazon has not given a breakdown of how many jobs are in each province or city.

## Pay and benefits

Seasonal roles pay an **average base wage of nearly $24 an hour**. Benefits start from the **first day**, including the Employee Assistance Program and a **10% discount** on groceries and everyday items bought on Amazon.ca.

Seasonal work can also lead to a permanent job. Following a $1 raise announced this autumn, Amazon says permanent staff now earn an average of **over $26 per hour**.

## How to apply

Apply through Amazon's official hiring site, [hiring.amazon.ca](https://hiring.amazon.ca). Amazon says this is its **only** official application site and applicants are **never asked to pay** anything. Jobs are posted on a rolling basis and can fill quickly, so check back often.

If you receive a suspicious job offer that claims to be from Amazon, report it to Amazon and to the **Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Apply early.** Holiday jobs go fast, and the best shifts are taken first.
- **Pick a site you can reach.** Many warehouses are outside city centres, and some shifts start early or finish late.
- **Treat it as a trial.** Good attendance and performance can turn a seasonal job into a permanent one.
- **Never pay for a job.** Ignore anyone asking for money to "secure" an Amazon position.
- **Good timing:** with Canada's job market softer this autumn, seasonal hiring is a useful way to earn income and build Canadian work experience.
