The holiday rush means big **seasonal jobs USA** hiring in the Kansas City area. In **Lenexa, Kansas**, **UPS** needs **over 1,500 seasonal staff** for the holiday peak, paying **$21 to $37 an hour**, and is holding job fairs on **6 and 7 November 2026**.

## Which UPS jobs are open and what do they pay?

| Role | Hourly pay |
|---|---|
| Seasonal support driver | $26 |
| Delivery driver | $23 |
| Driver helper | $21 |
| Warehouse worker | $21 |

UPS says the overall pay range for these seasonal roles is $21 to $37 an hour, with **flexible schedules**. Some seasonal workers go on to **permanent jobs**.

## When and where are the job fairs?

| Date | Time | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Friday, 6 November 2026 | 10am – 2pm | 14650 Santa Fe Trail Dr., Lenexa, KS |
| Saturday, 7 November 2026 | 10am – 2pm | 14650 Santa Fe Trail Dr., Lenexa, KS |

UPS asks applicants to **complete an online application first**, then come to the fair.

## Who can apply?

Exact requirements depend on the role. Typically:

- Driver roles need a valid licence and a safe driving record
- Warehouse and helper roles involve lifting, walking and working in all weather
- All applicants must be eligible to work in the US

Check each listing on the UPS jobs website for the full requirements.

## How do I apply?

Apply on the official **[UPS jobs website](https://www.jobs-ups.com/)**, then attend one of the Lenexa job fairs. If you cannot make it, you can still apply online.
