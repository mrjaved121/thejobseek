Canada's best-known AI company is set to become a major employer. **Cohere**, founded in Toronto, said on **30 October 2026** that it expects to employ **more than 1,500 people in Canada by 2031**, a strong signal for **Canada hiring in 2026** and beyond for tech graduates and engineers.

The company tied the jobs to a planned investment of **more than C$4 billion** in Canadian research and AI computing between 2024 and 2031.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Cohere |
| Total Canadian roles by 2031 | 1,500+ |
| Technical roles | About 1,000 |
| Paid student roles | 250+ by 2029 |
| Investment | Over C$4 billion (2024–2031) |
| Offices | Toronto and Montreal |

## What kinds of jobs

About two-thirds of the new roles will be highly skilled technical positions in:

- AI research
- Software and machine learning engineering
- Product development
- Infrastructure and cloud computing
- Deployment, helping businesses and governments put AI to work

The rest will cover business functions such as sales, operations and support.

## Students and graduates

Cohere is building new links with universities. It has a partnership with the **University of Toronto** on responsible AI adoption, and with the **University of Waterloo's Future of Work Institute** on certificates and **co-op placements**. It already works with Canada's three AI institutes: Amii, Vector and Mila. The **250+ paid student roles** planned by 2029 will be a good entry point for computer science and engineering students.

The company also signed a C$309 million deal for GPU cloud capacity through Bell AI Fabric, which supports the build-out.

## Why this matters for Canada's job market

Much of Canada's top AI talent has historically moved to the United States for work. A homegrown company promising more than a thousand technical roles at home gives graduates a reason to stay, and it should also create spin-off demand for cloud, data centre and consulting staff around Toronto and Montreal.

## How to apply

Open roles are posted on Cohere's official site: [cohere.com/careers](https://cohere.com/careers). Filter by Toronto or Montreal and check back often, as hiring is spread over several years.

## What it means for job seekers

- **AI skills pay off.** Python, machine learning and cloud experience are core requirements.
- **Not only PhDs.** Deployment, product and infrastructure roles suit experienced software engineers.
- **Students should target co-op** routes through Waterloo and Toronto.
- **Foreign tech workers** may find chances too, but should check whether a specific role supports a work permit.
