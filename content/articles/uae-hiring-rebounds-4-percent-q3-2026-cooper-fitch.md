Hiring in the UAE picked up again in the third quarter of 2026. The **Cooper Fitch Gulf Employment Index**, reported on **20 October 2026**, shows UAE hiring rose **4%** from July to September, the **strongest gain of the six GCC countries**.

The rise reverses a **4% fall in Q2 2026**, when the regional conflict slowed recruitment. Cooper Fitch's founder and CEO, **Dr Trefor Murphy**, described the result as a solid recovery and pointed to an 8-point swing in hiring volume between the two quarters.

## Hiring across the Gulf, Q3 2026

| Country | Change in hiring |
|---|---|
| **UAE** | **+4%** |
| Saudi Arabia | +1.5% |
| Oman | +0.5% |
| Qatar | −1% |
| Kuwait | −2% |
| Bahrain | −2% |
| **Gulf overall** | **+2%** (after −3% in Q2) |

Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain have now seen hiring fall for **two quarters in a row**.

## Which jobs are growing

| Sector | Change in Q3 2026 |
|---|---|
| Energy | **+7%** |
| Senior finance | +6% |
| Strategy | +6% (first rise this year) |
| Finance | +5% |
| Real estate | +4% |
| Software | +4% |
| Cyber security | +4% |
| UAE public sector | +3% |
| Sales and marketing | +3% |
| Banking | −4% |
| Investment management | −5% |

Energy led the way as delayed projects restarted, helped by multi-year gas contracts in Saudi Arabia and the UAE. The report says specialists such as **project directors, commissioning specialists and contracts leads** are hard to find, and strong candidates often hold several offers.

## Month by month in the UAE

According to Murphy, April was the weakest month and May was still down, but June improved, and **September was strong**, roughly level with September in 2024 and 2025.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Momentum is back.** If you paused your UAE job search earlier this year, Q4 is a better time to restart.
- **Specialists are in demand.** Energy, finance, cyber security and software skills are getting the most attention.
- **Employers are still cautious.** Many firms are planning for 2027, so expect careful, longer hiring processes.
