This page brings together the main rules of the **UAE Labour Law** for private-sector workers, in plain English. Each section links to a detailed guide with worked examples.

The law is **Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021**, and it is enforced by the **Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE)**.

## The rules at a glance

| Topic | The rule | Detailed guide |
|---|---|---|

| Annual leave | **30 days** a year after 1 year; 2 days a month from 6 months to 1 year | [Annual leave rules](/uae/uae-annual-leave-rules-private-sector/) |
| Probation | Max **6 months**, cannot be extended | [Probation rules](/uae/uae-probation-period-rules-notice/) |

## Your contract

- All private-sector contracts are now **fixed-term**. They can be renewed, and renewals count towards gratuity.
- Work can be full-time, part-time, temporary or flexible.
- Make sure your contract on the MoHRE system shows the same **basic salary** as your offer letter. Gratuity, overtime and leave pay are all calculated on basic salary, not total pay.

## Pay and the Wage Protection System

Under **Ministerial Resolution No. 340 of 2026**, wages for each month are due on the **first day of the following month** and must be paid through the **Wage Protection System (WPS)** or another approved system. Pay after that date is legally treated as delayed.

## Leaving a job

- **During probation:** give 1 month's notice to move to another UAE employer, or 14 days to leave the UAE. An employer must give you 14 days.
- **After probation:** follow the notice in your contract, between 30 and 90 days.
- **Without notice:** only in specific cases, such as unpaid wages that the employer fails to fix after you have informed MoHRE.
- **Unfair dismissal:** a court can award up to **3 months' wages** in compensation.

## Health and safety at work

- After **5 hours in a row**, you are entitled to at least **1 hour** of breaks.
- From **15 June to 15 September**, outdoor work in the sun is banned between **12:30 pm and 3 pm**.

## Visas and working legally

- You **cannot work on a visit visa**. Your employer must get you a work permit and residence visa. Read: [Can you work on a visit visa in the UAE?](/uae/can-you-work-on-visit-visa-in-uae/)
- Your employer pays for your work permit. Being asked to pay for a job or visa is a classic scam sign. Read: [Warning signs of a fake UAE job offer](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/)

## How to get help

1. Put your complaint to your employer **in writing** and keep a copy.
2. If it is not resolved, file a complaint with **MoHRE** through its app, website or call centre on **600590000**. It is free, and you do not need a lawyer.
3. Keep your contract, payslips, bank statements and any messages as evidence.

> This guide is general information, not legal advice. Rules can change, so check MoHRE's official channels for your own case. We update this page when the rules change.
