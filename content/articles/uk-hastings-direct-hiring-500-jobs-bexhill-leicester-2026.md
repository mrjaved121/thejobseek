Graduates and career changers interested in insurance have a fresh target. **Hastings Direct**, the UK online car and home insurer, plans to add **500-plus staff within a year**, mostly at its **Bexhill-on-Sea** and **Leicester** sites. It wants experienced hires, **graduates and apprentices**, so watch its official careers channels for new adverts.

## What has Hastings Direct announced?

The firm is growing fast and expects to pass **5 million customers** soon. To serve them it is adding people across many teams, while also spending more on its app, automation and AI. Chief executive **Toby van der Meer** said the aim is to grow customer numbers faster than headcount, but the business still needs hundreds of new staff.

## Which roles are open?

The hiring covers seven broad areas:

- **Customer service** advisers
- **Claims** handlers and specialists
- **Pricing** analysts
- **Underwriting**
- **Commercial** teams
- **Data** roles, such as analysts and data scientists
- **Technology**, including software and digital jobs

Exact job titles and salaries were not published; the list above reflects the teams named in the announcement.

## Who can apply?

People with insurance or financial services experience are wanted, but so are school leavers and new graduates through apprentice and graduate routes. Data, maths, IT and economics graduates may find pricing and data roles a good fit. Applicants need the right to work in the UK (or in Gibraltar for roles there).

## Why is this news important?

The plan stands out in an insurance market where some rivals are shrinking teams. Back in September, Admiral announced plans to shed roughly 500 jobs in Britain. Specialist skills in claims, pricing and data are in short supply, so Hastings joining the race for talent could push up pay and training offers for candidates.

## How do I apply?

Search for "Hastings Direct careers" and use the company's own careers site, filtering by Bexhill, Leicester or London. Avoid paying any agency or website that claims to guarantee a place.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Good entry route into finance:** customer service and claims roles often need no degree and lead to promotion.
- **Graduate pathway:** pricing, data and technology teams suit numerate graduates.
- **Two regional hubs:** Bexhill and Leicester are cheaper places to live than London.
- **Flexible working:** the company says it offers flexible arrangements across sites.
