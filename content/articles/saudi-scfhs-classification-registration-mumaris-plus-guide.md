Every doctor, nurse, pharmacist and allied health worker who wants to practise in Saudi Arabia must be licensed by the **Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS)**. The whole process runs online through the commission's **Mumaris Plus** system. It has two separate stages, and many applicants confuse them.

## Stage 1: Professional Classification

Classification is where SCFHS reviews your degree and experience and decides your **professional grade**, for example technician, specialist or consultant. You can apply for it **while still outside Saudi Arabia**.

You will usually need:

- Your degree, internship certificate and academic transcript
- A clinical training certificate, if your course included training
- Ministry of Education equivalence for degrees earned outside the Kingdom
- A licence or experience certificate from your home country
- Your passport
- A medical report if you are over 65

A transcript alone is not enough to be classified. If your qualification is not on the approved Mumaris Plus list, you first apply for a **Qualification Study**, and only then for classification.

### Document verification

Your certificates are checked at source by **Dataflow**. SCFHS needs Dataflow's final report before it can complete your file, so if the report is late, follow up with Dataflow directly.

## The exam and how long decisions take

SCFHS decides your classification file in one of three ways. The official service times are:

| Route | Decision time |
|---|---|
| Direct decision (no exam) | 7 working days |
| Written computer exam (Prometric or Pearson VUE) | 30 working days |
| Practical evaluation | 35 working days |

Some specialties also have an **oral exam** with one to four clinical stations. It lasts up to 90 minutes, but SCFHS advises keeping the whole day free. The exam link is emailed to your Mumaris Plus address **48 hours** before the test, and the result appears in your account within **14 working days**.

## Stage 2: Professional Registration

Registration is what actually lets you work. You can only apply once you are **legally resident in Saudi Arabia** with an Iqama. You need:

- A valid resident ID
- A job identification letter signed and stamped by your employer's HR, dated within the last 3 months

The service time is **5 working days**, and registration is valid for **two years**.

## Renewal and CPD hours

You can renew up to **three months before** your registration ends. A late-renewal fine is issued once the registration has been expired for 31 days. If it has been expired for more than a year, you must apply again from the start.

Renewal needs continuing professional development (CPD) hours:

| Profession | CPD hours per registration period |
|---|---|
| Technicians | 20 |
| Specialists | 40 |
| Pharmacists | 40 |
| Doctors | 60 |

If you are short of hours, SCFHS can grant a **one-year renewal**, but only once.

## Practical tips

- **Start classification before you accept a job offer.** It can be done from home, and it is usually the slowest part.
- **Fees are non-refundable**, so check your eligibility before paying.
- Keep your Mumaris Plus email address active, because exam links and results arrive there.
- Be wary of anyone selling "guaranteed" exam passes. Only SCFHS issues decisions.
