US employers are posting more jobs than a year ago, and the gains are spreading to more types of work. That is the main message from **Indeed Hiring Lab's** October snapshot, published on **22 October 2026**.

Indeed's **Job Postings Index** reached **103.8** on **16 October**, which is **2.8% higher than a year earlier**. Twelve months ago the index was falling by more than 8% a year, so the turnaround is clear.

## Key numbers

| Indicator | Latest reading |
|---|---|
| Job Postings Index (16 Oct 2026) | 103.8 (Feb 2020 = 100) |
| Change on a year earlier | +2.8% |
| Occupation groups with more postings than last year | 68% (49% in early September) |
| AI-related share of all postings | 7.3% |
| Data centre postings, last three months | +20% |
| Posted wage growth (September) | 2.4% a year |
| Unemployment rate (September) | 4.2% |

## Where the openings are

Indeed singles out **engineering** as the standout. Mechanical, civil and electrical engineering are three of the five strongest sectors in its index.

| Sector | Index level (approx.) | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering | ~145 | Highest of the main groups |
| Health care | ~131 | Physicians, surgeons and therapy roles strong |
| Production and manufacturing | ~124 | Well above pre-pandemic |
| Human resources | ~99 | Around 2020 level |
| Software development | ~79 | Still below 2020 |

**Data centres** are a hot spot. Postings tied to them have jumped by a fifth in three months and are about two and a half times their mid-2024 level. Wages for electrical engineers advertised on Indeed are up **5.6%** on a year ago, and IT systems roles are up **4.7%**.

## The catch

The market is better, not booming. There is about **one opening per unemployed person**, below the 2019 ratio. Advertised pay is rising more slowly than prices, and new postings have been moving sideways rather than climbing.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Aim at engineering and the trades around it.** Electrical, mechanical and civil skills are in short supply, and pay is rising fastest there.
- **Health care stays reliable.** Clinical and therapy roles continue to post well above pre-pandemic levels.
- **Look at data centres.** Construction, electrical, cooling and operations roles at data centre sites are growing fast, even where general tech hiring is flat.
- **Add AI skills to any CV.** More than 7% of all postings now mention AI, a record share.
- **Pure software roles are still tough.** Pair coding skills with cloud, data or AI experience to stand out.
