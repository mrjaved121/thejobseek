New **Oman jobs** opportunities are opening for self-employed nationals. Oman's **National Employment Program (NEP)** has published a guide that lets government bodies award services and projects worth **up to OMR 5,000** directly to **Omani freelancers** who are not in regular employment. The guide was reported on 2 November 2026.

## What has the National Employment Program announced?

The NEP released a manual called the *Guide to Procedures for Contracting with Independent Employers in the Sultanate of Oman*. It puts into practice the Ministry of Finance's **Financial Circular No. (1) of 2026**, which followed a decision by the Council of Ministers' Financial and Economic Committee.

In simple terms, ministries and other public bodies now have a clear, standard way to give paid work to Omani freelancers instead of only to companies.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Issued by | National Employment Program (NEP) |
| Legal basis | Ministry of Finance Financial Circular No. (1) of 2026 |
| Who benefits | Non-employed Omani freelancers |
| Maximum contract value | OMR 5,000 |
| Type of work | Services, project execution, specialised product supply |
| Where jobs are posted | Official government platforms, licensed freelance channels, official media |

## What kind of work is covered?

The framework applies to three types of work:

- **Service delivery** – for example design, translation, training, consulting or technical support
- **Project execution** – short, defined projects with a clear deliverable
- **Specialised product supply** – items that a skilled freelancer can provide

## What rules must freelancers follow?

Each contract must clearly state:

- The details of both parties and the freelancer's **official freelance licence number**
- The scope of work and delivery timeline
- The payment schedule
- Data confidentiality and intellectual property terms

Freelancers must do the work **themselves**. Sub-contracting is only allowed with the agency's prior approval. Work and documents must be delivered on time, or within seven business days if a contract ends early. Payments can be made in stages linked to agreed milestones.

## How do I apply?

According to the guide, projects will be advertised through official government platforms, licensed freelance channels and official media outlets. Make sure your freelance licence is valid and watch official NEP and ministry announcements for posted projects. The guide does not name a single application portal, so only trust official government channels.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Get licensed first.** Without an official freelance licence, you cannot take part.
- **Build a short portfolio.** Government buyers will want proof that you can deliver.
- **Price carefully.** Each contract under this route is capped at OMR 5,000.
- **It supports Vision 2040.** The move is part of Oman's push to grow local talent and the freelance economy.
