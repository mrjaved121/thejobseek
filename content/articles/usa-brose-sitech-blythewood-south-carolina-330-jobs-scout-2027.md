Car seat maker **Brose Sitech** is building its **first plant in the United States**, creating about **330 jobs** in **Blythewood, South Carolina**, South Carolina Public Radio reported on 28 October 2026. The $29.5 million factory will supply seats to electric vehicle start-up Scout Motors, and is one of the newest **USA jobs for foreigners** and local workers in the fast-growing South Carolina car industry.

## About the plant

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Brose Sitech |
| Location | Scout Motors Supplier Park, Blythewood, Richland County, SC |
| Investment | $29.5 million |
| Jobs | About 330 |
| Size | 140,000 sq ft |
| Products | Complete seat sets for Scout Traveler SUV and Scout Terra pickup |
| Opening | Expected in 2027 |

The seats will be delivered **just in time** to the nearby Scout Motors assembly plant, so production at Brose Sitech will be closely linked to Scout's build schedule.

## Who is Brose Sitech?

Brose Sitech is a seating supplier headquartered in **Poland**. It is a joint venture between German auto parts group **Brose** and **Volkswagen**, and employs more than **8,000 people** at 22 sites in nine countries. Its North American headquarters in **Auburn Hills, Michigan** will look after sales, engineering, HR and other support functions for the new plant.

## What jobs to expect

The company describes the 330 jobs as **manufacturing and support** roles, and says it plans to **hire and train local workers**. A seat assembly plant usually recruits:

- **Production associates** for seat assembly lines
- **Maintenance technicians** for robots and conveyors
- **Quality inspectors**
- **Logistics and materials handlers**
- **Supervisors, engineers and HR staff**

Wages and incentives were not disclosed.

## A note for international job seekers

To take these jobs you will need the right to work in the US. Brose Sitech has not said whether it will sponsor visas, and factory floor roles are rarely sponsored. Engineering and management posts are more likely candidates if sponsorship is offered later.

## How to apply

Brose Sitech says openings will be posted on the **careers section of its official website**. Search for South Carolina or Blythewood roles there. Because the plant opens in 2027, the first hires are likely to be managers, engineers and trainers, with production hiring later.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Get in early.** Supervisors and technicians hired first often help train later teams.
- **Factory skills transfer.** Experience at other auto suppliers in the region is a strong selling point.
