More Omani women are working than ever before. Figures from Oman's **National Centre for Statistics and Information (NCSI)**, published by the Oman News Agency on **17 October 2026**, show **270,696** Omani women in employment in **2025**, a rise of **9%** on the year before.

## Key numbers

| Indicator | 2025 |
|---|---|
| Employed Omani women | 270,696 (+9%) |
| Newly appointed Omani women | 31,808 |
| Economic participation rate (15+) | 32.9% (2024: 32.4%) |
| Share of active women who are employed | About 85% |
| Women as share of employed Omanis | About 30% |
| Women in specialist professions | About 25% of working women |

## Education is feeding the workforce

The same data shows women are studying longer:

| Education indicator | Figure |
|---|---|
| Women in higher education (2024/25) | 79,250 (+5%) |
| Women with postgraduate qualifications | Up about 11% |

More graduates mean more women qualified for professional and technical jobs, which helps explain why a quarter of working Omani women are now in **specialist scientific, technical and human science** roles.

## The wider picture

Overall Omani employment has been rising, with the **private sector** adding jobs faster than government. Official data for mid-2026 showed private-sector employment of Omanis up **8.6%** on a year earlier. Oman has also set targets to create tens of thousands of jobs for citizens each year, including in the [digital economy](/oman/oman-digital-economy-programme-3000-tech-jobs-a-year-2026-2030/).

## What it means for job seekers

- **Employers are hiring Omani women in larger numbers.** Banking, education, health, government and retail are common fields.
- **Postgraduate study pays off.** Specialist roles are a growing share of women's jobs.
- **The private sector is the growth area.** Look beyond government jobs to companies with Omanisation targets.
- **Expats:** Roles with Omanisation quotas are increasingly filled by trained Omani staff, including women, so focus on specialist skills.

Oman is also seeing more jobs for [Omanis with disabilities](/oman/oman-employment-persons-with-disabilities-rises-10-percent-2025/).
