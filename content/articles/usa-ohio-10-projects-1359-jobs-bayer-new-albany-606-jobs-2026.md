Ohio has approved a fresh wave of job-creating projects. Governor **Mike DeWine** announced on **26 October 2026** that the **Ohio Tax Credit Authority** backed **10 projects** expected to create **1,359 new jobs**, add more than **$142.3 million in new annual payroll** and bring in over **$2.2 billion** of investment. The projects will also keep **1,824 existing jobs** in the state.

The largest by far is **Bayer's** planned pharmaceutical campus in **New Albany**, near Columbus.

## All 10 projects

| Employer | Location | New jobs | New annual payroll |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bayer U.S. / Bayer HealthCare | New Albany | 606 | $69.2 million |
| Rittal | Urbana | 182 | $12.0 million |
| Avtron Power Solutions | Valley View and Seven Hills | 150 | $10.9 million |
| Delaware Nation Investments | Beavercreek | 125 | $15 million |
| Forgent Power | Columbus | 85 | $12 million |
| AMEND Consulting | Cincinnati | 80 | $6.8 million |
| Certus Medical | Westerville | 45 | $7.3 million |
| Kyocera Senco Industrial Tools | Anderson Township | 37 | $3.2 million |
| Spray Products Corporation | Medina | 32 | $2.2 million |
| J.F. Taylor | Beavercreek | 17 | $3.6 million |

The projects were brought forward by **JobsOhio** and its regional partners. Tax credits are tied to the companies actually creating the jobs and payroll they promised.

## Bayer's New Albany campus

Bayer revealed earlier in October a plan for a **$2.2 billion** pharmaceutical manufacturing site in the **New Albany International Business Park**, home to other big health employers. Key points:

- About **600 jobs**, now confirmed as 606 in the state approval
- A flexible, modular campus making medicines for cancer, heart and kidney conditions
- High levels of automation
- First production module expected to be running in **2031**

To prepare local workers, Bayer plans to use the new **Ohio Life Science Training Center**, due to open in summer 2027, to train biomanufacturing operators and technicians.

## Types of jobs across the projects

With power equipment makers, an enclosure manufacturer, consultants and a medical device firm in the mix, the openings will be varied:

- Pharmaceutical operators, lab technicians and quality staff (Bayer)
- Production, assembly and warehouse workers (Rittal, Avtron, Forgent, Kyocera Senco)
- Electrical and mechanical engineers
- Consultants, analysts and data specialists (AMEND Consulting)
- Medical device and engineering roles (Certus Medical)

## What it means for job seekers

- **Most hiring will be gradual.** Most projects have until the end of 2029 to reach their job numbers; Bayer has until 2034.
- **Biomanufacturing is a growth area** in central Ohio. Training as a lab or process technician now could pay off when Bayer starts hiring.
- **Electrical and power equipment is a strong field.** Several of the approved firms make power or industrial equipment, a sector that is hiring across the US.
- **Check each company's own careers page** for openings, and be wary of agencies charging fees.
