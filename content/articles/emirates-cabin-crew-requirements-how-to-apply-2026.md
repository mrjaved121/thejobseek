**Emirates hires cabin crew all year, and you apply on the [Emirates Group careers website](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/cabin-crew/), not through an agent.** The airline asks for applicants aged 21 or over, at least 160 cm tall with a 212 cm reach, with Grade 12 education and one year in hospitality or customer service. Emirates publishes no post count and no deadline. Instead, you create a profile and Emirates invites you to a recruitment day in or near your city.

This guide was rewritten on 25 November 2026 from the official Emirates cabin crew page and its recruitment fraud page, both opened that day, plus India's eMigrate portal and the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs for the paperwork steps.

## Who can apply for Emirates cabin crew?

The Emirates Group careers page sets these minimums. You should be:

- Aged 21 or older
- 160 cm or taller, with a standing reach of 212 cm
- Fluent in written and spoken English (other languages help)
- A team player who is comfortable with people
- Able to meet UAE employment visa requirements

And you need to have:

- At least one year of hospitality or customer service experience
- High school (Grade 12) education or higher
- No tattoos that show while wearing the Emirates uniform

Two uniform points are worth knowing before you apply. The page says alternative head coverings, including the hijab, are not part of the current uniform, which includes the red hat and cream scarf. It asks applicants to be sure they are comfortable with the uniform and grooming rules first.

## Emirates cabin crew salary and benefits

Emirates publishes its starting pay for Grade II (Economy Class). It describes the figures as approximate, because flying pay depends on your roster.

| Item | What the Emirates careers page states |
|---|---|
| Basic salary | AED 4,980 a month |
| Flying pay | AED 69.6 an hour, based on an average of 80–100 hours a month |
| Average monthly pay | About AED 11,244 (USD 3,061) |
| Meal allowance | Paid for overseas night stops, one month in arrears |
| Housing | Furnished shared flat, free, utilities included |
| Transport | To and from work and the training college |
| Leave | 30 calendar days a year plus one free annual ticket home |
| Medical | Medical and dental care at Emirates clinics; worldwide life and accident cover |
| End of service | Non-contributory lump sum at the end of the contract |
| Base | Dubai only |

Crew live with two or three colleagues of the same gender, each with a private bedroom. You must live in crew housing unless your husband or wife already lives in the UAE, or you have close family there. The airline says crew get about eight days off a month in Dubai.

## Where Emirates is recruiting now

The events list on 25 November 2026 showed walk-in open days in cities such as Sofia, Calgary and Venice, and invite-only assessment days in many others. For our readers, the key entries were:

- **Delhi, Kolkata and Mumbai:** "Recruiting now", invite only. You must apply online to be invited.
- **Dubai:** "Recruiting now", invite only, also through an online application.
- **Pakistan:** listed as a country in the filter, but no event was shown on that date.

Emirates says that if your city is not listed, you should still apply online and it will tell you when it visits nearby. You may also attend an open day in another country. If you plan that, check the exact date on the official events list before you buy a ticket, because events change.

## How do I apply for Emirates cabin crew? Step by step

1. **Check you meet every rule above.** Measure your standing reach on tiptoe against a wall: 212 cm with one arm.
2. **Prepare your documents as digital copies on your phone.** Emirates wants a current English CV, your school or college certificate and a PDF of your passport. You also need a valid photo ID to enter the venue; if you have no passport yet, a national ID or driving licence is accepted at the assessment day.
3. **Create your account.** Go to [emiratesgroupcareers.com/cabin-crew](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/cabin-crew/) and press Apply. The cabin crew form has three parts: your CV, general information and job-specific questions.
4. **Wait for an invitation, or walk in.** For invite-only events (including all Indian cities listed now) you need an email invitation. For walk-in open days, arrive on time on the date shown.
5. **Attend the recruitment day.** Emirates describes three stages: an introduction to the role and life in Dubai, an assessment with group activities and an online test at the venue, and a final interview about your motivation and customer service experience.
6. **If you cannot attend,** reply to your invitation email and ask for another date.
7. **After selection,** fill in a self-declaration medical form and a dental form. Emirates says you pay any costs for these. Further medicals for your residence visa and for GCAA rules are done after you join.
8. **Visa and joining.** Emirates says it processes the work permit and visa for selected candidates. You then move to Dubai for training.

No closing date is published. Recruitment runs throughout the year, so check the events list on the official careers site for current dates.

## What happens in Emirates cabin crew training?

New crew train for about seven and a half weeks at the Emirates training college in Dubai. The careers page lists the stages:

| Stage | Length |
|---|---|
| Induction | 3 days |
| Safety and emergency procedures | 13 days |
| Group medical training (CPR, burns, emergency childbirth) | 5 days |
| Security training | 2 days |
| Emirates hospitality | 2 days |
| Uniform and grooming | 1 day |
| Service training on A380 and B777 mock-ups | 14 days |

You get your first roster on graduation day and fly first as an extra crew member while your work is observed. All staff are on probation for six months, and you can take leave after that.

## Tips that help on the day

- **Show your service work clearly.** Your CV should list each customer-facing job with dates, so the one-year rule is easy to see. Hotels, restaurants, shops and call centres all involve customers.
- **Prepare two short stories:** one about calming an upset customer and one about solving a problem in a team. The interview asks about both kinds of experience.
- **Practise speaking in a group.** In group tasks, listen, let others speak and help the team finish. Talking the most is not the goal.
- **Follow the dress code.** The events page links a separate dress guide for women and men; read it before the day.
- **List your languages.** Hindi, Urdu, Punjabi, Bengali, Tamil or Arabic all count as extra languages.

## Tips for applicants from Pakistan and India

**Attestation.** Emirates' page does not say certificates must be attested. If the airline asks for it after selection, the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs [attestation service](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Services/Attestation) offers a route called attestation through UAE missions abroad for papers issued in other countries. Only do this when Emirates asks, so you do not pay for papers you may not need.

**Police clearance.** The cabin crew page does not list a police certificate. Get one only if Emirates requests it in writing, from your own passport office or police service.

**Medicals.** Emirates uses its own forms and checks. Ignore any "approved Emirates medical centre" that contacts you before you have a written offer.

**India: eMigrate, ECR and ECNR.** India's Ministry of External Affairs, on the [eMigrate portal](https://emigrate.gov.in/), lists the UAE among the 18 ECR countries and regions. If your passport is stamped ECR, you need emigration clearance through eMigrate from the Protector of Emigrants before you travel for work. Holders of ECNR passports are exempt from clearance, though the portal lets them sign up voluntarily. ECR workers must also buy the Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana policy, the portal says, with INR 10 lakh of cover; it costs INR 275 for a two-year policy or INR 375 for a three-year one.

**Pakistan: BE&OE and the Protector of Emigrants.** Pakistani workers going abroad for a job register with the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment (BE&OE) and get Protector of Emigrants clearance before departure. The bureau's website, [beoe.gov.pk](https://beoe.gov.pk/), did not open for us on 25 November 2026, so we have not listed fees or documents here. Check the current list on that site or at your nearest Protectorate office once you have an offer.

**Scam warning.** The Emirates [recruitment fraud page](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/recruitment-fraud/) says any job offer asking for money is fraudulent, and that Emirates never asks for advance payment for travel, visas or work permits. Real emails come only from Emirates Group addresses such as @emirates.com, and the airline will never ask for your careers account password. Offers sent through WhatsApp or Gmail-type addresses are likely fake. Do not hand money to any agent who promises an Emirates job, visa or interview. More red flags are in our article on [spotting fake UAE job offers](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/).
