Emirates recruits cabin crew from around the world all year, and it is one of the most searched jobs in Dubai. Here is what the airline asks for and how its selection process works, based on the official Emirates Group careers page.

## Minimum requirements

| Requirement | What Emirates asks for |
|---|---|
| Age | At least 21 |
| Height and reach | At least 160 cm tall, and able to reach 212 cm |
| Education | High school (Grade 12) or higher |
| Experience | At least 1 year in hospitality or customer service |
| Language | Fluent written and spoken English; other languages are a plus |
| Appearance | No tattoos that would be visible in the Emirates uniform |
| Visa | Able to meet UAE employment visa requirements |

## How selection works

1. **Apply online.** Create a profile on the Emirates Group careers website. Emirates uses it to invite candidates to recruitment events in or near their city.
2. **Attend an open day or assessment day.** These are held **in person only**. Emirates does not run online assessment days.
3. **Group activities.** Expect teamwork, communication and problem-solving exercises.
4. **Individual interview.** Questions focus on why you want the role and your customer service experience.
5. **Training in Dubai.** Successful candidates complete roughly **seven and a half weeks** of training before flying.

If you cannot attend on your invited date, reply to the invitation email to ask for another one.

## What to bring

- An updated CV
- Photo ID. If you don't have a passport yet, a national ID or driving licence is fine for the event; a passport is requested after selection.

## Tips from the requirements

- **Count your customer service experience carefully.** Hotel, restaurant, retail and call-centre jobs all count as customer-facing work.
- **Practise the reach test** on your toes: 212 cm, with one arm.
- **Prepare examples** of handling difficult customers and working in a team. These come up in the interview.

## Stay safe

Emirates **never charges** candidates to apply, attend an open day or get a job. Apply only through the official Emirates Group careers website. Anyone offering a "guaranteed" cabin crew job for a fee is running a scam. Read our guide to [spotting fake UAE job offers](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/).
