Since 2020, workers in Qatar no longer need their employer's **No Objection Certificate (NOC)** to move to a new job. Here is how changing employers works and what notice you must give.

## The basics

- You can change jobs **at any time during your contract**, without an NOC.
- Apply through the **Ministry of Labour's electronic notification system** using the signed and stamped **"Change Employer" form**. You will receive a reference number.

## How much notice to give

| Your situation | Notice |
|---|---|
| During probation (up to 6 months) | Apply **at least 1 month** before leaving |
| Up to 2 years with your employer | **1 month** |
| More than 2 years with your employer | **2 months** |

Your employer must give you the **same notice** if they end your contract.

## If you leave before the notice ends

You must pay your employer your **basic wage for each day** of notice you didn't work.

*Example:* basic wage QAR 1,500, one-month notice, you stop two weeks early → you owe about **QAR 750**.

If your **employer** ends your contract without notice, they must pay you basic wage for the notice period.

## What you are owed when you move

If you have worked **at least one year**, you are entitled to your **end-of-service benefit**, **accrued annual leave** and other benefits in your contract when you change employers. In some cases, your employer must also pay your **return ticket**.

## When you can leave without notice

If your employer **hasn't met legal obligations**, such as **not paying your wages**, you can change jobs **without notice** and still claim unpaid wages, end-of-service benefits (after more than one year) and pro rata annual leave.

## Important warning

If you **leave Qatar without completing your notice**, you may not be able to return to work in Qatar for **one year**.
