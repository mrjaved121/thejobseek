Tech talent looking for **Edinburgh jobs** has a new opportunity in medical technology. US firm **Spacelabs Healthcare** is creating **30 jobs**, mostly in **software engineering and cybersecurity**, after investing **£8.7 million** in a new base at **Edinburgh Park**. The move more than doubles its Scottish team, from **22 to 52** people.

## What has Spacelabs announced?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Spacelabs Healthcare, part of OSI Systems |
| Location | Edinburgh Park, Edinburgh |
| Investment | £8.7 million |
| New jobs | 30 |
| Main skills | Software engineering, cybersecurity |
| Team size | From 22 to 52 |
| Public support | £3.1 million grant from Scottish Enterprise |

The new office and lab is about **four times bigger** than its old base in Moray Place. Spacelabs has been in Edinburgh for almost **40 years**.

## What does Spacelabs do?

Spacelabs develops **patient monitoring** and **diagnostic cardiology** technology used in hospitals worldwide. Its Edinburgh team works on research and development for the next generation of these products.

## Could there be more jobs later?

Yes. The company says it is already considering growing the Edinburgh operation by a **further 20–30%** as it keeps investing in technology. OSI Systems CEO **Ajay Mehra** said Edinburgh's highly trained workforce was a key reason for investing.

## Which roles are likely?

Medtech software teams typically hire:

- Embedded and application software engineers
- Cybersecurity engineers and analysts
- Test and verification engineers
- DevOps and cloud engineers
- Regulatory and quality specialists

These are typical roles for this kind of team, not a confirmed vacancy list.

## How do I apply?

Look for Edinburgh vacancies on the careers pages of Spacelabs Healthcare and its parent company, OSI Systems.
