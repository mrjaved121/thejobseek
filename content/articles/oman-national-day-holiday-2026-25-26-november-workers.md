Oman's **National Day** holiday this year falls on **Wednesday 25** and **Thursday 26 November 2026**, according to the **Ministry of Labour's** holiday announcements reported on **22 October 2026**. Because it runs straight into the Friday–Saturday weekend, many workers will enjoy **four days off**, from 25 to 28 November.

## Holiday dates

| Day | Date |
|---|---|
| National Day holiday | Wednesday, 25 November 2026 |
| National Day holiday | Thursday, 26 November 2026 |
| Weekend | Friday 27 and Saturday 28 November |
| Back to work | Sunday, 29 November |

## Oman's 2026 holiday calendar

Oman now announces official holidays in advance, so employers and staff can plan. Holidays set so far for 2026:

| Occasion | Date |
|---|---|
| Sultan's Accession Day | Thursday, 15 January |
| Isra and Mi'raj | Sunday, 18 January |
| Hijri New Year | Thursday, 18 June |
| Prophet's Birthday | Thursday, 27 August |
| National Day | Wednesday–Thursday, 25–26 November |

Eid holidays depend on moon sighting and are announced separately.

## What about workers who are on duty?

Some jobs cannot stop, such as hospitals, utilities, security, hotels and shops. The ministry's guidance says government bodies and private businesses that need to keep operating must follow the holiday schedule and **compensate staff** who work on those days. Check your contract and ask HR how compensation is given, whether as extra pay or days off in lieu.

## What it means for job seekers and workers

- **Plan travel early.** Flights and hotels fill up around the long weekend.
- **Know your rights.** If you are asked to work, you should receive compensation.
- **Hiring slows briefly.** Interviews and onboarding often pause during holidays, so time applications for early November or December.
- **Check your employer's notice.** Companies usually confirm holiday arrangements by internal circular.
