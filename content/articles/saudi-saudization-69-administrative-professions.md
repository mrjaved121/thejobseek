If you are looking for an office or administrative job in Saudi Arabia, check the latest Saudization rules first. The Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD) has added **69 administrative support professions** to the list of jobs that must be done **only by Saudi nationals** in the private sector.

The update took effect on **5 April 2026**, according to the Saudi Press Agency. Reports say 19 of the professions applied immediately and the other 50 followed after a six-month grace period, on **4 October 2026**, so all 69 are now in force.

## Which jobs are covered

The 69 professions fall under the Unified Saudi Occupational Classification and include roles in:

- secretarial work
- translation
- data entry
- general administrative support

The rule applies to every private establishment that employs **one or more workers** in these professions.

HRSD has published a procedural guide on its website listing all 69 professions and explaining how the rule is applied.

## What this means for expat job seekers

- **Avoid offers for these roles.** A Saudi employer cannot legally hire an expat into a 100% Saudized profession. An offer like this may be a scam or lead to problems with your work permit later.
- **Check the job title on your visa.** Your official profession must match a role open to expats.
- **Look at technical and specialised roles instead.** Saudization is applied profession by profession, so many skilled, technical and specialist jobs remain open to foreign workers.
- **Already working in one of these roles?** Speak to your employer about your status and options, and keep your documents in order.

HRSD says companies that do not comply face regulatory penalties.

Saudization is also rising in other fields: **60% of 18 marketing and sales jobs** must now go to Saudis, and a new Nitaqat phase targets more than 340,000 jobs. Read our [Saudization 2026 update](/saudi-arabia/saudi-saudization-marketing-sales-60-percent-nitaqat-340000-jobs/).
