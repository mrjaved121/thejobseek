Good news for anyone searching for **Tamkeen jobs** support. Bahrain's Labour Fund, **Tamkeen**, has signed an agreement with **KPMG** to build the Kingdom's first **digital skills platform**, an AI-powered system that will connect Bahraini talent with jobs in the labour market. The deal was signed at Gateway Gulf 2026 and announced on 1 November 2026.

## What is the new Skills Bahrain platform?

The platform is being developed under **Skills Bahrain**, Tamkeen's skills initiative. It will bring everyone in the job market onto one system with a shared national skills framework, a common language for describing skills.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Who | Tamkeen (Labour Fund) through Skills Bahrain |
| Partner | KPMG |
| What | Bahrain's first AI-powered digital skills platform |
| Users | Students, job seekers, employees, employers, training providers, policymakers |
| First phase | Scheduled for next year (2027) |
| Strategy | Tamkeen 2026–2030 |

## How will it help job seekers?

Users will be able to:

- Create a **digital skills profile**
- Receive **personalised recommendations** for training and professional development
- See how their skills match what employers need
- Make career decisions based on skills, not just job titles

## How will it help employers?

Employers will be able to find and develop talent and plan hiring using **supply and demand indicators** from the labour market. The aim is also to line up training courses with current and future job requirements, making Bahraini workers more competitive.

## How do I apply or sign up?

The platform is not live yet; the first phase is due in 2027. Until then, Bahraini job seekers can use Tamkeen's existing training and wage-support programmes and register with the Ministry of Labour's national employment services. Watch Tamkeen's official channels for the launch.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Skills will matter more than job titles.** Start listing your skills, certificates and projects now.
- **Expect better job matching.** The platform aims to show you which training leads to real openings.
- **Employers are planning ahead.** Tamkeen's 2026–2030 strategy focuses on preparing Bahrainis for future jobs.
