The flow of UAE visas for **Pakistani nationals** has returned to its normal pace after months of slower processing, according to a report in Pakistan's **The News** on **22 October 2026**. Diplomatic sources quoted by the paper said the UAE is now issuing about **30,000 visas a month** to Pakistanis, with travel agents handling further applications on top of that.

## What TheJobSeek could verify

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| About 30,000 visas a month to Pakistanis | Reported by unnamed diplomatic sources (The News, 22 Oct 2026); **no official figure published** |
| Visa issuance back to its earlier pace | Reported by the same sources |
| Visit visas for Pakistani families restarted | Reported in early August 2026 by The Nation, citing travel agents |
| Relations between the two countries improving | Described by sources; credited partly to the new UAE ambassador, Salem Mohammed Al Zaabi |

The report does **not** say how many of the visas are **visit visas** and how many are **employment visas**. Neither the UAE nor the Pakistani government has made a public statement confirming the number, so treat it as an estimate.

## Background

For several months, Pakistani applicants faced long delays and extra checks, especially for family visit visas. In early August, travel agents said the UAE had started issuing visit visas again to Pakistani families applying through **registered travel agents**, and that decisions were arriving faster. Air traffic between Pakistan and Dubai picked up around the same time.

The News also reported that about **3,500 Pakistanis** were sent home from the UAE over several months, and that sources say nobody has been made to leave in recent weeks.

A steadier visa flow makes it easier for employers to bring in Pakistani staff, and for families and job seekers to travel for interviews. It also supports remittances, which are a major source of income for Pakistan.

## How to apply safely

- Apply only through **registered travel agents** or the official UAE channels: [ICP](https://icp.gov.ae/) for Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the northern emirates, and [GDRFA Dubai](https://gdrfad.gov.ae/) for Dubai.
- Check job offers and work permits on the [MoHRE website](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/).
- In Pakistan, use only recruiters licensed by the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Faster visas, same rules.** A visit visa does not allow you to work. Your employer must get you a work permit.
- **Get your documents ready.** Attested degrees, a clean police record and a valid passport speed up processing.
- **Avoid "guaranteed visa" agents.** Real visa decisions are made by UAE authorities, not agents.
- **Expect checks to continue.** Even at normal pace, some applications will still be refused.
