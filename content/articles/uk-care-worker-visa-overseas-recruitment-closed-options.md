The UK has stopped recruiting **care workers from overseas**. Since **22 July 2025**, employers cannot sponsor someone outside the UK for a **care worker (SOC 6135)** or **senior care worker (SOC 6136)** job. The change was made in the **Statement of Changes HC 997**.

For many job seekers in South Asia, Africa and the Gulf, this was the most popular route to the UK. Here is what is still possible.

## The rule at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Closed since | 22 July 2025 |
| Jobs affected | Care worker (6135), senior care worker (6136) |
| New applications from abroad | Not allowed |
| Switching inside the UK | Allowed until 22 July 2028 (under review) |
| Switching condition | At least 3 months' legal work for the sponsoring provider |
| Existing care workers | Can extend, update or change sponsor |
| England employers | Must be registered with the Care Quality Commission |

## Who can still work in UK care

1. **Care workers already sponsored** in these jobs can renew their visa or move to a new sponsor.
2. **People already in the UK on another visa** can switch into care work until 22 July 2028, if they have worked legally for the care provider for at least three months.
3. **Qualified nurses** are not affected. Nursing jobs can still be sponsored under the **Health and Care Worker** visa.

Dependant restrictions for care workers remain in place.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Ignore "UK care visa" offers from abroad.** Any agent selling a care worker visa from outside the UK is offering something the rules no longer allow.
- **Nurses still have a route.** If you are a registered nurse, focus on NMC registration and Health and Care Worker jobs.
- **Look at other countries.** Some European countries, such as Italy, still have quotas for care and domestic workers.

## Protection from abusive sponsors

Since **8 October 2026**, Skilled Workers recognised as victims of modern slavery can leave an abusive sponsor and do any lawful work for the rest of their visa. The Home Office says more than **6,600 sponsors** have lost their licence since the current government took office.
