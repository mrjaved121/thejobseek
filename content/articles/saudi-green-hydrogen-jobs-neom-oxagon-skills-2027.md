**NEOM jobs** in clean energy are on the horizon. Saudi Arabia is preparing to start commercial operations at the **world's largest green hydrogen plant**, in **NEOM's industrial city of Oxagon**, in **2027**, and experts say the country now needs to train enough Saudis to run this new industry, Arab News reported on 6 November 2026.

## What is being built at Oxagon?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Location | Oxagon, NEOM, Saudi Arabia |
| Project | World's largest green hydrogen production facility |
| Start of commercial operations | 2027 (planned) |
| Output | As much as 1.2 million tonnes of green ammonia yearly, for export |
| Source of figures | ACWA |

## What do experts say about skills?

**Sebastian Sons**, who researches the Saudi labour market at the German think tank **CARPO**, told Arab News that hydrogen production is progressing well but education still needs to catch up. He suggests four priorities:

- **Institutionalisation** – formal structures for hydrogen education
- **Integration** – linking universities, research centres and industry
- **Public awareness** – helping young Saudis see green energy as a career
- **International cooperation** – partnerships with countries experienced in hydrogen

His research, written with Cian Mulligan, highlights gaps in specialised training and vocational education.

## Where can Saudis study hydrogen today?

- **King Saud University** – a renewable energy master's programme with hydrogen-related courses, and a Sustainable Energy Technologies Centre researching hydrogen production, storage and use
- **King Fahd University of Petroleum and Minerals** – an undergraduate concentration related to the field

Rather than building new systems from scratch, Sons recommends expanding the hydrogen work these institutions already do.

## Which jobs could green hydrogen create?

Hydrogen and ammonia plants typically need:

- Process, chemical and electrical engineers
- Plant operators and control-room technicians
- Maintenance and instrumentation technicians
- Health, safety and environment specialists
- Logistics and export operations staff

These are typical roles in this industry, not confirmed vacancies.

## How do I prepare?

Saudi students and engineers interested in green energy should look at renewable energy and chemical engineering programmes, vocational training in plant operations, and graduate schemes from energy companies working on NEOM projects.
