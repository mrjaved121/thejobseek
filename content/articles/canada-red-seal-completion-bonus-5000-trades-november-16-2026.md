Canada is short of skilled trades workers, and Ottawa is now paying people to finish their training. A new **$5,000 Red Seal Completion Bonus** opens for applications on **16 November 2026**. For newcomers looking at **Canada jobs for foreigners**, it is a reminder that the trades are one of the country's strongest hiring areas – and that permanent residents can share in the rewards.

## What the bonus offers

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Amount | $5,000 per eligible Red Seal certification |
| Maximum | $50,000 |
| Who qualifies | People certified in a designated Red Seal trade on or after 1 April 2025 |
| Applications open | 16 November 2026 |
| Run by | Employment and Social Development Canada |
| Programme | Team Canada Strong |

The bonus forms part of the federal **Team Canada Strong** plan, which aims to **recruit, train and hire 80,000 to 100,000 new Red Seal trades workers by 2030–31**.

## Who can apply

According to the official page and the government's 23 October announcement, you must:

- have **completed an apprenticeship** and gained certification in a **Red Seal trade** designated in your province or territory
- have been certified **on or after 1 April 2025**
- be a **Canadian citizen, permanent resident or protected person**
- not be in high school

The announcement also lists deadlines: people certified between **1 April 2025 and 15 November 2026** have until **31 May 2027** to apply, while those certified later get **12 months** from their certification date.

## Which trades are Red Seal

Red Seal is the national standard that lets a certified tradesperson work across Canada. Common Red Seal trades include electricians, plumbers, welders, carpenters, heavy-duty mechanics, automotive service technicians and cooks. Check the official Red Seal list to confirm your trade.

## How to apply

Applications are made through the official [Red Seal Completion Bonus page](https://www.canada.ca/en/employment-social-development/services/funding/red-seal-completion-bonus.html) on Canada.ca from 16 November 2026. Have your Social Insurance Number, trade name, certification date, apprentice ID and banking details ready for direct deposit.

## What it means for job seekers

- **The trades are hiring.** The government itself says there are good jobs waiting and not enough certified workers.
- **Finish your apprenticeship.** If you are close to certification, the bonus is a strong reason to complete it.
- **Multiple trades pay more.** The bonus applies per trade, up to $50,000.
- **Newcomers:** permanent residents qualify, so a trade career can pay off as you settle in Canada.
