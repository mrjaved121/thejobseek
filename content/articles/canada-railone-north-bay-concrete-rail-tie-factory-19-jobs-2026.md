New **Ontario jobs** are coming to the North. **RailOne Rainbow Canada Corp.** is setting up a **concrete railroad tie factory in North Bay**, backed by **$1 million** from Ontario's Northern Ontario Heritage Fund Corporation (NOHFC). The project is expected to create **19 jobs**.

## What is RailOne building?

RailOne is a joint venture created to make **concrete rail ties** in Canada. Its North Bay plant will use concrete railway technology developed in Germany. CEO **Saurav Mittal** said the company is bringing 70 years of German expertise to Canada.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | RailOne Rainbow Canada Corp. |
| Location | North Bay, Ontario |
| New jobs | 19 |
| Provincial funding | $1 million (NOHFC) |
| Products | Concrete railroad ties |
| Markets | Heavy-haul freight, high-speed rail, urban transit |

The funding will help refurbish an existing building, buy manufacturing equipment and support marketing.

## Why does this matter?

Local MPP **Vic Fedeli** said this will be Ontario's **first facility** making concrete ties for transit projects. Today many concrete ties are imported from overseas, so a domestic plant cuts transport costs and strengthens Canada's rail supply chain. Concrete ties are also creosote-free and need less maintenance than wooden ones.

The NOHFC has backed **9,011 projects** in Northern Ontario since mid-2018, worth over **$1.1 billion**, and says they have created or kept **over 13,900 jobs**.

## What jobs could be available?

A concrete tie plant of this size typically needs:

- Production and machine operators
- Concrete and quality control technicians
- Maintenance mechanics and millwrights
- Forklift and yard operators
- Supervisors and shipping staff

These are typical roles for this kind of factory, not a confirmed list.

## How do I apply?

RailOne has not yet published job postings for North Bay. Watch for listings in the local press and through Employment Ontario offices in North Bay.
