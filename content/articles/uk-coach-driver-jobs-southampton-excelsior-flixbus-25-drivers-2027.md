**Coach driver jobs in Southampton, Poole and Bournemouth are opening up: Excelsior, the Dorset operator that runs FlixBus services on the South Coast, is recruiting around 25 drivers as nine new coaches join its network in 2027.** You do not need a coach licence to apply, because the company trains new drivers, and its official advert gives pay of £15.17 an hour. Applications go through Excelsior's own website.

## Why is Excelsior hiring?

The [Daily Echo](https://www.dailyecho.co.uk/news/26715042.new-jobs-southampton-flixbus-expands-network/) reports that Excelsior's partnership with FlixBus is being extended again. Nine new coaches arrive next year, bringing the FlixBus-branded fleet it runs to 14. Extra services are planned between Southampton, Poole, Bournemouth, Heathrow and London.

Excelsior's commercial director, David Lee-Kong, said the growth will bring jobs to both Poole and Southampton, with new drivers joining over the coming months. Trade title Bus-News adds that Excelsior belongs to the Go-Ahead Group and first teamed up with FlixBus in May 2025.

## What does the official vacancy say?

Excelsior's [Coach Drivers vacancy](https://www.excelsior-coaches.com/vacancies/2) confirms the main points. The company offers 40-hour, 30-hour and 20-hour contracts as well as casual ad-hoc work, so part-time applicants are welcome. PCV stands for passenger carrying vehicle, the licence category needed for coaches, and Excelsior says it trains people who do not hold one.

Benefits named in the advert are free travel and discounts at major retailers. The company wants drivers who look smart, turn up reliably, are flexible and communicate well with passengers. The advert describes drivers as ambassadors for the business.

The page does not say which depot each job is based at, and it gives no closing date. Ask about the base, routes and shift patterns when the recruiter contacts you.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Open the official advert.** Go to the [Coach Drivers vacancy on excelsior-coaches.com](https://www.excelsior-coaches.com/vacancies/2).
2. **Click "Apply here".** This opens the company's online application form.
3. **Fill in the form carefully.** Have your driving licence details, contact information and recent work history to hand.
4. **State the contract you want.** Say whether you are after 40, 30 or 20 hours or casual work, and which area suits you: Southampton, Poole or Bournemouth.
5. **Wait for a reply.** Excelsior's related bus driver adverts say applicants are contacted within two working days of applying online. The coach advert names Paul Knight as the contact.
6. **Training follows an offer.** If you have no PCV licence, the company arranges the training. Ask how long it lasts and what you are paid while training, as the advert does not give those details.

The Daily Echo also points readers to the website of Bluestar, Excelsior's sister bus company in Southampton, for more information.

## Do I need an agency?

No. Excelsior hires directly, and its application form is free to complete. Nobody should charge you for an application, an interview or a place on a training course linked to this job. If a third party asks for money to "secure" a driving job, walk away.

## Tips that improve your chances

- **Check your licence first.** The advert asks for a full, clean car licence, so penalty points may be an issue. Be upfront about them.
- **Highlight customer service.** Coach passengers include airport travellers and day-trippers, so mention any work where you looked after the public.
- **Show reliability.** Early starts and timed departures matter in coaching. A steady attendance record or shift-work history helps.
- **Be flexible on hours.** Candidates open to weekends and varied shifts are easier to place.
- **Common mistake:** assuming training makes the job a formality. You still have to pass the licence tests, so ask what support is given.

## What it means for job seekers

- This is a paid route into professional driving for people with only a car licence.
- Hiring is spread over the coming months, so there is time to apply, but earlier applicants get earlier training places.
- The adverts do not mention visa sponsorship; assume you need the right to work in the UK.
- An application does not guarantee a job. A clean licence and a solid work record are what count most.
