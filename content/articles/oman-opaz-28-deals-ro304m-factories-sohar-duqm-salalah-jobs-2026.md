There is encouraging news for anyone hunting **Oman jobs** in industry. Oman's **Public Authority for Special Economic Zones and Free Zones (OPAZ)** has signed **28 investment agreements worth more than RO304 million** (about $790 million) for new plants across the country's industrial cities, free zones and special economic zones. The deals were reported on 28–29 October and are aimed at expanding manufacturing and creating jobs.

## The biggest projects

| Project | Location | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Atlas Steel – iron and steel products plant | Sohar | Over RO112m |
| Al Jazir – shrimp-feed plant | Duqm | RO40m |
| Sohar Automotive & Advanced Industries – car assembly complex | Sohar | RO38m |
| Ceramics plant | Sohar | RO20m |
| Al Nawras – steel structures plant | Salalah | RO19.25m |
| Voltamp – transformer plant | Sohar Free Zone | RO15m |
| Gypsum plant | – | RO7.7m |
| Marai Dhofar – dairy plant | – | RO6m |

Other agreements cover **electric vehicle chargers** and **precast concrete** production at Khazaen Economic City.

## Why it matters for jobs

OPAZ has not given a total job count, but new factories need staff at every stage: first for construction, then for operations. Typical roles at plants like these include:

- Production operators and machine technicians
- Electrical and mechanical maintenance staff
- Welders, fabricators and steel fitters
- Quality control and lab technicians
- Engineers (process, electrical, civil)
- Logistics, warehouse and HSE staff
- Admin, HR and finance roles

Under Omanisation rules, many of these positions will be set aside for Omani nationals, especially at entry and technician level. Skilled expats may still find openings in specialist engineering roles.

OPAZ chairman Qais Al-Yousef said the authority is focused on clear rules and efficient services to attract investors. The projects support Oman's **11th Five-Year Plan (2026–2030)**, which targets growth outside oil.

## How to apply

These are company projects, so hiring will be done by each investor once their plant moves ahead. No vacancies have been announced yet. Omani job seekers should keep their profile updated with the Ministry of Labour, and watch the official careers pages of companies such as Voltamp and the steel firms named above.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Sohar is the hotspot** – five of the largest projects are there or in Sohar Free Zone.
- **Technical diplomas pay off** – welding, electrical and mechanical skills will be in demand.
- **Duqm and Salalah** are worth watching for food processing and steel jobs.
- **Be patient** – factory hiring usually starts months after a deal is signed.
