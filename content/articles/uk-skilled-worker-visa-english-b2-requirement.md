The UK raised its English language bar for skilled workers on **8 January 2026**. New applicants for the **Skilled Worker** visa now have to prove English at **B2** level (upper intermediate) instead of **B1**. The change came in the **Statement of Changes HC 1333**, laid on 14 October 2025.

## The rule at a glance

| Situation | English level |
|---|---|
| New Skilled Worker application | **B2** |
| Scale-up and High Potential Individual visas | **B2** |
| Extending or updating a Skilled Worker visa first held before 8 January 2026 | B1 (no new proof needed) |
| Switching from Health and Care Worker visa | No new proof needed |
| Switching from any other visa to Skilled Worker | **B2** |

## How you can prove your English

- Pass an approved **Secure English Language Test (SELT)**.
- Have a **UK degree**, or a degree from abroad taught in English and confirmed by **Ecctis**.
- Have certain UK school qualifications, such as GCSE or A level English, taken while under 18.
- Have proved English at the right level in a previous successful UK application.

### Approved SELT providers

| Test | Where |
|---|---|
| IELTS for UKVI and IELTS Life Skills | UK and abroad |
| Pearson PTE Academic UKVI and PTE Home | UK and abroad |
| LanguageCert International ESOL SELT | UK and abroad |
| Trinity College London ISE / GESE | UK only |
| PSI Skills for English UKVI | Outside the UK |

## Who is exempt

You do not need to prove English if you are a national of a listed **majority English-speaking country**, such as the USA, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Malta or several Caribbean states. **Doctors, dentists, nurses, midwives and vets** who passed an English assessment with their UK regulator are also exempt.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Book the right test.** Only SELT versions count. A normal academic IELTS is not enough.
- **Aim higher than you think.** B2 is a clear step up from B1, so allow time to prepare.
- **Already in the UK?** If your first Skilled Worker visa was granted before 8 January 2026, your extension stays at B1.
