**Jabil wants to fill over 300 full-time vacancies at the new factory it is opening in Byhalia, Mississippi, and is holding walk-in interviews there until the end of November 2026 or until every post is filled.** You can also apply online through [Jabil Careers](https://careers.jabil.com/). The plant makes equipment for AI data centers and should open officially early next year.

## Which roles are open?

The company named these job types:

- assemblers
- material handlers
- quality inspectors
- shipping and receiving clerks
- engineers
- supply chain staff
- supervisors and managers

All are full-time. Senior operations director Raj Kalyanarajan said the jobs come with chances for personal and professional development. Pay rates were not published.

## When are the walk-in interviews?

Interviews take place at **800 Gateway Global Drive, Byhalia**.

| Day | Time | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tuesdays | 1 p.m. – 3 p.m. | Not on 24 November |
| Wednesdays | 9 a.m. – 12 noon | Not on 25 November |
| Thursdays | 1 p.m. – 3 p.m. | Not on 26 November |
| Saturday 14 November | 9 a.m. – 11 a.m. | One date only |

The sessions stop at the end of November, or sooner if all positions are taken. Going early gives you the widest choice of roles.

## What does the Byhalia plant make?

The site builds infrastructure for data centers used by artificial intelligence companies. Products include liquid cooling systems, switchgear, power distribution units and large assemblies.

Byhalia is in Marshall County, around 30 minutes east of Jabil's Memphis site, which has run since 2004. In July, Jabil said it would bring roughly 2,200 jobs to Mississippi with an investment above $119 million, so this hiring round is one part of a bigger plan.

Jabil is a contract manufacturer with about 140,000 employees at more than 120 locations worldwide.

## Who can apply?

No formal requirements were listed in the announcement. As a rule, US factory employers ask for proof that you may work in the United States, and many entry-level assembly and material handling jobs take people without a degree. Engineering and management posts will need relevant experience. Bring a résumé and photo ID to a walk-in session.

## How do I apply?

1. **In person:** attend one of the walk-in sessions listed above.
2. **Online:** search "Byhalia" on the [Jabil Careers website](https://careers.jabil.com/) and apply there.

There is no charge for applying or for an interview.
