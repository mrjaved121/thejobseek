US financial giant **State Street** is moving faster on its new operations centre in **Al Ain**, with an opening now targeted for **1 July 2027**. The hub is planned to grow to around **300 employees by 2030**, and roughly **100 of those jobs** are expected to go to **Emiratis**, The National reported on **14 October 2026**.

State Street is one of the world's biggest custody banks, looking after and servicing investments for pension funds, asset managers and governments. It has worked in Abu Dhabi since 2018.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | State Street |
| Location | Al Ain, Abu Dhabi emirate |
| Target opening | 1 July 2027 |
| Staff by 2030 | About 300 |
| Roles for Emiratis | About 100 |
| Main job areas | Technology, AI, operations, client services |
| Partner | Abu Dhabi Investment Office |

## Who will be hired

The bank expects its workforce to come from three groups:

| Source of staff | Share (approx.) |
|---|---|
| UAE nationals | About 100 roles |
| UAE-based talent pool | About one third |
| Transfers from State Street's global centres | About one third |

The work will cover **technology, AI, operations and client services**, which in a custody bank means tasks such as processing trades, reconciling accounts, fund support and building the systems behind them.

## Graduate route

Through its partnership with the **Abu Dhabi Investment Office**, State Street is building links with **UAE universities**. It expects to identify possible graduate recruits in the **first quarter of 2027**, ahead of the opening.

## How to apply

Roles will be posted on State Street's official careers site, [careers.statestreet.com](https://careers.statestreet.com/). Search for "Al Ain" or "Abu Dhabi" and set an alert. Emirati students should also ask their university careers office about the State Street link.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Emirati graduates should get ready now.** Finance, accounting, IT and data students are the natural fit for the first intake.
- **Experienced UAE residents have a chance too.** About a third of jobs are expected to go to people already in the UAE job market.
- **Operations experience counts.** Back-office work in banking, funds or payments is directly useful.
- **Al Ain is growing as a jobs hub.** Living costs are lower than in Abu Dhabi city or Dubai.
