Saudi Arabia's **Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (HRSD)** says its **Skills Accelerator** initiative is on course to train **more than 300,000** Saudi workers in the private sector. An update shared by the Saudi Press Agency on **19 October 2026** said the scheme has already run **more than 590 programmes**.

The aim is to help Saudis who already have jobs pick up the skills employers are asking for, so they can move up, change roles or stay competitive as work changes.

## Key numbers

| Detail | Figure |
|---|---|
| Target beneficiaries | 300,000+ |
| Training programmes delivered | 590+ |
| Priority sectors covered | More than 10 |
| Trainees in AI courses | 44,000+ |
| Run by | Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development |

## What the training covers

The programmes are a mix of **specialist professional** and **technical** courses chosen to match labour market demand. **Artificial intelligence** is the stand-out theme: more than **44,000** people have joined AI skills courses, making it one of the most popular tracks.

## What comes next

The ministry plans to:

| Next step | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Finish skills surveys in priority sectors | Find out exactly which skills employers lack |
| Widen how results are measured | Check whether training leads to better jobs and pay |
| Link up digitally with government, employers and training providers | Make it easier to enrol and track progress |

## Why this matters for hiring

Saudi Arabia is raising local hiring targets across many professions, from accounting to marketing and sales. Employers need trained Saudi staff to fill those roles, and the Skills Accelerator is one of the tools meant to supply them. Workers who finish recognised courses are better placed when companies recruit or promote.

## How to take part

Details of HRSD's skills and training services are on the ministry's official site: [hrsd.gov.sa](https://www.hrsd.gov.sa/en/care-about-you/skillsandtrain). Saudi employees can also ask their HR department whether the company is enrolled. Job seekers can find openings on [Jadarat, the national jobs platform](/saudi-arabia/saudi-jadarat-government-jobs-platform-how-to-apply/).

## What it means for job seekers

- **Saudi employees should ask about free courses.** Many programmes are offered through employers.
- **AI skills are the hot ticket.** The large AI enrolment shows where employers see future demand.
- **Training supports Saudization roles.** Professions with rising local hiring targets need certified Saudi staff.
- **Expats: expect more competition for some roles.** Better-trained Saudi workers will be first in line for localised jobs, so specialist skills matter more than ever.
