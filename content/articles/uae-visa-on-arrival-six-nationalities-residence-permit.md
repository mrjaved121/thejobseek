The UAE has extended its visa-on-arrival facility to nationals of **six more countries**, as long as they already live in certain countries. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs announced the decision on 25 June 2026, and the Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs and Port Security (ICP) set out the details.

## Who is eligible

Ordinary passport holders from:

- Indonesia
- Viet Nam
- Thailand
- the Philippines
- Kenya
- South Africa

They must hold a **valid residence permit** from one of these: the United States, an EU member state, the United Kingdom, Singapore, Japan, South Korea, Australia, New Zealand or Canada. Accompanying family members are included.

## Visa options and fees

| Visa | Fee | Extension |
|---|---|---|
| 14 days | AED 100 | Can be extended once inside the UAE |
| 60 days | AED 250 | Cannot be extended |

Overstaying costs **AED 50 per day**.

## Can you look for work on this visa?

You can use a visit to meet employers and attend interviews. You **cannot start working** until your employer arranges a proper work permit and residence visa. Working on a visit visa can lead to fines, deportation and a ban.

Before you travel, check your eligibility with your airline or on ICP's official channels, as entry conditions can change.
