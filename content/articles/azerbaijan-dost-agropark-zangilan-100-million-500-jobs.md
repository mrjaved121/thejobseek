Farming investment is creating new **Azerbaijan jobs**. The **DOST Agropark**, a **$100 million** smart agricultural complex in **Zangilan district**, is expected to create **500 jobs** in livestock breeding, meat processing and seed production, AzerNews reported on 9 November 2026.

## What is DOST Agropark?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Project | DOST Agropark smart agricultural complex |
| Location | Zangilan district, Azerbaijan |
| Investment | $100 million (AZN 170 million) |
| Area | 6,000 hectares |
| Jobs target | 500 |

AzerNews notes that **500 is the planned employment figure**, not jobs already filled.

## What will the agropark do?

It brings several stages of farming into a single operation:

- **Pedigree livestock breeding**
- **Meat processing and packaging**
- **Seed and seedling production**

The aim is to link raw material growing, livestock and processing, using modern farming technology.

## Why Zangilan?

The complex is a major **private investment** in Azerbaijan's **liberated territories**, where the government is rebuilding towns and encouraging new production facilities.

## Which jobs are likely?

An integrated agro-complex of this kind typically employs:

- Farm and livestock workers
- Veterinarians and animal breeding specialists
- Meat processing and packaging staff
- Agronomists and greenhouse technicians
- Machinery operators, drivers and warehouse staff

These are common roles for this kind of project, not a published vacancy list.

## How can I find these jobs?

Watch for announcements from DOST Agropark and Azerbaijan's state employment services as the complex moves towards operation.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Agriculture is a growth sector** in Azerbaijan's regions.
- **Food processing skills** will be in demand.
- **Vets and agronomists** may find specialist roles.
