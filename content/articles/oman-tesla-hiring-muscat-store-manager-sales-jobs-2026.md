Electric car fans looking for **Muscat jobs** have a new employer to watch. **Tesla** is building its **first team in Oman**, with **three full-time vacancies** posted in Muscat before the brand launches there. The news was reported on 5 November 2026.

## Which Tesla jobs are open in Muscat?

| Role | Type | Location |
|---|---|---|
| Store manager | Full-time | Muscat |
| Sales adviser | Full-time | Muscat |
| Customer experience specialist | Full-time | Muscat |

The **store manager** role points to plans for a **physical showroom** in Muscat, although Tesla has not announced where it will be.

## When is Tesla launching in Oman?

In October, EV news site EVwire said Tesla was aiming for a 2027 Oman entry, based on a LinkedIn post by **Pawel Trzpis**, Tesla's Middle East market lead. The edited post confirms the Oman expansion but no longer gives a launch year. Trzpis said Tesla is building its local team from scratch, starting with sales.

Details of Tesla's service centre, Supercharger network, models and Omani pricing have not been announced yet.

## Where else does Tesla operate in the Gulf?

| Qatar | Online orders opened in December 2023 |
| Saudi Arabia | Entered in April 2025; sales and service centres in Riyadh and Jeddah |
| Oman | First jobs advertised in November 2026 |

## What skills do these roles need?

Retail car sales roles like these typically look for:

- Customer-facing sales experience, ideally in automotive or premium retail
- Strong English; Arabic is a plus in Oman
- Product knowledge and comfort explaining technology to buyers
- For the store manager: team leadership and sales targets experience

These are typical requirements for such roles; check Tesla's job listings for the exact criteria.
