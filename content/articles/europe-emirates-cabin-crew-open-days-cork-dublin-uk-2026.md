Dubai airline **Emirates** is recruiting **cabin crew in Ireland**, starting with a walk-in **open day in Cork on Wednesday 28 October**. The event was flagged by Yay Cork on **27 October 2026**, and the airline's own careers site lists more open days in **Dublin** and across the **UK** through November.

## Open day dates in Ireland and the UK

All events start at **9am**, and you can walk in without an invitation. Registering online first is recommended.

## Requirements

According to the official Emirates careers page, you need to:

- Be at least **21 years old**
- Be at least **160 cm tall** and able to reach **212 cm**
- Speak and write **fluent English** (other languages help)
- Have at least **1 year of hospitality or customer service experience**
- Have finished **Grade 12** (high school, or Leaving Certificate level)
- Have **no visible tattoos** while in uniform
- Be eligible for a **UAE employment visa**

## Pay and benefits

| Item | Amount (approx.) |
|---|---|
| Basic salary | AED 4,980 a month |
| Flying pay | AED 69.6 an hour (80–100 hours a month on average) |
| Average monthly pay | About AED 11,244 (around US$3,061) |

On top of pay, crew get **free furnished shared accommodation** in Dubai, **company transport**, **30 days' annual leave** with a yearly ticket home, **medical and dental care**, travel discounts and an end-of-service benefit. Night-stop meal allowances are paid separately.

Cork-based Emirates crew member **Rachel Murray** told Yay Cork that the airline's training and support helped her build confidence in the job.

## How to apply

Create a profile and register for your nearest event on the official site, [emiratesgroupcareers.com/cabin-crew](https://www.emiratesgroupcareers.com/cabin-crew/). Bring an up-to-date CV and arrive on time, as open days can be busy.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Dress smartly** in business wear. First impressions matter at open days.
- **Be ready for teamwork.** Emirates looks for natural team players, so show how you work with others.
- **Highlight service experience**, such as hotels, restaurants, retail or call centres.
- **Think about the move.** The job is based in Dubai, so you will relocate.
- **Ignore paid "agents".** Emirates never charges candidates to apply.
