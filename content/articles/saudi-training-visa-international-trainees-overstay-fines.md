Saudi Arabia is creating a **new visa for international trainees**. The **Council of Ministers** approved it at its session in Jeddah on **14 July 2026**, according to the official gazette **Umm Al-Qura**. The decision says the visa will be granted to international trainees "according to a number of arrangements", but those arrangements have **not been published yet**.

That means there are **no confirmed details** on fees, duration, who qualifies or how to apply.

## Overstay penalties: a reminder, not a new rule

In **July 2026** the **Ministry of Interior** reminded visitors and expats that staying after their entry visa expires is a serious offence. Some reports called this a tightening, but the penalties are the **same ones announced in April 2025**.

| Penalty | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Fine | Up to SAR 50,000 |
| Prison | Up to 6 months |
| Deportation | Yes |

The ministry asks people to report violations on **911** in Makkah, Madinah, Riyadh and the Eastern Province, and **999** in other regions.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Do not pay anyone for a "training visa" yet.** Until the government publishes the rules, any agent selling one is not following an official process.
- **Watch official channels.** Visa services are announced through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs visa platform and the Saudi Press Agency.
- **Never overstay.** A visit or work entry visa must be converted or exited on time. Overstaying can lead to a ban as well as a fine.
