Job seekers following **Canada hiring 2026** have a well-paid public service career to look at this month. The **Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA)** has published a list of **nine recruitment events** for **border services officers (BSOs)**, running from **3 November to 2 December 2026**. The agency updated its recruitment events page on 30 October.

Border services officers work at airports, land crossings, ports and mail centres, checking travellers and goods coming into Canada.

## In-person job fairs

| Date | Event | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Tue 3 Nov, 3pm–7pm ET | Chatham-Kent education and training fair | John D. Bradley Convention Centre, Chatham, ON |
| Wed 25 Nov, 1pm–6pm ET | Windsor Regional Employment Training Fair | Caboto Club, Windsor, ON |
| Thu 26 Nov, 11am–2pm ET | Black Community Employment and Empowerment Services job fair | Covent Garden Market, London, ON |

## Online information sessions (MS Teams)

| Date | Session | Who it is for |
|---|---|---|
| 10 and 26 Nov | Indigenous recruitment, Northern Canada | Indigenous applicants interested in northern postings |
| 13, 20 and 27 Nov | Bilingual recruitment, Prairies | Bilingual applicants for AB, SK, MB or NWT |
| 17 Nov and 1 Dec | Indigenous recruitment, Prairies | First Nations, Inuit and Métis applicants |
| 18 Nov and 2 Dec | Women in the Prairies | Women applicants |
| 19 Nov | Southern Ontario | Applicants for Southern Ontario posts |
| 24 and 25 Nov | National BSO session | Everyone (English or French) |

Each online session has room for **300 people**, and registration closes **24 hours** before it starts.

## Pay and benefits

Figures below come from the CBSA's official BSO job description page.

| Stage | Level | Annual salary |
|---|---|---|
| Officer trainee (at the CBSA College) | FB-02 | $80,344 – $89,462 |
| Border services officer | FB-03 | $86,915 – $103,079 |

Trainees get paid accommodation, meals and a living allowance during college training. Officers join public service health, dental, disability and pension plans, and bilingual posts may pay a bonus.

## Main requirements

- Willing to **relocate anywhere in Canada**, including rural and remote areas
- Ready for **shift work, overtime, weekends and holidays**
- A **valid, unrestricted driver's licence**
- Willing to **wear a uniform and carry defensive equipment**
- Strong judgement, integrity and writing skills

Check the CBSA's "find out if you qualify" page for full eligibility rules before you apply.

## How to apply

Register for an event through the official [CBSA recruitment events page](https://www.cbsa-asfc.gc.ca/job-emploi/events-evenements-eng.html). The same CBSA careers section explains the assessment process and how to apply for the BSO job.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Strong starting pay.** Even trainees earn over $80,000 a year.
- **Be ready to move.** Postings can be anywhere in Canada.
- **Bilingual candidates are in demand,** especially in the Prairies.
- **Attend a session first.** It is the easiest way to ask recruiters about tests and timelines.
