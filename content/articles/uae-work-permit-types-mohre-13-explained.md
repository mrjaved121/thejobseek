Anyone working legally in the UAE private sector needs a work permit from the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MoHRE). The ministry issues **13 types** of work permit, each for a different situation. Knowing which one applies to you helps you check that your employer is doing things correctly.

In 2026 MoHRE also cut paperwork for these services under its Zero Government Bureaucracy programme, removing supporting documents and most mandatory data fields from many applications.

## The 13 work permit types

1. **Recruitment from outside the UAE.** For hiring a worker who is abroad. Valid for 2 years.
2. **Transfer work permit.** For a worker moving from one UAE employer to another. Valid for 2 years.
3. **Work permit for family-sponsored residents.** For people living in the UAE on a family member's visa who take a job. Valid for 2 years.
4. **Temporary work permit.** For work of a limited period.
5. **Mission work permit.** For temporary work on a specific project or task.
6. **Part-time work permit.** Allows working fewer hours, including for more than one employer. Valid for 2 years.
7. **Juvenile work permit.** For young people aged 15 to 18, under special conditions.
8. **Student training and employment permit.** For students aged 15 and over.
9. **UAE and GCC national work permit.** For Emirati and GCC citizens. Valid for 2 years.
10. **Golden residency holder work permit.** For Golden Visa holders taking a job.
11. **Trainee UAE national work permit.** For training Emirati nationals.
12. **Freelance work permit.** For foreign nationals on self-sponsored residency who work for themselves.
13. **Private tutoring work permit.** Lets qualified people give private lessons. It is free and valid for 2 years. Tutoring without it can lead to fines.

## Important points for job seekers

- **Your employer handles the permit.** For standard jobs, the employer applies and pays. Fees for issuing or renewing a permit range from **AED 250 to AED 3,450**, depending on the company's classification.
- **A visit visa is not a work permit.** Working on a visit visa is illegal in the UAE. Read our guide: [Can you work on a visit visa in the UAE?](/uae/can-you-work-on-visit-visa-in-uae/)
- **Check before you start work.** You can check your work permit status through MoHRE's app or website.
- **Never pay to get a permit.** If anyone asks you to pay them for a work permit, treat it as a warning sign. See: [8 warning signs of a fake UAE job offer](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/)
