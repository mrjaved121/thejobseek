**Air Arabia engineering jobs in Sharjah are open now, and the airline is reported to be interviewing engineers in Colombo, by invitation only, from 17 to 19 November 2026.** The way in is the same for everyone: apply for a listed role on Air Arabia's official careers site, and attend the event only if the airline shortlists you and sends an invitation. Applying costs nothing and no agent is involved.

## What has been announced?

According to a report by [Newswire](https://www.newswire.lk/2026/11/11/air-arabia-plans-engineering-recruitment-event-in-colombo-this-month/), a Sri Lankan news site, the Sharjah-based carrier plans three days of engineering interviews in Colombo this month. The report says the vacancies sit in line maintenance, base maintenance and the workshops, and the profiles wanted are licensed B1 and B2 engineers, technicians, people who maintain aircraft cabins and sheet-metal specialists.

One honest note: when we checked [Air Arabia's engineering careers page](https://www.airarabiagroupcareers.com/gb/en/engineering) on 12 November, the Sharjah roles were listed, but the job titles did not carry a Colombo label. Venue and timing details are therefore something only the airline's recruiters can confirm to invited candidates.

## Which roles are open on the official site?

These Sharjah engineering posts were live on the careers site when we looked. Requirements are taken from each listing.

| Role | Main requirements in the listing |
|---|---|
| B2 Certifying Engineer, Line Maintenance | Part 66 or CAR 66 B2 licence with A320 approvals; 2 years certifying on the A320 family; 5 years in line maintenance |
| Sheet Metal Certifying Technician | Diploma or certificate in aircraft maintenance (structures); 4 years on aircraft structure, Airbus preferred |
| Sheet Metal Engineer | Certifies structural repairs and supervises shop technicians |
| Paint Technician | Aircraft painting training; 2 years on large commercial aircraft; 2 years in a base maintenance workshop |
| Maintenance Planning Engineer | Listed for Sharjah; see listing for details |
| Shift Manager, Line and Base Maintenance | Two separate listings for Sharjah |

Several listings also ask for good technical English, basic computer skills and the ability to obtain a UAE driving licence. Pay is not shown on any of the pages we read.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Open the official page.** Go to [airarabiagroupcareers.com](https://www.airarabiagroupcareers.com/gb/en/engineering) and choose Engineering. Type the address yourself instead of following links in messages.
2. **Pick one role that matches your licence.** Read the full listing. A B1 mechanic applying for a B2 avionics post is likely to be screened out.
3. **Prepare your file before you start.** Have your CV, licence copy (front and back, with type ratings), company authorisation records, training certificates such as human factors, and your passport ready as clear scans.
4. **Create a profile and submit.** The site sends you to the airline's application system. Fill in every field; do not write "see CV".
5. **Wait for contact.** If you are shortlisted for Colombo, the invitation should come from the airline's recruitment team with the date and venue. No invitation means no interview slot.
6. **After the interview.** Successful candidates normally go through document checks and licence verification before an offer. Do not resign from your current job until you hold a written offer.

## Tips that improve your chances

- **Lead with numbers.** Put aircraft types, years on the A320 family and your certifying privileges in the first lines of the CV.
- **Match the listing's wording.** If the post says line maintenance, show line maintenance years clearly, with dates.
- **Keep licences current.** An expired licence or missing type rating is a common reason for rejection in aviation hiring.

Short checklist: CV in PDF, licence scans, logbook or experience summary, passport valid for at least six months, two referees.

## Do I need an agent, and what about scams?

No. Air Arabia takes applications directly through its own careers site and does not charge a fee. Be careful with anyone on WhatsApp or social media who offers a "guaranteed slot" at the Colombo event for money. A real invitation will not ask you to pay for an interview, a visa or a medical in advance. If you are unsure, log in to your candidate profile on the official site and check your application status there.
