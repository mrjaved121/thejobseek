Business jet maker **Bombardier** says it is recruiting for **500 jobs in the United States right now** and plans **300 more** as it opens and expands sites. The company announced the plan on **23 October 2026**, taking total US hiring to about **800 roles**.

Bombardier, based in Montreal, builds its Challenger and Global business jets in Canada, the US and Mexico. Much of the new US work is in **aircraft service and maintenance**, where demand from business jet owners keeps growing.

## Where the jobs are

| State | What is happening |
|---|---|
| Texas | 150 of the 300 planned new jobs, described as high-skilled, with a new project in Dallas |
| Kansas | Ongoing hiring in Wichita, its US home base, where a new North American Learning Center recently opened |
| Indiana | New service centre at Fort Wayne International Airport opening November 2026 |
| Connecticut | Hiring plus a new expansion project |

The Fort Wayne centre covers about **64,500 sq ft** and can hold up to **six aircraft** at once. It will handle scheduled and unscheduled maintenance, modifications, avionics installs and urgent "aircraft on ground" support.

## What roles to expect

The announcement does not give a full list, but Bombardier's current US openings show the type of people it needs, such as **aviation maintenance technicians**, **avionics technicians**, flight test technicians and **apprentice technicians** in Wichita. Engineering, planning and customer support roles are also common at service centres.

## How to apply

All vacancies are listed on Bombardier's official careers site, [jobs.bombardier.com](https://jobs.bombardier.com/). Search by city (Wichita, Dallas, Fort Wayne or Connecticut locations) and set up job alerts.

## What it means for job seekers

- **An A&P licence opens doors.** FAA Airframe and Powerplant certification is the standard ask for maintenance technicians.
- **No licence yet? Look at apprenticeships.** Bombardier has advertised apprentice technician roles in Wichita.
- **Fort Wayne is hiring first.** With a November opening, that site is likely to fill roles soon.
- **Avionics is in demand.** Electronics and wiring skills are needed for upgrades on newer jets.
