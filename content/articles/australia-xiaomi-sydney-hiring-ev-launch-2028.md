Electric vehicle fans watching **Australia hiring 2026** have a new name to follow. Chinese tech giant **Xiaomi** has started recruiting **automotive staff in Sydney** as it prepares to sell right-hand-drive (RHD) cars, with an RHD expansion planned for the **first half of 2028**.

## Which Xiaomi roles are open in Sydney?

| Role | Focus |
|---|---|
| Senior Legal Counsel (Automotive), Australia | Vehicle sales, after-sales, contracts and regulatory work; possible team leadership |
| Product Manager, International Instalment Finance | Instalment-finance products across Asia-Pacific, working with banks and leasing firms |

The finance role also covers **pricing, residual-value management** and business-performance analysis.

## When is Xiaomi launching cars?

Xiaomi's president, **Lu Weibing**, gave this timetable to investors in April 2026:

| Market | Planned launch |
|---|---|
| Europe | Second half of 2027 |
| Right-hand-drive markets (incl. Australia) | First half of 2028 |

Australian motoring outlets **Drive** and **CarExpert** have reported expectations of local showrooms in 2028. Xiaomi has not yet announced its Australian models, prices or retail partners.

## What else is Xiaomi doing to prepare?

In Germany, Xiaomi signed memorandums of understanding with **eight dealer groups** at IFA Berlin in September to develop sales and after-sales networks. These are not yet final dealership contracts. Xiaomi has also discussed selling cars in Japan once RHD models are ready.

## Will there be more Xiaomi jobs in Australia?

Very likely as the launch nears. A car brand entering a new market usually needs:

- Sales, dealer development and marketing staff
- After-sales, service and technical trainers
- Finance, legal and compliance specialists
- Logistics and parts staff

These are typical roles for a new car brand, not confirmed vacancies.
