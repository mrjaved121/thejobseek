US heating and cooling giant **Carrier Global** has announced a new **advanced manufacturing and engineering centre in Fort Worth, Texas**, that will bring **1,500 jobs**. The company said on **22 October 2026** that it will put **more than $600 million** into the project over the next three years.

## What Carrier is building

The site sits inside **AllianceTexas**, a large business park in north Fort Worth. The building will cover **more than 1 million square feet**.

The plant will build **commercial chillers**, both air-cooled and water-cooled. These are the large cooling machines used to keep buildings and equipment at the right temperature. Carrier says demand is coming from:

- **Data centres**, which need huge amounts of cooling for AI and cloud servers
- **Hospitals and healthcare buildings**
- **Schools and universities**
- **Factories and other industrial sites**

Carrier says the extra capacity will help it keep up with orders, which are at record levels.

## What kinds of jobs to expect

Carrier describes the roles as **advanced manufacturing, engineering and other jobs**. It has not yet published a detailed list. A plant of this type usually needs:

- Assembly technicians, machine operators and welders
- Maintenance technicians and electricians
- Quality inspectors and test technicians
- Mechanical, electrical and manufacturing engineers
- Supply chain, warehouse and logistics staff
- Supervisors, planners and office support

The site will also have **engineering labs**, so there should be openings for design and product engineers, not only factory staff.

## Training and local partners

Carrier says it chose Fort Worth for its **skilled workforce** and manufacturing base. The company plans to work with **local schools and colleges** on training programmes, which could open routes for students and people moving into manufacturing for the first time.

Fort Worth Mayor **Mattie Parker** welcomed the project as more proof of the city's growing manufacturing sector.

## How to apply

Hiring for the new plant will build up ahead of the 2027 opening. Search for Fort Worth or Texas roles on Carrier's official careers site, [jobs.carrier.com](https://jobs.carrier.com/). You can also set up job alerts there so you hear about new openings first.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Start preparing now.** Most hiring is likely in 2027, but engineering and leadership posts often open months earlier.
- **Get trade skills.** HVAC, welding, electrical and industrial maintenance certificates will put you ahead.
- **Watch local colleges** in the Fort Worth area for training courses linked to Carrier.
- **Only apply through official channels.** Ignore anyone asking for money to secure a Carrier job.
