Victoria is making it cheaper to start a trade, a welcome step for **Australia hiring 2026** in sectors short of skilled workers. From **2027**, the state will pay the **first year of TAFE tuition** for apprentices in **six priority apprenticeships**, the Premier's office announced on **29 October 2026**.

Deputy Premier and Skills Minister **Gabrielle Williams** said apprentices are vital to the state's future and that the support helps businesses find the workers they need.

## Which apprenticeships are covered

The government has not yet named all six, but its examples point to areas with strong demand for workers:

- **Housing construction** – carpentry, bricklaying and other building trades
- **Clean energy** – such as electrical work on solar, batteries and grid upgrades
- **Hairdressing**
- **Baking**

The Victorian Automobile Chamber of Commerce has complained that motor trades were left out, so automotive apprentices should not expect free first-year fees under this change.

## Why it matters for jobs

Apprentices are paid employees who train on the job and study at TAFE. Cutting the first-year fee lowers the cost of starting a trade and should help employers sign up more apprentices. Free TAFE has helped **267,000 Victorians** since it started and saved them about **$930 million** in fees.

## How to apply

1. **Find an employer.** Apprentices need a job with an employer who signs a training contract. Look on employers' own careers pages and through Apprenticeship Connect Australia providers.
2. **Pick a TAFE.** Choose a TAFE that offers your trade.
3. **Check eligibility** for Free TAFE and Skills First on the official Victorian Government Free TAFE page (vic.gov.au/free-tafe).

## What it means for job seekers

- **2027 starters benefit most.** If you were planning to start a trade, a 2027 start could save you a year of fees.
- **Building and clean energy are growth areas** across Victoria.
- **Overseas workers should check rules.** These programmes are mainly for citizens, permanent residents and some eligible visa holders.
- **Start looking for an employer now** so you are ready when 2027 intakes open.
