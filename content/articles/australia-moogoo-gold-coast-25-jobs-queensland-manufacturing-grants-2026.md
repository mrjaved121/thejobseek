**Australia hiring in 2026** is getting a boost from Queensland's factories. Gold Coast natural skincare brand **MooGoo** will create **25 new jobs** after receiving **$1.3 million** from the state's **Transforming Queensland Manufacturing Grants Program**, the Queensland Government announced on **30 October 2026**. Across the program's first round, funded projects are expected to add **more than 300 jobs**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | MooGoo |
| Location | Gold Coast, Queensland |
| Grant | $1.3 million |
| New jobs | 25 |
| Staff upskilled | 42 |
| Round one total | $12.5m to 18 manufacturers |
| Round one jobs | 300+ new jobs, 550+ workers upskilled |

## What MooGoo will do

MooGoo makes skin and hair care products and currently sends some production to contractors in other states. The grant will pay for new equipment so it can make more of its range **in-house on the Gold Coast**. That means more local production work and training for existing staff.

## The wider program

The first round attracted **119 applications**. Funding went to **18 manufacturers**, and **11** of them are based in **regional Queensland**, spreading new jobs beyond Brisbane and the Gold Coast. Together, the projects are expected to create **300+ jobs** and train **550+ workers** in new skills.

## Likely roles

Manufacturers expanding in-house production typically recruit:

- Production and packing operators
- Machine operators and technicians
- Quality control and lab staff
- Warehouse and logistics workers
- Maintenance trades

## Why manufacturing grants matter

Grants like this pay part of the cost of new machines, which lets smaller firms grow faster than they could on their own. In return, the businesses commit to hiring and training targets. For job seekers, that makes funded companies a good place to look, because new roles are already planned and backed by public money.

## How to apply

No official vacancy page was published with the announcement. Watch MooGoo's own website and social channels for openings, and use Australia's official government job board, Workforce Australia, to search for manufacturing roles on the Gold Coast and in regional Queensland.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Local manufacturing is growing,** as firms bring production back into Queensland.
- **Regional jobs are on offer.** Most round one winners are outside the capital.
- **Upskilling is part of the deal,** so entry-level workers can build skills on the job.
- **Overseas workers** should check visa rules before applying, as many factory roles hire locally first.
