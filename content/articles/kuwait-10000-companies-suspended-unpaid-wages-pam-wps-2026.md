Kuwait has suspended about **10,000 company files** for failing to pay staff properly, First Deputy Prime Minister and Interior Minister **Sheikh Fahad Al-Yousef** told employers at a meeting on **22 October 2026**. The Public Authority for Manpower (**PAM**) has also switched on a direct electronic link to the **Wage Protection System (WPS)** to catch late payers faster.

## What the audits found

| Finding | Detail |
|---|---|
| Company files suspended | About 10,000 |
| Registered files (firms with 5+ staff) | 48,408 |
| Longest unpaid periods found | Four months, and up to 18 months in some cases |
| WPS compliance, July 2026 | 75.6% (up from 38.7% a year earlier) |
| WPS applications, Jan–Sep 2026 | 93,865 (up about 196%) |

The minister gave examples of abuse: a firm where 17 staff went unpaid while the owner's wife drew a salary, and a worker registered on KD 3,000 who actually received KD 180. He also criticised delivery companies running shifts of up to **18 hours**.

## How the new PAM–WPS link works

1. The employer sends its salary file to its bank.
2. The bank passes it to PAM through the WPS, which checks it against the firm's registered workers.
3. Once matched, the bank pays the salaries.
4. Results go back to PAM automatically.

Companies must pay salaries to at least **90%** of their registered staff, and must cover **all** registered workers for the latest month.

## Penalties for employers

Firms that fail lose access to:

- New work permits (renewals excluded)
- Registering government contracts
- Labour needs assessments
- Certificates for tenders and official approvals
- Registering company vehicles

PAM director **Rabab Al-Osaimi** said some firms pay salaries and then take the money back, or deduct more than **25%** of pay. Both are violations, and **cash salaries are banned** except during a new company's first two months.

## What it means for workers

- **Check your bank statements.** Your full salary should arrive by bank transfer every month.
- **Never hand money back.** Being asked to return part of your salary is illegal.
- **Visit PAM.** The minister told PAM to open its doors to the public one day a week, from 8am to 1pm.
- **Keep records.** Payslips, contracts and transfer slips help if you need to file a complaint.
