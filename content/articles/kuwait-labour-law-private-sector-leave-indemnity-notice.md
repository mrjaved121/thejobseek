Kuwait's private sector is governed by **Labour Law No. 6 of 2010**, with later amendments. The **Public Authority for Manpower (PAM)** enforces it. Here are the key rules for workers.

## Working hours

- Up to **48 hours a week**, or **8 hours a day**.
- During **Ramadan**: up to **36 hours a week**.
- No more than **5 hours in a row** without a break.
- At least **24 hours** of paid weekly rest.

## Overtime

Overtime is limited to **2 hours a day**, **3 days a week** and **180 hours a year**. It is paid on your basic wage:

| When you work | Overtime pay |
|---|---|
| Normal working day | **125%** |
| Weekly rest day | **150%**, plus a day off in lieu |
| Public holiday | **200%**, plus a day off in lieu |

## Annual leave

Workers get **30 days of paid annual leave** a year. Since a **2017 amendment**, weekends, public holidays and sick leave days **do not count** against it.

## Sick leave

| Period | Pay |
|---|---|
| First 15 days | Full pay |
| Next 10 days | Two-thirds |
| Next 10 days | Half |
| Next 10 days | One quarter |
| After that | Unpaid |

A doctor's certificate is needed.

## Notice period

For open-ended contracts, notice must be given **in writing**:

- **3 months** for workers paid monthly
- **1 month** for other workers

Your employer can ask you to stop working during notice, but must keep paying you.

## End-of-service indemnity

For workers paid **monthly**:

| Service | Indemnity per year |
|---|---|
| First 5 years | **15 days'** pay |
| Each year after | **One month's** pay |

The total is capped at **18 months' pay**. Part-years count pro rata. Since 2017, employers **cannot deduct** their social security contributions, so the indemnity is paid in full.

**Example:** 8 years' service, contract ended by the employer: 5 years × 15 days' pay, plus 3 years × one month's pay. Ask your employer or PAM how your daily wage is worked out, since this affects the final figure.

Different rates apply to workers paid daily, weekly or by piece.

## If you resign

Resigning from an open-ended contract after giving notice reduces the indemnity:

| Service when you resign | Share of indemnity |
|---|---|
| Over 3 and under 5 years | **Half** |
| Over 5 and under 10 years | **Two thirds** |
| 10 years or more | **Full** |

## Salary payment

Wages must be paid **in Kuwaiti dinars into a bank account in Kuwait**. Employers that fail to do so face penalties from the authorities.

## Tips

- Keep your **contract, payslips and bank statements**.
- Report unpaid wages or dues to **PAM**.
