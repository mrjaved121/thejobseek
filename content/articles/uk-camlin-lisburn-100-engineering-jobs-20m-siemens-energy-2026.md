Engineers and tech workers hunting for **jobs in UK with visa sponsorship** should keep an eye on Northern Ireland. **Camlin Group**, a Lisburn company that makes monitoring technology for power grids and railways, says it will create **at least 100 new jobs** through a **£20 million** investment, The Irish News reported on 30 October 2026. BBC News NI also covered the plan the next day.

The firm is paying for the expansion itself. It is the first growth step since **Siemens Energy** took over Camlin in **June 2026**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Camlin Group |
| Location | Lisburn, County Antrim, Northern Ireland |
| New jobs | At least 100 |
| Investment | £20 million (self-funded) |
| Current staff | About 700 |
| Owner | Siemens Energy (since June 2026) |

## Which roles are coming

Camlin listed six areas where it will hire:

- **Hardware engineering** – designing sensors and monitoring devices
- **Software development** – building the systems that read and analyse the data
- **Data science** – turning grid and rail data into useful warnings and insights
- **Operations**
- **Manufacturing** – building the products in Lisburn
- **Business support**

No salaries or hiring dates were given. Chief executive **Peter Cunningham** described the 100 jobs as only the start. He wants the business to be three times bigger within three to four years, which suggests more hiring later.

Camlin's own vacancy listings already show openings in Lisburn, including mechatronics and IT roles.

## About visa sponsorship

Plenty of engineering and software roles qualify for the Skilled Worker route, yet only firms holding a sponsor licence can use it, and the job must meet the pay thresholds. Camlin has given no word on sponsorship for these posts. If you are overseas, look the company up on the government's published sponsor list and read every advert closely. People who can already work in Britain or Northern Ireland, Irish passport holders among them, are free to apply straight away.

## How to apply

Go to the official [Camlin Group careers page](https://camlingroup.com/careers/) and follow the link to current vacancies. Filter for Lisburn, upload a CV that shows your technical skills, and check back often as new roles are added through the expansion.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Grid technology is a growth area.** Power networks are being upgraded across Europe, and firms like Camlin need engineers who understand both hardware and data.
- **Siemens Energy ownership** brings global projects and career paths beyond Northern Ireland.
- **Software and data people are wanted too,** not only engineers with factory experience.
- **Apply early.** The first wave of hires often moves into lead roles as the team grows.
