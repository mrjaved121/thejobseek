End-of-service gratuity is the lump sum a UAE private-sector employer owes an expat worker when the job ends. It is one of the most common questions workers have, and one of the most common disputes. Here is how it works under the current UAE Labour Law.

## Who gets gratuity

- You must have completed **at least one year of continuous service** with the employer. Below one year, no gratuity is due.
- Once you pass one year, **part-years count pro rata**.
- **Unpaid absence days do not count** as service.
- UAE nationals are covered by the pensions system instead.

## The formula

Gratuity is based on your **basic salary only**. Housing, transport and other allowances are not included.

| Years of service | Gratuity per year |
|---|---|
| Years 1 to 5 | 21 days of basic salary |
| Each year after 5 | 30 days of basic salary |

The **total can never be more than two years' wages**.

To get your daily basic wage, divide your monthly basic salary by 30.

## Worked examples

**Example 1: 3 years, basic salary AED 6,000**
- Daily wage: 6,000 ÷ 30 = AED 200
- 21 days × 3 years = 63 days
- Gratuity: 63 × 200 = **AED 12,600**

**Example 2: 7 years, basic salary AED 9,000**
- Daily wage: 9,000 ÷ 30 = AED 300
- First 5 years: 21 × 5 = 105 days
- Next 2 years: 30 × 2 = 60 days
- Total: 165 days × 300 = **AED 49,500**

## When it must be paid

Your employer must pay your gratuity, final salary and other dues **within 14 days** of the contract ending. The employer may deduct any amounts you legitimately owe the company.

## Part-time and other work models

For part-time workers, gratuity is reduced in proportion to the hours worked compared with a full-time job. Temporary contracts of less than one year do not earn gratuity.

## The voluntary savings scheme

Some employers have moved staff to the UAE's **voluntary end-of-service savings scheme**. Instead of a lump sum at the end, the employer pays a monthly contribution into an approved investment fund:

- **5.83%** of basic salary a month for staff with under 5 years' service
- **8.33%** a month for staff with more than 5 years

If you join, gratuity earned before joining must be settled, and you can withdraw your savings when your job ends.

## If your employer doesn't pay

1. Ask in writing for your final settlement statement.
2. Still unpaid after 14 days? Report it to **MoHRE**. Complaints are free and can be lodged online or by phone on 600590000.

> Check your contract and your basic salary figure carefully, because gratuity is calculated on basic pay, not your total salary.
