Want a job unlike any other? The **Australian Antarctic Program** is recruiting for **more than 200 contracts** for the **2027/28 season**. The campaign opened on **1 October 2026**, and applications close on **31 October 2026**, so there is only one week left to apply.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Positions | 200+ contracts |
| Season | 2027/28 |
| Contract length | 6 months to 12+ months |
| Workplaces | Davis, Mawson and Casey stations, plus Wilkins summer runway |
| Who can apply | Australian citizens |
| Closing date | 31 October 2026 |

## Roles on offer

The program needs people with practical skills to keep its research stations running. Roles fall into several groups:

| Area | Example roles |
|---|---|
| Trades | Electrician, carpenter, plumber, mechanic |
| Hospitality | Chef |
| Medical | Antarctic medical practitioner (doctor) |
| Voyage | Voyage leader |
| Aviation | Aircraft ground support officer |
| Field, logistics and communications | Field, logistics and telecommunications staff |

The official list includes more than 25 job types in total.

## Requirements

- **Australian citizenship** is required.
- Trade roles need **relevant qualifications** and **several years' experience**.
- Check each role description for any extra conditions, as staff live and work in remote, extreme conditions for long periods.

Contracts vary in length: some cover about six months, while others last more than a year.

## How to apply

Apply through the official jobs site at [jobs.antarctica.gov.au](https://jobs.antarctica.gov.au/jobs-in-antarctica/). Read each role description carefully and submit your application before **31 October 2026**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Tradespeople are in demand.** If you are a qualified electrician, plumber, carpenter or mechanic, this is a rare chance to use your skills in Antarctica.
- **Think about the lifestyle.** You will live with a small team, far from family, for months. Teamwork and resilience matter as much as technical skills.
- **Apply now.** With the deadline days away, prepare your CV, licences and references today.
- **Not eligible?** Non-citizens can look at other Australian government and trades jobs, where skills shortages remain.
