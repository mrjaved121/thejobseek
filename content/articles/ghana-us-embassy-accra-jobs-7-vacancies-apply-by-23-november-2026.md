**US Embassy Accra jobs are open now: seven vacancies are listed on the embassy's official recruitment portal, and three roles open to all applicants are still taking applications until 18, 19 and 23 November 2026.** You apply online yourself through the US State Department's ERA system. There is no fee, no paper form and no agent.

## Which vacancies are listed?

[YEN.com.gh reported](https://yen.com.gh/ghana/315518-ghana-embassy-announces-job-vacancies-including-driver-clerk/) the seven openings on 11 November. We then read each one on the [official ERA portal](https://erajobs.state.gov/dos-era/gha/vacancysearch/searchVacancies.hms) on 12 November. All are full-time posts based in Accra.

| Position | Who may apply | Salary a year (USD) | Closes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chauffeur | All applicants | 5,892 – 9,120 | 12 Nov |
| Lead Maintenance Technician | All applicants | 17,070 – 26,454 | 12 Nov |
| Mechanical Engineer Supervisor | All applicants | 32,246 – 49,982 | 18 Nov |
| Senior Political Specialist | All applicants | 26,520 – 41,112 | 19 Nov |
| Administrative Clerk, Health Unit | US Eligible Family Members only | 47,731 – 70,094 | 19 Nov |
| Consular Associate | US Eligible Family Members only | 47,731 – 87,709 | 20 Nov |
| Emerging Voices Exchanges Coordinator | All applicants | 17,070 – 26,454 | 23 Nov |

The first two close today, so only apply for those if your documents are already scanned. The portal sets most of these deadlines in GMT, which is Ghana time.

## What do the three remaining open roles require?

| Role | Education | Experience | Language |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mechanical Engineer Supervisor | Four-year BSc in Mechanical Engineering or a close equivalent | Five years as an engineer running preventive maintenance at a large site, plus one year supervising | Stated in the listing |
| Senior Political Specialist | Degree in political science, international relations, history or international law | Five years in political research, policy analysis, government affairs or political reporting | Fluent English and a good working knowledge of one Ghanaian language |
| Emerging Voices Exchanges Coordinator | Degree in international relations, communications, marketing, PR, American Studies or similar | Three years in a multicultural workplace with project, communication or education duties | Fluent English |

The listings describe these as permanent appointments, subject to a probation period.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Open the portal:** go to [erajobs.state.gov](https://erajobs.state.gov/dos-era/gha/vacancysearch/searchVacancies.hms). The Accra list shows every open post with its announcement number.
2. **Read the whole vacancy,** including the Duties and Qualifications tabs. Note the announcement number, for example Accra-2026-043 for the political post.
3. **Create an ERA account** with an email address you check daily.
4. **Scan your documents.** The political specialist listing asks all applicants for a university degree, plus a residency permit and work permit if these apply to you. Other posts list their own papers.
5. **Fill in every section.** The embassy asks you to address education under Education, and experience and job knowledge under Major Duties and Responsibilities. Incomplete forms can be disqualified.
6. **Submit before the closing date** and keep the confirmation.
7. **Afterwards:** the listings say HR contacts successful applicants. You may be asked to sit a language or skills test. Selected candidates go through a background investigation for a Public Trust clearance and possibly a medical exam.

Questions go to the embassy HR office at AccraHRRecruitment-DL@state.gov or +233 302 741 000, the contacts given on each vacancy.

## Tips that improve your chances

- **Mirror the requirements.** If the post asks for five years of policy analysis, show the dates and employers that add up to five years.
- **Do not write "see attached CV".** Screeners read the form fields first.
- **Name your Ghanaian language** and level if you apply for the political role.
- **Apply a day early.** Portals slow down near a deadline, and late forms are not accepted.

Checklist: ERA account, degree certificate, national ID or passport, permits if you are not Ghanaian, referees, announcement number.

## Do I need a middleman?

No. The embassy recruits only through ERA and does not charge for applications, interviews or forms. Anyone who says they can "connect" you to an embassy job for a fee is running a scam, and paying them will not help your application. An honest reminder too: these are competitive posts and applying does not guarantee an interview.

## What it means for job seekers

- Pay is quoted in US dollars, which makes these roles attractive, so expect many applicants.
- Engineers with facilities experience and analysts with political or media backgrounds are the main targets this round.
- The embassy posts new vacancies often. If you miss this batch, bookmark the portal and check weekly.
