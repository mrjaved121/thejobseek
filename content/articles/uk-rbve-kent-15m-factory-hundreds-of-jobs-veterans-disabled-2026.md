New **veterans jobs** are coming to Kent. Veterans' charity **Royal British Veterans Enterprise (RBVE)** has started building a **£15 million factory** that will create **hundreds of jobs, placements and training places** for veterans and disabled people. Falklands veteran **Simon Weston** joined the launch, announced on 5 November 2026.

## What is being built?

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Royal British Veterans Enterprise (RBVE) |
| Location | Kent, England |
| Investment | £15 million |
| Jobs | Hundreds of jobs, placements and learning places |
| Training | Training Academy with accredited qualifications |
| Who it is for | Veterans and disabled people |

Graduates of the academy will also be helped into jobs with **other local employers**, not only at RBVE.

## What does RBVE make?

RBVE has been making signs for over six decades. Its factories in **Kent, Surrey and Glasgow** turn out **over 250,000 signs a year** for roads, railways and businesses, and the charity believes about **one in five UK road signs** are made by its teams, including signs for Network Rail and National Highways. Over **70%** of its staff are veterans, disabled people, or both.

## Why does it matter?

Government data quoted by RBVE shows only **52.8%** of disabled people are in work, compared with **82.5%** of non-disabled people. Census data also shows that close to a third of the 1.85 million armed forces veterans in England and Wales have a disability. Chief Executive **Lisa Farmer** said work is the most powerful thing the charity can offer someone.

## What jobs are likely?

A sign-manufacturing factory typically needs:

- Production and assembly operatives
- Machine and print operators
- Warehouse, packing and dispatch staff
- Quality control and maintenance roles
- Admin, sales and supervisory jobs

These are typical roles for this type of factory, not a confirmed vacancy list.

## How do I apply?

The factory is being built now. Veterans and disabled job seekers interested in future roles or training should contact Royal British Veterans Enterprise through its official channels.
