**Aaru careers in New York are about to grow fast: the AI company intends to create over 650 jobs in the city within five years, and its official job board already lists 27 open roles in NYC.** Many engineering and research posts advertise pay from $200,000 a year plus equity. You apply directly at aaru.com/careers.

## What has been announced?

[FingerLakes1.com](https://www.fingerlakes1.com/2026/11/13/aaru-plans-more-than-650-new-nyc-jobs-with-state-tax-credit-offer/) reported on 13 November 2026 that Aaru's Manhattan team is set to grow from roughly 60 people to over 700. It cites a release from Governor Kathy Hochul's office.

Empire State Development has offered the firm up to $13.12 million in Excelsior tax credits. These are performance-based: the company only earns them by delivering the 650 jobs and spending $109 million on R&D. Aaru will also take over 27,000 sq ft of offices close to Union Square, which will house its research and development centre.

Aaru builds AI simulations of how groups of people behave, used to test products and pricing before launch.

## Which roles are open?

The state release says the new posts will cover software engineering, IT research and product development, along with enterprise sales, client management and business operations.

We counted the live adverts on [Aaru's careers page](https://aaru.com/careers) on 15 November 2026: 34 roles in total, of which 27 are in New York City, three in San Francisco, three in Singapore and one covering Europe, the Middle East and Africa. A sample of the New York roles with a published pay band:

| Role (New York City) | Posted pay band |
|---|---|
| Software Engineer, Product | $200K – $600K + equity |
| Software Engineer, Platform | $200K – $600K + equity |
| Research Engineer | $200K – $600K + equity |
| Engineer, Infrastructure | $250K – $800K + equity |
| Product Designer | $205K – $320K + equity |
| Deployment Lead, NYC | $180K – $300K + equity |

Other New York openings, such as Account Executive, Simulation Architect, Brand Designer and Legal Engineer, showed no salary band. Wide bands usually cover several seniority levels, so do not assume the top figure.

## Who can apply?

These are skilled roles. There is no single entry requirement, so read each advert. Two points apply across the board:

- **It is office-based.** The adverts state that Aaru is an in-person company working five days a week in the office, and candidates are expected to be in the country or open to relocating.
- **International candidates are not ruled out.** The benefits section of the adverts lists visa sponsorship and relocation support alongside medical, dental and vision cover. Sponsorship is still decided case by case and is never automatic.

## How to apply — step by step

1. Open [aaru.com/careers](https://aaru.com/careers) and filter by team (Technical Staff, Research, Product & Design or Revenue) and by location.
2. Click a role to read the full description, then press **Apply**. The form is hosted on the company's own applicant system.
3. Prepare a résumé of one or two pages, links to work you can show (GitHub, papers, portfolio, shipped products) and a short note on why this role.
4. State clearly whether you are already in New York, can relocate, and whether you would need sponsorship.
5. If nothing fits, the page invites speculative applications at jobs@aaru.com.
6. Then wait to be contacted. Aaru has not published its interview stages or timelines.

Apply only on the official site. No recruiter fee, agent or paid "referral" is needed, and a genuine employer will never charge you for a job offer or a visa.

## Tips to improve your chances

- **Show finished work, not just tools.** The product engineering advert stresses owning a problem from first conversation to a reliable release. Describe one project you carried all the way through, with the result.
- **Use evidence.** The adverts stress intellectual honesty and measurement, so put numbers on what you built or sold.
- **Match the team.** Do not send one generic résumé to five posts.
- **Common mistakes:** applying for a remote arrangement that is not offered and listing skills with no outcomes.

**Quick checklist:** tailored résumé · work samples · location statement · sponsorship need, if any.

## What it means for job seekers

- The 650 jobs will arrive over five years, not at once. The 27 live New York roles are the real opportunity today; check the page regularly as more are added.
- Most openings are senior and highly paid, so competition will be strong. Early-career candidates should watch for junior posts rather than expect them now.
- Nothing guarantees an interview; a specific, well-evidenced application improves your chances.
