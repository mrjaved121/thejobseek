Football fans looking for **seasonal jobs USA** can work at one of America's most famous stadiums. The **Green Bay Packers** are holding two **walk-in job fairs** at **Lambeau Field** in November 2026, with **on-site interviews** for gameday guest services, security and food and beverage roles.

## When are the job fairs?

| Date | Time | Place |
|---|---|---|
| Wednesday, 11 November 2026 | 4pm – 6pm | Johnsonville Tailgate Village, Lambeau Field |
| Wednesday, 18 November 2026 | 4pm – 6pm | Johnsonville Tailgate Village, Lambeau Field |

Parking is in **Lot 3**, on the stadium's east side by Oneida Street, with entry from Lombardi Avenue.

## Which jobs are open?

- **Jobs with the Packers:** gameday security and guest services
- **Food and drink jobs:** the stadium caterer, Delaware North Sportservice, is recruiting part-time and gameday staff and will be at the **18 November** fair

## Who can apply?

- At least **16 years old** for guest services and security roles
- Strong communication and problem-solving skills
- Able to stay focused and **stand for a full shift**
- Gameday staff must be available for **all home games**, possible playoff games and other stadium events

## How do I apply?

Go to either job fair for an on-site interview. If you cannot attend, apply online on the Packers' official **[employment page](https://www.packers.com/employment)**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Great for students and part-timers** who want flexible, short-term work.
- **Interviews happen on the spot**, so bring ID and be ready to talk about your availability.
- **Playoff availability matters.** Make sure you can work extra dates if the team goes deep.
