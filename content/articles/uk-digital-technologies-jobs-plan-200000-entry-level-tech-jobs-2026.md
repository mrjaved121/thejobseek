The UK has published a **Digital and Technologies Jobs Plan** that aims to open about **200,000 entry-level tech jobs** over the next ten years. The plan, released on **21 October 2026**, is the third of the government's sector Jobs Plans for young people.

The thinking behind it is simple: the country expects about **800,000 digital and tech roles** to need filling between 2025 and 2035, yet first jobs for newcomers have become harder to find.

## Key numbers

| Detail | Figure |
|---|---|
| Tech roles to fill, 2025–2035 | About 800,000 |
| Entry-level jobs target (10 years) | About 200,000 |
| Average per year | About 20,000 |
| Placements pledged by TechFirst partners | 10,000+ |
| Young people to reach with AI and tech learning | 1 million by 2028 |
| Adults to gain AI skills (AI Skills Boost) | 10 million by 2030 |

## Why entry-level jobs matter

The plan points to cyber security as an example of the problem. In 2022, about a quarter of cyber job adverts were open to people with less than a year's experience. By 2025, that had fallen to **16%**. The new plan tries to reverse this by bundling work experience, T Level placements, apprenticeships, degree routes and first jobs into a single **Skills Deal** with employers.

## Which companies are involved

Firms backing the **TechFirst** programme include:

| Company | Sector |
|---|---|
| Microsoft, IBM, Cisco | Big tech and cloud |
| Arm | Chip design |
| Kainos, Cognizant | IT services |
| QinetiQ, AtkinsRéalis | Defence and engineering |

Together they have promised **over 10,000** work experience, placement and learning opportunities.

## Other changes

- Public contracts worth **£5 million or more** will give more weight to "social value", such as hiring and training young people.
- From **April 2027**, the chartered IT body **BCS** will offer free professional recognition to people who finish a digital T Level, V Level or apprenticeship.
- Local delivery will be led with mayors, devolved governments and local partners, with extra help for small businesses.

## How to apply

Tech apprenticeships are listed on the official [Find an apprenticeship](https://www.findapprenticeship.service.gov.uk/) service. The full plan is on [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/digital-and-technologies-jobs-plan).

## What it means for job seekers

- **Target apprenticeships and placements.** These are the main doors the plan is opening.
- **Learn practical AI skills now.** Free AI training through government schemes is expanding fast.
- **Cyber and data are short of people.** Entry routes into these fields are a stated priority.
- **Check big suppliers to the public sector.** Firms bidding for large government contracts will be pushed to hire young people.

The same week, the government published [plans for 100,000+ youth openings in business services and construction](/uk/uk-industrial-strategy-jobs-plans-100000-youth-opportunities-apprenticeships-2026/).
