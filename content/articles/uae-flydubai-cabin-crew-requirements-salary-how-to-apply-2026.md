**flydubai hires cabin crew to be based in Dubai, and anyone who meets its minimum rules can apply online at [careers.flydubai.com](https://careers.flydubai.com/cabin-crew).** You must be at least 21, stand at least 158 cm tall, hold a high school diploma and have one year of customer service work. There is no fixed number of posts and no closing date: the airline opens cabin crew vacancies when it needs people, so the main deadline is simply whether a vacancy is live when you check.

This guide is built from the official flydubai cabin crew page, which we read on 19 November 2026, plus a [Gulf News report](https://gulfnews.com/business/aviation/flydubai-opens-hiring-salary-perks-and-travel-benefits-for-cabin-crew-revealed-1.500510916) from April 2026 that repeated the same pay figures.

## Is flydubai hiring cabin crew right now?

When we searched the flydubai careers site on 19 November 2026, it showed four open jobs, all in offices, IT and engineering. None was a cabin crew vacancy, and the events page listed no recruitment days.

That changes during the year. If nothing is open, join the airline's **Talent Community** (the form is linked at the bottom of every careers page). It keeps your details on file so you can hear when roles open. Check the [cabin crew vacancies list](https://careers.flydubai.com/jobs?page=1&categories=Cabin%20Crew) every week or two.

## flydubai cabin crew requirements: who can apply?

These are the minimum requirements as flydubai lists them. Nothing here comes from agents or job boards.

**Personal and physical**

- Age 21 or older
- Height of at least 158 cm, a rule the airline ties to safety
- A valid passport
- Healthy enough to pass an aviation (aero) medical check
- No tattoos that show while in uniform
- A neat, professional appearance that fits flydubai's grooming standards
- Confidence in water while wearing a life jacket or flotation aid

**Education, language and experience**

- A high school diploma (the page calls this essential)
- Fluent written and spoken English; other languages count in your favour
- One year in a customer service job, ideally in aviation, hospitality or retail
- International aviation experience is an advantage but not required

**Attitude and lifestyle**

- Willing to move to Dubai
- Willing to work irregular hours, including nights, weekends and public holidays
- Calm under pressure and safety-minded, able to handle emergencies
- Comfortable working in a team of many nationalities

Emirates, by comparison, lists a 160 cm minimum height and a 212 cm reach on its own careers page, while flydubai sets a 158 cm height minimum and mentions no reach test. ## flydubai cabin crew salary and benefits

The airline publishes its pay in two parts. Flying pay changes with your roster, so the total is an average, not a promise.

| Item | What flydubai lists |
|---|---|
| Basic salary + housing allowance + transport allowance | AED 8,275 a month |
| Variable flying pay | About AED 4,500 a month on average, based on 90 flying hours |
| Medical insurance | Provided |
| Annual leave | 30 days paid leave a year, plus an annual leave ticket |
| End of service benefit | Provided |
| Training bond | About USD 2,650 (AED 9,700) for the first year |
| Base | Dubai, UAE |

Two points matter here. First, housing appears as an allowance inside the AED 8,275; the page does not mention free company flats, so ask how housing works when you receive an offer. Second, the training bond means you may owe the airline money if you leave during your first year; read the bond terms in your offer letter before signing.

## How to apply for flydubai cabin crew: step by step

The process below is the one described on the official page. Every step is online until the assessment day.

1. **Check a vacancy is open.** Go to the [cabin crew jobs list](https://careers.flydubai.com/jobs?page=1&categories=Cabin%20Crew). If it is empty, join the Talent Community and come back later.
2. **Prepare your files.** Have an English CV in PDF, a scan of your passport photo page and a copy of your high school certificate. Make sure your CV shows at least 12 months of customer-facing work with dates.
3. **Create your candidate profile.** Fill in every field. The airline says it asks role-specific questions here, so answer them honestly; they are checked against your CV.
4. **Take the online tests.** flydubai sends a link to psychometric tests on verbal reasoning, logic, multitasking and how you behave at work. Do the practice questions first.
5. **Record the video interview.** You answer a fixed set of questions on camera, in your own time. Use a quiet room, good light and a plain background.
6. **Wait for the application review.** The airline checks your tests and profile. Only candidates who pass are invited to an assessment day.
7. **Attend the assessment day** if invited. Any offer, medical or visa step should come to you in writing from flydubai itself.

No closing date is published for cabin crew applications. Check the live vacancy list on the careers site for what is open.

## Tips that help your application

- **Write your service experience clearly.** Name the employer, your job title and what you did for customers. "Handled 80 guests a shift at a hotel front desk" says more than "good communication skills".
- **Show your languages.** Arabic, Urdu, Hindi, Russian or any other language is listed as an advantage.
- **Practise the reasoning tests.** flydubai offers practice questions before the real tests; use them and work at a steady pace.
- **Treat the video like a real interview.** Dress as you would for the assessment day and speak slowly.
- **Swimming:** if you cannot float calmly in a life jacket, practise before you apply. The requirement is on the official list.

## Tips for applicants from Pakistan and India

Most readers of TheJobSeek apply from Pakistan or India. These extra steps come from the government sites of each country, checked on 19 November 2026.

**Document attestation.** The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs explains on its [attestation page](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Services/Attestation) that documents issued outside the UAE are attested through UAE missions abroad, and it asks applicants to check the website of the UAE embassy or consulate in their own country. Ask flydubai, after an offer, whether your school certificate must be attested, and start early because it takes time.

**Pakistan: Protector of Emigrants (BE&OE).** Before leaving for a job abroad, Pakistani workers register with the Bureau of Emigration and Overseas Employment and get the Protector's stamp on the passport. For direct hiring, the bureau's published list includes your visa, passport, CNIC and the signed employment contract, plus insurance and welfare fund payments, and it lists police verification and medical reports for some destination countries. Check the current list at [beoe.gov.pk](https://beoe.gov.pk/how-to-get-emigrants-protection) or your nearest Protectorate office. A new protector clearance is needed if you later change employer.

**India: eMigrate and ECR/ECNR.** India's Ministry of External Affairs lists the UAE among 19 Emigration Check Required countries. If your passport is ECR, you must get emigration clearance through [emigrate.gov.in](https://emigrate.gov.in) before you fly for work. ECNR passport holders do not need clearance but may register on eMigrate voluntarily. The Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana insurance is mandatory for ECR workers; the ministry's April 2026 overview gives cover of INR 10 lakh for a premium of INR 275 (two years) or INR 375 (three years).

**Police clearance.** If you are asked for one, Indian passport holders apply for a Police Clearance Certificate through the [Passport Seva portal](https://www.passportindia.gov.in/). In Pakistan, check the BE&OE list above and your provincial police service.

**Medicals.** flydubai requires an aero medical exam. Do not pay a clinic in advance for a "flydubai medical" unless flydubai itself tells you where to go in writing.

**Scam warning.** flydubai says fake offers are circulating by email, letter, SMS and WhatsApp, often asking for bank details. It states that genuine messages come only from an @flydubai.com address, that it never refers applicants to a third party for applications or visas, and that suspicious messages can be reported to recruitment@flydubai.com. Never pay an agent for a job, a visa or an interview slot. Our guide to [fake UAE job offers](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/) lists more warning signs.
