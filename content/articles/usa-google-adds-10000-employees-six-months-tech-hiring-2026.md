Looking for **Google jobs**? The company is still growing. Between April and September 2026 Google's staff count rose by roughly **10,000**, reaching **204,757**, according to its third-quarter results. That puts Google on a different path from several tech giants that are trimming staff.

## How fast is Google growing?

| Measure | Figure |
|---|---|
| Headcount, end of September 2026 | 204,757 |
| Growth in six months | About 10,000 |
| Staff added via Wiz acquisition | About 2,000 (deal closed 11 March 2026) |
| Trend | Growing since the second quarter of 2024 |

Business Insider reported that Google's hiring pace picked up towards the end of 2025 and has continued into 2026.

## How much is real hiring?

Not all of the 10,000 are new recruits. Google completed its purchase of cybersecurity company **Wiz** in March 2026, bringing about **2,000 Wiz staff** onto the payroll. Google has not split out how much of the rest came from fresh hiring. Still, even after removing Wiz, the numbers point to thousands of new hires.

Google has also made smaller job cuts in some teams since its large 2023 layoff round, so the picture is mixed: some areas shrink while the company grows overall.

## How does Google compare with other tech firms?

While Google expands, some rivals are cutting back. Meta has gone through several rounds of cost-cutting, and Microsoft has offered a voluntary buyout programme in some teams. For job seekers, this makes Google one of the more active big-tech employers right now.

## How do I apply?

All Google vacancies are listed on the official **[Google Careers site](https://www.google.com/about/careers/applications/)**. You can filter by country, city, team, remote options and experience level, and set up alerts for new roles.

## What it means for job seekers

- **AI, cloud and security skills are in demand.** The Wiz deal shows Google is investing heavily in cybersecurity.
- **Target your applications.** Pick the few roles that best match your skills and tailor your CV to each one.
- **Prepare for structured interviews.** Expect coding, problem-solving and behaviour-based questions.
- **Google hires worldwide.** Many roles are in the US, but Google also hires across Europe, Asia and the Middle East.
