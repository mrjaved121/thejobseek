There is fresh **US hiring 2026** news from the car industry. **Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama (HMMA)** said on 28 October that it will spend close to **$450 million** upgrading its factory in **Montgomery, Alabama**, a move that brings **53 new full-time jobs** paying an average of **$30.03 an hour**. Governor Kay Ivey joined company leaders for the announcement.

## The expansion at a glance

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Hyundai Motor Manufacturing Alabama |
| Location | Montgomery, Alabama |
| Investment | Nearly $450 million |
| New jobs | 53 full-time |
| Average pay | $30.03/hour (about $62,467/year) |
| Current workforce | About 4,200 |

## What the money will build

The investment has two main goals. First, the plant will produce **more engines for hybrid and extended-range electric vehicles (EREVs)**, a fast-growing part of Hyundai's line-up. Second, assembly lines will be retooled so the factory can build the **refreshed Santa Fe** and the **all-new Tucson**, including hybrid versions of both.

With this project, Hyundai's total spending at the Montgomery site since 2005 climbs to around **$2.2 billion**.

## What the leaders said

Governor Ivey described the deal as the next step in a partnership between Hyundai and Alabama that goes back 24 years. HMMA's chief operating officer, Mark Rader, said the investment reflects the company's faith in its Alabama workforce, while state Commerce Secretary Ellen McNair said it will help prepare local workers to build the next generation of vehicles.

## Who might be hired

The company has not published a list of the 53 roles. Expansions like this usually need production team members, maintenance technicians, engine assembly staff, quality inspectors and engineers who understand electrified powertrains. The pay average is well above typical factory wages in the region, so competition is likely to be strong.

## How to apply

Hyundai posts Montgomery vacancies on its official [HMMA job search page](https://www.hmmausa.com/jobs-hmma/job-search/), which leads to the company's careers site. Create a profile and set up alerts so you see new production and maintenance jobs as soon as they are posted.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Watch for new listings** over the coming months as the retooling work moves ahead.
- **Highlight hybrid or EV experience** – battery, motor or engine assembly skills are in demand.
- **Maintenance skills pay** – PLC, robotics and industrial electrical experience stand out at large car plants.
- **Check local training routes** – Alabama's workforce programmes often run courses linked to big manufacturers.
- Only apply through the official Hyundai site; ignore anyone asking for fees to "guarantee" a job.
