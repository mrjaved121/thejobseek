**Mastercard Singapore jobs are growing: the payments company plans to hire 250 more people at its Asia-Pacific headquarters by the end of 2029, and its official careers site already shows 27 vacancies in Singapore.** The announcement came on 12 November 2026 with the launch of a new innovation centre. You can apply today, free and direct, on careers.mastercard.com.

## What did Mastercard announce?

According to [The Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/tech/mastercard-to-hire-250-more-staff-in-singapore-by-end-2029), Mastercard opened its first Innovation Centre of Excellence in Asia-Pacific inside its office at DUO Tower. The main points:

| Fact | Detail |
|---|---|
| New hires | 250 by end-2029 |
| Current Singapore staff | More than 850 |
| Target | Over 1,000 |
| Innovation centre | 7,200 sq ft, more than 55 staff today |
| Focus areas | Responsible AI, trusted digital commerce, cybersecurity, data, payments |
| Markets served | More than 40 across the region |

Four units sit inside the centre: the AI Centre of Excellence, the Fusion Centre, the Mastercard Experience Centre and the Makerspace. The newspaper says it will also watch for emerging cyber and fraud risks and let customers test prototypes.

Minister Tan See Leng, who opened the site, said Mastercard has already taken on over 100 early-career staff in Singapore via its graduate development programme, and he urged the company to keep training local talent, including new graduates.

## Which roles are open today?

We looked at [Mastercard's Singapore careers page](https://careers.mastercard.com/us/en/singapore) on 13 November 2026. It returned **27 jobs**. The newest ten were in engineering, sales, product and consulting, posted between 26 October and 10 November. Examples:

- Lead Platform Engineer, Network Operations
- Lead Engineer, Site Reliability Engineering
- Manager, Fraud Attack Response and Insights (Asia-Pacific)
- Director, Fraud and Decisioning Solutions, Regional Product Management
- Senior Specialist, Implementation
- Analyst, Business Development, Performance and Analytics
- Consultant, Advisors and Consulting Services

Most of these are for experienced people. The 250 new positions will be added over three years, so expect them to appear gradually, not all at once.

## How to apply — step by step

1. **Open the official page:** [careers.mastercard.com/us/en/singapore](https://careers.mastercard.com/us/en/singapore).
2. **Press "View open opportunities"** and filter by job category if you wish.
3. **Read one advert carefully.** Note the must-have skills and the seniority in the title (Analyst, Specialist, Manager, Lead, Director, Vice President).
4. **Apply online** with your CV. Each advert has its own apply button.
5. **No match yet?** The page invites you to join Mastercard's Talent Community so recruiters can see your resume when a suitable role opens.
6. **Wait for contact.** Mastercard does not state a response time on the page. Interviews follow for shortlisted candidates.

Apply only through the company's website. A genuine employer does not charge candidates, and no agent can sell you a job there. Treat any message asking for a "placement fee" or your banking details as a scam.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Mirror the focus areas.** AI engineering, data science, fraud prevention and cybersecurity are the skills named in the announcement. Put matching projects at the top of your CV.
- **Use numbers.** "Cut false fraud alerts by 20%" is stronger than "worked on fraud models".
- **Show regional experience.** The Singapore office supports more than 40 markets, so work across several Asian countries is worth mentioning.
- **Pick the right level.** A common mistake is applying for Director roles with three years of experience. Choose titles that fit.
- **Foreign applicants:** check your work pass eligibility before applying. The adverts we saw do not promise sponsorship, and the minister's remarks stressed local hiring.

Quick checklist: tailored CV in PDF, LinkedIn profile that matches it, two or three project examples with results, salary expectation, notice period.
