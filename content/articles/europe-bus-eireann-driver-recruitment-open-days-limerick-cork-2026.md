Looking for **jobs in Ireland** that you can land in a single day? **Bus Éireann**, the national bus and coach operator, has announced two new recruitment events for **professional bus drivers** and **heavy vehicle mechanics**. The first is an **open evening in Limerick on Wednesday 11 November 2026**, followed by an **open day in Cork on Wednesday 9 December**, according to the company's official careers site.

At both events, candidates can go through the main hiring steps on the spot, and strong applicants may be **fast-tracked**.

## Event dates

| Event | Date | Time | Location |
|---|---|---|---|
| Open evening | Wednesday 11 November 2026 | 5pm – 8pm | Roxboro Depot, Limerick (V94 EK65) |
| Open day | Wednesday 9 December 2026 | 10am – 2pm | Kinsale Road, Cork (T12 DN29) |

Bus Éireann says more details for each date will be released closer to the time.

## Which jobs are on offer

- **Professional bus drivers** – for people who already hold a category D licence
- **Trainee drivers** – Bus Éireann runs a training programme for people with a **car (B) licence**, helping them upgrade to a full bus licence
- **Heavy vehicle mechanics** – to maintain the fleet in its depots

The company runs a fleet of about **1,150 buses and coaches** from **16 depots**, serving around **225 routes** across Ireland. Pay rates were not listed on the open day page.

## What happens on the day

1. **Driving assessment**
2. **Written assessment**
3. **Interview**

Candidates who are successful are then given a **medical appointment**. Bring your driving licence and photo ID.

## Can foreigners apply?

You need the right to work in Ireland. EU and EEA citizens can apply freely. People from outside the EU need a valid permission to work. If your driving licence was issued abroad, check with the Irish licensing authorities whether it can be exchanged for an Irish one before you apply.

## How to apply

See the official [Bus Éireann open days page](https://careers.buseireann.ie/open-days-new) for updates, and browse the drivers' vacancies on the same careers site. Questions can be sent to **recruitment@buseireann.ie**.

## What it means for job seekers

- **No bus licence? You can still start.** The B-licence training route is a strong option for career changers.
- **Same-day progress.** Doing all the tests in one visit saves weeks of waiting.
- **Mechanics are needed too,** so heavy vehicle technicians should attend.
- **Stable public transport work** with regular hours and routes across the country.
