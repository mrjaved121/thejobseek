The Government of Canada has opened **Canada Summer Jobs (CSJ) 2027**, the yearly programme that pays employers to hire young people over the summer. Employers have until **11:59 pm Pacific Time on 3 December 2026** to apply, Employment and Social Development Canada (ESDC) announced on **27 October 2026**.

For youth, this is the first step towards next summer's job postings. Funded jobs go up on **Job Bank** from spring 2027.

## How Canada Summer Jobs works

The government covers part of the wage, which makes it easier for smaller employers to take on a young worker.

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Who the jobs are for | Youth aged 15 to 30 |
| Hours | Full time, 30 to 40 hours a week |
| Length | 6 to 16 weeks in a row (about 8 weeks on average) |
| Wage support (public and private employers) | 50% of the provincial or territorial minimum wage |
| Wage support (not-for-profits) | Up to 100% of minimum wage plus mandatory costs like EI and CPP/QPP |
| Employer deadline | 3 December 2026, 11:59 pm PT |
| Jobs posted | On Job Bank from April 2027 |

Private businesses can take part only if they have **50 or fewer full-time employees**. Not-for-profits and public sector employers can also apply.

## Priority sectors for 2027

This year the government wants more summer jobs in four areas:

1. **Construction**
2. **Environment and climate** activities
3. **Digital skills** and exposure to **artificial intelligence**
4. **Not-for-profits** that serve underserved and equity-deserving communities

Since 2019, CSJ has supported **more than 600,000 jobs**, according to ESDC. The programme is oversubscribed, so not every employer will be funded.

The announcement was made by **Patty Hajdu**, Minister of Jobs and Families, with support from **Anna Gainey**, Secretary of State (Children and Youth).

## How to apply

**If you are a young job seeker:** CSJ jobs are advertised on the government's official Job Bank. Visit the youth section at [jobbank.gc.ca/youth](https://www.jobbank.gc.ca/youth) and search for summer roles from spring 2027. You apply directly to the employer listed in each posting.

**If you are an employer:** Read the CSJ 2027 Applicant Guide and apply through the Grants and Contributions Online Services (GCOS) portal on canada.ca before 3 December.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Tell local employers about CSJ.** Small businesses, charities and community groups you already know may apply now and keep a spot for you.
- **Build skills in the priority areas.** Construction safety tickets, environmental volunteering or basic AI and digital skills can help.
- **Prepare your résumé early** so you can apply quickly when postings appear in April.
- **Set up Job Bank alerts** for summer jobs in your city.
- **Never pay for a CSJ job.** Real postings are free to apply for.
