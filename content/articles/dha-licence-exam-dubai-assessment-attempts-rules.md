To work as a doctor, nurse, pharmacist or allied health professional in Dubai, you need a licence from the **Dubai Health Authority (DHA)**. For most professions, that means passing a DHA assessment first. This guide is based on DHA's official manual for professional assessments.

## Two types of assessment

| Assessment | How it works |
|---|---|
| **Computer Based Test (CBT)** | Taken at a **Prometric** test centre |
| **Oral assessment** | Usually held online on a DHA platform; DHA can ask you to attend in person |

Which one you sit depends on your professional category. An oral assessment covers **at least five clinical scenarios** and lasts at least 20 to 30 minutes.

Some professionals are **exempt** from the exam under the UAE's unified Professional Qualification Requirements (PQR). An exemption only removes the exam. You still need credentialing, experience checks and primary source verification.

## How to book

1. Create an account on **Sheryan**, DHA's licensing system. This gives you an **Eligibility ID**.
2. **For the CBT:** book your slot on Prometric's DHA page using that Eligibility ID. Arrive 30 minutes early.
3. **For the oral exam:** choose a date or date range in Sheryan and pay the fee. DHA confirms the date based on availability. Arrive 15 minutes early.

You can reschedule an oral exam **once**, if you ask more than 5 working days ahead. Prometric bookings must be changed at least **5 calendar days** before the exam.

## Results

- **CBT:** uploaded to Sheryan within **2 working days**. The letter Prometric gives you on the day does not show whether you passed.
- **Oral:** released on Sheryan within **3 to 5 working days**.

You only see **PASS** or **FAIL**. DHA does not share scores or a topic breakdown. In the oral exam, all panel members must agree that you passed.

## The attempt limits

This is the part many candidates overlook:

- You get **three attempts** in total.
- DHA can cut this to one or two if serious competency gaps put patients at risk.
- After **three failures**, you cannot reapply to DHA for **two years**.
- You can get **three fresh attempts** after earning an extra recognised qualification, or after at least two years of licensed clinical experience since your last attempt.
- There is **no waiting period** between attempts, apart from slot availability.
- Cancelling or missing an exam does **not** use up an attempt, but the fee is not refunded.

## After you pass

Passing gives you a **Registration Certificate**, valid for **one year**. It is not yet a licence to practise. A licensed Dubai health facility must hire you and **activate** it into a full licence. So aim to have job offers lined up within that year.

## Tips

- Prepare for the format as well as the content. The CBT weights each subject area differently.
- Treat each attempt seriously, because the two-year block after three failures is long.
- Apply only through Sheryan and Prometric. Agents cannot speed up DHA decisions.
