**To work as a nurse in Dubai you need a licence from the Dubai Health Authority (DHA), and you apply for it yourself on DHA's Sheryan system.** A Registered Nurse needs a recognised bachelor's degree in nursing, a valid licence from home, a good standing certificate, a clean document check and, in most cases, a pass in a DHA exam. There is no closing date: Sheryan takes applications all year. Passing gives you a one-year Registration Certificate, which a Dubai hospital or clinic must then turn into a full licence when it hires you.

This guide was rebuilt on 28 November 2026 from three official documents opened that day: DHA's [Manual for Licensing Healthcare Professionals](https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf) (May 2025), DHA's [Manual for Professional Assessment](https://dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20for%20DHA%20Professional%20Assessment-Final2025533182.pdf) (May 2025), and the [Unified Healthcare Professional Qualification Requirements (PQR)](https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/072022/Unified%20Healthcare%20Professional%20Qualification202273235.pdf), the 2022 edition that all UAE health regulators share. The licensing manual notes that some PQR articles were amended again in 2024, so check your exact title in Sheryan's self-assessment.

## Who can apply for a DHA nursing licence?

The licensing manual says every healthcare professional must meet the PQR rules on qualifications, experience, home-country licensing, good standing, assessment and primary source verification. For nurses and midwives, the PQR adds these points:

- You graduated from a **recognised nursing college or university**. Degrees earned by distance learning, correspondence or as an honorary award do not count.
- A required bachelor's degree must have been a **full-time course of at least three years**.
- You hold a **valid licence or registration** to practise in your home country or your last country of work.
- You hold at least a valid **Basic Life Support (BLS)** certificate.
- You submit your **academic transcript**.
- You can show a **good standing certificate** from the authority that licensed you. DHA's manual defines this as proof that you have no disciplinary orders or criminal cases against you.

Assistant nurses cannot work alone in Dubai. The manual says they must work under a licensed Registered Nurse; the only exception is assisting a dentist in a dental clinic.

## Which nursing title can you get?

The PQR links each title to a qualification and, for some titles, years of experience. This table shows the main entry titles exactly as the 2022 PQR lists them.

| Title | Qualification in the PQR | Experience in the PQR |
|---|---|---|
| Registered Nurse | Bachelor's degree in nursing; or a nursing degree of at least two years plus RN registration in Canada, the USA, the UK, Ireland, South Africa, New Zealand or Australia | None required (gap-of-practice rules apply) |
| Assistant Nurse | Diploma of nursing of at least 18 months | None required (gap-of-practice rules apply) |
| School Nurse | Registered Nurse qualification plus valid PALS | Two years as an RN, one of them in paediatrics, ICU or emergency |
| Specialty Nurse | RN qualification plus a clinical master's in the specialty (at least one year) | One year in the specialty after qualifying |
| Specialty Nurse (bachelor's route) | Bachelor's degree in a nursing specialty (at least three years) | Two years in the related field |
| Nurse Practitioner | RN requirements plus a clinical master's or doctorate, or a national NP licence | Two years as a Nurse Practitioner |

**Salary:** DHA does not set nurses' pay. Salary is not published by the regulator and depends on the employer's offer.

If you hold a bachelor's degree but were licensed as an Assistant Nurse, the PQR lets you ask for an upgrade, provided your time as an assistant nurse is five years or less.

## What if you have not worked for a while?

The PQR calls this a gap of practice: a break from clinical work of more than two years. Nurses with a gap must earn CPD credits and complete accredited clinical training, then pass the exam.

| Break from clinical work | What nurses must complete |
|---|---|
| 2 to under 3 years | 20 CPD credits and 4 months of training |
| 3 to under 4 years | 40 CPD credits and 6 months of training |
| 4 to under 5 years | 60 CPD credits and 8 months of training |
| More than 5 years | Non-UAE nationals generally cannot apply |

## What is the DHA exam for nurses like?

DHA's assessment manual describes two formats. Your title decides which one you sit.

- **Computer Based Test (CBT):** taken at a Prometric centre. You book it on Prometric's DHA page with the Eligibility ID that Sheryan gives you, and pay by Visa, MasterCard or American Express. Arrive 30 minutes early with a passport that matches your Sheryan name exactly.
- **Oral assessment:** usually held online on a DHA platform, in English, with at least two panel members and at least five clinical scenarios. It lasts at least 20 to 30 minutes. You must sit alone in a closed room with no phone or smart watch.

Results come only through Sheryan, as PASS or FAIL. CBT results arrive within 2 working days; oral results within 3 to 5 working days. The letter Prometric hands you on the day shows strengths and weaknesses, not whether you passed.

**The attempt rules.** You have three attempts, with no waiting time between them. DHA can cut this to one or two if the panel sees a serious risk to patients. Failing the third time blocks you from applying to DHA for two years. You earn three fresh attempts after gaining a further recognised qualification or two years of licensed clinical work. Missing an exam does not count as a failed attempt, but exam fees are never refunded.

**Who skips the exam?** The PQR exempts nurses who hold a valid licence and good standing certificate from 20 listed countries, including the UK, the USA, Canada, Australia, Ireland, New Zealand, Singapore and South Africa. The degree must be under two years old, or you must have no gap since your last job. Nurses who studied in another language need IELTS 5.0, OET grade C, TOEFL 500 or Cambridge 169. An exemption only removes the exam; you still need the document check.

## How do I apply for a DHA license? Step by step

1. **Gather your papers.** Prepare your passport, nursing degree, transcript, home licence, experience letters with exact dates, BLS card and good standing certificate. The manual says documents not in English or Arabic need a certified legal translation.
2. **Create a Sheryan account.** Register on [DHA's Sheryan portal](https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home) and complete the self-assessment for your title. The manual says applications sent by email or on paper are refused.
3. **Declare everything.** List any past disciplinary action and every exam attempt with any UAE authority. Hiding a past fail or a complaint can lead to blacklisting.
4. **Complete primary source verification (PSV).** A third-party agency appointed by DHA checks your degree, experience, licence and good standing directly with the issuers. A "discrepancy" result can be appealed once; an "unable to verify" result twice. DHA also accepts PSV reports already done for DOH, MOHAP or Sharjah.
5. **Book and pass your assessment.** Use your Eligibility ID on Prometric for a CBT, or pick a date range in Sheryan for an oral exam.
6. **Receive your Registration Certificate.** It is valid for one year from your result date.
7. **Get hired and activated.** A Dubai facility with a valid DHA licence activates your registration into a licence. Until then you may not practise.

**Deadline:** no closing date is published. Apply on Sheryan when your documents are ready. The real time limit is the one-year life of your Registration Certificate.

## Tips for applicants from Pakistan and India

**Good standing first.** Request your good standing certificate early from the council that registered you, for example your provincial or national nursing council. The PSV agency verifies it at source, so a delay there delays everything.

**Attestation.** DHA's manual asks for original documents and, for some qualifications, an equivalency certificate from the UAE Ministry of Education. Where an employer asks for attestation, the [UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://www.mofa.gov.ae/en/Services/Attestation) handles documents issued outside the UAE through "Attestation through UAE Missions Abroad". It notes that only selected missions offer this, so check the UAE embassy or consulate website in Islamabad, Karachi, New Delhi or Mumbai.

**Police clearance and medicals.** DHA's manual does not ask for a police certificate. It needs a medical fitness report only in set cases, such as a health condition that could affect practice or an applicant aged 65 or older at activation. Your employer runs the UAE visa process separately.

**India – eMigrate.** The [eMigrate portal](https://emigrate.gov.in/) lists the UAE among 18 ECR countries. ECR passport holders need emigration clearance from the Protector of Emigrants before flying out for a job, plus the mandatory Pravasi Bharatiya Bima Yojana insurance (INR 275 for a two-year policy, INR 375 for a three-year one). ECNR holders who register are approved automatically. If a Dubai hospital hires you directly, eMigrate has a route for workers recruited by a foreign employer.

**Pakistan – Protector of Emigrants.** Pakistan's [Overseas Employment Corporation guide](https://oec.gov.pk/protector-process-guide) says no citizen may leave for paid work abroad, the UAE included, without the Protector stamp in the passport. Take your CNIC, passport, attested contract, medical certificate, insurance papers and four photos to a Protectorate of Emigrants office. The fee is paid through a bank challan and biometrics are taken there. The Protectorates sit under the Bureau of Emigration & Overseas Employment ([beoe.gov.pk](https://beoe.gov.pk/)); its site returned a security block on 28 November 2026, so ask the office for current fees.

**Scam warning.** You can open a Sheryan account and book the exam yourself. Never pay an agent for a DHA licence, a "guaranteed pass", a job, a visa or an interview slot. Anyone promising to skip the exam or PSV is lying: DHA blacklists applicants who submit false documents. Read our [warning signs of a fake UAE job offer](/uae/uae-job-offer-scam-warning-signs/).

## Quick checklist before you start

- Nursing degree and transcript (bachelor's: at least three years full-time)
- Valid home licence and a fresh good standing certificate
- Experience letters with start and end dates
- Valid BLS certificate
- Passport name that matches every document
- English test result, if your exemption route needs one
