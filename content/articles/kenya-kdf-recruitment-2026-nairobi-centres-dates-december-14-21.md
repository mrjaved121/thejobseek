**KDF recruitment 2026 runs from 14 to 28 December, and Nairobi has 18 sub-county slots at 17 venues between Monday 14 and Monday 21 December.** General Duty Recruits simply go to the centre for their sub-county on the right day. Cadets, Specialist Officers and Tradesmen/women must instead apply online at recruit.mod.go.ke by 12 December 2026. Everything is free.

## Who can apply?

All candidates must be Kenyan citizens with a valid national ID, no criminal record and good health.

| Category | Age | Minimum education |
|---|---|---|
| General Duty Recruit | 18–26 | KCSE D plain |
| GSO Cadet (Regular) | 18–23 | KCSE B plain, with C+ in English, Maths and one science |
| GSO Cadet (Graduate) | 18–26 | The cadet grades plus a listed degree |
| Specialist Officer | Up to 30 (medical consultants 35, chaplains and imams 39) | Cadet grades, degree and professional registration |
| Tradesmen/women (diploma) | 18–26 | KCSE C- and a relevant diploma |
| Tradesmen/women (certificate) | 18–26 | KCSE D+ and a craft certificate or Trade Test Grade II |

Physical rules: men at least 1.60 m and 54.55 kg; women at least 1.52 m and 50 kg; body mass index between 18.5 and 30. Women must not be pregnant during recruitment or training. Applicants with National Youth Service training have an advantage as recruits. Specialist Officers and trades need two years of practice after registration.

## How to apply — step by step

**General Duty Recruits (walk in):**

1. Find your sub-county in the table above or in the PDF.
2. Carry **original documents plus four copies each** of your national ID, KRA PIN certificate and academic certificates.
3. Go to the centre on the stated date. Identification is by biometrics.
4. Selected candidates face a final check at a KDF training institution.

**Cadets, Specialist Officers and Tradesmen/women (online):**

1. Open the official portal: [recruit.mod.go.ke](https://recruit.mod.go.ke).
2. Follow the portal's instructions and choose your category and field, such as engineering, ICT, education, medicine, law, vehicle mechanics, welding, plumbing or truck driving.
3. Upload what the form asks for and submit by **12 December 2026**. Only online applications count.
4. Check [mod.go.ke](https://www.mod.go.ke/) and the newspapers between **21 and 25 December** for the shortlist.
5. Stuck? Email the help desk at rms-helpdesk@mod.go.ke.

No broker, officer or politician can get you in. The ministry warns that anyone offering a "calling letter" is a fraudster, because none will be issued this year. Report offers of paid slots to the nearest police station or military camp, or call the hotline 0183749488.

## Tips that raise your chances

- **Check height, weight and BMI at home first.** Many people are sent away at the measuring stage.
- **Photocopy early.** Four sets of every document, in a clear folder.
- **Carry originals.** Copies alone are not accepted.
- **Train now.** Run several times a week; the advertisement demands physical and medical fitness.
- **Go to your own sub-county centre,** as the itinerary is organised by sub-county.
- **For online categories,** match your degree, diploma or certificate exactly to a field named in the advertisement.

Quick checklist: national ID, KRA PIN certificate, KCSE and other certificates, four copies of each, sports kit, water.
