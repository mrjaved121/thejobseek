Good news for anyone looking for **Bahrain jobs** in healthcare. **Mubadala Health Bahrain** is planning a new multispecialty hospital at **Bahrain Bay** that is expected to employ **more than 200 people**, News of Bahrain reported on 2 November 2026. On the same day, the Labour Fund **Tamkeen** signed a cooperation agreement with the hospital operator to support the hiring of Bahraini nationals.

The deal was signed at the **Gateway Gulf 2026** investment forum in Manama.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Mubadala Health Bahrain |
| Owners | Joint venture of M42 (Abu Dhabi) and HealthVision (Bahrain) |
| Location | Future Generations Reserve Tower, Bahrain Bay |
| Expected jobs | More than 200 |
| Hiring support | Tamkeen agreement, signed 2 November 2026 |
| Opening date | Not announced |

## Which jobs are expected

The hospital will need staff in four broad groups:

- **Medical consultants and specialist physicians**
- **Healthcare professionals**, such as nurses and allied health staff
- **Medical support staff**
- **Administrative and operational staff**, from patient services to facilities

The hospital plans to offer **20 medical specialties**, with six surgical inpatient specialties at launch. Named areas include **cardiology, oncology, neurology and orthopaedics**. It also plans services in longevity, precision medicine, genomics and AI-powered genetic testing, which points to demand for lab and data skills as well as clinical staff.

## Why Tamkeen matters

Tamkeen helps Bahraini job seekers by supporting employers that hire and train nationals. M42 says an earlier partnership with Tamkeen, which began in July, supported **more than 300 jobs** at **Amana Healthcare Bahrain**. The new agreement suggests a similar push to fill a good share of the 200+ roles with Bahrainis.

This will be M42's second healthcare facility in the kingdom. The project was first unveiled on 20 October 2026 at the Abu Dhabi Future Health Summit.

## How to apply

Recruitment has not opened yet, and no official application page has been named. The investment value and opening date were also not disclosed. Until hiring starts:

- Follow official M42 and Mubadala Health announcements.
- Bahraini job seekers should keep their profiles up to date on Tamkeen's channels, as Tamkeen-backed roles are often promoted there.
- Be wary of anyone asking for fees to "reserve" a hospital job. Real healthcare employers do not charge applicants.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Get your licence ready.** Doctors, nurses and allied health staff need a licence from Bahrain's health regulator (NHRA) before they can practise.
- **Specialists are in focus.** Experience in cardiology, oncology, neurology or orthopaedics will stand out.
- **Non-clinical roles count too.** Admin, front office and operations jobs make up part of the 200+ hiring.
- **Bahraini nationals have an edge** because of the Tamkeen agreement.
