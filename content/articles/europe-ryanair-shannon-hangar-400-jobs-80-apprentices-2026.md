**Ryanair** has won planning permission for a **€50 million** aircraft maintenance expansion at **Shannon Airport** in Co Clare. The airline said on **22 October 2026** that the new hangar will bring **more than 400 high-skilled jobs** and a new **engineering academy** to train hundreds of aircraft mechanics.

The approval from Clare County Council clears the way for building work to start in **early 2027**.

## Key facts

| Detail | Information |
|---|---|
| Employer | Ryanair |
| Location | Shannon Airport, Co Clare, Ireland |
| Investment | €50 million |
| New facility | Four-bay maintenance hangar |
| New jobs | 400+ high-skilled roles |
| Apprentice places | Up to 80 at a time |
| Mechanics to train | 400+ between 2027 and 2031 |
| Construction start | Early 2027 |
| Existing Shannon staff | 200+ in the current three-bay hangar |

## Ireland's biggest Ryanair maintenance base

The new hangar will add **four bays** to Ryanair's existing **three-bay** hangar at Shannon. Ryanair says this will make Shannon its largest maintenance facility in Ireland. Heavy maintenance means checking, repairing and overhauling Boeing 737 aircraft, so the jobs are mainly technical.

The project had faced delays over how contaminated soil on the site would be handled, and Ryanair had warned it could move the investment elsewhere. The planning decision settles that.

## Jobs and training

| Role type | What it involves |
|---|---|
| Licensed aircraft engineers (B1/B2) | Certifying maintenance on aircraft |
| Aircraft mechanics | Hands-on repairs and checks |
| Apprentices | Multi-year training through the new academy |
| Stores and planning | Parts, tools and maintenance scheduling |
| Supervisors | Leading hangar shifts |

The **Ryanair Engineering Academy** is the big opportunity for school leavers and career changers. With space for up to **80 apprentices**, it aims to turn out **more than 400 mechanics** over five years.

## How to apply

Ryanair posts engineering, mechanic and apprentice vacancies on its official careers site, [careers.ryanair.com](https://careers.ryanair.com/), which already lists roles at its Shannon hangar. Some engineering roles are advertised through Ryanair's engineering partner, so read each listing carefully to see who the employer is.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Aircraft engineers are in short supply,** and Ryanair is building a pipeline. Licensed engineers should apply now for current Shannon roles.
- **School leavers in the Mid-West** should keep an eye out for academy intakes from 2027.
- **Mechanics from other fields** (motor, plant, military) may find a route into aviation through apprenticeships.
- **Long-term work.** A training plan running to 2031 signals steady hiring for years.
