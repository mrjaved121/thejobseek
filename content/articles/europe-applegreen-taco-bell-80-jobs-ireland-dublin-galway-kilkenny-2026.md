New **jobs in Ireland** are coming in fast food. Fuel and forecourt retailer **Applegreen** announced on **2 November 2026** plans for **four more Taco Bell restaurants** in Ireland by year-end, bringing **80 new jobs**.

## Where are the new Taco Bell restaurants?

| Location | County | Opening |
|---|---|---|
| Applegreen Swords Village | Dublin | 3 November 2026 |
| Inchicore | Dublin | Before end of 2026 |
| Tuam Road | Galway | Before end of 2026 |
| Paulstown | Kilkenny | Before end of 2026 |

After these openings, Applegreen's Taco Bell count will reach **nine across the island**: eight south of the border and **one in Northern Ireland**.

## How has Taco Bell grown in Ireland?

Applegreen opened Ireland's first Taco Bell in September 2025 at its €15 million motorway service area at Junction 6 on the M3 in Dunshaughlin, Co Meath. It now also runs Taco Bell outlets at:

- Ballincollig, Co Cork
- Naas, Co Kildare
- Lusk North on the M1, Dublin
- Lisburn North, Co Antrim

**Seamus Stapleton**, who runs Applegreen's business in the Republic, said Taco Bell has been very popular with customers and that more locations will be added **next year**. That means more hiring is likely in 2027.

## What jobs are available?

New quick-service restaurants typically hire:

- Team members (food preparation, counter and drive-thru)
- Shift leaders and supervisors
- Restaurant and assistant managers

Many roles offer flexible and part-time hours, which suits students and people looking for a second job.

## How do I apply?

Look for Taco Bell roles on Applegreen's official careers channels, and check the company's social media for opening-day recruitment. Apply early: Swords opens on 3 November, and the remaining sites open within weeks.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Good entry-level option.** Fast-food jobs need little experience and offer training.
- **More openings to come.** Applegreen plans further Taco Bell sites in 2027.
- **Check your right to work.** Non-EU applicants need a valid permission to work in Ireland.
