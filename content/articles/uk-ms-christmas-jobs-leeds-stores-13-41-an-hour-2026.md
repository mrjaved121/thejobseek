**Marks & Spencer** has opened Christmas recruitment for its stores in **Leeds**, with seasonal roles paying **£13.41 an hour**. The drive, reported by The Hoot Leeds on **21 October 2026**, is part of M&S's wider UK-wide festive hiring.

## Stores hiring in Leeds

| Store | Area |
|---|---|
| Leeds city centre (flagship) | City centre |
| Moortown | North Leeds |
| Crown Point | South Leeds retail park |
| White Rose Shopping Centre | South-west Leeds |
| Pudsey | West Leeds |

M&S regional manager **Zeba Khan** said the business was looking forward to welcoming **more than 300 new colleagues** to its store teams. Her quote referred to Greater Manchester, so the exact number for Leeds itself has not been confirmed.

## Pay and roles

- **Pay:** £13.41 per hour
- **Contract:** Seasonal, covering the Christmas trading period
- **Training:** Provided, so you can get up to speed quickly

On its official Christmas careers page, M&S lists roles such as:

- **Customer Assistant** on the shop floor, tills and in the foodhall
- **Café Customer Assistant**
- **Stylist** in clothing and home
- **Visual Merchandising** support

Shift patterns are shown at the top of each job advert, and may change depending on what each store needs.

## Availability you will need

M&S is clear that Christmas staff must:

- Work **full time in the two weeks before Christmas**
- Be available on **22, 23 and 24 December**, the busiest days of the year

If you cannot commit to those dates, your application is unlikely to go through.

## How to apply

Apply on the official M&S Christmas jobs page at [jobs.marksandspencer.com/christmas](https://jobs.marksandspencer.com/christmas):

1. Fill in the online application (about 45 minutes). Have your **National Insurance number** and **right-to-work documents** ready.
2. Complete the **online assessment**.
3. Share your availability and book an **in-store interview**.

If you want more than one role at the same store, mention it at the interview rather than applying twice. If you applied for a similar M&S role in the last six months and were unsuccessful, you will need to wait until that period ends.

## What it means for job seekers

- **Apply now.** Christmas roles fill quickly once stores have trained their teams.
- **Be honest about availability.** Full-time cover before Christmas is a must.
- **Use it as a stepping stone.** Seasonal staff who perform well often get noticed when permanent roles come up.
- **Only apply on official sites.** M&S never charges a fee to apply.
