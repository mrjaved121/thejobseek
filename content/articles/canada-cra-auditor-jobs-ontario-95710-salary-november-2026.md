Accountants looking for **Ontario jobs** in the public sector should move fast. The **Canada Revenue Agency (CRA)** is hiring auditors at various locations across Ontario, with salaries of **$73,100 to $95,710 a year**. The posting closes on **Thursday 5 November 2026 at 11:59pm ET**.

## Which CRA jobs are open?

The roles are in the CRA's **Compliance Program Branch**:

| Position |
|---|
| Income Tax Auditor |
| Excise Tax Auditor |
| GST/HST Auditor |
| International Tax Auditor |
| Financial Reviewer |
| Business Intelligence Quality Assurance Officer |

## What is the salary?

The Ontario-based positions pay **$73,100 to $95,710 a year**, depending on the role and level.

## What will the job involve?

- Auditing tax returns, books, records and supporting documents
- Planning audits and researching legislation, case law and policy
- Writing reports and letters, and gathering asset information for debt collection
- Discussing technical issues, proposed reassessments and penalties with taxpayers or their representatives

Some roles may need overtime, evening, weekend or holiday work, shift work or travel.

## Who can apply?

- Canadian citizens and residents in Ontario
- A **post-secondary degree specialising in accounting**, or eligibility for a designation from a recognised professional accounting body
- Experience in audits or audit support at the CRA, or in auditing, tax preparation, accounting policy, tax advice or interpreting tax law for businesses, partnerships, corporations or trusts
- A **valid driver's licence**, because audits take place outside CRA offices

## How do I apply?

Apply through the Government of Canada jobs website (GC Jobs) by searching for Canada Revenue Agency auditor positions in Ontario. The deadline is **5 November 2026, 11:59pm ET**, so prepare your CV and answers to the screening questions today.
